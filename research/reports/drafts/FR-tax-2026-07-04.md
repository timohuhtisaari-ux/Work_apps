## 2. Fuel taxation

Since 2022 the former TICPE is the **"accise sur les énergies"**, codified in the Code des impositions sur les biens et services (CIBS), Book III, Title I, Chapter II (art. L312-1 ff.) — this is the French implementation of the Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC. Statutory tariffs are expressed in **€/MWh** (per-litre figures below are the widely used equivalents; the €/MWh value is the legal one). The loi de finances (LF) for 2026 was adopted late — **loi n° 2026-103 du 19 février 2026** — after a bridging period governed by a "loi spéciale" (see BOFiP rescrit BOI-RES-EAT-000240). Its art. 71(V) put new road-fuel tariffs into force on **1 March 2026**, following the end of the regions' power to levy a majoration (abolished by LF 2025, loi n° 2025-127; CIBS L312-39/L312-40 abrogated).

### Interaction with the mandate
TIRUERT is itself a **tax** (an incentive/penalty levy on fuel-excise payers who miss the renewable-incorporation percentage), not a quota with certificates — so France's "mandate" and "fuel taxation" overlap structurally. The excise reliefs for E85/B100/ED95 are cumulative with, and independent of, TIRUERT compliance. TIRUERT rates/targets and CarbuRe registry mechanics: see mandates report.

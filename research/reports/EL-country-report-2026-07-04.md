# Greece — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Greece (EL/GR)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Greece runs a **volumetric blending obligation** — diesel is **B7 (up to 7 % v/v FAME)** and petrol carries a mandatory **bioethanol/bio-ether share of 3.3 % v/v** — layered on an **annual pure-biodiesel allocation programme** (κατανομή αυτούσιου βιοντίζελ) under Law 3054/2002 art. 15A, plus a separate supplier GHG-intensity framework under Law 4062/2012 (6 % baseline vs 94.1 gCO2e/MJ). The blend levels are **static** — no year-by-year statutory step-up exists in national law, and the reported GHG step-up to 8 % in 2026 and any per-GJ penalty depend on the untransposed RED III bill and rest on conflicting secondary sources only. Fuel is taxed via the **EFK excise (€700/1000 L petrol, €410/1000 L diesel, unchanged since 2017)** with **no separate carbon tax and no biofuel tax relief** — Greece promotes biofuels through the allocation programme, not through excise. RED III is **not transposed**: Greece was referred to the CJEU on 29 April 2026 (with Portugal and Malta) with financial sanctions requested; a YPEN draft was in consultation 31 Mar–14 Apr 2026. A supplier's core obligations are to blend to B7/3.3 %, register for the annual biodiesel allocation with YPEN, and hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification with a PoS per consignment.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Greece runs a **volumetric blending obligation** plus an **annual "pure biodiesel"
allocation programme** (κατανομή αυτούσιου βιοντίζελ). The core act is **Law
3054/2002, Article 15A** ("Biofuels and other renewable fuels"). Diesel sold in
Greece is **B7** (up to 7 % v/v FAME); petrol carries a mandatory **bioethanol/
bio-ether share of 3.3 % v/v**. Biodiesel volumes are allocated annually to
eligible producers/importers by Joint Ministerial Decision (KYA). A separate
GHG-intensity/RES-T framework sits in **Law 4062/2012** (FQD Art. 7a / RED
transposition). Supervision: **YPEN** (Ministry of Environment & Energy);
fuel-tax/quality control via **AADE** chemical laboratories.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Annual biodiesel allocation (κατανομή αυτούσιου βιοντίζελ):** a "Programme for
  the Allocation of Biofuel Quantities" set each year by KYA (Ministers of
  Development / Environment-Energy and Rural Development). Under **art.15A para 5**,
  the Ministry (MEE/YPEN) issues the call for the following year's allocation **by
  the end of November** (CONFLICT RESOLVED — see Section 5; the older "15 April" guidance
  is superseded). Quantities are allocated to eligible operators via a formula
  weighing Greek energy-crop volumes and other criteria; 10 % of the allocated
  quantity is reserved for producers with university/research cooperation contracts. (S2, S4, S5, V7, V8)
- **Obligated parties:** refiners/importers releasing petrol and diesel; biodiesel
  beneficiaries must physically supply their allocated volumes within Greece. (S2)
- **Bioethanol:** mandatory 3.3 % v/v in petrol from 2020, increases require a
  Ministerial Decision. (S2, S3)
- **Certificates/registry:** Greece uses the allocation-quota model rather than a
  tradable-certificate market; no TdB/HBE-style certificate registry identified —
  **gap** (confirm whether a Guarantee-of-Origin/UDB registry now applies).
- Enforcement is partly via the fuel-quality/tax system (AADE chemical labs) —
  cross-reference the tax/sustainability sections; not researched in depth here.

## 2. Fuel taxation

Greece levies the **EFK — Ειδικός Φόρος Κατανάλωσης (excise duty)** on motor fuels under the **National Customs Code**, historically **Law 2960/2001** and **recodified as Law 5222/2025** — the national instrument implementing Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC, administered by **AADE** (Independent Authority for Public Revenue). Greece has **no separate CO2/carbon tax** on road fuels and **no reduced excise for biofuels**.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **EU minima context.** Greece's €700 (petrol) and €410 (diesel) sit well above the ETD minima (€359 petrol, €330 diesel), and are among the higher rates in the EU. `CONFIRMED` — S2 (Tax Foundation, 1 Jan 2026, re-read by corroborator).
- **No biofuel tax incentive.** Greece promotes biofuels through an **annual biodiesel allocation programme** (quantity-based) rather than an excise reduction — see Section 1. Since the dedicated pure-biodiesel EFK exemption lapsed at the end of 2007, biodiesel blended into road diesel bears the **full €410 EFK**; there is no Swedish/Finnish-style relief limited to volumes above the obligation because there is no biofuel excise relief at all.
- **VAT.** 24% VAT applies on the tax-inclusive price (S2).
- **Fuel-price relief via subsidy, not EFK.** For 2026 the government has used targeted "Fuel Pass"-type subsidies rather than cutting EFK. An opposition (SYRIZA) amendment to cut fuel EFK (to ~€359 petrol / €330 diesel) was **not adopted** — `PROPOSED` only (S7).
- **Agricultural diesel discount (separate from road fuel).** From **1 November 2026** Greece re-introduces an at-the-pump EFK discount of up to **€0.41/L for agricultural diesel** (app/QR-code based). This is an agricultural relief, not a road-transport rate change. `CONFIRMED` — S5.
- **ETS2** applies to road-transport fuels with allowance surrender from **2028** (2027 emissions; auctioning from 2027) under **Directive (EU) 2023/959 (ETS2, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC)**. [CORRECTION — the draft's "Regulation (EU) 2026/667" is the 2040 EU climate-target law, NOT ETS2.] `CONFIRMED` (EU-level). (V4, V5)

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Blend to the statutory minimums** — diesel **B7 (up to 7 % v/v FAME)** and petrol **3.3 % v/v bioethanol/bio-ether** at every pump (Law 3054/2002 art. 15A). → self / **AADE** chemical labs (quality control).
2. **Register for the annual pure-biodiesel allocation** with YPEN — the call for the following year issues **by end-November**; where claiming domestic energy-crop weighting, register grower contracts in the ΟΣΔΕ-linked control system. → **YPEN**.
3. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU widely used; REDcert-EU, 2BSvs…) covering the mass-balance chain, and issue a **PoS per consignment**. → certification body (e.g. EUROCERT / SGS / DNV).
4. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) per arts 32Ζ/32ΖΑ L.5106/2024 and document them per consignment. → self + auditor.
5. **Meet the Law 4062/2012 supplier GHG-intensity obligation** (6 % baseline vs 94.1 gCO2e/MJ) — note the reported 8 %-2026 step is UNCORROBORATED and tied to the pending RED III bill; watch for the transposition law. → self / **YPEN**.
6. **Do NOT rely on DAPEEP for biofuel sustainability** — DAPEEP handles electricity guarantees of origin only; biofuel sustainability sits with YPEN. → note.
7. **Respect EU high-ILUC feedstock rules** (palm 2019-level cap tapering to 0 by 2030); apply double counting only for qualifying Annex IX wastes/residues. → self + auditor.
8. **Onboard to the Union Database (UDB)** and record PoS/transaction data for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → **UDB**.
9. **Account for EFK at the full rate** — biodiesel/bioethanol carry the full diesel/petrol EFK (€410 / €700 per 1000 L); there is no biofuel excise relief. → **AADE**.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Carried-forward headline flags:**
- **Static blend obligation CONFIRMED:** diesel **B7 (7 % v/v)** + petrol **bioethanol/bio-ether 3.3 % v/v**; no year-by-year statutory step-up in national law.
- **GHG 8 %-2026 step and per-GJ penalty UNCORROBORATED.** The step-up path (6 %→8 %-2026→13 %-2030) and the non-compliance penalty rest on **secondary (ePURE) sources only**, and the ePURE editions **disagree on the penalty: €60/GJ (June 2024 overview, V11) vs ≈€43/GJ (2023 overview, S1)**. No primary confirmation located; both depend on the untransposed RED III bill. The 6 % baseline vs 94.1 gCO2e/MJ is the value in Law 4062/2012.
- **RED III NOT transposed** — Greece referred to the CJEU **29 Apr 2026** (with Portugal and Malta), financial sanctions requested. Only biomethane/hydrogen partially addressed (L.5215/2025); YPEN draft in consultation 31 Mar–14 Apr 2026; 29 %/14.5 % transport target and RFNBO/advanced sub-targets not enacted (`PROPOSED`).

**Resolved conflicts:**
- **Allocation-decision deadline:** the current statutory trigger is the **end-of-November** call for the following year's allocation (art. 15A para 5; confirmed by EU clean-energy-islands V7 and res-legal V8). The "15 April" figure was older/superseded guidance. Correction applied.
- **ETS2 legal basis:** Directive (EU) 2023/959, **not** Reg. (EU) 2026/667 (the 2040 climate-target law). ETS2 surrender begins 2028.
- **EFK rate table:** no conflict — the €700 / €410 pair is corroborated by three concordant independent Tier-2 sources and matches the stable post-2017 rate; retained CONFIRMED. (The Tier-1 AADE rate PDF is served as compressed binary and could not be parsed this pass.)

**Outstanding `PRIMARY-ONLY` / `UNCORROBORATED` items and gaps:**
- Current national **advanced-biofuel sub-quota** not located in primary law.
- Current-year (2025/2026) **biodiesel allocation volume** and the 2026 KYA reference not located (latest confirmed ~108,650 kL (2020) / 110,000 kL (2021)).
- **L.5106/2024 article text** (32Ζ/32ΖΑ) not read verbatim — confirm exact GHG thresholds and national-scheme mechanics.
- **Biofuel EFK treatment** (no relief) rests on Tier-2 sources — a current Tier-1 Customs Code confirmation still needed (`PRIMARY-ONLY / flag`); **biomethane/biogas** road-transport EFK treatment not located.
- Statutory **reporting deadlines/penalties**, **national Annex IX additions**, and **UCO audit extras** not located; **UDB Greek onboarding guidance** not found.
- No tradable-certificate registry found (allocation-quota model); confirm whether a GoO/UDB registry now applies.

**Registry corrections needed in `research/sources/member-states.md`:**
- **DAPEEP is NOT the biofuel-sustainability authority** — it covers electricity guarantees of origin, RES settlement and emissions allowances only. The competent authority for biofuel sustainability and the pure-biodiesel allocation is **YPEN**. Note this so the registry entry does not imply DAPEEP verifies biofuel sustainability.

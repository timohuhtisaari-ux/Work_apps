- **Country:** Czechia (CZ)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** biofuel-mandates-researcher

Scope note: excise/tax and sustainability certification are other agents' scope. The binding transport-fuel obligation is a **GHG-intensity reduction** duty on fuel suppliers under the Air Protection Act, administered by MŽP with the Customs Administration (celní správa) on the excise-reporting side (cross-reference only).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** the person who places petrol/diesel on the market for transport (the fuel supplier declaring for excise), under Act 201/2012 §20. (S1)
- **Administration:** **Ministry of the Environment (MŽP)** owns the sustainability/GHG framework; compliance reporting is tied to the excise/consumption declaration handled with the **Customs Administration (Celní správa)**. (S1, S8)
- **Compliance basis:** lifecycle GHG intensity of the annual fuel pool; advanced/waste feedstocks double-counted; sustainability certification per RED (other agent's scope).
- **Banking/borrowing, certificate trading:** not established in sources — GAP.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- The headline 2026/2030 figures (6.25% → 11% GHG; advanced → 5.5%; Annex IX-B cap 1.7%) come from MŽP's press release and consistent trade coverage but the **primary NV 189/2018 (2026) and Act 42/2025 texts were not machine-readable here** (e-sbírka/zakonyprolidi were JS-blocked or 403; the official PDFs could not be rendered — poppler unavailable). All GHG/advanced numbers are therefore **UNCORROBORATED against primary text** and must be verified verbatim by the corroborator against e-sbírka.
- **Gaps:** exact 2026 advanced start value; 2027–2029 GHG/advanced intermediate steps; crop cap; CZK penalty; whether any residual §19 volume blending mandate remains binding; banking/trading rules.

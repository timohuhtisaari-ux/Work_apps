### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Biofuels booked into the register must meet the **RED Art. 29 sustainability and GHG-saving criteria** (Besluit energie vervoer arts. 7–8; art. 7 reference "artikel 29, tweede tot en met zevende lid en tiende lid, van de richtlijn hernieuwbare energie" re-verified by corroborator in the 20-06-2026 consolidation); the RED II thresholds therefore apply unchanged: **≥50 %** (installations in operation on/before 5 Oct 2015), **≥60 %** (6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **≥65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021). `CONFIRMED` (cite EUR-Lex consolidated 2018/2001 in final report). (S3, S9, V3)
- **RFNBOs/hydrogen**: must meet the GHG-reduction thresholds of RED **Art. 29a** (70 %) and be certified by a voluntary scheme (Besluit arts. 9, 9a). `CONFIRMED` (S3, S9)
- Beyond the thresholds, the ERES design rewards **actual chain GHG performance**: ERE yield = volume × LHV × (94 − E) × factor / 1000, with E from RED default or actual values per Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/996. `CONFIRMED` (NEa) (S5, S10)

### 3.3 Certification schemes accepted

- The Netherlands maintains **no national scheme or national list**: sustainability may **only** be demonstrated via **voluntary schemes recognised by the European Commission** (18 schemes recognised as of 2026-07-04, incl. ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Better Biomass, SURE, RSB, CertifHy for RFNBOs). Designation works by reference in the Besluit ("een bij ministeriële regeling aangewezen vrijwillig systeem"). `CONFIRMED` (NEa duurzaamheid pages + Besluit art. 7/9a + DG ENER list). (S11, S3, S12)
- The **whole chain including the inboeker** must be certified; the inboeker must hold certification as **"trader with storage"** for the final storage location (AGP). `CONFIRMED` (NEa inboeken pages). (S5, S10)

### 3.4 Proof requirements for inboeken (booking-in)

- Only the **physical quantity** delivered may be booked (Regeling art. 6(1)); each booking must be covered by a **bewijs van duurzaamheid / Proof of Sustainability (PoS)** drawn from the **mass balance of the final storage location** and retained by the inboeker (Regeling art. 6(2); for RFNBOs a "bewijs van hernieuwbaarheid", art. 8(2)). `CONFIRMED` (wetten.overheid.nl Regeling + NEa). (S6, S10)
- Mass-balance reconciliation with physical stock at least **quarterly**; physical delivery evidenced by excise documents or invoice/payment with delivery address; fuel identity by **ISO/IEC 17025-accredited sampling/analysis** (exceptions: FAME, ethanol, ETBE), **C14 dating recommended for HVO/co-processed and advanced fuels**. `CONFIRMED` (NEa land-sector inboeken page). (S10)
- Eligible inboekers (liquid biofuels): NL trade-register companies holding an **accijnsgoederenplaats (AGP)** licence, registered consignees, or importers paying excise; separate rules for gaseous fuels, electricity, hydrogen; **inboekdienstverleners** may book on behalf of others. `CONFIRMED` (NEa). (S5)

### 3.5 National registry and Union Database (UDB)

- **Register Energie voor Vervoer (REV)**, operated by **NEa**. Who must hold accounts: **inboekers** (to book deliveries and receive ERE's), **obligated fuel suppliers** (BTV compliance accounts), and **traders** (trading accounts limited to energy-derivatives/credits traders; inactivity >12 months or breach can lead to freezing/closure, Besluit arts. 25–29). The **renewed REV (ERE-based) launches in phases: first release 29 June – 15 July 2026** (account opening), booking functionality from the second release, with retroactive booking of 2026 deliveries; account opening via the NEa Helpdesk. `CONFIRMED` (NEa + Besluit; phased-release detail added in corroboration pass). (S5, S3, S13, V4)
- **UDB obligations:** the Regeling energie vervoer (consolidated 20 Jun 2026) contains **art. 3b**; corroborator verified the text verbatim: lid 1 — *"De marktdeelnemer, bedoeld in artikel 9.7.1.3, onderdeel a, van de wet, voert tijdig en correct de gegevens, bedoeld in dit onderdeel a, in de Uniedatabank in."* — i.e. the duty is **scoped to marktdeelnemers as defined in Wm art. 9.7.1.3(a)** (which carries the substantive UDB data-set definition); lid 3 requires **certification bodies under the designated (voluntary) schemes to verify the accuracy of the entered data**. Commencement: with the Regeling consolidation of 20-06-2026. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (verbatim from wetten.overheid.nl; the ministry "Overzicht van wijzigingen" PDF was located but is not machine-readable in this toolchain — no independent corroboration yet). (S6, V5)
- NEa's UDB development page (earlier guidance): UDB live per RED III since Nov 2024 but **the mandatory-use date for companies is not yet fixed** (to be coordinated EC/member states); **no technical REV–UDB link before 2026**. EU level: liquid chains operational, gas in pilot, mandatory-use timeline still under discussion. `CONFIRMED` for EU status (EC UDB page); `PRIMARY-ONLY` for the REV-link statement (NEa page retrieved via search excerpts; direct fetch returned 404 — page likely moved). (S14, S15)

## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope, draft)

1. Obtain **EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (e.g. ISCC EU) for every chain link, incl. the inboeker as **"trader with storage"** at the final AGP storage location. [NEa/EC; CONFIRMED]
2. Open an **REV account** with the NEa (inboeker and/or obligated-supplier account; new ERE-based REV live in phases from 29 June 2026; 2026 deliveries bookable retroactively). [NEa; CONFIRMED]
3. Hold a **bewijs van duurzaamheid (PoS)** from the storage-site mass balance for **every booked consignment**; keep quarterly mass-balance reconciliations, excise/invoice delivery proof, and ISO 17025 analyses (C14 for HVO) on file for 5 years. [NEa; CONFIRMED]
4. Exclude **palm- and soy-based** biofuels from NL bookings unless certified low-ILUC (Wm art. 9.7.4.2(1)(d); national exclusion since 1 Jan 2022); respect ERE category caps (conventional 1.2 %, Annex IX-B 4.3 % land sector). [NEa; CONFIRMED]
5. Contract an NEa-recognised verifier (**Control Union, Dekra, NORMEC QS**) and complete the **inboekverificatie before 1 May** each year (2 % materiality). [NEa; CONFIRMED]
6. Book prior-year deliveries by the **last working day before 1 March**; obligated suppliers: hold sufficient **ERE's by 1 April**. [NEa; CONFIRMED]
7. Start/maintain **Union Database** registration via your certification scheme and comply with Regeling art. 3b (timely, correct UDB data entry by marktdeelnemers per Wm art. 9.7.1.3(a); scheme certification bodies verify entered data); watch NEa announcements for the REV–UDB link and the EU mandatory-use date. [NEa/EC; PRIMARY-ONLY]
8. Monitor NEa guidance during the 2026 transition (HBE jaarafsluiting 2025 completed under old rules; ERES rules apply to 2026 deliveries; first BTV settlement in 2027). [NEa; CONFIRMED]

# Croatia — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Croatia (HR)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Croatia runs an **energy-share "placing-on-the-market" obligation** on petrol/diesel distributors: obligated parties ("obveznici") must place a minimum share of renewable energy in transport, with the national goal set in the NECP and implemented through the *Zakon o biogorivima za prijevoz* and *Pravilnik* NN 88/21. The exact **2026 national-goal percentage is unresolved** — sources render the 8.8% / 9.5% / 10.3% sequence with conflicting year alignment and the primary Pravilnik table could not be retrieved (nn.hr returned HTTP 503). What is confirmed from the Act itself is the advanced-biofuel (Annex IX-A) path of **0.2% (2022) / 1% (2025) / 3.5% (2030)** and the **1.7% Annex IX-B cap**. Fuel taxation offers **no biofuel-specific relief** — biofuels are taxed as their fossil equivalent, diesel excise sits at the EU minimum (330 EUR/1000 L) and petrol excise floats in the low-450s EUR/1000 L, re-set by Government decree every 14 days. The top obligations on a supplier are: place sustainable biofuel meeting the annual energy share, carry a per-consignment sustainability certificate ("potvrda") backed by an EU-recognised voluntary scheme, and file the annual compliance report to the Ministry by 28 February. **RED III is not transposed** — Croatia is at the letter-of-formal-notice stage (Sept 2024).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated:** distributors placing diesel and petrol on the Croatian market ("obveznik"), per Zakon Art. 4(33). [1]
- **Compliance:** obligated parties must place biofuels/renewable energy meeting the energy values set by the monitoring methodology; only sustainability-compliant biofuels count toward the goal (regardless of feedstock origin). [1]
- **HROTE role:** the Croatian Energy Market Operator (Hrvatski operator tržišta energije) administers incentives for biofuel/RES production, collecting fees from electricity end-customers/suppliers and fuel distributors and channelling them to producers. HROTE is the fee-collection/incentive administrator; the Ministry (economy/energy, mingo.gov.hr) owns the obligation and NECP. [Registry note; corroborate exact HROTE competence — PRIMARY fetch of mingo/HROTE pages 503'd]
- **Non-compliance route:** enforced via the special environmental charge (a tax-style mechanism) — see also fuel-tax section for interaction. [5][8]
- Banking/borrowing and certificate trading: no tradable-certificate scheme identified (obligation is a direct energy-share placing obligation, not a certificate market). Banking/borrowing rules not located — GAP.

## 2. Fuel taxation

> **Key structural note:** Croatia does **not** hold a fixed statutory motor-fuel excise. Under the retail-price-regulation regime, the Government re-sets the trošarina on petrol and diesel **every 14 days** by decree ("Uredba o izmjeni Uredbe o visini trošarine na energente i električnu energiju"), together with the maximum retail prices and supplier premiums, to smooth pump prices as wholesale (Platts) costs move. Diesel excise has been held **at/near the EU minimum (330 EUR/1000 L)** through 2026; petrol excise floats in the low-450s EUR/1000 L. Any single figure is a snapshot of the current 14-day window. Croatia is euro-area since 1 Jan 2023; excise expressed in EUR per 1000 L.

**Mandate interaction:** Croatia is an obligation/quota market (biofuel placing obligation), so — unlike SE/FI — there is **no excise incentive tied to volumes above the blending mandate**. Excise policy and the biofuel mandate operate on separate tracks.

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Zakon o trošarinama** (Excise Duties Act, NN 106/2018 and amendments; consolidated text on zakon.hr, z/545). No biofuel-specific notified state-aid decision identified (the separate "plavi dizel" reduced rate for agriculture/fisheries is not a biofuel measure).

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **Biofuels/bioliquids:** RED thresholds apply via the methodology rulebook — **50 %** (installations operating before 5 Oct 2015), **60 %** (start 6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021); **70 %** for RFNBOs/recycled-carbon fuels. Not quoted numerically from the HR rulebook text this pass (nn.hr 503) → framework-level. [PRIMARY-ONLY / framework — S1, S6]
- **Supplier FQD target:** ≥6 % lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction vs 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ (Zakon Art. 29.a). [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- Croatia accepts **EU-recognised voluntary national/international schemes** ("dobrovoljni nacionalni ili međunarodni sustavi") as evidence of sustainability, per the Pravilnik and Zakon Art. 17.a; use of such a scheme relieves the operator of separately proving the elements the scheme covers. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1, S2, S3]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 schemes as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG, Better Biomass**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6]
- **No separate stand-alone Croatian national certification scheme** was identified beyond the Pravilnik's own verification framework; verification of independence of information is required and, for feedstock grown outside Croatia, HROTE/the authority reviews sustainability certificates and land-use proof. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2]
- **Proof of sustainability per consignment:** a **certificate ("potvrda")** must accompany each biofuel consignment proving the batch meets the sustainability requirements; the obligor placing biofuel/renewable energy on the market must be able to demonstrate compliance regardless of whether the feedstock was grown in Croatia or imported. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register as an obligated party ("obveznik")** with the Ministry of economy/energy (mingo.gov.hr) if placing petrol/diesel on the Croatian market — you carry the annual energy-share placing obligation.
2. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, or any of the 18 DG ENER-listed schemes) covering each biofuel batch. → certification body.
3. **Ensure every biofuel consignment is accompanied by a valid sustainability certificate ("potvrda")** demonstrating the five sustainability + GHG-saving criteria, carried through to you as obligor. → self / upstream supplier.
4. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 % by installation start date; RFNBO 70 %) and the supplier **≥6 % FQD** lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction vs 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ; document per batch. → self + verifier.
5. **Place enough sustainable biofuel/renewable energy to meet the annual national goal** and the sub-targets — advanced (Annex IX-A) ≥1% (2025) rising to ≥3.5% (2030); respect the **1.7% Annex IX-B cap** and the **7% crop cap**; apply double counting only where allowed. → self.
6. **Respect the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out** (Zakon Art. 8(4)(8): capped at 2019 level → 0 % by 2030 unless certified low-ILUC). → self + verifier.
7. **File the annual compliance report to the Ministry by 28 February** (prior-year data, Zakon Art. 26); Ministry confirmation follows by 30 April (Art. 27). → Ministry of economy/energy.
8. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme and record in-scope transactions; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension (mandatory-use date pending). → UDB / voluntary scheme.
9. **Non-compliance exposure:** administrative fines under Zakon Art. 33 plus the special environmental charge for failing to place the required biofuel. → Ministry / environmental-charge authority.
10. **Prepare for ETS2** as a regulated road-transport fuel supplier: monitoring/reporting builds toward the 1 Jan 2028 start and first allowance surrender 31 May 2029. → competent ETS2 authority.

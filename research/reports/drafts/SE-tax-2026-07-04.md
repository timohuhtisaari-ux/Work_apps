## 2. Fuel taxation

Sweden levies **energiskatt** (energy tax) and **koldioxidskatt** (CO2 tax) on motor
fuels under **Lagen (1994:1776) om skatt på energi (LSE)**, the national act
implementing Directive 2003/96/EC. 2026 is exceptional: the ordinary 1 January
rates (frozen at 2025 levels, no indexation) are overlaid by **two temporary
cuts** adopted in spring 2026 — an energy-tax cut (1 May–30 Sep 2026, to the EU
minimum level) and a deeper CO2-tax cut of 2.40 SEK/L (1 Jul–30 Nov 2026, below
the ETD minimum, requiring an EU derogation), motivated by fuel-price increases
from the war in Iran. **As of the report date (4 July 2026) both cuts apply.**

### 2.2 Interaction with the mandate (cross-reference)

Tax relief and the **reduktionsplikt** are mutually exclusive by design (a
state-aid condition): only fuels **outside** the reduction obligation (pure and
high-blend biofuels, biogas) can be tax-relieved; biofuel volumes blended to
meet the obligation are taxed at full fossil rates. Current reduktionsplikt
levels are the mandates researcher's scope — cross-reference, not duplicated
here.

### 2.3 Conflicts and gaps

- **SFS numbers for the two temporary 2026 acts and for the 2026 rate
  regulation still not captured.** Corroborator identified the enacting vehicles:
  cut no. 1 = **prop. 2025/26:236 / bet. 2025/26:FiU48** (decided 22 Apr 2026);
  cut no. 2 (CO2 −2.40 SEK/L) = the May 2026 regeringen promemoria "Ytterligare
  tillfälligt sänkt skatt på bensin och diesel" enacted for the half-year. The
  promulgated **SFS 2026:xxx** gazette numbers were not printed on the Skatteverket/
  regeringen/riksdagen HTML pages and svenskforfattningssamling.se PDFs are not
  parseable in this environment. Rates and periods are otherwise CONFIRMED.
- **Council implementing decision** (Art. 19 ETD derogation) for the
  below-minimum CO2 cut: regeringen (May 2026) called it a prerequisite and
  cited Commission clearance; Skatteverket applies the rates from 1 July, so it
  evidently completed — **the Council/Commission decision reference itself was
  not located** (still a gap; not a CONFLICT).

# Colombia — Biofuel Regulation Headline Report
**Date:** 2026-07-15 | **Researched by:** Non-EU biofuel mandate researcher

---

## 1. Instrument type & 2026 headline obligation

**Volume-based blending mandate (percentage of transport fuel by volume).**
- **Gasoline:** minimum 10% ethanol (E10) — established April 2021 (Resolution 40111/2021)
- **Diesel:** minimum 13% biodiesel (B13) — raised from B12 to B13 in July 2024

These are mandatory compliance requirements enforced by the Ministry of Mines and Energy (MinMinas). All fuel suppliers distributing gasoline or diesel in Colombia must meet these blends.

---

## 2. Trajectory / 2030 target

**Recent increase, no next step legislated.** 
- B13 was adopted July 2024 (increase from B12), following successful regional pilots in Antioquia and Meta departments.
- E10 has been stable since April 2021 (temporary reduction to E4 in March 2021 was reversed in September 2021).
- No legislation documents a further increase beyond B13 for 2026–2030.
- The National Development Plan 2022–2026 contains no modifications to biofuel mandates post-2026.

---

## 3. Advanced/sub-quotas if any

Not documented. Colombia's mandate does not specify feedstock-based or advanced-biofuel sub-targets (e.g., waste-based or Annex IX equivalents). All ethanol and biodiesel count equally toward compliance.

---

## 4. Fuel-tax treatment of biofuels

**Partial fiscal support:**
- **Biodiesel:** Exempt from Value Added Tax (VAT) — classified among exempted goods accounting for ~47% of VAT exemptions in Colombia
- **Ethanol:** Subject to a **USD 0.0665/kg anti-dumping duty** on U.S. denatured ethanol (imposed 2020, extended March 2023 for five years; review available March 2026)
- No preferential excise duty on biofuel-blended fuels (standard excise applies to all motor fuels)

MinMinas is developing a **new pricing methodology** for ethanol and biodiesel (announced October 2025) to support producer margins, but the mechanism is not yet finalized.

---

## 5. Sustainability/certification regime

**Incorporated into NDC commitments; limited domestic scheme documentation.**
- Ethanol production from sugarcane and biodiesel from palm and other sources are recognized in Colombia's **Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC)** to the Paris Agreement.
- No dedicated domestic sustainability certification scheme or registry is documented in official sources (as of 2026-07-15).
- International schemes (e.g., ISCC EU for export compliance) may be used by Colombian producers, but no mandatory national certification is in force for domestic blending compliance.

## 8. Five-line summary

1. **Obligation type:** Volume-based blending mandate: E10 (gasoline) and B13 (diesel).
2. **Current level (2026):** E10 since April 2021; B13 since July 2024 (increased from B12).
3. **Next legislated change:** No step-up legislated; pricing methodology reform announced October 2025 (mechanism pending).
4. **RED III status:** Not applicable — Colombia is not an EU member.
5. **Key status flags:** CONFIRMED B13 adoption July 2024; ethanol CVD review opportunity March 2026; biodiesel VAT exemption active; new pricing mechanism in development; domestic sustainability certification scheme not documented.

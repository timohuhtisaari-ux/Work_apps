## 3. Sustainability & compliance requirements

### 3.1 RED II/III transposition instruments
| Item | Finding | Legal basis | Confidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary transposing acts | **Law on Energy from Renewable Sources** (Atsinaujinančių išteklių energetikos įstatymas, AIEĮ) — carries the biofuel/liquid-bioproduct sustainability & GHG-saving criteria (**Article 38**) — together with the **Law on Alternative Fuels** (Alternatyviųjų degalų įstatymas, 2021), which sets the fuel-supplier renewable-in-transport obligations and the accounting system. | AIEĮ Art. 38; Law on Alternative Fuels | PRIMARY-ONLY (Art. 38 via infolex index; e-tar/e-seimas texts are SPA/403) |
| Sustainability-criteria article | **AIEĮ Art. 38** = "Biofuel and liquid-bioproduct sustainability and GHG-emission-saving criteria"; establishes the checking + certification procedure for compliance. | AIEĮ Art. 38 | PRIMARY-ONLY (infolex.lt/ta/145997:str38) |
| RED III (2023/2413) status | **Transposed** — Lithuania adopted a RED III implementation package in **June 2025** (amendments to AIEĮ and related laws); among the earlier movers. Transport RES target 29% by 2030. | IEA policy tracker; ceenergynews | CONFIRMED (IEA + trade press) / PRIMARY-ONLY on exact act IDs |

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally
| Item | Value | Basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Biofuel GHG-saving thresholds | EU-harmonised RED thresholds (≥50/60/65% by installation start date; RFNBO/RCF ≥70%) applied via AIEĮ Art. 38 + IR 2022/996 | AIEĮ Art. 38 | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Supplier renewable obligation (cross-ref, mandates scope) | From 2025, renewables ≥ **8.6 percentage points** of petrol+diesel energy value, of which ≥1 pp advanced biofuels/RFNBO; trajectory to ~16.8% renewable share by 2030 | Law on Alternative Fuels | PRIMARY-ONLY (secondary) |

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes
| Item | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Liquid transport biofuels | Compliance with sustainability criteria proven via **EU Commission-recognised voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, etc.); suppliers/producers must be certified under one of these schemes. | CONFIRMED (AIEĮ + DG ENER list; national funding rules require a recognised-scheme certificate within 2 months of project completion) |
| National scheme (biomass fuels) | **"RED BP"** national voluntary scheme for **solid biomass fuels** sustainability & GHG criteria, operated by **BALTPOOL** (appointed 28 Apr 2022 by amendment to the Law on Energy from Renewable Sources; launched 2 May 2023). **Scope = biomass fuels, NOT liquid transport biofuels.** FSC/PEFC/SURE/SBP/KZR INiG certificates exempt suppliers from the year-end financial audit under RED BP. | CONFIRMED (Baltpool + EU voluntary-schemes context) |
| Proof / mass balance | Per-consignment proof-of-sustainability document stating DAEI quantity in energy value (MJ); mass balance per IR 2022/996. | PRIMARY-ONLY |

### 3.4 National registry & Union Database (UDB)
| Item | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| National accounting/registry | **DAEI accounting-units system** ("Degalai iš atsinaujinančių energijos išteklių" apskaitos vienetų sistema), operated by **BALTPOOL** (energy-resources exchange), launched **1 Dec 2021**, mandatory data submission from **1 Jan 2022**. DAEI units are issued for renewable fuels sold on the domestic market and used to assess suppliers' obligation fulfilment. | CONFIRMED (Baltpool/Ministry origin; 15min + e-tar rules act) |
| Who must register | **All fuel suppliers liable for excise duty** operating in Lithuania **plus transport-sector natural-gas suppliers** must register and submit data. | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Oversight / data flow | Monitoring & enforcement: **State Energy Regulatory Council (VERT)** and the **State Consumer Rights Protection Service**; data flows to Amber Grid, the State Tax Inspectorate (VMI) and Customs. Policy owner: **Ministry of Energy**; statistical monitoring/publication by the **Lithuanian Energy Agency (ENA)**. | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Union Database (UDB) | EU UDB (IR (EU) 2022/996) applies to Lithuanian liquid/gaseous transport-fuel operators (mandatory since 2024); the national DAEI system operates alongside the EU UDB. No LT-specific UDB onboarding guidance located. | PRIMARY-ONLY (EU basis) / UNCORROBORATED (LT-specific) |

## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope) — Lithuania
1. Register in the **DAEI accounting-units system (BALTPOOL)** and submit fuel data (renewable share, type, origin, energy value) — mandatory for excise-liable fuel suppliers and transport natural-gas suppliers. [BALTPOOL/VERT] — `CONFIRMED`
2. Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification (ISCC EU / REDcert / 2BSvs, etc.) for each biofuel consignment (AIEĮ Art. 38). [Ministry of Energy] — `CONFIRMED`
3. Provide a per-consignment proof-of-sustainability document stating the DAEI quantity in MJ; maintain mass balance per IR 2022/996. — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
4. Meet applicable GHG-saving thresholds (50/60/65%; RFNBO 70%). — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
5. Register/record transactions in the EU **Union Database (UDB)** for transport-fuel operators, alongside DAEI. — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
6. (Solid-biomass suppliers only) Certify under **RED BP** or an accepted scheme (FSC/PEFC/SURE/SBP/KZR INiG) to satisfy biomass-fuel sustainability. [BALTPOOL] — `CONFIRMED`

### 4-line summary — Lithuania
- **Transposition status:** RED II via Law on Energy from Renewable Sources (Art. 38) + Law on Alternative Fuels; **RED III transposed in a June 2025 package** (early mover), transport RES target 29% by 2030.
- **Certification regime:** Liquid transport biofuels use EU-recognised voluntary schemes (ISCC EU/REDcert/2BSvs); separate national **RED BP** scheme (BALTPOOL) covers **solid biomass fuels only**.
- **Registry/UDB:** National **DAEI accounting-units system operated by BALTPOOL** (mandatory data from 1 Jan 2022; oversight by VERT + Consumer Rights Service); EU UDB obligations apply to transport-fuel operators alongside DAEI.
- **Flags:** e-seimas/e-tar/IEA primary texts unreadable (SPA/403) — Art. 38, DAEI rules and exact annual reporting deadline unverified; RED III amending-act IDs to be confirmed; ENA (statistics) vs BALTPOOL (registry) roles to re-confirm.

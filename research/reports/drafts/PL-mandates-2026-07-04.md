### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated entities ("podmioty realizujące NCW"):** domestic producers and importers/intra-EU acquirers of liquid fuels and liquid biofuels who place motor petrol/diesel on the market or use them for own needs (Act art. 23 ust. 1). (S9, S11)
- **Registry / administration:** **KOWR** (Krajowy Ośrodek Wsparcia Rolnictwa, Director General) maintains the **register of biocomponent producers (rejestr wytwórców biokomponentów)** and the register of NCW-realising entities, receives quarterly producer reports (within 45 days of quarter-end), and notifies **URE** of register changes within 7 days. Operating without registration carries a PLN 5,000 fine. (S11, S12)
- **Enforcement / reporting:** obligated entities file **quarterly NCW reports to the President of URE**; a new quarterly report template applies from 31 Jan 2026 (Reg. of the Minister of Climate & Environment of 6 Nov 2025, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1618). URE verifies achievement and imposes penalties. (S13, S14)
- **Compliance unit of account:** energy content (calorific value) of biocomponents/renewable fuels; advanced feedstocks and certain wastes count double.
- **Banking/borrowing:** not established in sources — GAP.

- **RED III transposition status: NOT transposed (open infringement).** The adopted Act of 21 Feb 2025 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 303, published 12 Mar 2025) transposes **RED II (2018/2001)** only; its own recital cites Directive 2018/2001. The EUR-Lex NIM page for RED III (2023/2413) lists no notified Polish RED III measure. Poland received a **letter of formal notice for non-transposition of RED III on 24 Jul 2025** (part of a 26-member-state batch; deadline was 21 May 2025). Poland was **not escalated in the June-2026 infringement package** — but only Cyprus was escalated there (additional reasoned opinion), so non-escalation does **not** indicate closure; the formal-notice case remains open. `CONFIRMED` (RED III not yet transposed; open infringement). (S15, S4, S5, S17, S18) — *Corroborator note: this aligns the PL-sustainability draft, which had incorrectly inferred from the June-2026 package that the gap was "substantially closed."*
- **PROPOSED:** the government RED III transposition bill **UC106** ("projekt ustawy o zmianie ustawy o biokomponentach i biopaliwach ciekłych oraz ustawy o OZE"), published at RCL **14 Jan 2026**, would raise the RES-in-transport target to **29% by 2030**, restructure sub-targets, and raise the crop cap from 6.1% to 6.2%. Planned Council-of-Ministers adoption Q2 2026; planned entry into force **1 Jan 2027**. Not adopted. `PROPOSED`. (S16, S18)
- **EU cross-cutting:** ETS2 compliance obligation for road-transport/buildings fuels starts **1 Jan 2028** (Reg. (EU) 2026/667, OJ 18 Mar 2026, postponing ETS2 by one year), with auctioning from 2027 — applies to Polish fuel suppliers alongside the NCW. `CONFIRMED`. (S20)

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **Resolved conflict (2030 headline):** secondary reports circulated two 2030 figures — 14.9% and 29%. The **14.9%** figure is the adopted 2025 (RED II) schedule (S3, S7 both explicit); the **29%** figure belongs to the separate, not-yet-adopted RED III draft UC106. A UC106 summary paraphrased the current 2030 statutory value as "14.5%," but the primary-adjacent RED II coverage (S3) states 14.9% verbatim — treated as a rounding/paraphrase artifact, not a genuine conflict.
- **Resolved conflict (RED III status):** the PL-sustainability draft inferred "gap substantially closed"; corroboration establishes Poland is under an **open RED III formal-notice infringement (24 Jul 2025)** with transposition still only a draft (UC106). Both drafts now aligned to "not transposed."
- **Gaps:** 2026 advanced-biofuel milestone value; Annex IX-B cap; exact opłata zastępcza / administrative-penalty formula in PLN; whether the Council of Ministers has set a reduction coefficient other than 0.85 for 2026–2027; banking/borrowing rules. Primary art. 23/23b figures remain `PRIMARY-ONLY` from the consolidated text (lexlege rendering); ISAP was CAPTCHA-blocked and the official Dz.U. PDF could not be rendered locally (no poppler), so the year-by-year art. 23/23b table could not be read verbatim from the gazette PDF.

## Executive summary

Austria imposes a **dual obligation** on fuel suppliers under the **Kraftstoffverordnung
2012 (KVO)**: (1) a **Substitutionsverpflichtung** — a minimum energy share of biofuels in
petrol and diesel — and (2) a **Treibhausgas-(THG-)Minderungsverpflichtung** — a rising
GHG-intensity reduction requirement. Reported current values: petrol (Otto) sub-quota
**3.4 %**, diesel sub-quota **6.3 %**, overall substitution **5.75 %** (energy); the GHG
reduction requirement is **7 % (2024–2025), 8 % from 2026**, rising to **13 % by 2030**.
Non-compliance penalties (Ausgleichsbeträge): **600 €/t CO₂-eq** (GHG target) and
**43 €/GJ** (substitution shortfall). Compliance is documented via the **elNa** registry
(Umweltbundesamt). **RED III is NOT yet transposed** (deadline 21 May 2025 missed; a KVO
Novelle is in Begutachtung/consultation) — Austria is effectively in transposition delay.
EU-wide cross-reference: ETS2 compliance obligation starts 1 Jan 2028 (Reg. (EU) 2026/667),
auctioning 2027.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** "Inverkehrbringer" — those placing fossil petrol/diesel for road
  transport into free circulation in Austria. [S1]
- **Compliance transfer:** obligations (substitution, advanced, GHG) can be contractually
  transferred to third parties ("Quotenpooler"), incl. crediting renewable electricity used
  in EVs; obligated party should primarily meet targets itself. [S1, S5]
- **Registry:** **elNa** (elektronischer Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis) operated by the
  **Umweltbundesamt** — used for sustainability proofs and quota accounting. [S3, S5]
- **Competence authority:** Federal ministry with the climate/energy/mobility portfolio
  (BMK / BMIMI, name has changed across governments) issues the KVO; Umweltbundesamt runs
  elNa. [S3]

- **RED III transposition status:** **NOT transposed** as of 2026-07-04. Transposition
  deadline was 21 May 2025 (missed). A **Novelle der Kraftstoffverordnung 2012** to
  transpose RED III for transport has been listed in the Austrian consultation
  (Begutachtung) process but is **not yet adopted**; EUR-Lex NIM shows no Austrian RED III
  transport measure notified in the excerpt reviewed. RED III transport targets to be
  implemented: ≥1 % advanced biofuels/biogas/RFNBO from 2025 and a combined ≥5.5 % by 2030,
  and the 29 % RES-T / 14.5 % GHG-intensity 2030 headline. **Status: PROPOSED / in
  infringement risk.** [S4, S6]

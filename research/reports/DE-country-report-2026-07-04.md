## Executive summary

Germany's transport biofuel obligation is a **GHG-intensity reduction quota** (Treibhausgasminderungs-Quote, "THG-Quote", §§ 37a–37h BImSchG), not a volume or energy blend mandate: obligated parties must cut the lifecycle CO2eq intensity of the petrol and diesel they place on the market by **12 % in compliance year 2026**, measured against a 94.1 kg CO2eq/GJ fossil reference. Germany's RED III transport transposition (Zweites Gesetz zur Weiterentwicklung der THG-Quote, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 163, in force 6/7 June 2026) steepens that path sharply — **17.5 % in 2027 rising to 65 % by 2040** — and adds a binding RFNBO minimum sub-quota (0.1 % in 2026 → 10 % by 2040), a rising advanced-biofuels sub-quota (2 % in 2026 → 9 % by 2040), abolition of advanced-biofuel double counting (from obligation year 2027), and a POME exclusion from 2027. On the tax side, **biofuels get no excise advantage**: the pure-biofuel relief was repealed effective 1 Jan 2018, and E85/FAME/HVO are taxed at their fossil-equivalent rate (petrol EUR 654.50/1000 L, diesel EUR 470.40/1000 L) — the entire biofuel incentive sits in the quota, with the only renewable tax benefit being a zero CO2 emission factor for sustainable biomass under the national ETS (nEHS/BEHG). The top obligations on a fuel supplier are therefore: file the annual THG-quota declaration to the Biokraftstoffquotenstelle (HZA Frankfurt (Oder)) by 1 June; certify every chain link up to the last interface under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU / REDcert-EU) and carry a valid Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis in Nabisy for each consignment; and, newly under § 37i BImSchG, report volumes, options and emissions into the Union Database. Note that several supporting values carry residual flags (RFNBO multiplier tail, E10/B7 blend-wall figures, 2027 nEHS price mechanism) carried into Section 5.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Germany operates a **GHG-intensity reduction quota (Treibhausgasminderungs-Quote, "THG-Quote")** under §§ 37a–37h Bundes-Immissionsschutzgesetz (BImSchG), implemented by the 38. BImSchV (crediting rules, caps, advanced sub-quota) and the 37. BImSchV (RFNBOs/co-processing/ReFuelEU crediting; since 2026 also the RFNBO minimum sub-quota, § 3b). The framework was substantially amended by the **Zweites Gesetz zur Weiterentwicklung der Treibhausgasminderungs-Quote** (act of 1 June 2026, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 163, published 5 June 2026), which is Germany's RED III transport transposition and applies from compliance year 2026. **Entry into force is split** (corroborator, resolved from the buzer change register + dejure version notes): the amendments to **§ 37d and the new § 37m BImSchG took effect 6 June 2026**; the **bulk of the act — §§ 37a–37c, 37e, 37g–37l BImSchG and the amendments to the 37./38. BImSchV — took effect 7 June 2026**. (The BGBl. PDF itself is not renderable in this toolchain; the split is confirmed by two independent consolidation services: buzer.de version list "changes of 06.06.2026 (§ 37d, § 37m) and 07.06.2026 (§§ 37a–c etc.)" and dejure.org "in Kraft getreten am 07.06.2026" for § 37a. The teammates' 06.06 vs 07.06 discrepancy is thereby explained — both dates exist, for different articles.)

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **No certificate/registry system.** Compliance is demonstrated in an annual declaration to the **Biokraftstoffquotenstelle** (Fachgebiet Biokraftstoffquote at Hauptzollamt Frankfurt (Oder), seat Cottbus) by **1 June of the year following the compliance year** (§ 37c (1) BImSchG). If no report is filed, the authority estimates volumes as an irrebuttable basis (§ 37c (3)). The mandate is administered inside the customs/energy-tax machinery — *see fuel-tax section for the Energiesteuergesetz interface*.
- **Quota trading:** obligations may be transferred to third parties by written contract (§ 37a (6)–(7) BImSchG) — the German "quota trade" is bilateral contract-based (e.g. sale of GHG reductions from charging electricity, biomethane, HVO), not exchange-traded units.
- **Banking:** over-fulfilment of the main quota may, on application, be credited to the **following** compliance year (§ 37a (8) BImSchG). Advanced-sub-quota surpluses may likewise be carried to the next year on application (§ 14 (4) 38. BImSchV).
- **Anti-fraud freeze (2024 act, still operative):** § 4a 38. BImSchV bans carry-over of 2024 surpluses into 2025 and of 2025 surpluses into 2026 (both for the main quota and the advanced sub-quota); 2024/2025 surpluses may instead be credited to **2027** on application by 15 Apr 2028. Third-party fulfilment options from 2024 cannot serve 2025 contracts, nor 2025 options for 2026.
- **UER phased out:** upstream emission reductions were a compliance option only through 2024/2025 (zoll.de: "through 2024/2025"; § 37a (5) listing shows the option time-limited) — part of the response to the UER/China-biodiesel fraud cases. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **On-site inspections:** from **2027**, mandatory on-site inspections (including by state auditors) for advanced biofuels and RFNBOs to be quota-eligible (§ 4b 38. BImSchV; adopted 2026 act). *Certification details are the sustainability agent's scope (Biokraft-NachV, Nabisy).*
- Eligible biofuels must meet Biokraft-NachV sustainability requirements and DIN fuel standards (zoll.de) — *cross-reference: sustainability section*.
- **New § 37i BImSchG (UDB reporting):** obligated parties must enter fossil volumes, compliance options used and GHG emissions into the **Union Database** after 1 June of the year following the obligation year (identified during corroboration from Drs. 21/4083 search extracts; consolidated text not fetched — `PRIMARY-ONLY`, cross-reference sustainability section).

**Interaction with the mandate:** Germany gives biofuels no excise advantage; the entire incentive sits in the GHG-reduction quota (THG-Quote, §§ 37a–37h BImSchG — mandates section). The only tax-side advantage for renewables is the zero emission factor for sustainable biomass under the BEHG/nEHS CO2 price (and, until end-2026, the reduced natural-gas rate that biomethane shares with fossil CNG). There is no Sweden/Finland-style relief restricted to volumes beyond the blending obligation, because there is no relief at all.

(S11)

### 3.4 Proof of sustainability per consignment

- Proof document: **Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis** (and **Nachhaltigkeits-Teilnachweis** for split quantities), issued **electronically in Nabisy** by the certified **letzte Schnittstelle** (last interface — the plant producing final-quality biofuel). Recognised proof types are enumerated in **§ 8 Biokraft-NachV** (proofs under §§ 9/16, certificates under §§ 14/15, plus grandfathered 2009-ordinance proofs issued by 7 Dec 2021). `CONFIRMED` (gesetze-im-internet.de § 8 + ble.de Nabisy pages). (S13, S15)
- On every change of ownership in the trading chain, the (Teil-)Nachweis is **transferred in Nabisy to the new owner**; the obligated quota party finally presents proofs to the Biokraftstoffquotenstelle. `CONFIRMED` (ble.de + zoll.de). (S15, S12)

### 3.5 National registry — Nabisy — and Union Database (UDB)

- **Nabisy** ("Nachhaltige-Biomasse-System"), operated by **BLE** (nabisy.ble.de). Account holders: **producers/interfaces (Schnittstellen)**, **suppliers/traders (Lieferanten)**, **plant operators** (CHP, biogas, ETS installations, aircraft operators), **grid operators**. Registration is a precondition for issuing/receiving proofs relevant to the German market. `CONFIRMED` (ble.de Nabisy info page). (S15)
- **UDB status (EU level):** UDB live since Nov 2024 for liquid supply chains; gaseous chains in pilot; ~31,000 certified economic operators onboarded via voluntary schemes; the Commission is **still coordinating the timeline for full mandatory use** with member states; a delegated regulation extending traceability upstream is in preparation. `CONFIRMED` (energy.ec.europa.eu / EC UDB info-site + scheme pages ISCC/2BSvs as Tier 2). (S16, S17)
- **German specifics:** proofs continue to run through Nabisy; **no bidirectional Nabisy–UDB interface is implemented yet** (German authority/industry material: DEHSt newsletter on alternative fuels, industry position papers demanding the interface before mandatory use). Practical onboarding obligation for German operators currently arises **via voluntary-scheme rules (e.g. ISCC)**, not via a Biokraft-NachV deadline. **New (2026 act): § 37i BImSchG obliges quota-obligated parties to enter fossil volumes, compliance options and GHG emissions into the UDB after 1 June of the year following the obligation year** (in force 7 Jun 2026; from Drs. 21/4083 extracts — consolidated § not fetched). `PRIMARY-ONLY` (DEHSt Tier 1 + Tier 2 industry; no BLE page stating a mandatory UDB date was found; § 37i from parliamentary text only). (S18, S19, V1)

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

Numbered, actionable list for a party placing petrol/diesel on the German market (the energy-tax debtor under § 2 (1) Nr. 1/4 EnergieStG is the obligated quota party). Authority in brackets.

1. **Register as energy-tax debtor with the customs administration (Zoll / Hauptzollamt)** — the THG-quota obligation attaches to whoever releases taxable petrol/diesel for consumption; excise registration is the entry point (§ 2 EnergieStG; enforcement via customs). [Zoll / competent Hauptzollamt]
2. **Certify every chain element up to and including the last interface (letzte Schnittstelle)** under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU / REDcert-EU dominate in DE), using a **BLE-recognised/registered certification body**; re-audit every 12 months. Verify GHG saving meets § 6 Biokraft-NachV thresholds (≥ 50/60/65 % by installation date; 70 % for RFNBOs). [BLE / certification body]
3. **Open the appropriate Nabisy account** (Schnittstelle / Lieferant / Anlagenbetreiber) with BLE before trading sustainable volumes destined for the German market. [BLE]
4. **Carry a valid Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis / Teilnachweis in Nabisy** with every consignment and transfer it in Nabisy on each change of ownership — fuels without a valid proof count as fossil (zero quota benefit). [BLE]
5. **If crediting charging electricity toward the quota**, register the charge points / vehicles with the **Umweltbundesamt (UBA)** and submit annual electricity data by **28 February** of the following year (UBA fees €94.60–€6,500). [UBA]
6. **File the annual THG-quota declaration** for the previous compliance year with the **Biokraftstoffquotenstelle (Hauptzollamt Frankfurt (Oder), seat Cottbus)** by **1 June** (§ 37c (1) BImSchG); submit the accompanying sustainability proofs (zoll.de indicates a **15 April** sustainability-proof submission — treat as the earlier of the two dates and confirm with the authority, see §5). A missing declaration triggers an irrebuttable volume estimate. [Biokraftstoffquotenstelle / HZA Frankfurt (Oder)]
7. **Meet the sub-quotas and caps**: advanced-biofuels minimum 2 % (2026, if > 2 PJ supplied), RFNBO minimum 0.1 % (2026); stay within the crop cap (4.9 % in 2026) and Annex IX-B cap (1.9 %); above-cap volumes earn no quota benefit. Cover any shortfall by buying quota via bilateral third-party contracts (§ 37a (6)–(7)) or accept the Ausgleichsabgabe (€600/t CO2eq main quota; €45/GJ advanced; €120/GJ RFNBO), due 1 June. [Biokraftstoffquotenstelle]
8. **Do not rely on palm-based fuels** (0 % since 2023) and **plan the exit from POME/palm-residue fuels by the 2027 quota year**; ensure third-country waste-based chains permit **state on-site inspection** (a quota-eligibility condition from 2027 under § 4b 38. BImSchV). [HZA / BLE]
9. **Comply with the new § 37i BImSchG Union Database duty**: enter fossil volumes, compliance options used and GHG emissions into the **UDB** after 1 June of the year following the obligation year (first report after 1 June 2027 for obligation year 2026); maintain UDB onboarding via your certification scheme and watch for the mandatory-use date and the Nabisy–UDB interface. [BLE / European Commission]
10. **For UCO/waste chains**, keep supplier self-declarations audit-ready, watch BLE Nabisy fraud notices, and prepare for stricter DAkkS-accredited audits once the pending Biokraft-NachV/BioSt-NachV recast is adopted (draft: DAkkS accreditation mandatory from 1 Jan 2027). [BLE]
11. **Note the tax position**: biofuels get no excise relief (E85/FAME/HVO taxed at fossil-equivalent rates, § 2 Abs. 4 EnergieStG); claim the zero CO2 emission factor for the sustainable biomass share under the nEHS/BEHG (EBeV 2030 reporting to DEHSt), evidenced by the same sustainability certificates. [Zoll / DEHSt]

# UDB status & the ACTUAL current reporting channels for aviation and maritime fuel

- **Purpose:** correct the implication (in the existing ReFuelEU / FuelEU / cross-border
  reports) that the EU **Union Database (UDB)** is already the live, mandatory single
  reporting channel. Establish, from primary + independently corroborated sources, how
  aviation-fuel and maritime-fuel volumes are ACTUALLY reported as of **July 2026**.
- **Instruments touched:** Dir (EU) 2018/2001 (RED II) Art. 28(2); Dir (EU) 2023/2413
  (RED III) Art. 31a; Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996 Art. 18 (+ amendments); Reg (EU) 2023/2405
  (ReFuelEU Aviation) Arts. 8, 10; Reg (EU) 2023/1805 (FuelEU Maritime) Art. 16;
  Impl. Reg. (EU) 2026/394 (FuelEU database).
- **Facts date-stamped / checked:** 2026-07-05 (a few cross-checks 2026-07-06).
- **Method:** EUR-Lex primary text + Commission DG ENER / DG MOVE + EASA + EMSA Tier-1
  pages, each key fact corroborated by ≥2 sources of different origin per METHODOLOGY.md.
  Confidence labels on every row; unpinned items flagged GAP / MOVING TARGET.

## Headline current-state picture (July 2026)

There is **no single mandatory channel today.** Volumes are reported through **three
different systems**, and the UDB is only one of them and is not yet mandatory:

| Domain | Who / what is reported | Live channel NOW (July 2026) | UDB role |
|---|---|---|---|
| RED road-transport biofuels/RFNBOs (context) | consignment traceability data | **UDB** — operational (liquids since 15 Jan 2024) but **use not yet mandatory/sanctioned** | is the UDB itself |
| ReFuelEU Aviation — **aircraft operators** (Art. 8) | Annex II uplift/anti-tankering + SAF-purchased data | **EASA Sustainability Portal** (invitation-only digital reporting tool) | **not used by operators** |
| ReFuelEU Aviation — **fuel suppliers** (Art. 10) | per-Union-airport aviation-fuel + SAF + synthetic volumes | **UDB** (designated channel; used transitionally for 2024 & 2025 data) | is the designated channel, but not yet mandatory |
| FuelEU Maritime (Art. 16) | energy used on board / GHG-intensity / compliance balance | **EMSA THETIS-MRV / FuelEU database** | **not used**; maritime does not depend on the UDB |

**The correction:** the existing reports' line — suppliers "report per Union airport into
the Union Database by 14 February" — is *substantively correct as the designated legal
channel* for **aviation fuel suppliers** (Art. 10 + DG MOVE guidance + Commission FAQ),
and the UDB is operational. What is **inaccurate** is any implication that the UDB is the
**live, mandatory, single** reporting route for ReFuelEU/all transport-fuel reporting:
(1) UDB use is **not yet mandatory** (no sanctions date set as of July 2026); (2) **aircraft
operators do NOT report into the UDB** — they use the **EASA Sustainability Portal**; (3)
**FuelEU Maritime does not use the UDB at all** — it runs on **THETIS-MRV**. See §A–§C.

## What changes when the UDB becomes mandatory

- Once the Commission (with the RED Committee / Member States) sets the UDB **mandatory-use
  date and sanctions**, UDB recording becomes a **condition of RED compliance** for economic
  operators (raw-material suppliers, producers, traders, fuel suppliers) — including
  **aviation fuel suppliers'** Art. 10 reporting, which will then be a sanction-backed UDB
  obligation rather than the current transitional one.
- It does **not** automatically fold in **aircraft-operator** reporting (EASA Sustainability
  Portal) or **FuelEU Maritime** reporting (THETIS-MRV); those remain their own systems. The
  UDB is a **traceability/sustainability** backbone, not a merger of the aviation-operator or
  maritime MRV channels.

### 5-line summary — how volumes are reported RIGHT NOW (July 2026)

1. **UDB = phased-in, NOT yet the mandatory single channel:** operational for liquids since
   **15 Jan 2024** (gases from late-2024/pilot) under RED Art. 31a + Impl. Reg. 2022/996 Art. 18
   (amended by 2024/805 & 2025/196), but the Commission has set **no mandatory-use/sanctions
   date** — 2024/25 are transitional. (2023/1640 is the co-processing act, not the UDB act.)
2. **ReFuelEU Aviation aircraft operators** report via **EASA's Sustainability Portal** (digital
   reporting tool) — **not** the UDB — by **31 March** (2024 data filed 31 Mar 2025; 2025 data
   31 Mar 2026).
3. **ReFuelEU Aviation fuel suppliers** report **per Union airport into the UDB** (Reporting
   Template B) by **14 February** (2024 data 14 Feb 2025; 2025 data 14 Feb 2026) — the correct
   designated channel, but on the non-sanctioned transitional UDB footing.
4. **FuelEU Maritime** volumes/GHG-intensity are reported entirely through **EMSA THETIS-MRV /
   the FuelEU database** (specs set by **Impl. Reg. 2026/394**), independent of the UDB; first
   full compliance cycle (2025 data → DoC by 30 Jun 2026) just completed.
5. **When the UDB becomes mandatory:** aviation-supplier UDB reporting becomes sanction-backed
   and a condition of RED compliance, but aircraft-operator (EASA portal) and maritime
   (THETIS-MRV) channels remain separate — the UDB is a traceability backbone, not a single
   merged reporting system.

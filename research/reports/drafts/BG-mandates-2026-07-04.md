## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.1 Current obligation

Bulgaria imposes a **volumetric blending obligation** on persons placing
petroleum-derived liquid transport fuels on the market, set by the **Energy
from Renewable Sources Act (ЗЕВИ / ZEVI), Art. 47**. Blending is done in
licensed tax warehouses (Excise Duties and Tax Warehouses Act). Final
distributors must supply blended fuel two months after the party placing it on
the market. ZEVI's overarching aim is the RED transport target (14 % renewable
energy in transport by 2030 per the RED II / 2023-2025 ZEVI amendments). The
Ministry of Energy is the responsible ministry.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** persons placing petroleum-derived liquid transport fuels
  on the market at release for consumption; blending only in licensed tax
  warehouses. Final distributors must offer blended fuel two months later
  (ZEVI Art. 47).
- **Metric:** minimum biofuel content by **volume** per fuel grade, with advanced
  sub-quotas expressed as a share of the biocomponent volume.
- **Authority:** Ministry of Energy (me.government.bg); fuel-quality control is
  exercised by DAMTN (State Agency for Metrological and Technical Surveillance)
  under the liquid-fuel quality ordinance.
- **Certificates/registry/banking:** the 2023/2025 ZEVI amendments introduce the
  option to meet the transport RES share via **direct investment or
  certification** (details not read from primary text — gap).

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **Petrol 9 % effective-date CONFLICT:** BG sources quoting ЗЕВИ čl.47 state the
  bioethanol content in petrol has been **9 % since 1 March 2019** (base čl.47 text
  originally 7 %, then 9 %); USDA GAIN 2025 instead states bioethanol was **"raised
  to 9 % from 1 March 2025"** with ≥1 % advanced. The **9 % value is corroborated
  by two independent Tier-2 sources**, but the effective date differs (2019 vs 2025)
  and no machine-read Tier-1 quote of the petrol clause was obtained (me.gov Tier-1
  page describes only diesel/advanced). Report both dates; do not pick silently.
- **Advanced sub-quota correction:** the draft's "**2 % of biodiesel must be
  advanced from Sep 2024**" is contradicted by me.gov (Tier-1: min **1 %** new-
  generation in diesel from 1 Sep 2018) and by USDA GAIN — treat the 2 % figure as
  UNCORROBORATED/likely erroneous. The corroborated advanced picture: diesel ≥1 %,
  bioethanol ≥1 % (from Mar 2025), overall 0.2/1/3.5 % (2022-24/2025/2030).
- **Clean Tier-1 ZEVI read still not obtained:** lex.bg returned HTTP 403 on retry;
  the diesel 6 %/1 % advanced figure IS now Tier-1 (me.gov) + independent (USDA);
  petrol 9 % remains without a machine-read Tier-1. Follow-up: obtain consolidated
  ЗЕВИ Art. 47 from lex.bg / Държавен вестник (dv.parliament.bg) and the DAMTN
  fuel-quality ordinance for the exact 2026 petrol %, sanctions, IX-B/crop caps.
- **14 % 2030 target** equals the RED II statutory transport target; not confirmed
  as the current operative figure (RED III would raise it) → UNCORROBORATED.
- **Penalty amounts, IX-B cap, crop cap, double-counting, certification mechanics**

### 5-line summary — Bulgaria
1. Obligation type: volumetric blending obligation on fuel suppliers under ZEVI Art. 47, aimed at the RES-in-transport target (Ministry of Energy; DAMTN fuel-quality control).
2. Current level: diesel min. 6 % biodiesel (of which ≥1 % advanced) — CONFIRMED (me.gov Tier-1 + USDA); petrol min. 9 % bioethanol/bio-ethers — value corroborated (USDA + čl.47 quotes) but effective-date CONFLICT (2019 vs 2025).
3. Advanced: diesel ≥1 % (2018), bioethanol ≥1 % (2025), overall 0.2/1/3.5 % — the draft's "2 % biodiesel advanced" is contradicted by Tier-1 and USDA (corrected). 14 % RES-T 2030 = RED II target (UNCORROBORATED).
4. RED III status: partially transposed (28 measures notified incl. ЗЕВИ 10 Jun 2025); **reasoned opinion outstanding on permitting (Feb 2025, INFR(2024)0211)**. ETS2 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959) surrender from 2028.
5. Flags: no machine-read Tier-1 ЗЕВИ (lex.bg 403); petrol 9 % date conflict; "2 % biodiesel advanced" corrected; penalties/IX-B cap/crop cap/double-counting are gaps.

- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** fuel-tax-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Scope note:** Poland delivers biofuel support through the National Indicative Target (NCW / blending obligation), **not** through excise reductions — see mandates agent. Fuel is subject to three parallel state charges: excise (akcyza), the fuel charge (opłata paliwowa), and the emission fee (opłata emisyjna).

### Interaction with the mandate
Poland's biofuel policy is **quota-based (NCW)**, not tax-incentive-based. There is no reduced excise or exemption for high blends (E85, B100, HVO100) — biocomponents used as standalone fuels are taxed at the diesel rate (1 160 PLN/1000 L). Consequently there is no Swedish/Finnish-style "relief limited to volumes above the obligation" condition, because there is no excise relief to condition. Support flows entirely through the NCW obligation and certificate value (cross-ref mandates agent).

## 4-line summary — Poland
- Petrol excise (akcyza): **1 529,00 PLN/1000 L** (standard rate restored 16 Jun 2026 after the EU-minimum fuel-package reduction lapsed); plus opłata paliwowa 210,29 PLN/1000 L and emission fee 80 PLN/1000 L.
- Diesel excise (akcyza): **1 160,00 PLN/1000 L**; plus opłata paliwowa 453,52 PLN/1000 L and emission fee 80 PLN/1000 L.
- Biofuel relief: none via excise — biocomponents as standalone fuels taxed at the diesel rate (1 160 PLN/1000 L); all biofuel support runs through the NCW blending obligation.
- Flags: no separate CO2 tax (emission fee is a fixed levy, not a carbon price); PROPOSED 3 Jul–30 Sep 2026 VAT+excise cut bill not adopted; ETS2 from 1 Jan 2028; DG TAXUD/TEDB cross-check outstanding.

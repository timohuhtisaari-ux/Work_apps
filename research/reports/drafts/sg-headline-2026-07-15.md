# Singapore — Biofuel Instruments (Non-EU) — 2026

**Date-stamped: 2026-07-15**

## 1. Instrument Type & 2026 Headline Obligation

Singapore does **not** maintain a mandatory road-fuel blending mandate for gasoline or diesel. Instead, Singapore's biofuel/sustainable fuel policy focuses exclusively on **aviation (Sustainable Aviation Fuel, SAF)** via a government-mandated levy mechanism.

### Road Fuels (Gasoline & Diesel)

**2026 Headline Obligation:** None identified. No mandatory biofuel blending for road transport fuels (petrol, diesel).

**Regulatory framework:** Singapore's fuel supply is market-driven; refiners and importers are not obligated to blend biofuels into road fuels. No national biofuel standard for road transport.

**Confidence:** `PRIMARY-ONLY` — absence of road-fuel mandate confirmed by lack of transport ministry or energy regulator announcements; Singapore focuses aviation and maritime decarbonization.

### Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF)

**2026 Headline Obligation:**
- **1% SAF mandatory uplift** in Jet A-1 fuel for all flights departing Singapore from **October 1, 2026** (levy applied to tickets sold from April 1, 2026).
- **Mechanism:** Passenger levy system (not a fuel-supplier blending mandate); airlines and cargo operators must pay into a statutory **SAF Fund** managed by the Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore (CAAS).
- **Fund administration:** Singapore Sustainable Aviation Fuel Company Ltd. (SAFCo) procures SAF and environmental attributes (EAs) on behalf of CAAS.
- **Obligated parties:** Airlines, cargo operators, general aviation, and business aviation flights departing Singapore.

**Legal basis:**
- **Civil Aviation Authority of Singapore (Amendment) Bill** (introduced 2025, passed/enacted 2026): Establishes CAAS power to create and manage SAF Fund and levy.
- No single consolidated act found online; regulatory architecture via Bill amendments and CAAS policy directives.

**Confidence:** `CONFIRMED` — 1% SAF uplift from Oct 1, 2026 confirmed by CAAS official announcements (multiple sources: CAAS Newsroom, Ministry of Transport, Singapore Government Portal). Levy structure and SAFCo role confirmed in CAAS communications and Bird & Bird (law firm) guidance.

## 4. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

### Road Fuels

**Not applicable.** Singapore has no road-fuel biofuel mandate; fuel taxation is standard mineral-fuel excise (not biofuel-specific).

### Sustainable Aviation Fuel

**SAF Levy (Passenger/Cargo Tax on Flights):**

| Component | Rate / Structure | Effective Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Passenger SAF Levy | USD ~1.18–49.33 per ticket | Oct 1, 2026 (sales from Apr 1, 2026) | Distance-based (4 geographical bands) and cabin class dependent |
| Cargo SAF Levy | Based on weight and distance | Oct 1, 2026 | Separate from passenger levy |
| General/Business Aviation | Proportional to distance | Oct 1, 2026 | Smaller operators also liable |

**Levy structure:**
- **Quantum:** Calculated to raise funds sufficient for SAF procurement to meet 1% uplift target.
- **Fund destination:** 100% of levy (less admin costs) flows to SAF Fund for SAFCo procurement of SAF and environmental attributes (EAs).
- **Passenger display:** Airlines must show SAF levy as discrete line item on ticket; no bundling with other taxes.

**Mechanism:** This is a **revenue-raising levy**, not a tax incentive for SAF. Airlines/operators pay, not fuel suppliers. SAF suppliers themselves receive no tax break; cost is borne by passengers/shippers.

**Confidence:** `CONFIRMED` — SAF levy rates (per-passenger USD range) and distance-band structure confirmed by CAAS official statements and Ministry of Transport announcements. Levy effective date Oct 1, 2026 confirmed.

## 5. Sustainability / Certification Regime

### Sustainable Aviation Fuel

**Required certification:** SAF procured via the SAF Fund must comply with **CORSIA (Carbon Offsetting and Reduction Scheme for International Aviation)** offsetting requirements and demonstrate **lifecycle carbon savings** of ~65% vs. conventional Jet A-1.

**Specific standards:**
- **SAF Environmental Attributes (EAs):** Explicitly tied to lifecycle CO₂ performance; SAF must demonstrate quantified emissions reduction per CORSIA methodology.
- **No explicit mention of ASTM D7566** in CAAS announcements, but SAF procurement likely adheres to international specs (D7566 or equivalent).
- **Third-party certification schemes:** ISCC, REDcert-EU, and others may be used by SAF suppliers, but CAAS/SAFCo do not mandate a specific scheme; procurement is performance-based (lifecycle emissions reduction).

**Confidence:** `PRIMARY-ONLY` — CAAS states CORSIA-aligned requirements and ~65% lifecycle emissions reduction goal; specific certification scheme (ASTM D7566, ISCC) not mandated in CAAS documents as of July 15, 2026. SAFCo procurement criteria likely reference international specs but not yet published in detail.

### Marine Fuels

**No SAF mandate for maritime shipping found.** Singapore has **FuelEU Maritime compliance obligations** (EU regulation applicable to ships calling Singapore), but this is external to Singapore's own framework. No national marine biofuel mandate identified.

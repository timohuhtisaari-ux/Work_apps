# Czechia — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Czechia (CZ)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Czechia's binding transport-fuel obligation is a **GHG-intensity reduction duty** on fuel suppliers under the Air Protection Act (No. 201/2012 Coll.), with the former fixed volume-blending mandate made non-binding in 2022; from 2026 it is joined by a **minimum advanced-biofuels/renewable-fuels sub-obligation**. The binding 2026 GHG-reduction figure is reported at **≈6.25%**, on a trajectory toward **11% GHG reduction and ≥5.5% advanced by 2030** with an Annex IX-B (UCO/animal-fat) cap of ~1.7% — but these headline figures rest on **Tier-2 sources only** because the primary legal texts (e-sbírka, NV 189/2018) were JS-blocked, and the 2026 ≈6.25% value is single-source. On tax, petrol excise is **12 840 CZK/1000 L** and diesel is under a **live temporary cut to 8 011 CZK/1000 L** (from 8 Apr 2026, scheduled to revert to 9 950 CZK/1000 L ~20 Jul 2026); a state-aid-based reduced excise exists for pure/high-blend sustainable biofuels (B100, SMN30) but its 2026 values are unverified. The key supplier obligations are to use an **MoE-registered certification body**, certify under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (Czechia runs no national scheme), carry mass-balance proof-of-sustainability, record in the EU **UDB**, and meet the annual GHG duty. **RED III is transposed** (Act No. 42/2025 Coll., biofuel provisions from 1 Jan 2026), but the reliance of the core numeric targets on secondary sources is the report's highest-risk gap, alongside a shared gap on penalty amounts. ETS2 compliance begins 1 Jan 2028.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** the person who places petrol/diesel on the market for transport (the fuel supplier declaring for excise), under Act 201/2012 §20. (S1)
- **Administration:** **Ministry of the Environment (MŽP)** owns the sustainability/GHG framework; compliance reporting is tied to the excise/consumption declaration handled with the **Customs Administration (Celní správa)**. (S1, S8)
- **Compliance basis:** lifecycle GHG intensity of the annual fuel pool; advanced/waste feedstocks double-counted; sustainability certification per RED (see Section 3).
- **Banking/borrowing, certificate trading:** not established in sources — GAP.

### Interaction with the mandate
Czechia runs a blending + GHG-reduction obligation (Air Protection Act 201/2012) — see Section 1. The excise system layers a **state-aid-based reduction for pure/high-blend sustainable biofuels** (B100, SMN30) on top of that. Because the relief is conditional on RED sustainability criteria and is a notified state aid, it functions like the Sweden/Finland model of tax relief for qualifying biofuels; the exact 2026 rates and the current state-aid authorisation must be re-verified from the consolidated §45/§48 text (see Section 5 gaps).

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Czechia does **not run a national scheme**: it relies on the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes approved under RED Art. 30(5)** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, Bonsucro, RSB, KZR INiG, etc.). [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication; DG ENER]
- **Certification bodies must register with MoE CZ (§21a, from 1 May 2025)** before performing any certification audit on Czech territory for entities in §21(1)–(4) and §21c(1)–(2) (biomass for biofuel production, intermediates, biofuels, RFNBOs, RCFs). Registration requires proof of authorisation by an EU-approved voluntary scheme. MoE CZ publishes the **register of authorised certification bodies** on its website. [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication]
- **Exclusions:** the registration duty does **not** apply to bodies verifying solid biomass or biomethane. [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication]
- Proof-of-sustainability per consignment = scheme **certificate** + chain-of-custody sustainability declaration issued under the voluntary scheme. [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Use a §21a-registered certification body** — verify the certifier is entered in MoE CZ's register of authorised certification bodies before commissioning an audit (registration duty in force from 1 May 2025). → **MoE CZ** (Air Protection Department).
2. **Obtain certification** under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme approved under RED Art. 30(5) (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, …); Czechia runs no national scheme. → chosen scheme / certifier.
3. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving threshold** (50% ≤5 Oct 2015 / 60% to 2020 / 65% from 2021; 70% for RFNBO/RCF) and **exclude high-ILUC palm volumes** per Del. Reg. (EU) 2019/807. → self + auditor.
4. **Carry proof-of-sustainability** (scheme certificate + chain-of-custody declaration) per consignment on a **mass-balance** basis; RFNBOs/RCFs are now in scope (Act 42/2025). → scheme.
5. **Record transactions and sustainability data in the EU UDB** (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → **UDB** (European Commission) — confirm the CZ mandatory-use date.
6. **Meet the annual GHG-intensity reduction duty** for the fuel pool placed on the market (≈6.25% reported for 2026, subject to the Tier-2 caveat in Section 5), including the phased advanced-biofuels sub-obligation and the ~1.7% Annex IX-B cap; advanced/waste feedstocks count double. → self; reported via the excise/consumption declaration to the **Customs Administration (Celní správa)** / **MoE CZ**.
7. **Track certifier duties**: certificate copies sent immediately to MoE CZ, pre-audit notifications before each verification, and the certification body's **28 February** annual summary — ensure your auditor complies. → MoE CZ / certifier.
8. **Undergo the periodic certification audit** and retain documentation; expect enforcement by **ČIŽP** (Czech Environmental Inspectorate) for non-compliant proof. → certifier / ČIŽP.
9. **Prepare for ETS2**: road-transport fuel suppliers face an EU allowance-surrender obligation from **1 Jan 2028** (auctioning from 2027) under ETS2 (Directive (EU) 2023/959, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC; the one-year postponement was adopted alongside Reg. (EU) 2026/667, the 2040 climate-target amendment); MŽP is building the national framework. → MŽP / national ETS2 authority.

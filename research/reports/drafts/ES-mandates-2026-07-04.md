## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Scheme identification

Spain imposes **annual minimum sale-or-consumption targets for biofuels and other renewable transport fuels, in energy content, on the combined petrol+diesel pool** (no separate per-pool targets in the current framework). Core acts: **Real Decreto 1085/2015** (as amended, most recently by **Real Decreto 5/2026 of 8 January 2026**), **Orden TED/728/2024 of 15 July 2024** (mechanism/certificates/payments) and **Orden TED/1342/2022** (crop cap). Compliance is proven with certificates in the **SICBIOS** system. A parallel 6 %+ GHG-intensity reduction obligation exists under RD-ley 6/2022 (FQD Art. 7a implementation) — the 2026 mandate increase was explicitly calibrated to it.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Certificates:** "certificados de biocarburantes y otros combustibles renovables"; 1 certificate = **1 tep** (tonne oil equivalent) of eligible fuel; double-counting fuels earn 2 certificates/tep (types A/E etc.). System: **SICBIOS** (Sistema de Certificación de Biocarburantes y otros combustibles renovables). [S3]
- **Administrator:** ⚠ verified verbatim in the Orden (Art. 5): "La Dirección General de Política Energética y Minas … se encargará de la gestión del sistema de certificación", and the preamble states CNMC rules on the mechanism "pierde su aplicabilidad" — **SICBIOS is administered by MITECO–DGPEM**, not CNMC (management transferred by RD 500/2020, operative resolutions/instructions issued by DGPEM, e.g. "Instrucciones SICBIOS 2024" of 12 July 2024; Resolución provisional certification 2025-Q3 published 2026). **Registry correction confirmed:** `member-states.md` should list MITECO–DGPEM, with CNMC only for pre-2019 settlements. **CONFIRMED.** [S3][S8][S9]
- **Obligated parties (RD 1085/2015 Art. 3):** wholesale operators (Ley 34/1998 Art. 42) for annual national sales; retail distributors (Art. 43) for sales not sourced from wholesale operators; direct consumers for volumes not supplied by the above. [S2][S3]
- **Banking:** up to **30 %** of a year's obligation may be met with previous-year certificates (traspaso), notified by 1 April (Orden TED/728/2024 Arts. 20.8/22.3). No borrowing found. [S3]
- **Verification:** quarterly provisional + annual definitive certification against sales data (formula in Art. 7.2 of the Orden); deficits settled by compensatory payments. [S3]
- Cross-reference: the mandate interacts with the hydrocarbons excise regime (Agencia Tributaria) only administratively; no tax incentive component — see tax section.

### Conflicts and gaps (mandates scope)

- **RESOLVED (was CONFLICT — penalty level):** 2.029 vs 1.623 €/certificate was a false conflict: **2.029 €/cert is the Art. 28 *provisional* (quarterly) payment parameter µ** (read verbatim on BOE 2026-07-04) and **1.623 €/CCR (general) / 2.110 €/CTA (advanced) are the Art. 29 *definitive* annual payment parameters α** (Congreso.es government reply + three concordant legal summaries). The "45 €/tep" figure from one automated extraction was an **extraction error** — it appears nowhere in any verbatim fetch or summary.
- **RESOLVED (was UNCORROBORATED):** the "1.2 % advanced biofuels for 2026" figure **is in the consolidated RD 1085/2015 Art. 2.4** (verbatim BOE re-fetch 2026-07-04); full schedule 0.2/0.3/0.5/1/1.2 → 3.5 %. Still PRIMARY-ONLY (no independent secondary restating the 2026 value was found; RD 376/2022 amending text itself truncated in fetches).
- **RESOLVED (was GAP):** operative crop-cap values under Orden TED/1342/2022 read: 3.5 % (2023) / 3.0 % (2024) / 2.6 % (2025), 2025 value rolled forward from 2026 (Art. 2.4 of the Orden).
- **Residual gap:** Art. 29.3 ff. of Orden TED/728/2024 (deficit-band escalations, inhabilitación) — BOE fetches truncate; congreso PDF is binary; escalation percentages rest on Tier 2 only.
- **GAP:** EUR-Lex NIM page for 2023/2413 rendered only BE/BG; Spain's notified measures not listed here.

**ES 5-line summary**
1. Obligation type: energy-content % of total petrol+diesel sales met with biofuels/renewable fuels, certificate-based (SICBIOS, 1 cert = 1 tep, administered by MITECO–DGPEM).
2. Current level (2026): 14 % overall (RD 5/2026, in force 11 Jan 2026); advanced sub-target 1.2 % (2026) rising to 3.5 % (2030); IX-B cap 1.7 %; operative crop cap 2.6 % (ceiling 7 %).
3. Next legislated change: none adopted beyond 2026 — 14 % rolls forward; GHG-intensity model (draft RD, RED III) still PROPOSED.
4. RED III status: NOT transposed — reasoned opinions Feb/Dec 2025, still outstanding in the June 2026 infringement package.
5. Flags resolved: penalty "conflict" was two different parameters (provisional 2,029 vs definitive 1,623/2,110 €/cert); 1.2 % advanced 2026 confirmed in BOE (PRIMARY-ONLY); crop cap 2026 = 2.6 %. Residual: Art. 29.3 escalation bands verbatim; EUR-Lex NIM; GAIN.

# Estonia — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Estonia (EE)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Estonia imposes an **energy-share (renewable-content) obligation** on fuel suppliers under the Liquid Fuel Act (Vedelkütuse seadus, VKS § 21): the 2026 level is **≥ 7.5 %**, measured as a **half-year weighted average** (30 June / 31 December) of the pooled petrol/diesel/biofuel energy, with a **≥ 0.5 % advanced-biofuel sub-target**. Compliance is administered by the Environmental Board (Keskkonnaamet) via monthly supplier reports; the July-2025 amendment replaced the old flat misdemeanour fine with a **€125 per GJ of shortfall** penalty. Biofuels receive **no excise relief** — petrol is taxed at EUR 591/1000 L and diesel at EUR 428/1000 L (the legislated 1 May 2026 rise to 621/459 was cancelled), so the incentive runs entirely through the mandate, not the tax code. On sustainability, Estonia relies on EU-recognised voluntary schemes with mass-balance proof of sustainability and EU Union Database obligations; there is no Nabisy-style national registry. **RED III is not fully transposed** — Estonia received a Commission letter of formal notice on 24 July 2025 — so a fuel supplier's top obligations are: hit the 7.5 %/0.5 % shares on a half-year basis, report monthly to the Environmental Board, and carry valid sustainability certification for every consignment.

#### CONFLICT — RESOLVED (current overall level)
The three figures that surfaced are **not** competing statutory values; they are different metrics:
- **7.5 %** — the **statutory § 21 supplier obligation**, expressed as a *half-year weighted average at 30 June / 31 December*. This is the operative 2026 value. Confirmed independently by (a) the § 21 primary text, (b) the **Ministry of Climate** penalty-amendment page [10], and (c) the **European Commission** Clean-Energy-Islands Estonia page [9]. All three give 7.5 %.
- **10 %** — a **National Indicative Target (NIT)** for the *renewable share in transport*, a policy/planning target, not the § 21 supplier obligation. (The "10 % for 2026–2029" phrasing that circulates in secondary retrievals traces to Poland's *Law on Biocomponents and Liquid Biofuels* NIT and is a cross-country contamination — it does not describe Estonian statute.)
- **6.4 %** — the **older per-litre** requirement in force from 1 April 2018; historical, superseded.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** fuel suppliers releasing petrol/diesel for consumption.
- **Administrator / supervisor:** the **Environmental Board (Keskkonnaamet)**. Suppliers file a **monthly report by the 15th** of each month (quantities and gross-energy data of petrol, diesel and biofuel released for consumption in the prior month), plus GHG-intensity data. Compliance is assessed on a **half-year** basis (30 Jun / 31 Dec) since the July-2025 amendment. The Environmental Board maintains the national biofuel-statistics database and exchanges data with the EU database referenced in Directive (EU) 2018/2001 (Union Database). [2][3][10]
- **FOKA register** is Estonia's F-gas (fluorinated-greenhouse-gas) handling register, **NOT** a biofuel registry (the Keskkonnaamet page merely groups the two topics). Confirmed by the sustainability pass. [3]
- Compliance is verified against the weighted-average share; enforcement now uses the **€125/GJ** shortfall charge plus substitutive-enforcement penalty payments. [4][10]

**ETS2 (EU cross-cutting):** road-transport fuels come under EU ETS2, **established by Directive (EU) 2023/959** (inserting Chapter IVa into Directive 2003/87/EC). The original 2027 start was **postponed by one year to 1 January 2028** as part of the 2026 climate package (Council 5 Nov 2025 / EP 13 Nov 2025; adopted early 2026 alongside Regulation (EU) 2026/667, the 2040 climate-target amendment). **First allowance surrender is now 2029** (for 2028 emissions); auctioning begins 2027. Directly applicable — not an Estonian mandate but interacts with supplier costs. (The establishing instrument is Dir. (EU) 2023/959; Reg. (EU) 2026/667 is the postponing package.) [11][12]

**Interaction with the mandate:** Estonia promotes biofuels through the biofuel/RES-share obligation under the Liquid Fuel Act (Section 1), *not* through excise relief — biofuels are taxed at the equivalent fossil rate, so there is no tax-side incentive layered on the mandate.

**National act implementing the Energy Taxation Directive (2003/96/EC):** the **Alcohol, Tobacco, Fuel and Electricity Excise Duty Act (ATKEAS)** sets motor-fuel excise and implements Directive 2003/96/EC minimum rates. Consolidated text on Riigi Teataja (riigiteataja.ee). No notified biofuel state-aid decision applies (Estonia grants no biofuel excise relief).

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register as an obligated fuel supplier** and meet the § 21 renewable-energy obligation of **≥ 7.5 %** (half-year weighted average at 30 June and 31 December 2026), including the **≥ 0.5 % advanced-biofuel (Annex IX-A) sub-target**. Authority: **Environmental Board (Keskkonnaamet)**.
2. **File the monthly report by the 15th** of each month to the Environmental Board (email / EMTA e-system): volumes of petrol, diesel and biofuel released for consumption in the prior month, gross-energy data, and GHG intensity (gCO2eq/MJ). Authority: **Environmental Board**.
3. **Submit the advanced-biofuel reporting-period return** (first mandatory period began 2025; report due **14 February 2026**). Authority: **Environmental Board**.
4. **Hold a valid EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certificate** (e.g. ISCC EU / REDcert EU / 2BSvs) covering each biofuel consignment, and operate a **mass-balance system** carrying per consignment: quantity, type, feedstock, production method, country of origin, place of purchase, proof method, and carbon intensity. Authority: **Environmental Board**.
5. **Ensure each biofuel meets the applicable RED GHG-saving threshold** (50/60/65 %; RFNBO 70 %). Authority: **Environmental Board**.
6. **Register and record transactions in the EU Union Database (UDB)** as required for transport-fuel operators. Authority: **EU / Environmental Board**.
7. **Retain proof-of-sustainability and prepare for Environmental Board sampling / lab verification** of biological origin (especially UCO/waste feedstocks). Authority: **Environmental Board**.
8. **Budget for the shortfall penalty of €125 per GJ** of any unmet renewable-energy obligation (in force since 18.07.2025). Authority: **Environmental Board** (enforcement via the Substitutive Enforcement and Penalty Payment Act).
9. **Note ETS2 exposure:** the fuel-supplier surrender obligation begins **1 Jan 2028**, first allowance surrender **2029**; monitor for national implementing rules. Authority: EU (Dir. (EU) 2023/959).

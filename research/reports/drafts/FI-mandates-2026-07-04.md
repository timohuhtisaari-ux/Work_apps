### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** road-transport fuel distributors supplying for consumption **≥ 1 million
  litres/year** of motor petrol, diesel, natural gas, biofuels, biogas and RFNBOs (**9 GWh**
  threshold for gaseous fuels). Smaller distributors and RFNBO suppliers may opt in
  voluntarily (first voluntary RFNBO distributor approved by Energiavirasto).
  CONFIRMED (Energiavirasto).
- **Administrator:** Energiavirasto (Energy Authority) supervises, receives the annual
  **jakeluvelvoiteilmoitus** (declaration) — due **by the end of March** after the compliance
  year — and imposes seuraamusmaksu. No tradable certificate instrument; compliance is
  supplier-level energy accounting. Renewable share of grid electricity for transport-
  electricity accounting is published annually (2025: 53.34 %).
- **Flexibility:**
  - **Carry-over:** over-fulfilment may be counted toward the following year (historic rule:
    up to an amount equal to half of the year's obligation, per 2018 Tax Administration
    guidance) — current limit not re-verified; the Energiavirasto haitarit page checked by
    the corroborator does not state a carry-over figure. UNCORROBORATED.
  - **Joustomekanismi (from 2025):** a distributor may fulfil up to **5.5 percentage points**
    of its obligation by financing alternative emission-reduction measures in Finland's
    effort-sharing (later also land-use) sector; implementing decrees in force end-June 2025.
    CONFIRMED (Energiavirasto; TEM).
  - Renewable transport electricity via public charging points counts toward the general
    obligation (from 1 Jan 2025, 841/2024).
- **Related but separate:** heating/off-road **biofuel-oil distribution obligation** under
  laki 418/2019 (out of transport-mandate scope; flagged for completeness).
- **Tax interaction:** none direct — the jakeluvelvoite is a quota; fuel excise is separate
  (*see tax section*; CO2-component relief for sustainable biofuels affects economics).

- No pending bill found changing the 2026 level (checked news/lausuntopalvelu to Jul 2026).
- **RED III transposition:** the transport articles were implemented by the **841/2024
  package** (HE 121/2024; confirmed 19 Dec 2024; in force 1 Jan 2025), including RFNBO
  minimum share, flexibility mechanism and transport electricity. Sustainability-criteria
  amendments (kestävyyslaki) proceeded separately via **laki 500/2025** (*sustainability
  agent's scope*). Despite this, the **Commission sent Finland a letter of formal notice on
  24 July 2025** for incomplete transposition of Directive (EU) 2023/2413 (among 26 MS;
  Finland also received the Sep 2024 formal notice on permitting provisions). Status:
  **partially transposed / infringement open.** CONFIRMED (Commission news 2025-07-24).

### 1.5 Conflicts and gaps

- **Resolved (was UNCORROBORATED):** the **temporary tiered jakeluvelvoite penalty for

  HE 121/2024 and its **enactment is corroborated by independent trade press** (qcintel:
  "Finland … eases penalties for higher targets", noting the President signed the law;
  fastmarkets). The exact numeric breakpoints are quoted from HE 121/2024 / the lausunto
  record because the promulgated 446/2007 § 11 gazette text is not machine-readable in this

  (jakeluvelvoite) and 0.03 €/MJ (lisävelvoite) are independently corroborated.
- **Still UNCORROBORATED / gaps:** (a) current carry-over limit; (b) end of double counting

## 5-line summary — Finland (FI)
1. Obligation type: energy-share distribution obligation (jakeluvelvoite) on fuel distributors ≥1 Ml/yr (9 GWh gaseous), incl. biogas, RFNBOs and transport electricity.
2. Current level (2026): 19.5 % of energy, with a 4.0 pp advanced/Annex IX-A lisävelvoite; crop-based counting capped at 2.6 pp.
3. Next legislated change: 22.5 % in 2027, then 31 % (2028), 32 % (2029), 34 % + 10 pp advanced + 4 pp RFNBO (2030) — all adopted (841/2024).
4. RED III status: transport articles transposed via the 841/2024 package (in force 1 Jan 2025), but Commission letter of formal notice 24 Jul 2025 for incomplete overall transposition.
5. Flags: temporary tiered penalty 2024–2027 now CONFIRMED (enactment corroborated by qcintel; breakpoints from HE 121/2024); still UNCORROBORATED — carry-over limit, end of double counting, national Annex IX-B cap; promulgated act text unretrievable in this environment (Finlex rendering / PDF).

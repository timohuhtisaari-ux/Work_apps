# Vietnam — Biofuel Instruments (Non-EU) — 2026

**Date-stamped: 2026-07-15**

## 1. Instrument Type & 2026 Headline Obligation

Vietnam's biofuel framework is a **volume-based blending mandate** for gasoline transport fuels, implemented via government decisions and circular regulations from the Ministry of Industry and Trade (MoIT).

### Road Fuels (Gasoline)

**2026 Headline Obligation:**
- **E10 (10% ethanol by volume)** becomes mandatory nationwide **from June 1, 2026**, replacing RON 95 mineral gasoline as the primary unleaded fuel.
- **E5 RON92 (5% ethanol)** continues availability through **December 31, 2030** for vehicle-compatibility purposes (older engines).
- **Obligated parties:** Fuel importers, refiners, distributors, gas station operators.
- **Effective from:** June 1, 2026 (E10 mandate official start).

**Legal basis:**
- **Decision No. 53/2012/QD-TTg** (November 22, 2012, Prime Minister): Original roadmap for biofuel blending (E5 from 2015, E10 from 2017). Repealed December 11, 2025.
- **Decision No. 46/2025/QD-TTg** (December 11, 2025): Repeals Decision 53/2012; delegates fuel-blending authority to MoIT.
- **Circular 50/2025/TT-BCT** (Ministry of Industry and Trade): Implements E10 mandatory blending from June 1, 2026; governs fuel-market transition.
- **Law on Special Consumption Tax No. 66/2025/QH15** (2026): Tax rates on biofuels (E10 at 7%, E5 at 8%, mineral gasoline at 10%).

**Confidence:** `CONFIRMED` — E10 mandate date (June 1, 2026) confirmed by multiple primary and secondary sources: Vietnam.vn (government portal), Circular 50/2025, MOIT official announcements, Vietnam+ (state news). Excise tax rates confirmed in Law 66/2025/QH15 (passed/enacted 2026).

**Confidence:** `PRIMARY-ONLY` — E10 blending is established; no legislated step-up post-2026 found in Ministry or government announcements as of July 15, 2026. Broader energy targets (Resolution 70-NQ/TW) are national, not road-fuel specific.

## 3. Advanced/Sub-Quotas

**No advanced-biofuel sub-quota or sub-target carve-out found.**

- E10 consists of 10% ethanol from any renewable source (sugarcane ethanol, cassava ethanol domestically produced; no distinction by feedstock type in the mandate).
- No waste-derived or advanced-biofuel sub-target within the E10 mandate.
- **Ethanol feedstock:** Domestic supply from sugarcane and cassava; no sustainability-based sub-category in the blending obligation.

**Confidence:** `PRIMARY-ONLY` — MoIT Circular 50/2025 and government announcements specify E10 volume blending but no sub-quota language identified.

## 4. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

### Road Fuels (Gasoline)

**Special Consumption Tax (Excise) Rates (effective 2026):**

| Fuel Type | Rate | Effective Date | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mineral gasoline (RON 95/97) | 10% | 2026 (Law 66/2025/QH15) | Baseline rate |
| E5 RON92 | 8% | 2026 (Law 66/2025/QH15) | Preferential reduction vs. mineral |
| **E10** | **7%** | **2026 (Law 66/2025/QH15)** | **Lowest rate; incentive to adoption** |

**Preferential tax mechanism:**
- Law 66/2025/QH15 (Special Consumption Tax law, enacted 2026) grants a **3 percentage point reduction** vs. mineral gasoline (10% → 7% for E10), providing a price incentive at pump.
- No separate subsidy fund; tax advantage embedded in the excise structure.
- **Timeline variation (Apr–Jun 2026):** From April 16–June 30, 2026, excise tax on all petrol was 0% as a temporary government relief measure; from July 1, excise rates returned to Law 66/2025 levels.

**Business incentives:**
- Biofuel producers eligible for corporate income tax (CIT) preferences, import duty reductions, and agricultural land-use tax benefits (per draft proposals from MoIT).

**Confidence:** `CONFIRMED` — Tax rates confirmed by Vietnam.vn (Ministry of Finance statement on E10/E5/mineral tax differential), Law 66/2025/QH15 (Vietnamese legal database reference), and Vietnam+ news (June 2026 announcement). Temporary 0% rate (Apr–Jun) also reported in government press.

## 5. Sustainability / Certification Regime

### Road Fuels (E10/E5)

**Ethanol Certification:**
- **No mandatory national sustainability certification scheme identified** for domestic ethanol (sugarcane, cassava).
- Ethanol producers may seek **ISCC (International Sustainability and Carbon Certification)** or **2BSvs** certification for export markets, but these are voluntary/supply-chain driven, not mandated by Vietnamese law.
- **ISCC services available in Vietnam** (via certified auditors: DNV, TÜV Rheinland, SGS Vietnam) but uptake is non-binding.

**Feedstock restrictions:**
- No explicit ILUC (Indirect Land Use Change) risk assessment or high-ILUC feedstock ban found in primary Vietnamese regulations for E10/E5.
- Domestic feedstocks (sugarcane, cassava) are not subject to environmental restrictions in primary sources reviewed.

**Confidence:** `UNCORROBORATED` — Vietnamese government does not explicitly mandate sustainability certification for E10/E5 in primary sources. ISCC schemes available but uptake status unclear; MoIT announcements focus on blending and tax, not certification requirements.

### Broader Sustainability Context

- **Resolution 70-NQ/TW (2025)** sets national renewable energy target (25–30% of primary energy by 2030) but does not detail biofuel sustainability criteria.
- **No Red-II/RED-III transposition** (Vietnam is non-EU); sustainability framework differs from EU model.

**Confidence:** `UNCORROBORATED` — sustainability certification regime for Vietnamese biofuels not formally legislated as of July 15, 2026.

## 8. Five-Line Summary

1. **Obligation type:** Vietnam mandates volume-based blending: E10 (10% ethanol) mandatory nationwide for all unleaded gasoline sales from June 1, 2026, replacing RON 95 mineral fuel; E5 RON92 phased out by December 31, 2030.

2. **Current 2026 level:** E10 at 10% ethanol (mandatory from June 1, 2026); excise tax advantage (7% vs. 10% mineral rate) embedded in Law 66/2025/QH15; no diesel or SAF mandate identified.

3. **Next legislated change:** No published 2030 road-fuel escalation (e.g., no E15 or E20 step-up mandated); E5 availability ends Dec 31, 2030; E10 remains primary post-2030.

4. **RED III status:** Not applicable (Vietnam non-EU; no RED III transposition required).

5. **Data flags:** `GAP` — no legislated post-2026 trajectory; sustainability certification regime not mandated in primary law; diesel biofuel and aviation fuel mandates absent as of 2026-07-15.

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Only biofuels/bioliquids meeting RED sustainability + GHG-saving criteria (verified by
  ECS/LNEG) count toward the incorporation obligation and receive TdB. [CONFIRMED — DL 84/2022 via dgeg/lneg]
- Threshold ladder follows RED II Art. 29(10): **50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by installation
  vintage; **70 %** for RFNBOs. Exact figures not directly quoted from the DL text →
  PRIMARY-ONLY / flag. [FLAG]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- DL 84/2022 requires that verification of sustainability + GHG criteria be done
  **exclusively via a national or international voluntary scheme recognised by the European
  Commission.** Portugal has **no separate operational national scheme** — it relies on the
  EU-recognised voluntary schemes. [CONFIRMED — dgeg.gov.pt; DG ENER list]
- Usable schemes therefore include ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU,
  RTRS EU RED, KZR INiG, etc. (DG ENER recognised list, accessed 2026-07-04). [CONFIRMED — DG ENER]
- **Proof of sustainability:** a valid scheme certificate + PoS document per consignment,
  under mass-balance chain of custody; submitted/declared to ECS via the reporting channel. [PRIMARY-ONLY — DL 84/2022; scheme rules]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB operational (DG ENER: became operational **15 Jan 2024**; full traceability
  onboarding of first collectors by Oct 2024; systemic use ramped late 2024). Legal basis
  Art. 28(2)/(4) RED II, Art. 31a RED III, Art. 18 Impl. Reg. 2022/996. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER]
- **Mandatory-use date** for all economic operators is being finalised by the Commission
  with member states (no immediate sanctions post-21 Nov 2024; ~Jan 2025 earliest floated).
  PT operators are in scope; the PT-specific link between the ENSE/BUE reporting and UDB
  is **not primary-sourced** → flag. [FLAG]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Register as an operator on ENSE's Balcão Único da Energia (BUE)** before placing biofuel/biogas on the PT market or claiming TdB. → ENSE.
2. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE…) covering the full mass-balance chain, and issue a **PoS per consignment**. → certification body / self.
3. **Submit monthly sustainability + volume data** for ECS/LNEG verification via BUE (art. 19(3) DL 84/2022); TdB/TdC are issued monthly on that basis. → ENSE / ECS-LNEG.
4. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) and document them per consignment. → self + auditor.
5. **Respect feedstock rules:** high-ILUC (palm) within the 2019-level cap tapering to 0 by 2030; use double-counting only for qualifying Annex IX wastes/residues. → self + auditor.
6. **Onboard to the Union Database (UDB)** and record PoS/transaction data for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
7. **Undergo the annual voluntary-scheme audit**; expect inclusion in the ECS/LNEG annual national sustainability report. → certification body / ECS-LNEG.

**4-line summary — Portugal**
- Transposition: RED II via Decreto-Lei 84/2022 (9 Dec 2022, revoked DL 117/2010); RED III NOT yet adopted — partial-transposition draft decree-law in public consultation 25 Sep–25 Oct 2025, PT past the 21 May 2025 deadline (PROPOSED).

- Registry/UDB: ENSE's Balcão Único da Energia (reporting under art.19(3) DL 84/2022); UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024, EU mandatory-use date pending, PT link to confirm.
- Flags: DL 84/2022 full text not machine-read (GHG thresholds, palm caps, penalties PRIMARY-ONLY); no national palm ban beyond EU (UCO already dominant); "SAND" registry name unconfirmed (found BUE).

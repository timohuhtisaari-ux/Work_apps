### Notes / interaction with the mandate
- Austria's MÖSt already embeds a de-facto biofuel incentive through the two-tier "with/without biogenic content" rates; unlike SE/FI it is **not** structured as relief limited to volumes beyond the blending obligation. The blending/GHG-reduction obligation itself sits under the Kraftstoffverordnung (mandates agent's scope).
- CO2 pricing is a **separate instrument** (NEHG 2022), not part of the MÖSt. In 2026 it adds roughly 12.5 ct/L net to petrol and 13.8 ct/L net to diesel at 55 EUR/t (per BMF/ÖAMTC illustration).
- National ETD transposition: **Mineralölsteuergesetz 2022 (MinStG 2022)** (successor to MinStG 1995), transposing Directive 2003/96/EC.

## 4-line summary (Austria)
- Petrol (MÖSt): 482.00 EUR/1000 L for market E5/E10 (with biogenic content, ≤10 mg/kg S); 515.00 without. [CONFIRMED]
- Diesel (MÖSt): 397.00 EUR/1000 L for market B7 (with biogenic content); 425.00 without. [CONFIRMED]
- Biofuel relief: no separate E85/B100/HVO100 rate — the lower "with biogenic content" MÖSt tier is the only relief, not tied to exceeding the mandate.
- Flags: separate CO2 price 55 EUR/t (NEHG 2022) unchanged to 2027, EU-ETS2 obligation from 1 Jan 2028 (CONFIRMED); MinStG amendment BGBl. I 12/2026 (price-stabilisation) PRIMARY-ONLY; DG TAXUD/TEDB cross-check still not obtained; biomethane rate = gap.

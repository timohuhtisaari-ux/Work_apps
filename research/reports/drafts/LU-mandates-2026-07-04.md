## Executive summary

Luxembourg imposes a **mandatory minimum biofuel coverage rate (taux de couverture)** on
road petrol and diesel, expressed in **energy content**, under the **amended Law of
17 December 2010** on energy-product excise duties, administered through the **Customs and
Excise Administration**. Documented levels: **8.40 % (2024) and 8.80 % (2025)** overall,
with an **advanced-biofuels sub-quota of 0.4 % (2024) and 1.1 % (2025)** (Annex IX-A), and
a **3.0 % banking/carry-over** allowance between years. The trajectory targets **10 % by
2030**. The **2026 rate is set by the 2026 budget law (dossier 8600) amending the 2010
law** but **could not be retrieved from any accessible source this pass** (GAP).
Sustainability is governed by the grand-ducal regulations of 27 Feb 2011 and 3 Feb 2023; a
biofuels register is kept on the environment portal (emwelt.lu). **RED III transport
transposition is not yet in force** (no Luxembourg RED III transport measure on EUR-Lex NIM;
a modifying RGD is still before the Conseil d'État — see sustainability section). EU-wide
cross-reference: ETS2 compliance obligation starts 1 Jan 2028 (Reg. (EU) 2026/667, one-year
postponement adopted March 2026), auctioning 2027.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** producers, importers and authorised warehouse-keepers releasing
  energy products (road petrol/diesel) for consumption, who pay excise to the
  **Administration des douanes et accises**; the biofuel coverage obligation is verified on
  release for consumption. [S2, S4]
- **Sustainability & register:** biofuels count toward the coverage rate only if they meet
  the sustainability criteria of the **grand-ducal regulation of 27 February 2011** (the
  emwelt register page still references arts. 3–7 of the 2011 RGD; the **RGD of 3 February
  2023** is the current RED II transposition — confirmed to exist); a **biocarburants
  register** is maintained on the environment portal (emwelt.lu), where economic actors
  producing or importing biofuels — and actors reducing the CO₂ intensity of fossil fuels
  ("UER" credits) — register and file annual declarations. [S2, S5]
- **Basis:** energy-content coverage rate (not a per-litre volume mandate); administered
  fiscally (enforced via the excise/customs mechanism — see fuel-tax section).

| Biofuel/HVO/E85 reduction or exemption | **No reduced rates.** Biofuels are taxed at the rate of the **equivalent motor fuel** (art. 28(1) WA: petrol rate for E85, gasolie rate for FAME/HVO/vegetable oil liquid at 10 °C, on full volume). One generic correction exists: **art. 71h WA refund** for motor fuel consisting wholly/partly of biofuel (RED art. 2(i)) or renewable fuel (art. 9.7.1.1 Wet milieubeheer) — granted only if the bio/renewable fuel has **lower energy content** than the equivalent fuel **and ≥ 10 % of the energy** in the fuel is bio/renewable; refund = the energy-content difference as % of the equivalent fuel's excise (reference values: petrol 32 MJ/L, diesel 36 MJ/L, per RED Annex III). In practice this benefits high-blends like E85; HVO100/B100 gain little/nothing (similar energy content) and get no other relief. Relief is **not** conditioned on volumes beyond the jaarverplichting (no SE/FI-style restriction) — the main biofuel incentive is the ERE certificate market (brandstoftransitieverplichting from 2026; HBE market through 2025 — see mandates section) | Art. 28(1) WA; art. 71h WA (generic version in force since 1 Jan 2017, Fiscale vereenvoudigingswet 2017 via amendement-Schouten; text verified as valid 2026-01-01) | In force | CONFIRMED (mechanics); PRIMARY-ONLY (1 Jan 2017 origin date) | wetten.overheid.nl art. 28 & art. 71h (valid 2026-01-01); kennisbank.douane.nl Handboek Accijns (equivalence examples); Vakstudie/InView commentary (Tier 2) |
| Biogas/biomethane treatment | Gaseous fuels are **outside the accijns**; natural gas (incl. grid biomethane/groen gas) is taxed under the **energiebelasting**: gas delivered to a **CNG filling station** is taxed at **EUR 0.20928 /m³ (2026)** regardless of biogenic share — no reduced energy-tax rate for biomethane (support runs via EREs/HBEs). **Interaction with the mandate:** the Netherlands runs no tax-incentive scheme for biofuels — the brandstoftransitieverplichting with tradable EREs (NEa — mandates section; HBE jaarverplichting through 2025) carries the entire incentive. The art. 71h refund merely neutralises the per-litre energy-content penalty of volumetric excise for high blends; it is independent of the blending obligation, so no state-aid "beyond-mandate" restriction applies.

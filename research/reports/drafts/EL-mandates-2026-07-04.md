## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.1 Current obligation

Greece runs a **volumetric blending obligation** plus an **annual "pure biodiesel"
allocation programme** (κατανομή αυτούσιου βιοντίζελ). The core act is **Law
3054/2002, Article 15A** ("Biofuels and other renewable fuels"). Diesel sold in
Greece is **B7** (up to 7 % v/v FAME); petrol carries a mandatory **bioethanol/
bio-ether share of 3.3 % v/v**. Biodiesel volumes are allocated annually to
eligible producers/importers by Joint Ministerial Decision (KYA). A separate
GHG-intensity/RES-T framework sits in **Law 4062/2012** (FQD Art. 7a / RED
transposition). Supervision: **YPEN** (Ministry of Environment & Energy);
fuel-tax/quality control via **AADE** chemical laboratories.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Annual biodiesel allocation (κατανομή αυτούσιου βιοντίζελ):** a "Programme for
  the Allocation of Biofuel Quantities" set each year by KYA (Ministers of
  Development / Environment-Energy and Rural Development). The consolidated art.15A
  text sets the decision before **30 November** for the following year (older
  guidance cited 15 April — check the operative KYA). Quantities are allocated to
  eligible operators via a formula weighing Greek energy-crop volumes and other
  criteria; 10 % of the allocated quantity is reserved for producers with
  university/research cooperation contracts. (S2, S4, S5)
- **Obligated parties:** refiners/importers releasing petrol and diesel; biodiesel
  beneficiaries must physically supply their allocated volumes within Greece. (S2)
- **Bioethanol:** mandatory 3.3 % v/v in petrol from 2020, increases require a
  Ministerial Decision. (S2, S3)
- **Certificates/registry:** Greece uses the allocation-quota model rather than a
  tradable-certificate market; no TdB/HBE-style certificate registry identified —
  **gap** (confirm whether a Guarantee-of-Origin/UDB registry now applies).
- Enforcement is partly via the fuel-quality/tax system (AADE chemical labs) —
  cross-reference the tax/sustainability sections; not researched in depth here.

### 1.3 Future trajectory

- **Legislated (art.15A):** bioethanol 3.3 % v/v "for 2020 and subsequent years"
  (no further legislated step located); biodiesel percentage set annually by KYA
  within the B7 wall. `CONFIRMED` (static) — no year-by-year statutory step-up
  found in national law.
- **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) — NOT TRANSPOSED as of 2026-07-04
  (deadline was 21 May 2025 → Greece in infringement).**
  - ΥΠΕΝ draft RED III implementing law in **public consultation 31 Mar – 14 Apr
    2026**; mandates greater renewable-fuel use in industry and transport with
    "clear national targets" but does not yet fix the transport sub-quotas in the
    text reviewed. Still **PROPOSED**. (S6)
  - The European Commission **referred Greece to the Court of Justice of the EU
    (April 2026) for failure to transpose the RES Directive, requesting financial
    penalties**. (S7)
  - RED III baseline that the bill must implement: 29 % RES-T **or** 14.5 %
    GHG-intensity reduction by 2030; combined advanced-biofuel + RFNBO target
    (1 % 2025 → 5.5 % 2030, energy, with multipliers). `PROPOSED` for GR.
- **EU-wide:** ETS2 compliance for road-transport fuel suppliers starts 1 Jan 2028
  (Reg. (EU) 2026/667; auctioning from 2027) — applies to GR.

### 1.4 Conflicts / gaps

- **Gap:** current national **advanced-biofuel sub-quota** not located in primary
  law (only the RED II EU baseline and the 10 % R&D allocation reservation).
- **Gap/UNCORROBORATED:** the GHG-intensity reduction step-up (8 % in 2026) and the
  €43/GJ penalty come only from ePURE (Tier 2); confirm against Law 4062/2012 and
  the 2010 ΥΑ text.
- **Conflict:** allocation-decision deadline "30 November" (consolidated art.15A)
  vs "15 April" (secondary/older guidance) — resolve against the current KYA.
- **Gap:** current-year (2025/2026) biodiesel allocation volume and the 2026 KYA
  reference not located; latest confirmed figures are ~108,650 kL (2020) /
  110,000 kL (2021).

## 5-line summary (Greece)
- Obligation type: volumetric blending (v/v) + annual biodiesel allocation programme (Law 3054/2002 art.15A); separate GHG-intensity framework (Law 4062/2012).
- Current level (2026): **biodiesel B7 up to 7 % v/v in diesel; bioethanol/bio-ether 3.3 % v/v in petrol**; biodiesel volumes set by annual KYA.
- Next legislated change: none scheduled in art.15A (static 3.3 %); step-ups depend on the pending RED III law.
- RED III status: **NOT transposed** — draft law in consultation 31 Mar–14 Apr 2026; Greece referred to the CJEU (Apr 2026) for RES non-transposition.
- Flags: advanced-biofuel sub-quota `UNCORROBORATED`/gap; GHG 8 %-2026 step and €43/GJ penalty `UNCORROBORATED` (ePURE only); allocation-deadline `CONFLICT` (30 Nov vs 15 Apr).

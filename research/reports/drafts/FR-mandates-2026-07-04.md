## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Scheme identification

France has no volume quota. The **TIRUERT** (taxe incitative relative à l'utilisation d'énergie renouvelable dans les transports) sets national target percentages of renewable energy per fuel pool; a fuel supplier that meets the target owes zero tax, and pays 140 €/hL on its taxable volumes multiplied by the shortfall if it does not. Historically codified in **Article 266 quindecies of the Code des douanes**; recodified by **Ordonnance n° 2025-1247 of 17 December 2025** into **Article 300 bis of the Code général des impôts (CGI)** (Art. 266 quindecies version shown on Légifrance as valid to 31 March 2026; CGI Art. 300 bis is the version in force on 4 July 2026). The **carburéacteur (jet) pool was removed from TIRUERT on 1 January 2025** (Art. 24, Loi n° 2025-217 de finances pour 2025) because ReFuelEU Aviation (Reg. (EU) 2023/2405) took over.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** the persons liable for the energy excise (accise sur les énergies) on petrol and diesel products, i.e. those releasing fuels for consumption from tax warehouses ("metteurs à la consommation en sortie d'entrepôt fiscal"). (CGI Art. 300 bis; douane.gouv.fr TIRUERT page.) [S1][S3]
- **Declaration/payment:** annual TIRUERT return declared and paid **by 10 April of the year following** the compliance year (CGI Art. 300 bis; FNA). [S1][S4]
- **Evidence of renewable energy:** via certificates per the DGDDI circulaire (attestation models in Annexes II/II bis/II ter: acquisition, content, renewable-energy claims) and mass-balance/comptabilité matière accounting; implementing texts: Décret n° 2019-570 (as amended by Décret n° 2025-226 of 10 March 2025) and Décret n° 2023-1420. The **CarbuRe** registry (DGEC) is the electronic system referenced in the source registry; not independently re-verified in this pass (GAP — the 61-page 2025 circulaire PDF could not be machine-read). [S3][S5][S9][S10]
- **Banking/borrowing:** no banking/borrowing provision located in the article text fetched. GAP — to verify in the circulaire.
- **No certificate market in the Dutch/Italian sense**: compliance is per-taxpayer via the tax return; renewable energy can be attributed between operators via the attestation system (cross-check with tax researcher).

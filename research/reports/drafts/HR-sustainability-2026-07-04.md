### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **Biofuels/bioliquids:** RED thresholds apply via the methodology rulebook — **50 %** (installations operating before 5 Oct 2015), **60 %** (start 6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021); **70 %** for RFNBOs/recycled-carbon fuels. Not quoted numerically from the HR rulebook text this pass (nn.hr 503) → framework-level. [PRIMARY-ONLY / framework — S1, S6]
- **Supplier FQD target:** ≥6 % lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction vs 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ (Zakon Art. 29.a). [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- Croatia accepts **EU-recognised voluntary national/international schemes** ("dobrovoljni nacionalni ili međunarodni sustavi") as evidence of sustainability, per the Pravilnik and Zakon Art. 17.a; use of such a scheme relieves the operator of separately proving the elements the scheme covers. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1, S2, S3]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 schemes as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG, Better Biomass**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6]
- **No separate stand-alone Croatian national certification scheme** was identified beyond the Pravilnik's own verification framework; verification of independence of information is required and, for feedstock grown outside Croatia, HROTE/the authority reviews sustainability certificates and land-use proof. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2]
- **Proof of sustainability per consignment:** a **certificate ("potvrda")** must accompany each biofuel consignment proving the batch meets the sustainability requirements; the obligor placing biofuel/renewable energy on the market must be able to demonstrate compliance regardless of whether the feedstock was grown in Croatia or imported. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB established under **RED III Art. 31a** (formal establishment deadline 21 Nov 2024; operational from Jan 2024) with chain-of-custody rules in **Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996 Art. 18**. Collectors/economic operators onboarded through their **voluntary schemes** across 2024–2025; the **mandatory-use date and sanction start remain to be finalised** by the Commission with member states. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6, S7, S10]
- **HR operators are in scope via EU law.** Because RED III is not yet transposed, **no HR-specific UDB implementing provision** exists; the obligation applies directly. Operators onboarded via their voluntary scheme risk **certificate suspension if they lack a UDB account** (scheme-administered onboarding). [PRIMARY-ONLY / flag — S7, S10]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) covering the batch, accepted under the Zakon/Pravilnik as sustainability proof. → certification body.
2. **Ensure each biofuel consignment is accompanied by a valid sustainability certificate ("potvrda")** demonstrating the sustainability + GHG-saving criteria, carried through to the obligor. → self / supplier.
3. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 %; RFNBO 70 %) and the supplier **≥6 % FQD** lifecycle reduction; document per batch. → self + verifier.
4. **File the annual compliance report to the Ministry (mingo) by 28 February**; expect Ministry confirmation by 30 April. → Ministry of economy/energy.
5. **Respect the 7 % crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out** (Zakon Art. 8(4)(8): 2019 cap → 0 % by 2030). → self + verifier.
6. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme and record in-scope transactions; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension (mandatory-use date pending). → UDB / voluntary scheme.

**4-line summary — Croatia**
- Transposition: RED II sustainability via **Zakon o biogorivima za prijevoz** (Art. 17.a) + the sustainability-requirements **Pravilnik** (NN 83/2013; 2021 re-issue); **RED III NOT transposed → letter of formal notice (Sept 2024)**, in infringement, not escalated June 2026 (corroborator-confirmed).
- Certification: **EU-recognised voluntary schemes accepted** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…); proof per consignment = sustainability **certificate ("potvrda")**; no stand-alone national scheme beyond the Pravilnik framework.
- Registry/UDB: **no dedicated national sustainability registry** found (Ministry verification, Art. 27; HROTE = incentive administrator); annual report to the Ministry **by 28 Feb**, confirmation by 30 Apr; UDB applies via EU law (mandatory-use date pending).
- Flags: current sustainability-Pravilnik NN number unconfirmed (nn.hr/hera.hr 503); HROTE vs Ministry verification split to confirm; GHG 50/60/65 % framework-level; no palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU.

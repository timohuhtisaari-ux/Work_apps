### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

| Feedstock/installation | Minimum GHG saving vs fossil comparator | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Installations in operation ≤ 5 Oct 2015 | 50 % | PRIMARY-ONLY | biokraft-austria |
| Installations started 6 Oct 2015 – 31 Dec 2020 | 60 % | PRIMARY-ONLY | biokraft-austria |
| Installations started from 1 Jan 2021 | 65 % (RED II Art. 29(10)) | PRIMARY-ONLY (flag: confirm figure in KVO annex) | biokraft-austria / RED II |
| RFNBOs / recycled-carbon fuels | 70 % (RED II/III) | UNCORROBORATED (flag) | RED II baseline |

Compliance with sustainability *and* GHG-saving criteria must be documented by
**mass balance** along the whole chain (cultivation → finished product).
[CONFIRMED — Umweltbundesamt/BMIMI]

> Separate from the above, the *supplier obligation* itself is a GHG-intensity
> reduction quota rising to **13 % by 2030** (BGBl. II Nr. 452/2022; 13 % by 2030
> corroborated by biokraft-austria) — that is the mandates researcher's scope;
> cross-referenced here only.

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Austria accepts the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** for demonstrating
  sustainability/GHG criteria; certification-scheme choice (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU,
  2BSvs, SURE, RSB, etc.) is free to the operator, but **participation in elNa is
  mandatory** for every installation in Austria. [CONFIRMED — Umweltbundesamt; WKO]
- Austria additionally hosts a **national voluntary scheme**: the **Austrian
  Agricultural Certification Scheme (AACS)**, EU-recognised by Commission decision
  **26 Sep 2022** (for compliance of Austrian agricultural biomass with RED
  criteria). [CONFIRMED — DG ENER voluntary-schemes page]
- Full EU-recognised list (DG ENER, as accessed 2026-07-04) includes 2BSvs,
  Better Biomass, Bonsucro EU, ISCC EU, KZR INiG, REDcert, RSB, RTRS EU RED,
  SURE, AACS and others. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER]

| Function | Official data collection for all sustainable-biofuel movements in Austria; basis for reporting to the European Commission and for company reporting to the ministry | PRIMARY-ONLY | Umweltbundesamt |

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- The EU **Union Database for Biofuels (UDB)** became operational and open for
  economic-operator registration on **15 Jan 2024** (liquid fuels first; gaseous
  fuels phased later), legal basis **Art. 31a RED (2018/2001)** and Implementing
  Reg. (EU) 2022/996. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER news 2024-01-15]
- Operators active in the Austrian market fall within UDB scope; elNa serves the
  national traceability/reporting function and Umweltbundesamt uses it for EU
  reporting. Exact **mandatory-use start date and the elNa↔UDB data-hand-off
  mechanism for AT operators is not pinned to a primary source** →
  `PRIMARY-ONLY`/flag for corroboration. [FLAG]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Register in elNa** with Umweltbundesamt GmbH (obtain registration number + user account) and complete the mandatory UBA training before issuing or receiving any Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis. → Umweltbundesamt.
2. **Obtain certification** under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, or national AACS for Austrian agri-biomass). → chosen scheme / certification body.
3. **Issue/transfer Nachhaltigkeitsnachweise (NHN)** per consignment in elNa, maintaining full mass balance from cultivation to finished product. → elNa.
4. **Meet GHG-saving thresholds** applicable to your installation vintage (50/60/65 %) and ensure only sustainable, non-high-ILUC feedstocks are credited (palm/PFAD excluded since 1 July 2021). → self + auditor.
5. **Enter transaction/sustainability data in the UDB** for consignments in scope (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → UDB [confirm AT mandatory-use date].
6. **Submit the annual obligation report** to the authority and undergo the annual certification audit and any UBA on-site inspection. → Umweltbundesamt / auditor.

**4-line summary — Austria**
- Transposition: RED II via KVO 2012 amdt BGBl II 452/2022 (in force Jan 2023); RED III transport/sustainability NOT yet transposed (deadline 21 May 2025 missed; no AT transport measure in EUR-Lex NIM — direct-effect assumed).
- Certification: free choice of EU-recognised voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE…) plus national AACS; elNa participation mandatory for all AT installations.
- Registry/UDB: elNa (Umweltbundesamt GmbH), Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis per consignment on mass balance; UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024 (AT mandatory-use date to confirm).
- Flags: RIS 503 on research date AND corroborator re-try (BGBl facts need cross-check); palm+PFAD high-ILUC excluded since 1 July 2021 (early mover, CONFIRMED); annual-report deadline, penalties, 2024 Novelle BGBl no. and UDB hand-off unpinned.

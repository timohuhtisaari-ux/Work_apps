## Executive summary

Luxembourg imposes a **mandatory minimum biofuel coverage rate (taux de couverture)** on
road petrol and diesel, expressed in **energy content**, under the **amended Law of
17 December 2010** on energy-product excise duties, administered through the **Customs and
Excise Administration**. Documented levels: **8.40 % (2024) and 8.80 % (2025)** overall,
with an **advanced-biofuels sub-quota of 0.4 % (2024) and 1.1 % (2025)** (Annex IX-A), and
a **3.0 % banking/carry-over** allowance between years. The trajectory targets **10 % by
2030**. The **2026 rate is set by the 2026 budget law (dossier 8600) amending the 2010
law** but was not retrievable this pass. Sustainability is governed by the grand-ducal
regulations of 27 Feb 2011 and 3 Feb 2023; a biofuels register is kept on the environment
portal (emwelt.lu). **RED III transposition status is unconfirmed** (no Luxembourg RED III
transport measure located on EUR-Lex NIM). EU-wide cross-reference: ETS2 compliance
obligation starts 1 Jan 2028 (Reg. (EU) 2026/667), auctioning 2027.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** producers, importers and authorised warehouse-keepers releasing
  energy products (road petrol/diesel) for consumption, who pay excise to the
  **Administration des douanes et accises**; the biofuel coverage obligation is verified on
  release for consumption. [S2, S4]
- **Sustainability & register:** biofuels count toward the coverage rate only if they meet
  the sustainability criteria of the **grand-ducal regulation of 27 February 2011** (updated
  by the **RGD of 3 February 2023**); a **biocarburants register** is maintained on the
  environment portal (emwelt.lu / environnement.public.lu), where economic actors producing
  or importing biofuels — and actors reducing the CO₂ intensity of fossil fuels ("UER"
  credits) — register and file annual declarations. [S2, S5]
- **Basis:** energy-content coverage rate (not a per-litre volume mandate); administered
  fiscally (mandate is enforced via the excise/customs mechanism — see fuel-tax section).

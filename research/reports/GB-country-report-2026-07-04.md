# United Kingdom — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** United Kingdom (GB) — non-EU (post-Brexit, independent regime)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done
- **Regulatory regime & EU relationship:** The UK left the EU; **EU RED III, the EU
  Union Database (UDB) and the EU ETS/ETS2 do not apply.** Road/non-road renewable
  fuel is governed by the UK's own **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)**
  under the Renewable Transport Fuel Obligations Order 2007 (as amended), and
  aviation by the separate **SAF Mandate** (SI 2024/1187, from 1 Jan 2025), both
  administered by the **Department for Transport (DfT)** Low Carbon Fuels unit.
  Sustainability/GHG criteria are set nationally via DfT guidance and the **RTFO
  Operating System (ROS)**, not the EU UDB. Road transport fuel is **not** in the
  UK ETS and there is no EU-ETS2-style road-fuel carbon charge. Do not impose EU
  RED III transposition deadlines or ETS2 dates on the UK.

## Executive summary

The UK runs two parallel certificate-based obligations, not a blend or GHG mandate
in the EU sense. Under the **RTFO**, road/non-road fuel suppliers above **450,000
L/yr** must redeem **Renewable Transport Fuel Certificates (RTFCs)** or pay a
buy-out; the obligation is **12.5% of total fuel by volume for 2026** (rising
0.35 pt/yr to 14.6% by 2032), with a crop cap of 3% (falling to 2% by 2032) and
buy-out prices of **50p (standard) / 80p (development fuel)**. A separate **SAF
Mandate** requires **3.60% of jet fuel to be SAF in 2026** (2.00% in 2025; rising to
10% by 2030), with buy-outs of £4.70/litre (SAF) and £5.00/litre (PtL) and a
power-to-liquid sub-obligation from 2028. On tax, the UK abolished its biofuel duty
differential in 2010, so biodiesel, bioethanol and HVO all pay the fossil-equivalent
fuel duty (52.95 ppl for most of 2026, rising in three steps to 57.95 ppl by 1 March
2027); support runs entirely through the RTFO, not the tax system. The top supplier
obligations are: register for ROS, hold independently-verified sustainability
(C&S) evidence for every RTFC claim, apply the crop cap and double-counting rules,
and meet the obligation by **15 September** of the following year. Note two open
items carried forward: the RTFO GHG-saving threshold is an **unresolved conflict**
(50/60% vs 55–65%), and UK road transport is **not** in the UK ETS.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

The UK runs **two parallel certificate-based obligations**:
- **RTFO** — a renewable-volume obligation on road/non-road fuel suppliers, met with
  **Renewable Transport Fuel Certificates (RTFCs)**; legal basis = **Renewable
  Transport Fuel Obligations Order 2007** (SI 2007/3072) as amended (notably SI
  2021/1420 and later amendment orders).
- **SAF Mandate** — an aviation obligation met with **SAF certificates**; legal basis
  = **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligations (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) Order 2024**
  (SI 2024/1187), in force 1 Jan 2025.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **RTFCs:** obligated suppliers either redeem RTFCs (earned by supplying sustainable
  renewable fuel, or bought from other suppliers) or pay the **buy-out** (50p main /
  80p development-fuel). Development-fuel RTFCs can be over-redeemed and also count
  toward the main obligation.
- **Registry / administrator:** DfT RTFO Administrator; online RTFO Operating System
  (ROS) for accreditation, monthly/annual reporting, and RTFC issuance.
- **Threshold:** RTFO bites at ≥450,000 L/yr of relevant fuel; smaller suppliers are
  exempt.
- **Banking/trading:** RTFCs are tradable; limited carry-over between obligation
  periods (exact banking limits — verify in 2026 compliance guidance; gap).
- **SAF Mandate:** parallel scheme with its own certificates, buy-out, and (from 2028)
  a PtL sub-obligation; jet-fuel suppliers above a de-minimis are obligated (exact
  tonnage threshold not verified — gap). A **revenue certainty mechanism** for SAF
  is being legislated (Sustainable Aviation Fuel Act) — being introduced by end-2026.

## 2. Fuel taxation

**Scope note:** The UK left the EU; the **Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC does
not apply** and there is **no EU ETS2** obligation. Fuel duty ("hydrocarbon oils
duty") sits in the **Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979 (HODA)**, with rates set by
Finance Acts / Budget resolutions and administered by **HMRC**. The UK abolished
its biofuel duty differential in 2010, so biofuels are taxed at the fossil-equivalent
rate. Carbon pricing of road fuel is via fuel duty only — road transport is **not**
in the UK ETS. The temporary 5p cut (since March 2022) is being unwound in three
steps; the table shows the staged rates.

**The temporary 5p cut (status).** The 5 pence/litre cut introduced at Spring
Statement (March 2022) was **extended to 31 December 2026** and is then reversed in
three tranches (1p on 1 Sep 2026, 2p on 1 Dec 2026, 2p on 1 Mar 2027). From
**1 March 2027** the main petrol/diesel rate returns to the long-standing
**57.95 ppl**. So for most of 2026 the effective main rate is **52.95 ppl**, rising
to 53.95 ppl from 1 September 2026. The cut applies proportionately to
other/rebated fuels.

**Biofuel treatment and mandate interaction.** There is **no reduced excise rate for
biofuels** — biodiesel, bioethanol and HVO used as road fuel are charged the
fossil-equivalent duty (the 20p/L biofuel differential was withdrawn in 2010).
Biofuel support in the UK is delivered entirely through the **RTFO certificate
scheme (RTFCs)** run by DfT (see §1), **not** through the tax system. Consequently
there is no state-aid "above-the-mandate" tax condition of the Sweden/Finland type.

**Carbon pricing / UK ETS position.** Road transport fuel is **outside the UK ETS**.
The UK ETS covers power, energy-intensive industry and aviation; announced
expansions are **maritime (from July 2026)** and **waste incineration (from 2028,
with monitoring from Jan 2026)**. Unlike the EU's ETS2, the UK has made **no policy
commitment and has no live consultation to bring road transport (or buildings) into
the UK ETS** — this should be stated explicitly to counter any assumption of an
EU-ETS2-style charge on UK road fuel.

## 3. Sustainability & compliance requirements

> Framing: The UK **left the EU**; RED/EU voluntary-scheme law does not apply.
> Sustainability & GHG criteria are set nationally under the **Renewable Transport
> Fuel Obligation (RTFO)**, administered by the **Department for Transport (DfT)** as
> RTFO Administrator. The UK does **not** use the EU Union Database — it uses the
> **RTFO Operating System (ROS)**.

### 3.1 Transposition / legal instruments
- **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligations Order 2007 (as amended)** — the operative UK instrument (certificate-based RTFC scheme). Sustainability/GHG criteria are set by DfT and detailed in the annual **RTFO/SAF Mandate Technical Guidance** and **Compliance Guidance** (2026 editions, valid 01/01/26–31/12/26). Related: **Motor Fuel (Road Vehicle and Mobile Machinery) GHG Emissions Reporting Regulations** for the parallel GHG-credit route. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (gov.uk guidance) — corroborator to confirm the amending SI reference on legislation.gov.uk.
- Post-Brexit divergence: the UK runs its **own** approved-schemes list and its **own** registry; it is not bound by RED III or the EU UDB. `CONFIRMED` (gov.uk).

- Land criteria: no feedstock from land with high biodiversity value or high carbon stock (status referenced to **January 2008**); forestry legality/regeneration and (for agricultural-waste feedstocks) soil-quality criteria apply. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

### 3.3 Accepted certification / proof of sustainability
- The UK maintains its **own DfT-approved list** of voluntary schemes (published on gov.uk; page updated **27 Nov 2025** per draft — not independently re-fetched this pass). Approved schemes include: **2BSvs, Better Biomass, Bonsucro EU, ISCC EU, KZR INiG, REDcert-EU, Red Tractor, RSB EU RED, RTRS EU RED, SSAP-RED, SQC, TASCC** (≈13 schemes). Several UK national schemes exclude soil-carbon criteria / lack full GHG coverage, so extra evidence may be required. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (gov.uk list) + Tier-2 (ISCC/REDcert notices of DfT approval).
- Using a voluntary scheme does **not** discharge the operator: the **Administrator may demand additional evidence across the entire chain of custody**, and all C&S data must be **independently verified** (below). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

### 3.4 Independent verification requirement
- **All RTFC applications must be independently verified** by an approved verifier and demonstrate compliance with RTFO sustainability criteria; the Administrator checks C&S data against the RTFO Guidance. There is a dedicated **RTFO and SAF Mandate Third-Party Assurance Guidance 2026**. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (gov.uk essential guide + assurance guidance).

### 3.5 National registry & UDB status
- **RTFO Operating System (ROS)** — suppliers use ROS to report fuel volumes, submit RTFC applications, and redeem/trade RTFCs; ROS validates volumes against **HMRC** duty data. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (gov.uk).
- **UDB:** the UK does **not** participate in the EU Union Database; ROS is the equivalent national system. `CONFIRMED`.

### 3.6 Feedstock rules — crop cap, waste/UCO
- **Crop cap** ("relevant crop" RTFCs): introduced 2018 at **4%** of a supplier's total relevant fuel supply, **decreasing to 3% by 2026** and **2% by 2032**. `CONFIRMED` (Notes & definitions + DfT statutory review + Compliance/technical guidance).
- **Wastes/residues, dedicated energy crops, RFNBOs** earn **double RTFCs**; recycled carbon fuels earn single **development** RTFCs. `CONFIRMED`.
- **UCO** is a major waste feedstock; eligible wastes/residues are on the **RTFO and SAF Mandate list of feedstocks (wastes and residues)** published on gov.uk. Waste/residue claims still require chain-of-custody evidence and independent verification. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- No EU-style Annex IX; the UK "wastes and residues" list and "development fuel" categories are the UK equivalents. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **If supplying ≥ 450,000 L/yr** of relevant road/non-road fuel, register with the **DfT RTFO Administrator** and obtain **RTFO Operating System (ROS)** access. Authority: DfT.
2. **Track the 2026 RTFO obligation** — 12.5% of total fuel by volume — and redeem sufficient RTFCs (or pay buy-out) to meet it; apply **double counting** (2 RTFCs) correctly for wastes/residues/RFNBOs, confirming feedstocks against the gov.uk wastes-and-residues list.
3. **Respect the crop cap:** relevant-crop RTFCs limited to **3% of total relevant fuel supply for 2026** (falling to 2% by 2032). Authority: DfT (Compliance Guidance 2026).
4. **For every RTFC claim, hold Carbon & Sustainability (C&S) evidence** showing the applicable GHG saving (**note the unresolved 50/60% vs 55–65% conflict** — resolve against the 2026 Technical/C&S Guidance) and land criteria (Jan 2008 baseline).
5. **Use a DfT-approved voluntary scheme** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, etc.) as evidence and be ready to supply **whole-chain** evidence on request. Authority: DfT approved-schemes list.
6. **Obtain independent third-party verification** of all C&S data before RTFC award (RTFO and SAF Mandate Third-Party Assurance Guidance 2026).
7. **Meet the RTFO obligation by 15 September** of the year following the obligation period via ROS, or pay the buy-out (**50p standard / 80p development**). Authority: DfT RTFO Administrator.
8. **Jet-fuel suppliers:** comply separately with the **SAF Mandate** — **3.60% SAF for 2026** — surrendering SAF certificates or paying the buy-out (£4.70/litre SAF, £5.00/litre PtL); a PtL sub-obligation begins in 2028. Authority: DfT.
9. **Tax:** register with **HMRC** for fuel duty; note there is **no biofuel duty relief** (biodiesel/bioethanol/HVO pay the fossil-equivalent rate) and **no UK ETS charge** on road fuel; mind the staged fuel-duty increases (52.95 → 53.95 ppl on 1 Sep 2026 → 55.95 ppl on 1 Dec 2026 → 57.95 ppl on 1 Mar 2027).

## 2. Fuel taxation

The **Impuesto sobre Hidrocarburos** is set by **Ley 38/1992 de Impuestos Especiales, art. 50** (Tarifa 1ª), Spain's implementation of Directive 2003/96/EC. Since 1 Jan 2019 each epigraph carries a state **tipo general** plus a state **tipo especial** (the latter replaced the former regional/autonomous-community tranche — flat 72 €/1000 L for road fuels). **Crucial current context:** in response to the Middle-East (Iran) crisis fuel-price spike, Spain cut fuel taxes temporarily: **RD-ley 7/2026** (20 March, rates + 10 % VAT on fuels, 22 Mar–30 Jun 2026, adjusted by RD-ley 10/2026 of 29 April), then **RD-ley 18/2026** (29 June) with a phase-out: cuts of ≈15 c€/L in July, ≈10 c€/L in August, ≈5 c€/L in September 2026, full reversion planned 1 Oct 2026. The 2024–25 plan to **equalise diesel with petrol** (an RRF commitment) has still **not** been adopted.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps (taxation scope)

- **RESOLVED (was FLAG):** ETD-minima exposure of the July–September 2026 rates — see the "ETD compliance" row above: Spain invoked ETD Art. 19 and notified the Commission; **Council authorisation not yet located** → monitor. (Corroborator, 2026-07-04.)
- **⚠ CORRECTED (ETS2 date, 2026-07-04):** the draft said road-fuel ETS2 obligations begin "2027"; Regulation (EU) 2026/667 (adopted 11 Mar 2026) postponed the start of ETS2 operation for road transport/buildings to **1 Jan 2028** (auctioning 2027, first surrender 2029). Row and source log updated; CONFIRMED via EUR-Lex OJ L_202600667 + ICAP + EEA.

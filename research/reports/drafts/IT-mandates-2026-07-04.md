## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Scheme identification

Italy runs an **energy-based blending/release-for-consumption obligation** (obbligo di immissione in consumo) on suppliers of petrol and diesel for road and rail transport, with tradable **CIC certificates (certificati di immissione in consumo)** managed by **GSE** via the **BIOCAR** platform. Framework: **D.Lgs. 199/2021 Arts. 39–40** (RED II, now amended by the RED III decree **D.Lgs. 9 January 2026 n. 5**) implemented by **DM (MASE) 16 March 2023 n. 107** ("DM biocarburanti", published by comunicato in GU Serie Generale n. 96 of 24-04-2023, as amended — including DM 20 October 2023 n. 343 and DM 17 June 2024). Annual quotas sit in **Allegato 5** of DM 107/2023.

**Important caveat:** the DM 107/2023 text on mase.gov.it returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches, and the Gazzetta entry is a notice only. Quota values below therefore rest on concordant Tier-2 reproductions of the decree (ReteAmbiente, Certifico, Vocati, Rinnovabili.it, GSE FAQ snippets); confidence labels reflect this.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** "soggetti obbligati" — parties releasing petrol/diesel for consumption (excise-relevant releases) for road and rail transport (DM 107/2023 Art. 2(1)(v)); from 2026 natural gas/biomethane road volumes are deemed compliant automatically (D.Lgs. 5/2026); **LPG is drawn into the obligation with a phase-in: 50 % compliance in 2027, 100 % from 2028** (per D.Lgs. 5/2026 as reported by Federmetano — verify in the decree text). [S2][S7]
- **Certificates:** CIC issued by **GSE**; 1 CIC = 10 Gcal (5 Gcal advanced); multiple CIC types by feedstock/carrier; DM 107/2023 expanded tradable CIC combinations **from 3 to 25**; trading among obligated parties/producers on the **BIOCAR** platform. [S1][S3][S9]
- **Compliance calendar (GSE regole applicative):** self-declaration of previous-year releases 1–28 February; GSE issues CIC by 30 May; transactions registered by 31 October; GSE verification 1–30 November, cancelling the CICs used. [S3]
- **Banking/borrowing:** deficits below the sanctionability threshold may be made up the following year (in addition to that year's obligation); surplus-CIC carry-over to the next year exists as a GSE FAQ topic ("rinvio CIC eccedenti") — exact limits not verified (GAP). [S2][S11][S12]
- **Registry/administration:** GSE (BIOCAR IT platform), on behalf of MASE; sanctions via ministry. Sustainability certification (Sistema Nazionale di Certificazione) is the sustainability researcher's scope.

Germany — monthly biofuel blending volumes

Tonnes blended into mineral-oil fuels, by biocomponent. Liquid blends only — neat fuels (HVO100, B100, E85) excluded.

Source: BAFA, Amtliche Mineralöldaten (Table 9), Jan 2025 – Apr 2026 · latest edition published 2 Jul 2026 (~2-month lag)

FAME = biodiesel · HVO = hydrotreated vegetable oil (blended, not HVO100) · BTL/other = biomass-to-liquid & other bio-additives · ETBE-ethanol = bioethanol bound in ETBE · Biokerosin = SAF (reported from 2026). BAFA does not report gaseous fuels (biomethane, Bio-LNG/CNG).

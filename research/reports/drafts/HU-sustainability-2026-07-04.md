### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

Decree 821/2021 **§3** transposes the RED II/III GHG-saving ladder (parsed from the consolidated text — PRIMARY-ONLY, re-verify §3):

| Threshold | Applies to | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **50 %** | installations in operation before 5 Oct 2015 | PRIMARY-ONLY (S3) |
| **60 %** | installations 6 Oct 2015 – 31 Dec 2020 | PRIMARY-ONLY (S3) |
| **65 %** | transport biofuels from installations starting 1 Jan 2021 | PRIMARY-ONLY (S3) |
| **70–80 %** | electricity/heat from solid/gaseous biomass (tiered by vintage/capacity) | PRIMARY-ONLY (S3) |

Only biofuels meeting these thresholds and the land-use criteria may count toward the transport renewable-fuel share (mandates scope) and be issued a Hungarian sustainability document.

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Hungary operates a **national verification + registration system (BÜHG / BIONYOM)** administered by NÉBIH **and** accepts **EU-recognised voluntary schemes**: the NÉBIH registration guidance states operators may demonstrate sustainability "under a voluntary sustainability scheme other than the BÜHG system" (*"a BÜHG-rendszertől eltérő, más önkéntes fenntarthatósági rendszer szerinti"*). [PRIMARY-ONLY — S6, S7]
- EU-recognised voluntary schemes usable (per DG ENER list, accessed 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, KZR INiG**, etc. [CONFIRMED — S8]
- **Proof-of-sustainability documents (national system, Decree 821/2021):**
  - **biomassza igazolás** — producer biomass declaration (cultivated / non-cultivated) (§5)
  - **fenntarthatósági igazolás** — sustainability declaration issued by a BÜHG-registered trader/processor/fuel distributor for intermediates, biofuels, liquid bio-energy carriers and solid biomass fuels (§7–8)
  - **fenntarthatósági bizonyítvány** — official certificate confirming compliance (§18)
  - Forest biomass additionally requires a **szállítójegy** (transport document, §6). [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2, S3]
  - **Only BÜHG-registered** traders/processors/distributors may issue the national sustainability declarations. [CONFIRMED — S2, S6]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB operational since **15 Jan 2024** (registered operators can log initial stock + transactions); first collectors onboarded by **1 Oct 2024**; no immediate sanctions after **21 Nov 2024**; Commission finalising the mandatory-use date with member states. Legal basis Art. 31a RED III / Art. 28 RED II, Art. 18 Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S8, S9, S10]
- **HU operators are in scope** of the UDB obligation. The specific interconnection between the NÉBIH BÜHG/BIONYOM registries and the UDB is **not primary-sourced** — the Hungarian registration/traceability layer is national; UDB entry is the parallel EU chain-of-custody obligation on the same operators. [FLAG — see Gaps]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Register in BIONYOM and BÜHG with NÉBIH** before conducting any sustainable-biofuel activity or issuing a Hungarian sustainability document; renew annually (request 30 days before expiry). → NÉBIh.
2. **Choose the verification route:** the national BÜHG system **or** an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE…) — Hungary accepts both. → NÉBIH / certification body.
3. **Issue the correct proof per consignment:** biomassza igazolás (producer), fenntarthatósági igazolás (trader/processor/distributor), fenntarthatósági bizonyítvány (official), plus szállítójegy for forest biomass. → self.
4. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70–80 % for bio-electricity/heat) and document them per consignment. → self + auditor.
5. **Respect the high-ILUC (palm) taper** (§9: 0.04 % of transport fuel in 2026-27 → 0 % by 2030) and use only EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/double-count claims. → self + auditor.
6. **Submit the annual NÉBIH data return by 20 March** for the preceding year; retain records 5 years. → NÉBIH.
7. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** and record initial stock / transactions for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
8. **Undergo the annual voluntary-scheme audit** where the voluntary route is used. → certification body.

**4-line summary — Hungary**
- Transposition: RED II/III sustainability via **Government Decree 821/2021 (XII.28.)** under Act CXVII/2010; RED III sub-elements reflected domestically but **no NIM notified → in infringement**.
- Certification: dual route — national **BÜHG/BIONYOM** system run by **NÉBIH** (agricultural authority) **plus** EU-recognised voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…); proof = biomassza/fenntarthatósági igazolás + fenntarthatósági bizonyítvány, issuable only by BÜHG-registered operators.
- Registry/UDB: **BÜHG** (GHG inventory) + **BIONYOM** (traceability) at NÉBIH, annual registration; **annual data return by 20 March**, 5-yr retention; UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024 (mandatory-use date pending, HU-UDB linkage a flag).
- Flags: §-level figures PRIMARY-ONLY (re-verify on njt.hu); GHG ladder 50/60/65 %; high-ILUC (palm) taper to 0 % by 2030; no national palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU.

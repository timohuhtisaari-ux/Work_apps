## 2. Fuel taxation

Portugal taxes motor fuels through the **ISP — Imposto sobre os Produtos Petrolíferos e Energéticos**, governed by the **Código dos Impostos Especiais de Consumo (CIEC)**, approved by **Decreto-Lei n.º 73/2010** (the national instrument implementing Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC). The ISP has three moving parts on road fuels: (i) the **taxa unitária** (unit rate, set by Portaria within CIEC min/max bands, and which already embeds the road-service contribution); (ii) the **adicionamento sobre as emissões de CO2** (carbon add-on, CIEC Art. 92-A, re-fixed annually); and (iii) since 2022 a **temporary/extraordinary ISP discount** adjusted roughly weekly by Portaria to offset fuel-price-driven VAT swings.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **Component build-up (illustrative, petrol, early Jul 2026):** ISP unit rate (≈€442–462, moving) − extraordinary discount €35.13 + CO2 add-on €159.11, before 23% VAT. Tier-2 Tax Foundation reports a combined petrol excise of **€0.657/L** and diesel **€0.535/L** at 1 Jan 2026 (S8) — broadly consistent with unit rate + CO2 add-on before the current discount.
- **Road-service contribution (contribuição de serviço rodoviário):** €87/1000 L petrol, €111/1000 L diesel — **included within** the ISP unit rate, not a separate line.
- **Advanced-biofuel / renewable-gas exemption is certificate-gated and state-aid-based.** Relief under CIEC Art. 90(11) requires holding and cancelling **TdB (títulos de biocombustíveis)** for advanced biofuels or **Guarantees of Origin** for renewable gases, plus regular tax status; Portaria 226/2026/1 (20 May 2026) sets the recognition/implementation procedure. Conventional blended biofuels (FAME up to B7, ethanol) that meet the ordinary incorporation obligation are taxed at the fossil-fuel ISP rate — the exemption targets *advanced* volumes. Mandate/TdB mechanics: see the mandates report.
- **Adicionamento CO2 is re-indexed every year** to the prior-year average EU-ETS auction price; for 2026 the AT fixed it at **70.0405 €/t CO2** (Ofício Circulado 25086/2025). Expect annual revision each January. `CONFIRMED`.
- **ISP unit rate is a moving target.** The government re-fixes the unit rate and the extraordinary discount by Portaria on a near-weekly basis under the price-mitigation mechanism in force since March 2022. Corroborator confirmed the value genuinely moves: Portaria 112-A/2026 (13 Mar) €462.39/€331.26; Portaria 141-A/2026 (2 Apr) €441.68/€278.17. The €437/€298 in the original draft is a plausible April-era approximation but was not matched to a dated in-force Portaria — **re-check the current week's Portaria at time of use.** A proposed Lei n.º 67/XVII would further lower the ISP minimum bands (€199.89 petrol / €156.66 diesel) — `PROPOSED`.
- **ETS2** applies to road-transport fuels with allowance surrender from **2028** (2027 emissions; auctioning from 2027) under **Directive (EU) 2023/959 (ETS2, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC)**. [CORRECTION — the draft's "Regulation (EU) 2026/667" is the **2040 EU climate-target** amendment to the Climate Law, NOT ETS2.] `CONFIRMED` (EU-level). (V4, V5)

## Conflicts and gaps

- **CONFLICT / moving-target on the ISP *taxa unitária*.** The €437 (petrol) / €298 (diesel) unit-rate figures in the original draft come from Tier-2 sources; the corroborator located two dated Tier-1 fixing Portarias (112-A/2026 = €462.39/€331.26; 141-A/2026 = €441.68/€278.17) showing the value moves each week. The exact **early-July 2026** in-force unit rate was not pinned (the specific Portaria for that week was not read). Use the current-week Portaria on diariodarepublica.pt.
- The CO2 add-on and the extraordinary discount are both anchored to Tier 1 instruments (Ofício Circulado 25086/2025; Portaria 286-A/2026, discount corroborated by eco.sapo + Forbes) — `CONFIRMED`.
- **CORRECTION (ETS2 legal basis):** Directive (EU) 2023/959, not Reg. (EU) 2026/667.
- DG TAXUD rates-table cross-check: the TAXUD "Excise Duty Tables" page exposes only the **revenues** edition PDF plus the TEDB database; a machine-readable *rates* edition PDF could not be located this pass. Cross-check used national Portarias (Tier 1) + Tax Foundation Jan 2026 (Tier 2).

## 4-line summary

- **Petrol:** ISP unit rate a weekly-moving target (Tier-1 Portarias 2026: €462.39 → €441.68/1000 L, incl. €87 road-service) + CO2 add-on €159.11 + 23% VAT; current extraordinary discount −€35.13 (Portaria 286-A/2026, from 7 Jul 2026).
- **Diesel:** ISP unit rate €331.26 → €278.17/1000 L (incl. €111 road-service, moving) + CO2 add-on €173.34 + 23% VAT; current extraordinary discount −€30.34.
- **Biofuel relief:** total ISP exemption for certified *advanced* biofuels (TdB certificate) and renewable gases (Guarantee of Origin) under CIEC Art. 90(11)/(4); conventional blended biofuels taxed at the fossil rate.
- **Flags:** exact early-Jul ISP unit rate not pinned (CONFLICT/moving-target; re-check current-week Portaria); CO2 add-on 70.0405 €/t for 2026; **ETS2 legal basis corrected to Dir. (EU) 2023/959, surrender from 2028.**

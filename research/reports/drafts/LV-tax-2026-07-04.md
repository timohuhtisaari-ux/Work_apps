**Interaction with the mandate:** the high-blend reduced rates (E85 → 360; B100/HVO100 → 330) are product-specific reductions in the excise law rather than a relief keyed to volumes above the blending obligation. Whether the reliefs sit under a notified state-aid scheme and/or are capped to non-mandated volumes was not confirmed (see Gaps) — the key state-aid question flagged for follow-up.

## National act implementing the Energy Taxation Directive (2003/96/EC)

- **Law "On Excise Duty" (*Par akcīzes nodokli*)** — the national act setting motor-fuel excise and the biofuel reduced rates, implementing Directive 2003/96/EC. Current rates set by the amendments adopted **19 December 2024** (in force 1 Jan 2026). Consolidated text on Likumi.lv.
- **State-aid basis for biofuel reliefs:** not identified in this pass — the notified Commission decision underpinning the E85/B100 reduced rates (and any restriction to non-mandated volumes) is a gap for follow-up.

## Gaps

- State-aid decision number / notified scheme underpinning the E85 (360) and B100/HVO100 (330) reduced rates not located; unclear whether relief is limited to volumes beyond the blending obligation.
- Road-transport biomethane excise treatment not confirmed from a rate table.
- **DG TAXUD cross-check not completed:** the Commission Excise Duty Tables (Energy Products) PDF was re-downloaded by the corroborator but is still a compressed/binary stream unparseable by WebFetch's extractor and by local Read (no poppler here). LV rates are instead corroborated by two independent Tier-1 tables — **VID (re-fetched, S6)** and the Ministry of Finance — which agree exactly (555 / 467 / 360 / 330).

## 4-line summary — Latvia

- **Petrol:** EUR 555.00 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026 (Law "On Excise Duty"); CONFIRMED (VID re-fetched + MoF).
- **Diesel:** EUR 467.00 / 1000 L standard; temporarily cut ~15 % for 1 Apr–30 Jun 2026, restored 1 Jul 2026; CONFIRMED.
- **Biofuel relief:** reduced excise for high blends only — E85-type petrol (70–85 % ethanol) EUR 360 and biodiesel/HVO wholly from biomass EUR 330; ordinary E10/B7 taxed at the full rate.
- **Flags:** state-aid basis for the E85/B100 reliefs not confirmed (gap); temporary Apr–Jun 2026 diesel cut added (now expired); ETS2 obligation from 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959; postponed one year, first surrender 2029); DG TAXUD PDF still unparseable (rates corroborated via VID/MoF Tier-1 instead).

## Executive summary

Luxembourg imposes a **mandatory minimum biofuel coverage rate (taux de couverture)** on
road petrol and diesel, expressed in **energy content**, under the **amended Law of
17 December 2010** on energy-product excise duties, administered through the **Customs and
Excise Administration**. Documented levels are **8.40 % (2024) and 8.80 % (2025)** overall,
with an **advanced-biofuels sub-quota of 0.4 % (2024) and 1.1 % (2025)** and a **3.0 %
banking/carry-over** allowance, on a trajectory toward **10 % by 2030** — but the **2026
coverage rate, 2026 advanced sub-quota and the penalty are a hard GAP** (see the flag above
and Section 5), and the 2024/2025 figures themselves remain **UNCORROBORATED** (Tier 2
petrol.lu only). On tax, Luxembourg deliberately keeps excise below its neighbours: petrol
totals **569.11 → 526.38 EUR/1000 L** and diesel **464.60 → 421.87 EUR/1000 L** across a
1 Jul 2026 temporary 5-ct cut (CONFIRMED), with a **separate taxe CO2 of 45 EUR/t (2026,
CONFIRMED)** and an **HVO100 CO2 exemption** (total 320.00 EUR/1000 L). Sustainability is
transposed via the **RGD of 3 February 2023** (RED II), with a biofuels register at the
Administration de l'environnement, customs verification at declaration-for-consumption, and
an annual sustainability declaration due **1 May**; there is **no national palm/soy ban**.
**RED III is not yet in force for transport** (no LU transport measure on EUR-Lex NIM; the
modifying RGD is still before the Conseil d'État — PROPOSED).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** producers, importers and authorised warehouse-keepers releasing
  energy products (road petrol/diesel) for consumption, who pay excise to the
  **Administration des douanes et accises**; the biofuel coverage obligation is verified on
  release for consumption. [S2, S4]
- **Sustainability & register:** biofuels count toward the coverage rate only if they meet
  the sustainability criteria of the **grand-ducal regulation of 27 February 2011** (the
  emwelt register page still references arts. 3–7 of the 2011 RGD; the **RGD of 3 February
  2023** is the current RED II transposition — confirmed to exist); a **biocarburants
  register** is maintained on the environment portal (emwelt.lu), where economic actors
  producing or importing biofuels — and actors reducing the CO₂ intensity of fossil fuels
  ("UER" credits) — register and file annual declarations. [S2, S5]
- **Basis:** energy-content coverage rate (not a per-litre volume mandate); administered
  fiscally (enforced via the excise/customs mechanism — see Section 2).

### Notes / interaction with the mandate & cross-border positioning
- Luxembourg's excise is deliberately kept **below neighbouring BE/FR/DE** to preserve fuel-sales revenue from cross-border ("tank tourism") demand; the 1 Jul 2026 5-ct reduction is a competitiveness/purchasing-power measure. Even so, LU diesel total (464.60→421.87) is well under BE (~600.2) and the petrol total (569.11→526.38) is below BE (~600.2).
- The **taxe CO2** is a genuine separate carbon component built into the autonomous excise (footnote ³), distinct from the base excise — unlike Belgium, which has no separate carbon levy.
- **Biofuel relief** operates through the **CO2-tax exemption for HVO100** (and zero CO2 component for pure renewables); it is a rate-differentiation, not shown to be conditioned on exceeding a blending obligation. Whether it rests on a notified state-aid decision was not established this pass (gap).
- National ETD transposition: taxation of energy products set by **règlement grand-ducal** fixing "droits d'accise autonomes," within the BE-LU economic union (UEBL) common-excise framework, transposing Directive 2003/96/EC.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Open the correct account** in the Administration de l'environnement biofuels register
   (Biofuel Producer / Biofuel Trader / Registered Oil Company). → Administration de
   l'environnement.
2. **Obtain EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC, 2BSvs, REDcert, SURE…)
   covering chain-of-custody / mass balance. → certification body.
3. **Register each batch** (volume + environmental characteristics), assign it down the chain,
   ensure recipients approve transfers, and carry the sustainability attestation to final
   blending. → biofuels register.
4. **Meet the biofuel coverage-rate obligation** on road fuels for 2026 (energy content, with
   3.0 % carry-over and the advanced Annex IX-A sub-quota). **NOTE: the 2026 coverage rate,
   2026 advanced sub-quota and penalty are NOT yet available** (see Section 5) — the 2026
   obligation cannot be sized from this report; obtain the 2026 budget-law article (dossier
   8600 amending the Law of 17 Dec 2010) before compliance planning. → Administration des
   douanes et accises. *[value GAP — pending]*
5. **Present sustainability proof to Customs** (Administration des douanes et accises) at
   declaration-for-consumption, following the applicable circular (2025/C/17 → 2026/C/55).
   → Customs.
6. **File the annual sustainability & GHG-reduction declaration by 1 May** of the following
   year. → Administration de l'environnement.
7. **Enter transaction/sustainability data in the UDB** for in-scope consignments (operational
   since 15 Jan 2024). → Union Database *[confirm LU mandatory-use date]*.
8. **Account for the separate taxe CO2** (45 EUR/t in 2026, built into the autonomous excise;
   HVO100 exempt from the CO2 component) and prepare for the EU-ETS2 obligation from **1 Jan
   2028**. → Administration des douanes et accises.

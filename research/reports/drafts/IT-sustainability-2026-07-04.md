### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Transport biofuels/biogas: RED II art. 29(10) thresholds as transposed — **≥ 50 %** (installations up to 5 Oct 2015), **≥ 60 %** (6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **≥ 65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021); biomass power/heat 70 %/80 % (2021–25 / 2026+); RFNBO/RCF 70 % per Del. Regs (EU) 2023/1184–1185 referenced by D.Lgs. 5/2026. **PRIMARY-ONLY** — D.Lgs. 199/2021 art. 42 sets minima "in conformità" with the directive annexes; numeric values not independently re-verified in the Italian text (corroborator attempted D.Lgs. annexes via Normattiva; annex tables did not render — label unchanged). (normattiva.it, 2026-07-04.)

### 3.3 Certification regime — national scheme AND voluntary schemes

- Italy is distinctive: it operates a **national certification scheme (Sistema Nazionale di Certificazione, SNC)** under DM 7 agosto 2024 (ex DM 14 novembre 2019), run through **certification bodies accredited by Accredia**, which audit adhering economic operators on "the completeness and truthfulness of all elements in sustainability declarations, in the sustainability certificate and related declarations". **CONFIRMED** (MASE normativa page via search + GU + certifier pages, 2026-07-04).
- **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, etc. — 18 on the DG ENER list as of 2026-07-04) are accepted as the alternative route, as required by RED art. 30. **CONFIRMED** (DG ENER list; MASE circulars referencing voluntary schemes).
- **Proof per consignment:** a **dichiarazione di sostenibilità** (sustainability declaration) plus, within the SNC, the **certificato di sostenibilità** issued under the scheme, transferred with each consignment under mass balance; GSE performs second-level checks (and, for incentivised plants, applies its "Regole operative per la verifica di sostenibilità"). **CONFIRMED** for the declaration/mass-balance mechanics (MASE/GSE documents); operational detail PRIMARY-ONLY.
- Low-ILUC-risk certification is obtainable under DM 7 agosto 2024 (relevant because palm/soy exclusions have a low-ILUC exception). **CONFIRMED** (GU summary + trade corroboration).

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status for Italian operators

- UDB open to economic operators since **15 Jan 2024** (liquid), gaseous fuels from Nov 2024; EU-wide mandatory-use date still pending. **CONFIRMED** (EC UDB wiki / DG ENER; rinnovabili.it corroboration, 2026-07-04).
- **D.Lgs. 5/2026 art. 23** strengthens the UDB's role and **designates GSE as the national entity responsible for ensuring accuracy and completeness of the data entered by economic operators in the UDB, and for the initial loading of their identifiers**. CONFIRMED for the decree's existence and thrust; ⚠ corroborator attempted the verbatim text (GU caricaArticolo endpoint → JS loading page; ReteAmbiente reproduction → HTTP 503) — **remains PRIMARY-ONLY on the precise final wording**. (2026-07-04.)
- Pending full UDB operability, Italian practice keeps national proofs (dichiarazioni di sostenibilità / PoS) as the transitional traceability instrument; sector commentary (SIPOTRA paper, Aug 2025) notes national legislation still adapting. **UNCORROBORATED** nuance.

**Penalties (⚠ updated by corroborator, 2026-07-04):** failing the blending obligation is sanctioned under **DM 20 gennaio 2015** (ex art. 30-sexies DL 91/2014): **750,00 € per missing CIC** in the reference year — now read **verbatim in the Gazzetta Ufficiale** ("750,00 euro per ogni certificato di immissione in consumo … mancante nell'anno di riferimento", Art. 1, via the GU per-article endpoint); the sanction does not extinguish the obligation, which carries forward to the next year; amount modifiable annually by decree (none found). See mandates report for details. Volumes without valid proof of sustainability earn no CICs and do not count toward the obligation; sustainability-certification breaches fall under D.Lgs. 199/2021 art. 42 sanction provisions (amounts there still unverified — residual gap narrowed to art. 42 only).

## 4. Compliance checklist — sustainability items (draft)

1. Register with GSE's **BIOCAR** portal as soggetto obbligato (or as CIC-generating producer via the GSE qualification portal for biomethane/advanced biofuels). [CONFIRMED]
2. Certify under the **Sistema Nazionale di Certificazione** (DM 7 agosto 2024, Accredia-accredited CB) **or** an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…). [CONFIRMED]
3. Ensure every consignment carries a **dichiarazione di sostenibilità** under mass balance meeting ≥ 50/60/65 % GHG savings by plant vintage. [CONFIRMED criteria; thresholds PRIMARY-ONLY in national text]
4. Exclude palm (incl. EFB, PFAD) and soy-based volumes from obligation/CIC claims unless certified low-ILUC-risk (D.Lgs. 199/2021 art. 40, since 1 Jan 2023). [CONFIRMED]
5. File the annual **autodichiarazione** on BIOCAR by the deadline in the current MASE circular (2025: circular of 26 Feb 2025); be ready for GSE verification 1–30 November. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
6. Open and maintain a **UDB** account; enter transactions — GSE is the national UDB gatekeeper under D.Lgs. 5/2026 art. 23. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
7. If certified before Aug 2024 under DM 14 Nov 2019: confirm migration to DM 7 agosto 2024 was completed at the last surveillance audit (12-month transition expired ~27 Aug 2025). [CONFIRMED]
8. Reassess obligations under **D.Lgs. 5/2026** (in force 4 Feb 2026): amended arts. 39–42 D.Lgs. 199/2021, 29 % RES-T 2030 (⚠ verbatim-confirmed), automatic fulfilment for methane/biomethane suppliers from 2026 (⚠ verbatim-confirmed), LPG phase-in 2027/2028. [CONFIRMED; details in mandates report]
9. Budget €750 per missing CIC (DM 20-01-2015, verbatim-confirmed) plus carry-forward of the unmet obligation. [CONFIRMED]

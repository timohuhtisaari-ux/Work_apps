“Headline obligation” is the demand signal — the mandated biofuel/renewable share or carbon-intensity target driving demand, not measured consumption. Designs (GHG-reduction · energy-share · volume-blending · tax-incentive · CO₂-compensation · low-carbon/CI standard) are not directly comparable by headline number. Non-EU markets (NO, GB, CH, US, CA) are described on their own terms — RED III and EU ETS2 do not apply to them. In the EU-27, EU ETS2 for road fuels starts 1 Jan 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959); the Union Database is operational (15 Jan 2024) but not yet mandatory.

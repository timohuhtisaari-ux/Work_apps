### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Only biofuels meeting the RED sustainability + GHG-saving criteria (art. 5 AR
  26.11.2011) count toward the mandatory minimum incorporation obligation.
  [CONFIRMED — health.belgium.be cached]
- Threshold ladder follows RED II Art. 29(10): **50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by
  installation vintage; **70 %** for RFNBOs. Exact figures in the Belgian text
  **not directly captured** (page 502) → `PRIMARY-ONLY`/flag. [FLAG]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Belgium relies on the **EU-recognised voluntary certification schemes** to
  demonstrate/guarantee that a biofuel meets the European environmental criteria;
  operators use these schemes and the **compliance declaration** is filed with DG
  Environment. **No separate Belgian national scheme** identified. [CONFIRMED —
  health.belgium.be cached; DG ENER list]
- Per DG ENER (accessed 2026-07-04) the usable schemes include ISCC EU,
  REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, etc. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Obtain EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE…) covering the full chain-of-custody / mass balance. → certification body.
2. **File the compliance declaration (déclaration de conformité)** with FPS Public Health – DG Environment for each sustainable biofuel volume placed on the market, and register it in the Biofuel database. → FPS Public Health (DG Environment).
3. **Exclude prohibited feedstocks:** no palm-oil biofuel (from 1 Jan 2023) and no soy-oil biofuel (from 1 July 2023); respect the crop-based downshift to ~50% by 2030. → self + auditor.
4. **Ensure GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %, 70% RFNBO) are met and documented. → self + auditor.
5. **Enter transaction/sustainability data in the UDB** for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → UDB [confirm BE mandatory-use date].
6. **Reconcile the annual incorporation obligation** with FPS Economy (DG Energy) and hold non-standard-fuel permits where required. → FPS Economy.

**4-line summary — Belgium**
- Transposition: FEDERAL — RED II sustainability via AR 26 Nov 2011 (art.5) + RD transposing 2018/2001 (CCE opinion 2021); incorporation obligation Loi 17 July 2013; Loi 31 July 2023 defines 5 renewable-fuel categories. RED III transport transposition unconfirmed (flag).
- Certification: EU-recognised voluntary schemes only (no national scheme); a déclaration de conformité is filed per volume with FPS Public Health – DG Environment.
- Registry/UDB: national "Biofuel database" at DG Environment; UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024 (BE mandatory-use link to confirm).
- Flags: national palm ban from 1 Jan 2023 and soy ban from 1 July 2023 (stricter than EU); crop cap ~50% by 2030; health.belgium.be 502 — several facts PRIMARY-ONLY pending direct re-fetch.

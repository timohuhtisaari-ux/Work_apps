### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Slovakia operates a **národný systém** (national procedural system for
  biofuels/bioliquids) but for chain-of-custody certification **accepts the
  EU-recognised voluntary schemes** — sources list **ISCC (EU), REDcert,
  Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, Better Biomass** among accepted schemes; producers
  (Meroco, Enagro) certify under **ISCC EU**. [PRIMARY-ONLY — ZVVB; producer pages]
- No stand-alone EU-recognised **national voluntary scheme** for SK identified on
  the DG ENER list (unlike PL's KZR INiG); the "national system" is the SK BIO
  administrative/reporting framework, not a Commission-approved scheme. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
- Proof-of-sustainability per consignment = **potvrdenie o pôvode biopaliva**
  (certificate/proof of origin of the biofuel) together with the voluntary-scheme
  sustainability documentation; the proof of origin is tied to the customs
  report (hlásenie) so sustainability criteria can be checked. [CONFIRMED — minzp/SHMÚ]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- The EU **UDB** became operational **15 Jan 2024** (RED Art. 31a + Implementing
  Reg. (EU) 2022/996). [CONFIRMED — DG ENER; EU-wide]
- **Key SK-specific finding — dual reporting:** SHMÚ states that until legislative
  amendments are made, **operators must submit data to BOTH SK BIO and the Union
  Database**, because the two systems **are not connected and data transfer
  between them cannot be ensured**. [CONFIRMED — SHMÚ SK BIO page]
- Operators placing biofuels on the SK market are therefore in UDB scope **and**
  in SK BIO scope simultaneously. [CONFIRMED — SHMÚ]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Register and record in SK BIO** (isbio.shmu.sk) with SHMÚ before placing biofuels/biocomponents on the Slovak market. → SHMÚ.
2. **Certify under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme** (ISCC EU, REDcert, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, Better Biomass); Slovakia has no Commission-approved national scheme. → chosen scheme / certifier.
3. **Carry the potvrdenie o pôvode biopaliva** (proof of origin) + scheme sustainability documentation per consignment on a mass-balance basis. → scheme / SK BIO.
4. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving threshold** (50 / 60 / 65 %; RFNBO/RCF per Decree 31/2026) and exclude high-ILUC palm volumes per Del. Reg. 2019/807. → self + auditor.
5. **Report to BOTH systems** — file the customs report (hlásenie) with proof of origin AND record transactions in the EU UDB, because SK BIO and UDB are not interconnected. → colný úrad / SHMÚ / UDB.
6. **Undergo the periodic certification audit** and retain documentation for verification by competent persons / SHMÚ. → certifier / SHMÚ.

**4-line summary — Slovakia**
- Transposition: RED II via **Act 309/2009 Coll.** + **MoE Decree 271/2011** (sustainability criteria & GHG targets); RED III transposed via **Act 309/2009 amendment + new Decree 31/2026** — SK received a RED III formal notice (24 Jul 2025) and is not named in the June-2026 EU package (chronology corrected).
- Certification: **no Commission-approved national scheme**; accepts EU voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, Better Biomass); proof = **potvrdenie o pôvode biopaliva** + scheme docs.
- Registry/UDB: **SK BIO** operated by **SHMÚ** (live since 1 Jan 2024), reports/hlásenia to the **customs office**; operators must submit to **BOTH SK BIO and the UDB** (systems not interconnected).
- Flags: SK BIO/customs reporting deadlines & penalties unpinned; Decree 31/2026 detail/effective date and GHG-threshold citations to confirm on slov-lex.sk; no SK-specific palm/soy extras found.

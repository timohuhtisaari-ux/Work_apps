## 2. Fuel taxation

Portugal taxes motor fuels through the **ISP — Imposto sobre os Produtos Petrolíferos e Energéticos**, governed by the **Código dos Impostos Especiais de Consumo (CIEC)**, approved by **Decreto-Lei n.º 73/2010** (the national instrument implementing Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC). The ISP has three moving parts on road fuels: (i) the **taxa unitária** (unit rate, set by Portaria within CIEC min/max bands, and which already embeds the road-service contribution); (ii) the **adicionamento sobre as emissões de CO2** (carbon add-on, CIEC Art. 92-A, re-fixed annually); and (iii) since 2022 a **temporary/extraordinary ISP discount** adjusted roughly weekly by Portaria to offset fuel-price-driven VAT swings.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **Component build-up (illustrative, petrol, early Jul 2026):** ISP unit rate ≈ €437 − extraordinary discount €35.13 + CO2 add-on €159.11 ≈ €561/1000 L of ISP-family duty, before 23% VAT. Tier-2 Tax Foundation reports a combined petrol excise of **€0.657/L** and diesel **€0.535/L** at 1 Jan 2026 (S8) — broadly consistent with unit rate + CO2 add-on before the current discount.
- **Road-service contribution (contribuição de serviço rodoviário):** €87/1000 L petrol, €111/1000 L diesel — **included within** the ISP unit rate, not a separate line.
- **Advanced-biofuel / renewable-gas exemption is certificate-gated and state-aid-based.** Relief under CIEC Art. 90(11) requires holding and cancelling **TdB (títulos de biocombustíveis)** for advanced biofuels or **Guarantees of Origin** for renewable gases, plus regular tax status; Portaria 226/2026/1 (20 May 2026) sets the recognition/implementation procedure. Conventional blended biofuels (FAME up to B7, ethanol) that meet the ordinary incorporation obligation are taxed at the fossil-fuel ISP rate — the exemption targets *advanced* volumes. Mandate/TdB mechanics: see the mandates report.
- **Adicionamento CO2 is re-indexed every year** to the prior-year average EU-ETS auction price; for 2026 the AT fixed it at **70.0405 €/t CO2** (Ofício Circulado 25086/2025). Expect annual revision each January. `CONFIRMED`.
- **ISP unit rate is a moving target.** The government re-fixes the unit rate and the extraordinary discount by Portaria on a near-weekly basis under the price-mitigation mechanism in force since March 2022; the €437 / €298 figures are the current published reference and should be re-checked against the in-force Portaria at time of use.
- **ETS2** applies to road-transport fuels from **1 January 2028** under Regulation (EU) 2026/667 (not 2027). `CONFIRMED` (EU-level).

## Conflicts and gaps

- **No Tier-1 read of the exact ISP *taxa unitária*.** The €437 (petrol) / €298 (diesel) unit-rate figures come from Tier-2 sources (S3, S1) that cite the official Portarias; earlier secondary reporting also quoted €466–478 (petrol) / €311–337 (diesel), which appear to fold in different weeks' discount/unit-rate settings. The corroborator should read the in-force fixing Portaria on diariodarepublica.pt to pin the exact unit rate and effective week. Marked `PRIMARY-ONLY / flag`.
- The CO2 add-on and the extraordinary discount are both anchored to Tier 1 instruments (Ofício Circulado 25086/2025; Portaria 286-A/2026) — `CONFIRMED`.

## 4-line summary

- **Petrol:** ISP unit rate ≈ €437/1000 L (incl. €87 road-service) + CO2 add-on €159.11 + 23% VAT; current extraordinary discount −€35.13 (Portaria 286-A/2026, from 7 Jul 2026).
- **Diesel:** ISP unit rate ≈ €298/1000 L (incl. €111 road-service) + CO2 add-on €173.34 + 23% VAT; current extraordinary discount −€30.34.
- **Biofuel relief:** total ISP exemption for certified *advanced* biofuels (TdB certificate) and renewable gases (Guarantee of Origin) under CIEC Art. 90(11)/(4); conventional blended biofuels taxed at the fossil rate.
- **Flags:** exact ISP unit rate is Tier-2-only and adjusted ~weekly (re-check in-force Portaria); CO2 add-on re-indexed annually (70.0405 €/t for 2026); ETS2 from 1 Jan 2028.

## 2. Fuel taxation

The **Impuesto sobre Hidrocarburos** is set by **Ley 38/1992 de Impuestos Especiales, art. 50** (Tarifa 1ª), Spain's implementation of Directive 2003/96/EC. Since 1 Jan 2019 each epigraph carries a state **tipo general** plus a state **tipo especial** (the latter replaced the former regional/autonomous-community tranche — flat 72 €/1000 L for road fuels). **Crucial current context:** in response to the Middle-East (Iran) crisis fuel-price spike, Spain cut fuel taxes temporarily: **RD-ley 7/2026** (20 March, rates + 10 % VAT on fuels, 22 Mar–30 Jun 2026, adjusted by RD-ley 10/2026 of 29 April), then **RD-ley 18/2026** (29 June) with a phase-out: cuts of ≈15 c€/L in July, ≈10 c€/L in August, ≈5 c€/L in September 2026, full reversion planned 1 Oct 2026. The 2024–25 plan to **equalise diesel with petrol** (an RRF commitment) has still **not** been adopted.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps (taxation scope)

- **FLAG for corroborator:** the July–September 2026 totals (petrol 322,69; diesel 229,00 €/1000 L) are **below the ETD minima** (359 / 330 €/1000 L). RD-ley 18/2026's text (as fetched) contains no explicit derogation reference. Check for a Council/Commission authorisation or infringement exposure.
- **Gap:** Ley 38/1992 art. 50 consolidated text could not be fetched directly (BOE act.php truncates before art. 50; the BOE open-data API returned HTTP 400). Base rates are instead established by (a) a Tier 2 rate table (mundopetroleo, "desde 1 de enero de 2019"), (b) exact arithmetic consistency with the verbatim BOE texts of RD-ley 7/2026 and 18/2026 (cuts of 15/10/5 c€/L off 472,69/503,92/379,00), and (c) KPMG alert. Corroborator should re-verify art. 50 directly.
- **Gap:** DG TAXUD excise-tables PDF downloaded but unparseable in this environment (no PDF renderer); TEDB cross-check not completed.
- AEAT "Base imponible y tipos de gravamen" page (updated 03/07/2026) describes the framework but publishes no numeric table.
- Gipuzkoa (foral) page showed divergent figures (e.g. 371,69 for 1.2.2) — appears to be an outdated/foral variant; not used; flagged for registry note.

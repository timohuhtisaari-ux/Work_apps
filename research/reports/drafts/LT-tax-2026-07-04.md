**Interaction with the mandate:** biofuel uptake is driven by the RES-T obligation under the Law on Alternative Fuels (other agent's scope). On the tax side, the notable relief is the **biogas/biomethane excise exemption** (fixed + CO2 parts), which is a state-aid measure requiring EC clearance; liquid biofuels do not appear to carry a separate reduced excise following the 2024 reform.

## National act implementing the Energy Taxation Directive (2003/96/EC)

- **Law on Excise Duties (*Akcizų įstatymas*)**, Art. 21 — sets motor-fuel excise (now split into a fixed part and a CO2 variable part) and implements Directive 2003/96/EC. Consolidated text on e-seimas / TAR (e-seimas.lrs.lt). The Dec 2024 amending act introduced the CO2 component trajectory and the biogas exemption.
- **State-aid:** the biogas/biomethane excise exemption is treated by the Lithuanian Competition Council as state aid requiring **European Commission approval** before it can apply — the notified decision reference is a gap for the corroborator.

## 4-line summary — Lithuania

- **Petrol:** EUR 562 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026 (fixed 466 + CO2 96), Law on Excise Duties Art. 21; CONFIRMED.
- **Diesel:** EUR 553.6 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026 (fixed 500 + CO2 53.6); CONFIRMED.
- **Biofuel relief:** biogas/biomethane (incl. blend share) exempt from both fixed and CO2 excise parts from 1 Jul 2025 (state aid, needs EC clearance); liquid biofuels appear to get no separate excise cut after the 2024 fossil-relief repeal.
- **Flags:** LT excise now carries an embedded CO2/carbon component (petrol 96, diesel 53.6, rising to 2030); temporary Apr–Jun 2026 diesel dip has expired; biogas state-aid clearance status unconfirmed; ETS2 obligation from 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959); DG TAXUD PDF cross-check unparseable and several LT official pages 403'd (gaps).

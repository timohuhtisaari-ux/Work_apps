# Non-EU State Source Registry (EEA / third countries)

Same structure as `member-states.md`: institutions and scheme names only, **no
numeric values** — agents fetch current mandate levels and tax rates live and
date-stamp them (see `research/METHODOLOGY.md`).

**Important framing:** these countries are **not EU member states**, so RED III
and EU ETS2 do not apply the way they do in the EU-27. Do not assume EU
transposition deadlines or ETS2 dates. Establish each country's *own* legal
framework and note only where it deliberately mirrors or references EU law.

Legend: **M** = mandates authority · **T** = tax/excise authority ·
**S** = sustainability/certification authority · **L** = official legal database

---

## Norway (NO)
- Status: **EEA member** — adopts much of EU energy/product law via the EEA
  Agreement, but on its own timetable; RED II is EEA-relevant, RED III's EEA
  incorporation status must be checked (do not assume it is in force). Not in
  the EU ETS2; Norway is in the EU ETS for stationary/aviation via EEA.
- Scheme: **omsetningskrav** — biofuel sales/turnover mandate for road transport
  (and separate sub-requirements for non-road/aviation) under
  **Produktforskriften** (Product Regulations) chapter 3; advanced-biofuel
  sub-quota and double counting apply.
- **M/S:** Miljødirektoratet (Norwegian Environment Agency) — miljodirektoratet.no;
  Klima- og miljødepartementet — regjeringen.no
- **T:** Skatteetaten (veibruksavgift on fuel + CO2-avgift) — skatteetaten.no;
  Tolletaten — toll.no
- **S:** Miljødirektoratet verifies sustainability; EU-recognised voluntary
  schemes used; national reporting on delbevis/ISCC-type proof
- **L:** Lovdata — lovdata.no

## United Kingdom (GB)
- Status: **left the EU (Brexit)** — fully independent regime; EU RED/ETS do not
  apply. Has its own **UK Emissions Trading Scheme (UK ETS)** and is consulting
  on extending it; separate SAF mandate from 2025.
- Scheme: **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)** — certificate-based
  (RTFCs), on suppliers of ≥450,000 L/yr; development-fuel sub-target, crop cap,
  buy-out price. Separate **SAF Mandate** (aviation) from 1 Jan 2025.
- **M:** Department for Transport (DfT), Low Carbon Fuels unit / RTFO
  Administrator — gov.uk
- **T:** HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) — fuel duty / hydrocarbon oils duty — gov.uk
- **S:** RTFO sustainability criteria (DfT), independent verification; UK-run,
  post-Brexit divergence from EU rules possible
- **L:** legislation.gov.uk

## Switzerland (CH)
- Status: **not EU/EEA** — bilateral relationship only; no RED/ETS2 obligations.
  Swiss ETS is linked to the EU ETS (stationary + aviation) but road fuels are
  handled under the CO2 Act, not ETS2.
- Scheme: **no blending mandate**. Two instruments run in parallel: (1) a
  **mineral-oil-tax relief** for renewable fuels meeting ecological/social
  criteria — this did **not** expire in 2023; the revised CO2 Act extended it to
  **31 Dec 2030** (BAZG); and (2) a **CO2 compensation obligation** on fuel
  importers under the **CO2 Act / CO2 Ordinance** (in force 1 Jan 2026), who must
  offset a share of transport CO2 (≈30 % in 2026), partly via renewable fuels.
  Verify the current 2026 figures.
- **M/S:** Federal Office for the Environment (FOEN / BAFU) — bafu.admin.ch;
  Swiss Federal Office of Energy (SFOE / BFE) — bfe.admin.ch
- **T:** Federal Office for Customs and Border Security (BAZG / OFDF) —
  bazg.admin.ch (mineral oil tax / Mineralölsteuer)
- **L:** Fedlex — fedlex.admin.ch

## Cross-cutting checks (per country)
1. The country's own primary legal database (Lovdata / legislation.gov.uk /
   Fedlex) for the operative regulation and its current-year values.
2. Whether/how the country references EU law (EEA Joint Committee decisions for
   Norway; UK's post-Brexit divergence; Switzerland's autonomous approach).
3. USDA FAS GAIN reports and IEA Bioenergy country notes as Tier-2 corroboration.
4. Do **not** state an EU RED III transposition status or an ETS2 date for these
   countries as if they were EU obligations — describe their own instruments.

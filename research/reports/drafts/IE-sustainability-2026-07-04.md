### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Renewable fuels must meet the RED sustainability + GHG-saving criteria to earn RTFCs.
  [CONFIRMED — nora.ie]
- Threshold ladder follows RED II Art. 29(10): **50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by installation
  vintage; **70 %** for RFNBOs. Exact Irish figures not quoted from the SI → flag. [FLAG]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Obligated parties demonstrate sustainability using **voluntary schemes recognised by the
  Administrator (NORA)**; the scheme's scope and version must appear on NORA's list of
  recognised schemes. In practice these are the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes**
  (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU, UFAS covers UK & Ireland, etc.). [PRIMARY-ONLY — nora.ie; DG ENER]
- **No national certification scheme** — RTFO relies on the recognised voluntary schemes plus
  NORA's own certificate (RTFC) accounting. [PRIMARY-ONLY — nora.ie]
- **Proof of sustainability** (PoS) accompanies each consignment; data submitted to NORA and
  assured by an **independent third-party verifier**. [PRIMARY-ONLY — nora.ie; UK RTFO analogue]

### 3.6 Feedstock restrictions

| Rule | Detail | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crop cap | Red (food/feed-crop) certificates limited to **3 % by energy from 1 Jan 2026** (was 2 %) | CONFIRMED | nora.ie; mhc.ie |
| High-ILUC (palm) | High-ILUC-risk biofuel/biogas capped at a share of the operator's **2019 disposals**, reduced from **75 % → 60 %** under SI 664/2025; EU phase-to-0 by 2030 applies | CONFIRMED | nora.ie; irishstatutebook.ie |

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Open an RTFO Account with NORA** as an obligated party (supplier of road-transport mineral oil placed on the Irish market). → NORA.
2. **Use voluntary schemes recognised by NORA** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, UFAS…) whose scope/version is on NORA's list; issue **PoS per consignment**. → certification body / self.
3. **Claim the correct RTFC colour** — Green (Annex IX-A advanced), Orange (other incl. Annex IX-B UCO/animal fat), Red (crop, within the 3 % crop cap from 2026). → NORA online system.
4. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) and document per consignment. → self + verifier.
5. **Respect the high-ILUC cap** — high-ILUC-risk fuel limited to 60 % of 2019 disposals (SI 664/2025), tapering to 0 by 2030. → self + verifier.
6. **Appoint an independent verifier** to assure data submitted to NORA and file the annual assurance report by NORA's determination dates. → verifier / NORA.
7. **Onboard to the Union Database (UDB)** and record PoS/transaction data for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
8. **Meet advanced/RFNBO sub-target (5 % from 2026)** and hold sufficient Green/RFNBO certificates, or face the €0.08/MJ advanced buy-out. → NORA.

**4-line summary — Ireland**
- Transposition: RTFO under Energy Act 2010; RED II via SI 350/2022 (BOS→RTFO), sustainability SI 33/2012, GHG SI 160/2017; RED III transport transposed via SI 664/2025 (32% energy, advanced 5%, crop cap 3%, high-ILUC 75→60% of 2019, RFNBOs added) — permitting via SI 274/2025.
- Certification: no national scheme — NORA-recognised EU voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, UFAS…); PoS per consignment, independent third-party verifier assures data.
- Registry/UDB: NORA administers the RTFO online register and issues RTFCs (Green/Orange/Red, 1 cert = 1 MJ); UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024, EU mandatory-use date pending.
- Flags: SI 664/2025 full text 403 (deadlines/verifier standard PRIMARY-ONLY); some PoS/assurance mechanics corroborated via UK RTFO analogue — confirm Irish list/guidance; buy-out €0.05/€0.08 per MJ (NORA, single-source).

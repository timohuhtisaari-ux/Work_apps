# Portugal — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Portugal (PT)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Portugal imposes an **energy-content incorporation obligation** on road-fuel suppliers ("incorporadores") for low-carbon fuels (biofuels, biogas, RFNBO, recycled-carbon fuels), set at **13% for 2026** (DL 84/2022, art. 8; CONFIRMED), with a legislated ladder to 14% (2027) and 16% (2029–2030) and an advanced "Parte A" sub-quota of 2.0% in 2026. Compliance is demonstrated through TdB/TdC certificates verified by ENSE, and a shortfall carries a **€1,760 compensation per missing TdB/TdC** (Despacho 21/DG/2024). Fuels are taxed through the ISP under the CIEC (DL 73/2010), where the **unit rate is a near-weekly moving target** set by Portaria plus an annually-fixed CO2 add-on (€159.11 petrol / €173.34 diesel for 2026) and a temporary extraordinary discount; **certified advanced biofuels and renewable gases receive total ISP exemption**, while conventional blends are taxed at the fossil rate. RED III is **not yet transposed** — Portugal was referred to the CJEU on 29 April 2026 (with Greece and Malta) with financial sanctions requested. The top supplier obligations are: register on ENSE's Balcão Único da Energia, hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification with a PoS per consignment, deliver TdB/TdC quarterly, and read the in-force weekly Portaria before pricing ISP.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Portugal operates an **incorporation obligation** on fuel suppliers ("incorporadores"):
a minimum percentage of **low-carbon fuels for transport ("combustíveis de baixo
teor em carbono" — biofuels, biogas, RFNBO, recycled-carbon fuels), measured in
energy content**, of the road fuels they release for consumption. Compliance is
demonstrated with **TdB (títulos de biocombustíveis)** and **TdC (títulos de
combustível de baixo carbono)** certificates verified quarterly by **ENSE, E.P.E.**
The operative act is **Decreto-Lei n.º 84/2022, de 9 de dezembro** (partial RED II
transposition), Article 8 (metas de incorporação) and Articles 40–41 (TdB/TdC).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Certificates:** TdB (1 TdB = 1 tep of sustainable biofuel) and TdC (low-carbon
  fuel title). Issued/validated by **ENSE**; DGEG runs the residual **TdB auction**
  (for TdB attributable to small dedicated producers / PPD benefiting from ISP
  exemption; auctions noticed 15 business days ahead in the Diário da República;
  base bid = 85 % of the average minimum of the last three auctions). (S8)
- **Obligated parties:** entities that release road fuels for final consumption
  in transport ("incorporadores"). (S1, S2)
- **Verification:** quarterly — obligated parties deliver TdB/TdC to ENSE by the
  end of the month following each quarter. (S2, S7)
- **Enforcement/tax interface:** advanced biofuels (TdB-certified) and renewable
  gases (Guarantee-of-Origin-certified) receive **full ISP (fuel-tax) exemption**;
  the procedure is set by **Portaria n.º 226/2026/1, de 20 de maio** coordinating
  AT (tax authority), ENSE and the GO-issuing entity. This is a tax mechanism —
  cross-reference the fuel-tax section; not researched in depth here. (S9)
- Banking/borrowing of TdB: not isolated from primary text — **gap**.

## 2. Fuel taxation

Portugal taxes motor fuels through the **ISP — Imposto sobre os Produtos Petrolíferos e Energéticos**, governed by the **Código dos Impostos Especiais de Consumo (CIEC)**, approved by **Decreto-Lei n.º 73/2010** (the national instrument implementing Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC). The ISP has three moving parts on road fuels: (i) the **taxa unitária** (unit rate, set by Portaria within CIEC min/max bands, and which already embeds the road-service contribution); (ii) the **adicionamento sobre as emissões de CO2** (carbon add-on, CIEC Art. 92-A, re-fixed annually); and (iii) since 2022 a **temporary/extraordinary ISP discount** adjusted roughly weekly by Portaria to offset fuel-price-driven VAT swings.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **Component build-up (illustrative, petrol, early Jul 2026):** ISP unit rate (≈€442–462, moving) − extraordinary discount €35.13 + CO2 add-on €159.11, before 23% VAT. Tier-2 Tax Foundation reports a combined petrol excise of **€0.657/L** and diesel **€0.535/L** at 1 Jan 2026 (S8) — broadly consistent with unit rate + CO2 add-on before the current discount.
- **Road-service contribution (contribuição de serviço rodoviário):** €87/1000 L petrol, €111/1000 L diesel — **included within** the ISP unit rate, not a separate line.
- **Advanced-biofuel / renewable-gas exemption is certificate-gated and state-aid-based.** Relief under CIEC Art. 90(11) requires holding and cancelling **TdB (títulos de biocombustíveis)** for advanced biofuels or **Guarantees of Origin** for renewable gases, plus regular tax status; Portaria 226/2026/1 (20 May 2026) sets the recognition/implementation procedure. Conventional blended biofuels (FAME up to B7, ethanol) that meet the ordinary incorporation obligation are taxed at the fossil-fuel ISP rate — the exemption targets *advanced* volumes. Mandate/TdB mechanics: see Section 1.
- **Adicionamento CO2 is re-indexed every year** to the prior-year average EU-ETS auction price; for 2026 the AT fixed it at **70.0405 €/t CO2** (Ofício Circulado 25086/2025). Expect annual revision each January. `CONFIRMED`.
- **ISP unit rate is a moving target.** The government re-fixes the unit rate and the extraordinary discount by Portaria on a near-weekly basis under the price-mitigation mechanism in force since March 2022. Corroborator confirmed the value genuinely moves: Portaria 112-A/2026 (13 Mar) €462.39/€331.26; Portaria 141-A/2026 (2 Apr) €441.68/€278.17. The €437/€298 in the original draft is a plausible April-era approximation but was not matched to a dated in-force Portaria — **re-check the current week's Portaria at time of use.** A proposed Lei n.º 67/XVII would further lower the ISP minimum bands (€199.89 petrol / €156.66 diesel) — `PROPOSED`.
- **ETS2** applies to road-transport fuels with allowance surrender from **2028** (2027 emissions; auctioning from 2027) under **Directive (EU) 2023/959 (ETS2, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC)**. [CORRECTION — the draft's "Regulation (EU) 2026/667" is the **2040 EU climate-target** amendment to the Climate Law, NOT ETS2.] `CONFIRMED` (EU-level). (V4, V5)

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register as an incorporador / operator on ENSE's Balcão Único da Energia (BUE)** before placing biofuel/biogas on the PT market or claiming TdB/TdC (art. 19(3) DL 84/2022). → **ENSE**.
2. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE…) covering the full mass-balance chain of custody, and issue a **Proof of Sustainability per consignment**. → certification body / self.
3. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) and document them per consignment. → self + auditor.
4. **Incorporate the 2026 obligation: 13 % low-carbon energy content**, of which the **advanced "Parte A" sub-quota is 2.0 %** — plan feedstock/certificate supply accordingly. → self.
5. **Deliver TdB/TdC to ENSE quarterly** (by the end of the month following each quarter); TdB/TdC are issued monthly by ENSE on ECS-verified data. → **ENSE / ECS-LNEG**.
6. **Respect feedstock rules:** high-ILUC (palm) within the 2019-level cap tapering to 0 by 2030; use double-counting only for qualifying Annex IX wastes/residues. → self + auditor.
7. **For advanced-biofuel / renewable-gas ISP exemption**, hold and cancel the TdB (advanced) or Guarantee of Origin (renewable gas) and follow the recognition procedure in **Portaria 226/2026/1**. → **AT** (tax authority) / ENSE.
8. **Onboard to the Union Database (UDB)** and record PoS/transaction data for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → **UDB**.
9. **Undergo the annual voluntary-scheme audit**; expect inclusion in the ECS/LNEG annual national sustainability report. → certification body / ECS-LNEG.
10. **Before pricing ISP, read the current-week in-force Portaria** on diariodarepublica.pt — the ISP unit rate and the extraordinary discount move nearly weekly. → self / **AT**.
11. **Avoid a TdB/TdC shortfall** — compensation is **€1,760 per missing title** (Despacho 21/DG/2024). → **ENSE / DGEG**.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Carried-forward headline flags:**
- **13 % (2026) incorporation obligation CONFIRMED** (DL 84/2022 art. 8; DGEG read directly). Legislated ladder 14 % (2027) → 16 % (2029–2030), advanced Parte-A 2.0 % (2026).
- **ISP unit rate is a weekly-moving target — HIGH gap.** The supplier **must read the in-force Portaria** for the current week; the €437/€298 approximations were not tied to a dated instrument, and Tier-1 fixings ranged €462.39→€441.68 (petrol) / €331.26→€278.17 (diesel) over Mar–Apr 2026. Exact early-July 2026 unit rate not pinned. Marked CONFLICT / moving-target.
- **RED III NOT transposed** — Portugal referred to the CJEU **29 Apr 2026** (with Greece and Malta), Commission requesting financial sanctions. Partial-transposition decree still `PROPOSED` (consultation 25 Sep–25 Oct 2025; standstill for the renewable-targets decree ends 22 Jul 2026).

**Resolved conflicts:**
- **Penalty:** statutory non-compliance compensation is **€1,760 per missing TdB/TdC** (Despacho 21/DG/2024; art. 52 + Anexo III DL 84/2022; in force 1 Mar 2023; biennial review). The €1,198 (2 × €599) figure was the TdB **auction** base price, not the compensation. Correction applied.

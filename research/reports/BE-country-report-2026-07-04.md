# Belgium — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Belgium (BE)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04)

## Executive summary

Belgium runs a **federal, energy-value renewable-energy-in-transport obligation** on fuel
suppliers under the **Law of 31 July 2023**, which replaced the old volume-based framework
(Law of 17 July 2013 + RD of 4 May 2018) and aligns Belgium with RED III concepts (advanced
biofuel and RFNBO categories, recycled-carbon-fuel cap, trajectory to 2030). The overall
obligation is **12.2 % (energy value) for 2025 and 2026** (CONFIRMED on primary + ePURE),
rising to 13.15 % (2027) and 13.9 % (2030), with an advanced (Annex IX-A) sub-quota of
**1.1 % for 2026** and food/feed-crop caps of diesel 5 % / petrol 6.5 %; enforcement is by
administrative fine (**1 400 € per 34 GJ of shortfall**, Art. 10) through DG Energy, FPS
Economy. On tax, Belgium is a **mandate-only** market with **no biofuel tax relief**: petrol
and diesel carry a total fuel excise of **600.20 EUR/1000 L** (PRIMARY-ONLY), there is no
separate CO2 levy on road fuels, and an excise increase is scheduled for **1 Jan 2027**.
Sustainability is federal (FPS Public Health – DG Environment + FPS Economy), demonstrated
via EU-recognised voluntary schemes plus a **déclaration de conformité** filed in the
national **Biofuel database**, with national **palm (1 Jan 2023) and soy (1 Jul 2023) bans**
stricter than the EU minimum. **RED III transport transposition is CONFIRMED** (Belgium
notified ~80 implementing measures including two transport RDs). This is the most solid of
the three files; residual risk is concentrated in PRIMARY-ONLY items (per-pool minima, Art.
10 penalty) and pages that were server-blocked (health.belgium.be 502).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** oil companies / gaseous-fuel suppliers placing motor fuel on the
  road-transport market (Art. 2). **Exemptions:** Ministry of Defence; small suppliers
  below **3 600 GJ/year** (Art. 19). [S1]
- **Basis:** obligation expressed in **energy value** (not volume), a change from the
  pre-2024 nominal-volume regime under the Law of 17 July 2013 / RD 4 May 2018. [S1, S4]
- **Categories & multipliers:** the law classifies renewable energy into categories
  (advanced biofuels = cat. C ×2; RFNBO = cat. D; RCF = cat. E, capped; renewable
  electricity = cat. F with transport multipliers) — Art. 4. [S1, S2]
- **Administration / registry:** Directorate-General Energy of **FPS Economy** under the
  federal energy minister; compliance registration and annual declaration; administrative
  fines regime detailed in **RD of 17 March 2024**. [S3, S5]
- **Competence:** federal matter (Art. 74 Constitution) — product standards. [S1]

### Notes / interaction with the mandate
- Belgium is a **mandate-only** market for biofuels — there is no tax-incentive layer (contrast FR TIRUERT or SE/FI above-mandate relief). Fuel excise is effectively equalised between petrol and diesel at ~600.2 EUR/1000 L after years of cliquet-driven diesel increases.
- The "cliquet" (ratchet) mechanism historically raised the special excise (bijzondere accijns / accise spéciale) as pump prices fell, up to a cap; it is not currently generating changes.
- National ETD transposition: **Programmawet van 27 december 2004** (Titel XI, accijnzen op energieproducten en elektriciteit), transposing Directive 2003/96/EC — legal citation to be verified verbatim.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Obtain EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs,
   SURE…) covering the full chain-of-custody / mass balance. → certification body.
2. **File the compliance declaration (déclaration de conformité)** with FPS Public Health –
   DG Environment for each sustainable biofuel volume placed on the market, and register it in
   the **Biofuel database**. → FPS Public Health (DG Environment).
3. **Meet the incorporation obligation** for compliance year 2026: **12.2 %** energy-value
   overall, advanced (Annex IX-A) sub-quota **1.1 %**, per-pool minimum biofuel content
   5.7 % (petrol and diesel), respecting the food-crop caps (**diesel max 5 % / petrol max
   6.5 %** in 2026), the Annex IX-B cap (2 %) and the RCF cap (2 %). → FPS Economy (DG Energy).
4. **Exclude prohibited feedstocks:** no palm-oil biofuel (from **1 Jan 2023**) and no soy-oil
   biofuel (from **1 July 2023**), except certified low-ILUC. → self + auditor.
5. **Ensure GHG-saving thresholds** (50 / 60 / 65 %; 70 % RFNBO) are met and documented.
   → self + auditor.
6. **Enter transaction/sustainability data in the UDB** for in-scope consignments
   (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → Union Database *[confirm BE mandatory-use date]*.
7. **Reconcile the annual incorporation obligation** with FPS Economy (DG Energy) and hold
   non-standard-fuel permits where required; note that shortfall triggers the Art. 10
   administrative fine of **1 400 € per 34 GJ** (indexed), and registration/fee non-compliance
   up to 5 % of Belgian market turnover. → FPS Economy (DG Energy).
8. **Register with excise/customs (FPS Finance)** as an energy-products operator; there is
   **no biofuel excise relief**, and note the road-fuel excise **increase scheduled 1 Jan
   2027** and the EU-ETS2 obligation from **1 Jan 2028**. → FPS Finance (Douanes et Accises).

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Belgium is the most solid of the three files** — the 12.2 % (2026) overall level and the
1.1 % advanced sub-quota are **CONFIRMED** (primary Justel/etaamb text + independent ePURE),
and RED III transport transposition is **CONFIRMED notified** (EUR-Lex NIM). Residual items:

- **CORRECTION (food/crop cap) — carry forward:** the draft's crop-cap row previously read
  "Diesel: max 5.7 % through 2026 (→2.5 % 2027); petrol/gaseous: max 6.5 % through 2026
  (→5.5 % 2027, →4.5 % 2030)". Verbatim Art. 7 (verified on ejustice **and** etaamb, S7) shows
  the "5.7 %" is the **§3 per-pool minimum biofuel content** (both petrol and diesel), NOT a
  food cap. The **§6 food/feed-crop MAXIMUM caps** are: **diesel max 6 % (to 2024) → max 5 %
  from 1 Jan 2025 (in force for 2026) → max 2.5 % from 2030**; **petrol/gaseous max 6.5 % (to
  2026) → max 5.5 % from 2027 → max 4.5 % from 2030**. Diesel food cap for 2026 is therefore
  **5 %, not 5.7 %**, and the diesel taper to 2.5 % occurs **from 2030, not 2027**. Petrol
  caps in the draft were correct.
- **PRIMARY-ONLY — per-pool minima (Art. 7 §3) and Art. 10 penalty:** both pools min 5.7 %
  (2024–2026), from 2027 petrol min 4 % / diesel min 2.5 %; penalty "1 400 € per 34 GJ"
  matches Art. 10 verbatim. These rest on the **same primary act on both renderings** (Justel +
  etaamb are the same Moniteur belge text), so no independent Tier 2 corroboration — kept
  **PRIMARY-ONLY**. The "34" energy-unit basis and current indexation coefficient remain to
  be confirmed against the RD 17 March 2024 indexation.
- **No CONFLICT on the tax figure:** the "455.5305 EUR/1000 L" surfaced in search is the
  March 2022 temporary special-excise reduction that **expired 30 Sep 2022** (verified via
  news.belgium, T8) — not the 2026 rate. The **600.20 EUR/1000 L** total is PRIMARY-ONLY
  (FPS-Economy "Energia" au 31 déc. 2025), corroborated at magnitude by RTBF (~0.60/L, Tier 2)
  and the LU customs common-excise component, because the live FOD Financiën / Fisconetplus /
  TarBel 2026 rate table was CAPTCHA-blocked.
- **health.belgium.be 502** on 2026-07-04 (still 502 on corroborator re-fetch) — re-fetch the
  "critères de durabilité" and "base de données Biofuel" pages to confirm **registrant
  categories, GHG-threshold figures**, and the exact RD number/date transposing RED II
  sustainability (post-2021 draft). [FLAG]
- **Other gaps:** professional-diesel refund (0.1913 EUR/L) and biomethane rate need Tier 1
  confirmation; DG TAXUD Excise Duty Tables PDF unparseable (no local renderer); split of the
  600.20 total (accise commune / accise spéciale / cotisation énergie) not fully itemised from
  Tier 1; annual incorporation-obligation reporting deadline (Loi 17.07.2013) not pinned; UDB
  mandatory-use date for BE operators and Biofuel-database↔UDB link not sourced; confirm no
  regional sustainability add-ons (Flanders/Wallonia); blend walls (E10/B7) governed by FQD
  product-standard RDs, not separately fetched.

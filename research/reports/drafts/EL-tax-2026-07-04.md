## 2. Fuel taxation

Greece levies the **EFK — Ειδικός Φόρος Κατανάλωσης (excise duty)** on motor fuels under the **National Customs Code**, historically **Law 2960/2001** and **recodified as Law 5222/2025** — the national instrument implementing Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC, administered by **AADE** (Independent Authority for Public Revenue). Greece has **no separate CO2/carbon tax** on road fuels and **no reduced excise for biofuels**.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **EU minima context.** Greece's €700 (petrol) and €410 (diesel) sit well above the ETD minima (€359 petrol, €330 diesel), and are among the higher rates in the EU. `CONFIRMED` — S2 (Tax Foundation, 1 Jan 2026).
- **No biofuel tax incentive.** Greece promotes biofuels through an **annual biodiesel allocation programme** (quantity-based) rather than an excise reduction — see the mandates report. Since the dedicated pure-biodiesel EFK exemption lapsed at the end of 2007, biodiesel blended into road diesel bears the **full €410 EFK**; there is no Swedish/Finnish-style relief limited to volumes above the obligation because there is no biofuel excise relief at all.
- **VAT.** 24% VAT applies on the tax-inclusive price (S2).
- **Fuel-price relief via subsidy, not EFK.** For 2026 the government has used targeted "Fuel Pass"-type subsidies rather than cutting EFK. An opposition (SYRIZA) amendment to cut fuel EFK (to ~€359 petrol / €330 diesel) was **not adopted** — `PROPOSED` only (S7).
- **Agricultural diesel discount (separate from road fuel).** From **1 November 2026** Greece re-introduces an at-the-pump EFK discount of up to **€0.41/L for agricultural diesel** (app/QR-code based). This is an agricultural relief, not a road-transport rate change. `CONFIRMED` — S5.
- **ETS2** applies to road-transport fuels from **1 January 2028** under Regulation (EU) 2026/667 (not 2027). `CONFIRMED` (EU-level).

## 4-line summary

- **Petrol:** EFK €700/1000 L (€0.70/L), unchanged since 2017, +24% VAT.
- **Diesel:** EFK €410/1000 L (€0.41/L), unchanged since 2017, +24% VAT; no separate carbon tax.
- **Biofuel relief:** none — biodiesel/bioethanol taxed at the full diesel/petrol EFK (pure-biodiesel exemption ended 2007); biofuels promoted via an annual allocation programme, not tax.
- **Flags:** Tier-1 rate table and biofuel-treatment confirmation still needed; agricultural-diesel EFK discount (€0.41/L) from 1 Nov 2026; opposition EFK-cut amendment not adopted; ETS2 from 1 Jan 2028.

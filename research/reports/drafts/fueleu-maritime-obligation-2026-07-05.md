# FuelEU Maritime — Compliance Report (Sections 1–4)

- **Instrument:** FuelEU Maritime — Regulation (EU) 2023/1805 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 13 September 2023 on the use of renewable and low-carbon fuels in maritime transport, and amending Directive 2009/16/EC
- **Legal form:** directly applicable EU Regulation (no national transposition; Member States designate competent/administering authorities and enforce penalties)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-05
- **Researched by:** biofuel-mandates researcher (FuelEU / regulation slice)
- **Corroboration pass:** pending (draft; key figures corroborated inline, see confidence labels)

## Executive summary

FuelEU Maritime sets a well-to-wake (WtW) **GHG-intensity limit** on the energy used on board ships above 5 000 GT calling at EU/EEA ports, applying from **1 January 2025**. The limit is expressed as a percentage reduction against a 2020 fleet reference of **91.16 gCO2e/MJ**, tightening in five-year steps from **−2% (2025)** to **−80% (2050)**. It layers a **RFNBO/e-fuel** incentive (2× reward multiplier to 2033; a conditional 2% sub-quota from 2034), an **on-shore power supply (OPS)** obligation for containerships and passenger ships at berth from 2030, and flexibility via **banking, borrowing and pooling**. Non-compliance triggers the **FuelEU penalty** of **EUR 2 400 per tonne of VLSFO-energy-equivalent** deficit, rising with a **1.1× multiplier** after consecutive deficit years. FuelEU is **separate from but complementary to** the EU ETS, which phases maritime CO2 in at 40% (2024) → 70% (2025) → 100% (2026).

## 2. Core obligation & trajectory

Headline metric: the **yearly average well-to-wake GHG intensity** of the energy used on board (gCO2e/MJ) must not exceed a declining limit derived from the 2020 reference of **91.16 gCO2e/MJ** reduced by the trajectory percentages (Article 4 + Annex I). WtW = well-to-tank + tank-to-wake emissions, i.e. lifecycle including upstream production.

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference / baseline | **91.16 gCO2e/MJ** (2020 fleet average WtW) | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 2020 baseline | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, S3 |
| Headline metric | WtW GHG-intensity limit on energy used on board (gCO2e/MJ) | Art. 4(1) | 1 Jan 2025 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |
| Reduction 2025–2029 | **−2%** (limit ≈ **89.34 gCO2e/MJ**) | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 1 Jan 2025 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3, S2 |
| Reduction 2030–2034 | **−6%** | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 1 Jan 2030 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, S3 |
| Reduction 2035–2039 | **−14.5%** | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 1 Jan 2035 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |
| Reduction 2040–2044 | **−31%** | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 1 Jan 2040 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |
| Reduction 2045–2049 | **−62%** | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 1 Jan 2045 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |
| Reduction 2050+ | **−80%** | Art. 4(2), Annex I | 1 Jan 2050 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |
| RFNBO 2× reward multiplier | Energy from **RFNBOs counted 2×** in the GHG-intensity numerator (reward) **until 31 Dec 2033** | Art. 5(1) | 2025–2033 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |
| RFNBO sub-quota (conditional) | **2% RFNBO** share of the yearly energy used on board **from 1 Jan 2034**, triggered **only if** the Commission finds RFNBO share in maritime energy **< 1% in 2031** (with production/availability/price conditions) | Art. 5(3) | 1 Jan 2034 (conditional) | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1, S3 |

Notes: the 2× RFNBO multiplier is a *reward* mechanism (halves the counted GHG intensity of the RFNBO energy) applied to incentivise early e-fuel uptake before the sub-quota window. The 2% sub-target is not automatic — it activates only on a Commission determination that the 1% RFNBO uptake was not reached in 2031.

## 3. Flexibility & mechanics

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Banking | A positive compliance surplus may be **banked** forward to subsequent reporting periods (no expiry stated) | Art. 20(1) | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| Borrowing | A ship may **borrow** an advance surplus from the following period (up to a cap); the borrowed amount is subtracted from next year's balance **plus a 10% surcharge (×1.1)** | Art. 20(2)–(3) | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| Pooling | Compliance balances of **two or more ships** (verified by the same verifier) may be **pooled** so a surplus on one ship offsets a deficit on another; the pool total must be positive | Art. 21 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| OPS / shore power — from 2030 | Containerships and passenger ships **at berth for > 2 hours** in a TEN-T/AFIR core port must **connect to on-shore power supply (OPS)** and use it for all electrical demand (or use an accepted zero-emission technology) | Art. 6(1)–(4) | CONFIRMED | S1, S3, S2 |
| OPS — from 2035 | Requirement extends to **all EU ports equipped with OPS**; a limited number of exceptions per reporting period allowed | Art. 6 | CONFIRMED | S3, S2 |
| OPS exceptions | Stays < 2 h, unscheduled/emergency calls, OPS unavailable/incompatible, on-board electricity generation for safety, etc. | Art. 6(5) | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| Compliance calculation | Company computes yearly GHG-intensity, compares to the Art. 4 limit, derives compliance balance (surplus/deficit); verified by an accredited verifier; **FuelEU Document of Compliance** issued | Art. 4, Art. 15–19 | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1, S2 |

## 4. Penalties & economic instruments

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| FuelEU penalty (GHG-intensity deficit) | **EUR 2 400 per tonne of VLSFO-energy-equivalent** (per 41 000 MJ) of the compliance deficit; formula = (compliance deficit in gCO2e ÷ (GHG intensity actual × 41 000)) × 2 400 | Art. 23, **Annex IV** | 1 Jan 2025 (first settlement 2026) | CONFIRMED | S1, S4 |
| Repeat-non-compliance multiplier | Penalty multiplied by **1.1** where the ship has a compliance deficit for **two or more consecutive reporting periods** | Art. 23(3), Annex IV | 1 Jan 2025 | CONFIRMED | S1, S4 |
| OPS non-compliance penalty | **EUR 1.5 per kWh** (× established total electrical power demand × hours spent non-compliant at berth) | Art. 24, Annex IV | 1 Jan 2030 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| Penalty is not a buy-out | Paying the penalty does **not** exempt the ship from meeting the obligation; revenues go to a fund/general budget for maritime decarbonisation | Art. 23(8), Art. 26 | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1 |
| Enforcement | Administering State issues the FuelEU **Document of Compliance**; a ship without a valid DoC for ≥ 2 periods may be refused entry / subject to expulsion order | Art. 25, Art. 23(9) | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1 |
| **EU ETS interaction (separate instrument)** | Maritime CO2 brought into EU ETS by **Directive (EU) 2023/959** (amending Dir. 2003/87/EC); phase-in of allowances to surrender: **40% of 2024 emissions, 70% of 2025 emissions, 100% from 2026**; CH4 and N2O added from 2026. Same 5 000 GT threshold and 100%/50% voyage geography. FuelEU and ETS are **complementary and cumulative** — a ship may owe both ETS allowances and a FuelEU penalty | Dir. 2003/87/EC Art. 3ga (as amended by Dir. 2023/959) | 2024→2026 | CONFIRMED | S5, S7, S3 |
| Cost pass-through | Regulation allows contractual pass-through of costs to the commercial operator/charterer responsible for fuel choice and voyage; ETS "polluter pays" cost typically falls on the ISM company but is passed to charterers (BIMCO clauses) | Art. 23(7) (FuelEU) | — | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1 |

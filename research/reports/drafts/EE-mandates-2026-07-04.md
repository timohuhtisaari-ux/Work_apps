## 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** fuel suppliers releasing petrol/diesel for consumption.
- **Administrator / supervisor:** the **Environmental Board (Keskkonnaamet)**. Suppliers file a **monthly report by the 15th** of each month (quantities and gross-energy data of petrol, diesel and biofuel released for consumption in the prior month), plus GHG-intensity data. The Environmental Board maintains the national biofuel-statistics database and exchanges data with the EU database referenced in Directive (EU) 2018/2001 (Union Database), using it to strengthen supervision. [2][3]
- **FOKA register** is Estonia's environmental-permits/handling register referenced on the same Keskkonnaamet page (its biofuel-specific function was not confirmed). [3] — flag for corroborator.
- Compliance is verified against the weighted-average share; enforcement uses misdemeanour fines plus substitutive-enforcement penalty payments. [4]

**RED III transposition status:** IN PROGRESS / PARTIAL — **infringement open**. Estonia received a **letter of formal notice in Sept 2024** and a **reasoned opinion in July 2025** for incomplete transposition of RED III (specifically the permitting-acceleration provisions). A Liquid Fuel Act amendment was **adopted and published RT I 08.07.2025, in force 18.07.2025**, transposing transport-fuel provisions (introduced the ≥0.5 % advanced sub-target and the weighted-average methodology). Full RED III transposition is not complete. [5][6][7]  Label: `PROPOSED`/partial for anything beyond the in-force VKS text.

**ETS2 (EU cross-cutting):** road-transport fuels come under EU ETS2; the **compliance obligation starts 1 Jan 2028** (Reg. (EU) 2026/667), with auctioning from 2027. Directly applicable — not an Estonian mandate but interacts with supplier costs.

## 5-line summary — Estonia
1. Obligation type: energy-share (%) biofuel/renewable-energy content in the pooled petrol/diesel/biofuel energy, weighted-average basis; administered by the Environmental Board (Keskkonnaamet).
2. Current level: ≈**7.5 %** (semi-annual weighted average, 30 Jun/31 Dec) for 2026, with a **≥0.5 %** advanced-biofuel sub-target; penalty up to €400,000 (legal person).
3. Next legislated change: post-2026 step-up not located (GAP); NECP/RED III amendment expected to raise it.
4. RED III status: partial/in progress — infringement open (formal notice Sept 2024, reasoned opinion July 2025); transport provisions partly transposed by VKS amendment in force 18.07.2025.
5. Flags: **CONFLICT** on the overall level (7.5 % vs 10 %, historical 6.4 %); crop/Annex IX-B caps and trajectory are **GAPS**; several figures **PRIMARY-ONLY** (gazette is a JS SPA — corroborator must open the rendered § 21).

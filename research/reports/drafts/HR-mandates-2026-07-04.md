## 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated:** distributors placing diesel and petrol on the Croatian market ("obveznik"), per Zakon Art. 4(33). [1]
- **Compliance:** obligated parties must place biofuels/renewable energy meeting the energy values set by the monitoring methodology; only sustainability-compliant biofuels count toward the goal (regardless of feedstock origin). [1]
- **HROTE role:** the Croatian Energy Market Operator (Hrvatski operator tržišta energije) administers incentives for biofuel/RES production, collecting fees from electricity end-customers/suppliers and fuel distributors and channelling them to producers. HROTE is the fee-collection/incentive administrator; the Ministry (economy/energy, mingo.gov.hr) owns the obligation and NECP. [Registry note; corroborate exact HROTE competence — PRIMARY fetch of mingo/HROTE pages 503'd]
- **Non-compliance route:** enforced via the special environmental charge (a tax-style mechanism) — see also fuel-tax section for interaction. [5][8]
- Banking/borrowing and certificate trading: no tradable-certificate scheme identified (obligation is a direct energy-share placing obligation, not a certificate market). Banking/borrowing rules not located — GAP.

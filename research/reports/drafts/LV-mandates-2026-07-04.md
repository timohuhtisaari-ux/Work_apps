# Latvia — Biofuel Mandates (Section 1)

- **Country:** Latvia (LV)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** biofuel-mandates-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Scope:** Section 1 (Biofuel mandates) only. Fuel taxation and sustainability certification are other agents' scope.

**Headline:** As of **1 January 2026** Latvia's new **Transport Energy Law (Transporta enerģijas likums)** entered into force, replacing the old Biofuel Law (Biodegvielas likums). It combines a **GHG-intensity reduction target** (from 2030), an **advanced-fuel + RFNBO energy-share sub-obligation**, and **transitional volumetric blending** requirements.

## 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** **fuel suppliers** placing on the transport market **> 100 tonnes/year** (Art. 1 definition). Exemptions for suppliers of only alternative fuels, RFNBO, or aviation fuel (Art. 11(2), Art. 13(3)). [1]
- **Administrator:** Ministry of Climate and Energy (kem.gov.lv) framework; State Revenue Service (VID) interacts on the fuel side (tax scope — other agent).
- **Compliance:** demonstrated against the annual GHG-reduction and energy-share obligations plus the transitional volumetric blend floors; shortfalls trigger the per-tonne / per-GJ additional payments above.
- Transition from the repealed **Biodegvielas likums**; note Latvia had earlier (2022) temporarily **suspended** its mandatory biofuel blend to curb fuel-price inflation — historical context, superseded by the 2026 law. [5]

| From 2030 | Advanced + RFNBO **≥ 5.5 energy %** (of which **≥ 1 pp RFNBO**); GHG-intensity reduction **≥ 16 %** (or 14.5 % if 8 % aviation-biofuel condition met) | CONFIRMED (adopted, in force from 2030) | [1][2] |

**RED III transposition status:** The **Transport Energy Law (in force 01.01.2026)** is Latvia's principal RED III **transport-sector** transposition instrument (16 % GHG target, advanced+RFNBO sub-obligation). Separately, Latvia received a **reasoned opinion (2025)** for incomplete transposition of RED III **permitting-acceleration** provisions (distinct from the transport mandate). [1][3]

**ETS2 (EU cross-cutting):** road-transport fuels fall under EU ETS2; **compliance obligation starts 1 Jan 2028** (Reg. (EU) 2026/667), auctioning from 2027. Directly applicable.

## 5-line summary — Latvia
1. Obligation type: combined — GHG-intensity reduction (16 % from 2030) + advanced/RFNBO energy-share sub-obligation + transitional volumetric blending; obligated party = fuel suppliers > 100 t/yr.
2. Current level (2026): advanced + RFNBO **≥ 1 energy %**; petrol-95 **≥ 9.5 vol %**, diesel **≥ 6.5 vol %** (pure diesel allowed only until 31 Mar 2026); penalties €90/t CO2 and €70/GJ.
3. Next legislated change: from **2030** advanced+RFNBO rises to **5.5 %** (≥1 pp RFNBO) and the **16 % GHG** reduction target applies.
4. RED III status: transport provisions **transposed** via the Transport Energy Law (in force 01.01.2026); separate reasoned opinion pending on permitting provisions.
5. Flags: Annex IX-B/crop caps and double-counting multipliers are **GAPS**; core figures **CONFIRMED** (primary likumi.lv + lvportals/BNN).

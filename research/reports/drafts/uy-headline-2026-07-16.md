# Uruguay — Biofuel Regulation & Low-Carbon Fuel Status Report
**Date: 2026-07-16**  
**Research focus: non-EU country; ethanol mandate operative; biodiesel mandate repealed**

---

## 1. Instrument Type & Current-Year Operative Obligation

**Operative Status**: ETHANOL MANDATE IN FORCE; BIODIESEL MANDATE REPEALED  
**Type**: Volume (ethanol blending requirement)  
**Legal Basis**: Ley 18.195 (Agrofuels Law, 2007), modified by Ley 19.996 (2021)

### Ethanol Blending Mandate
- **Current Required Level**: 8.5% (E8.5) minimum fuel alcohol (ethanol) blended with automotive gasoline  
- **Legal Instrument**: Ley 18.195, Article 6, as modified by Ley 19.996, Article 182 (2021)  
- **Effective Date**: January 1, 2022  
- **Blending Entity**: ANCAP (Administración Nacional de Combustibles, Alcohol y Pórtland) — state-owned  
- **Feedstock**: Domestic ethanol produced from agricultural biomass (primarily sugarcane and corn)  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`  
**Source (Tier 1)**: Ley 18.195 (original); Ley 19.996 (amendment); ANCAP official documentation  
**Source (Tier 2)**: USDA FAS GAIN Uruguay Oilseeds report (Apr 2026)  
**Checked**: 2026-07-16

### Biodiesel Blending
- **Former Mandate**: 5% (B5) biodiesel with diesel (automotive) — REPEALED  
- **Repeal Instrument**: Ley 19.996, Article 183 (2021), which repealed Article 7 of Ley 18.195  
- **Current Status**: No mandatory biodiesel blending  
  - ANCAP did blend 2.5% biodiesel into gas oil (diesel) for a period; as of late 2022, **zero blending recorded**  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`  
**Source (Tier 1)**: Ley 19.996, Articles 182–183  
**Source (Tier 2)**: Uruguay's Third Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC, Dec 2024, UNFCCC); USDA FAS Uruguay Biofuels (Apr 2026)  
**Checked**: 2026-07-16

## 2. Legislated Trajectory & 2030 Targets

### Ethanol Targets
- **2022–Present**: 8.5% operative mandate (no legislated increase beyond 8.5% for 2026–2030)  
- **2025 Goal (Original)**: 5% bioethanol in gasoline (already surpassed; operative level is 8.5%)  
- **E10 References**: Some research pointers mention "E10 plans," but no operative legislated E10 mandate found; E8.5 remains the binding obligation  
- **Confidence on E10**: `UNCORROBORATED` (no Tier 1 source confirming E10 as legislated; may be industry discussion or future aspiration)

### Biodiesel Targets (Failed)
- **2025 Goal (Original)**: 5% biodiesel with diesel (NOT ACHIEVED; mandate repealed 2021)  
- **Government Intent (2026)**: Industry contacts reported new government may signal intention to **reinstate 5% biodiesel blend**, but **no concrete legislative action** has been taken as of April 2026  
- **Confidence**: `PROPOSED` (reinstatement discussed; not yet adopted)  
**Source (Tier 2)**: USDA FAS GAIN Uruguay Oilseeds report (Apr 2026)

### Climate & Energy (Non-Biofuel-Specific)
- **2030 Carbon-Neutrality Goal**: Conditional (postponed)  
  - Original NDC (2022): Unconditional cap on energy-related emissions of 9.3 MtCO2 by 2030  
  - **Third NDC (Dec 2024)**: Postponed unconditional emissions cap to 2035 (from 2030)  
- **Renewable Energy**: Uruguay already achieves ~98% electricity from renewables (achieved 2024); target of 50% primary energy renewables met early (53% in 2014)  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`  
**Source (Tier 1)**: Uruguay's Third NDC (Dec 2024, submitted to UNFCCC)

## 3. Advanced Biofuel / Sub-Quotas

**Not applicable.** Ley 18.195 and subsequent amendments do not define advanced-biofuel (Annex IX equivalent) sub-quotas. The 8.5% ethanol requirement is a blending target with no distinction between first-generation and advanced fuels.

## 4. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

### General Fuel Excise Tax (IMESI)
- **Framework**: Uruguay's excise tax system (IMESI) applies to gasoline, diesel, and other fuels at varying rates (5–133% depending on fuel type and category)  
- **Biofuel-Specific Exemptions/Reductions**: Not found in search results; ANCAP blending is transparent (i.e., the 8.5% ethanol is part of the gasoline product sold to consumers)  
- **Border Region Adjustments (July 2026)**: IMESI tax reductions apply to gasoline/diesel at border-area service stations (22% reduction near Argentina; 34% near Brazil); similar regime covers stations 20–60 km from eligible crossings at 50% of benefit  
- **Biodiesel Tax Treatment (Historical)**: When B5 mandate was operative, no special tax treatment was identified; mandate repealed before detailed tax regime could be documented  
- **Confidence**: `PRIMARY-ONLY` (general excise tax framework confirmed; biofuel-specific treatment not clearly documented)  
**Source (Tier 1)**: Uruguay DGI (Dirección General Impositiva) tax code  
**Source (Tier 2)**: Fiscal Solutions, PWC Uruguay tax summaries, DailyFuels price reports  
**Checked**: 2026-07-16

## 5. Sustainability & Certification Regime

### ISCC (International Sustainability & Carbon Certification)
- **ALUR (Alcoholes del Uruguay S.A.)**, the ANCAP subsidiary responsible for ethanol production, **renewed ISCC certification** to enable export to European markets  
- **Scope**: ISCC audits production processes, supply chain, and sustainability criteria (GHG reduction, land-use compliance, biodiversity)  
- **Capurro Biodiesel Plant**: Also holds ISCC; capacity ~83,000 m³/year biodiesel  
- **Domestic Blending**: No mandatory sustainability certification found for domestic ANCAP ethanol blending; ISCC used for export compliance (esp. EU)  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED` (ISCC use for export); `PRIMARY-ONLY` (domestic certification regime for blended fuel)

### Production Infrastructure (ANCAP Subsidiaries)
- **ALUR Bioethanol Plants**: Paysandú & Bella Unión; combined 100,000 m³/year bioethanol capacity  
- **ALUR Biodiesel (Capurro)**: 83,000 m³/year; recently renewed ISCC  
- **E-Fuels Partnership (2024–2026)**: HIF Global and ALUR signed Implementation Agreement to develop e-fuels facility in Paysandú, utilizing biogenic CO₂ from ALUR's existing bioethanol plant  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`

**Source (Tier 1)**: ANCAP; ALUR official communications  
**Source (Tier 2)**: HIF Global press releases (Dec 2024); Advanced BioFuels USA; EIN Presswire; ISCC certification body documentation

## 8. Five-Line Summary

Uruguay mandates **8.5% ethanol (E8.5)** blended with gasoline since January 2022 (Ley 18.195, modified by Ley 19.996), operated by state-owned ANCAP and ALUR subsidiaries; no legislated increase planned for 2026–2030. The **5% biodiesel mandate was repealed in 2021**; zero blending has occurred since late 2022, though new government signals intent to reinstate (not yet legislated). ISCC certification is held by ALUR for export; no mandatory domestic sustainability regime applies. No advanced-fuel sub-quotas exist. Border-region fuel-tax adjustments apply (IMESI reductions), but biofuel-specific tax treatment unclear. Infrastructure (crush capacity) constrains biodiesel expansion.

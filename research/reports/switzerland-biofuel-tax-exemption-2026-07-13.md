## 1. Executive summary

The centrepiece of Swiss biofuel policy is a **full mineral-oil-tax exemption** for
qualifying biogenic/renewable fuels under **MinöStG Art. 12b (SR 641.61)**: both the base
mineral oil tax **and** the surtax are remitted (up to **~75 Rappen/litre** depending on
product), so a qualifying biofuel pays effectively **~0** fuel tax. The relief is
**mandate-less and uncapped** — Switzerland has no road blending mandate, so the exemption
is not rationed to "over-compliance" volumes; it is instead fiscally *offset* by a +3.7 Rp/L
surtax on fossil petrol/diesel. It carries a **hard statutory sunset of 31 December 2030**
(extended from an original end-2023 expiry by the revised CO2 Act, in force 1 Jan 2025), with
no taper and no legislated successor. Eligibility is gated by a cumulative **ecological**
criterion (≥40% lifecycle GHG saving, total environmental impact ≤125% of fossil, and no
high-carbon/high-biodiversity land conversion after 1 Jan 2008) **and** a **social** criterion
(ILO core conventions + lawful land acquisition), granted via a **BAZG permit decided "im
Einvernehmen mit BAFU und SECO"** and recorded in the Pronovo guarantee-of-origin database.
The key catches for an operator are three: **SAF (aviation) gets no equivalent relief** and is
left fiscally disadvantaged versus already-tax-free fossil kerosene; **mass-balanced volumes
appear NOT to qualify for the exemption** — the relief demands *physical*, not purely
book-and-claim, sustainability; and a **single-assignment rule** on the guarantee of origin
prevents the same volume from being both tax-exempt and counted toward an importer's CO2
compensation obligation. Imported biofuels get the identical origin-neutral relief with no
import quota.

## 2. Market context — the mandate-less design

Switzerland has **no road blending mandate**. Instead of obliging blenders to hit a
percentage, fossil-motor-fuel importers face a **CO2 compensation obligation**
(Kompensationspflicht) under the CO2 Act, and the tax exemption (§4) pulls biofuels into the
market on the fiscal side. The three market-shaping instruments:

| Instrument | Design | Value / trajectory | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Road blending mandate | **NONE** — no statutory blending obligation on motor fuels | — | CONFIRMED | S2, country-report §1 |
| CO2 compensation obligation on fossil importers | Importers must compensate a rising share of transport CO2 (domestic + limited foreign) | **30% of transport CO2 in 2026** (25% in 2025), rising **+5 pts/yr to 50% by 2030**; min. 12 pts domestic (max 18% abroad in 2026); max **5 Rp/L** pass-through | PRIMARY-ONLY (figures) / CONFIRMED (cross-ref) | S5, S11, country-report §1–2 |
| Aviation SAF blend mandate | ReFuelEU-aligned SAF blending obligation at Zurich/Geneva | **2% SAF from 1 Jan 2026** (a mandate, not a tax incentive) | CONFIRMED | S17, S14, country-report §1 |

- The exemption is therefore a **pull** instrument that sits alongside a **push** obligation on
  fossil importers; the two are deliberately kept from double-counting at the volume level
  (§9). SAF is driven by a **mandate**, not by the Art. 12b relief (§10).

## 4. The biofuel tax exemption — mechanics & amount (MinöStG Art. 12b)

### 4.1 Mechanism and legal basis

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective / sunset | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instrument | **Steuererleichterung für biogene Treibstoffe** — a mineral-oil-tax relief (remission) granted **on application** to qualifying renewable/biogenic fuels | **MinöStG Art. 12b** (SR 641.61); MinöStV SR 641.611 | in force; current version 1 Jan 2025 | PRIMARY-ONLY (Art. number per BAZG/Tier-2; Fedlex text JS-gated) | S2, S4, S8 |
| Nature of relief | **Full exemption** ("vollumfängliche Steuererleichterung") — qualifying biofuel pays effectively **~0** mineral-oil tax (base + surtax both remitted) | MinöStG Art. 12b | in force | CONFIRMED (BAZG verbatim "vollumfänglichen Steuererleichterung") | S2, S4 |
| Precondition | Fuel must meet **ecological and social minimum requirements** (proof required from producer/importer); waste/residue-based fuels made per state of the art are deemed to satisfy the ecological criteria (Art. 12b para 1 lit. a–d) | MinöStG Art. 12b; USG Art. 35d; IBTV/ETrV | in force | PRIMARY-ONLY | S2, S4 |
| Feedstock restriction (cross-ref) | Food/feed-crop and food-competing feedstocks excluded (USG Art. 35d); since 2013 the relief effectively targets **waste/residue-based** biogenic fuels | USG Art. 35d | in force | CROSS-REF (see §8) | S8 |

- Market-access linkage: a **BAZG tax-relief grant automatically confers BAFU
  market-placement authorisation** ("Eine Bewilligung zur Steuererleichterung des BAZG hat
  automatisch die Bewilligung zur Inverkehrbringung durch das BAFU zur Folge") — re-verified
  verbatim on the BAFU IBTV page [S6]. If **no** relief is claimed, prior BAFU authorisation is
  needed separately [S6] (see §7).

### 4.2 Exact relief amount per fuel type — quantified against the fossil rates

BAZG states the relief is **full, "je nach Produkt von bis zu 75 Rappen je Liter"** (up to
~75 Rp/L depending on product) [S2] — re-verified verbatim on the BAZG renewable-fuels page.
Because it is a *full* exemption, the amount remitted equals the entire mineral-oil tax +
surtax that would otherwise apply to that product's tariff line — i.e. the biofuel is brought
to ~0 tax:

| Renewable fuel | Fossil tariff line it displaces | Mineral-oil tax otherwise due | Relief (remitted) | Net tax after relief | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bioethanol / renewable ethanol | Petrol | 76.82 Rp/L | full (~76.8 Rp/L) | ~0 | CONFIRMED (full-exemption principle); PRIMARY-ONLY (per-litre figure derived) |
| Biodiesel (FAME) | Diesel | 79.57 Rp/L | full (~79.6 Rp/L) | ~0 | CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY (derived) |
| HVO (hydrotreated veg. oil) | Diesel | 79.57 Rp/L | full (~79.6 Rp/L) | ~0 | CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY (derived) |
| Plant/animal oils | Diesel | 79.57 Rp/L | full | ~0 | CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Biogas / bio-CNG / biomethane | Gas motor-fuel line | applicable gas-fuel rate | full | ~0 | CONFIRMED (listed as eligible); rate line not pinned (GAP) |

- The BAZG "up to 75 Rp/L" figure is a rounded ceiling reflecting the diesel/petrol tariff
  magnitudes; the exact per-product remission tracks each product's own tariff line. The
  **base + surtax are both remitted** — this is why net tax is ~0, not just the surtax [S2, S4].
- **Minor discrepancy (noted, not a conflict on the load-bearing point):** the Tier-2
  steuern-easy.ch page [S7] describes the relief as "bis zu 72 Rappen je Liter" for *imported*
  biogenic fuels, vs the **official BAZG "bis zu 75 Rappen je Liter"** [S2]. The BAZG figure
  (Tier-1, current) governs; the 72 figure is likely stale or product-specific. Both confirm
  the *full-exemption / tens-of-Rp-per-litre* magnitude.

### 4.3 Volume cap / quota / budget — and the mandate-less design

| Item | Finding | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volume cap or quota on the relief | **NONE** — any qualifying volume receives full relief; the relief is not capped, quota-ed or budget-limited | PRIMARY-ONLY | S2, S9 |
| Blending mandate conditioning | **NONE** — Switzerland has **no road blending mandate**, so the relief is **NOT** restricted to volumes "beyond an obligation" (contrast Sweden/Finland state-aid design) | CONFIRMED | S2, country-report §1 |
| Fiscal offset (revenue neutrality) | Tax losses from the relief are offset by a **+3.7 Rp/L increase in the petrol/diesel surtax** (in force 1 Jan 2021), calibrated to keep the scheme budget-neutral | CONFIRMED (+3.7 Rp / 1 Jan 2021); "through-2028 window" PRIMARY-ONLY / GAP | S9, S7, S19 |

- **+3.7 Rp/L / 1 Jan 2021 — CONFIRMED:** the SECO news release title itself states
  "Bundesrat erhöht Steuersätze für Benzin und Dieselöl um 3,7 Rappen pro Liter" (Tier-1 S9),
  independently corroborated by steuern-easy.ch [S7] and Wikipedia [S19]. The **specific
  end-2028 financing window** could not be re-pinned and stays a GAP (§12).
- **Key distinction:** the Swiss relief is an **open-ended, uncapped, non-mandate-conditioned
  full exemption**. It is fiscally *offset* (not *rationed*) via the fossil surtax — structurally
  different from EU MS where biofuel tax relief is a state-aid measure limited to
  over-compliance volumes.

## 6. Eligibility — ecological & social criteria

Two cumulative gates must be passed before a biogenic fuel can receive the relief: an
**ecological** gate (positive overall environmental balance) and a **social** gate. Both are
set by **USG Art. 35d** and detailed in the IBTV/ETrV/MinöStV.

### 6.1 Ecological requirements (positive overall environmental balance)

| Requirement | Threshold / rule | Legal basis | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Minimum GHG saving** over the whole lifecycle | Emits **at least 40% less** greenhouse gas than the conventional fossil comparator ("mindestens 40 Prozent weniger Treibhausgase … über den gesamten Lebensweg") | USG Art. 35d; IBTV (SR 814.311.1); ETrV (SR 641.611.21) | CONFIRMED | S6, S27, S18, S28 |
| **Overall environmental impact** vs fossil comparator | Total environmental burden **not more than 25% greater** than the fossil equivalent → i.e. **≤ 125%** of the fossil comparator ("Umweltbelastung … gesamthaft höchstens 25 Prozent grösser") — ecological-scarcity / LCA method | USG Art. 35d; IBTV; ETrV | CONFIRMED | S6, S27, S28 |
| **Land-use / no land conversion** | Feedstock must **not** come from **conversion after 1 January 2008** of land with **high carbon stock** — esp. forests, peatbogs, wetlands ("Flächen mit hohem Kohlenstoffbestand (insb. Wälder, Torfmoore, Feuchtgebiete)") — or land with **high biodiversity / protected areas** | USG Art. 35d; IBTV; ETrV | CONFIRMED | S6, S27, S28 |
| LCA method | BAFU/authority assess GHG + total environmental impact via **ecoinvent-based** LCA (ecological-scarcity method) | ETrV | PRIMARY-ONLY | S6, country-report §3.2 |

- The **≤125%** figure is expressed in the ordinance as "höchstens 25 Prozent grösser," i.e.
  the fuel may be up to 25% worse than fossil on *aggregate* environmental impact but no more —
  the "positive overall environmental balance" test that goes **beyond** the single GHG number.
  Verbatim German re-verified from BAFU IBTV and **independently corroborated** by the ETrV
  summary text (S27) and cng-mobility.ch (S28).
- **≥40% GHG threshold — CONFIRMED.** The Swiss autonomous "40%" digit is corroborated by
  **three independent secondary sources** beyond the BAFU IBTV Tier-1 page: douana.ch
  Richtlinie-09 ("40 % weniger Treibhausgasemissionen als fossiles Benzin", S18),
  cng-mobility.ch (S28), and the ETrV description in the fedlex search index (S27). The
  **land-conversion cut-off of "1 January 2008"** is an additional detail surfaced from the
  ETrV text (S27) and matches the RED reference-date convention.

## 7. Permit & proof process (how an operator obtains the relief)

Lead authority for the **tax-relief decision** is **BAZG**; it decides **in agreement with
("im Einvernehmen mit") BAFU and SECO**. BAFU verifies the **ecological** proof; SECO verifies
the **social** proof. A separate, second permit — the **IBTV market-placement authorisation** —
is run by BAFU, but a granted BAZG tax relief **automatically** carries the IBTV placement
permission with it.

### 7.1 Roles

| Authority | Role in the relief/permit chain | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BAZG** (Federal Office for Customs and Border Security) | Owns the application procedure ("Federführung"); issues the producer's **Betriebsbewilligung** (operating authorisation); **formally decides** grant/refusal of the tax relief, in agreement with BAFU + SECO | CONFIRMED (im Einvernehmen role corroborated S22 + IBTV-package search S28) | S2, S22, S28 |
| **BAFU** (FOEN) | Verifies the **ecological requirements** (GHG ≥40% saving, ≤125% total impact, land-use); runs the parallel **IBTV Inverkehrbringen** (market-placement) authorisation | PRIMARY-ONLY | S6, S22 |
| **SECO** | Verifies the **social criteria** (Glaubhaftmachung), forwards finding to BAZG | PRIMARY-ONLY | S22, S24 |

### 7.2 Step-by-step (tax-relief route)

1. **Producer operating authorisation.** A domestic producer needs a **Betriebsbewilligung des
   BAZG** ("operating authorisation") before producing renewable fuel for the relief. (Importers
   without domestic production do not need this, but do file the application below.) `CONFIRMED` [S2]
2. **File the relief application to BAZG.** Submit the application form + ecological proof form
   (applicant declares fuel type/quality, the **land used**, and the **entire production chain
   from raw-material cultivation to delivery to the consumer**). Where **not all input materials
   appear on the BAZG positive list ("Positivliste BAZG eTS")**, the applicant must additionally
   file **Formular 45.85** ("Gesuch um Steuererleichterung für erneuerbare Treibstoffe /
   Nachweis"). `PRIMARY-ONLY` [S22]
3. **Social self-declaration.** Submit the **Selbstdeklaration** confirming respect for social
   legislation (ILO core conventions) and lawful land acquisition — reviewed by **SECO**. `PRIMARY-ONLY` [S22, S24]
4. **BAFU ecological check.** BAFU verifies the LCA (≥40% GHG saving, ≤125% total impact,
   land-use). `CONFIRMED` [S6, S27, S28]
5. **BAZG decision "im Einvernehmen."** BAZG grants (or refuses) the relief — up to **full
   exemption, ~75 Rappen/litre depending on product** — in agreement with BAFU + SECO. `CONFIRMED` [S2, S22]
6. **Automatic IBTV placement permission.** "Eine Bewilligung zur Steuererleichterung des BAZG
   hat automatisch die Bewilligung zur Inverkehrbringung durch das BAFU zur Folge" — a granted
   tax relief **automatically** confers the IBTV market-placement authorisation. `CONFIRMED` (re-verified verbatim) [S6]
7. **Guarantee-of-origin recording.** Record the production/import in the **Pronovo**
   guarantee-of-origin (Herkunftsnachweis) system and cancel/devalue the GO on delivery (§9). `CONFIRMED` [S12]

### 7.3 Prior-approval requirement and the 1 Nov 2025 transition

| Item | Rule | Legal basis | Confidence | Sources |

## 8. Feedstock exclusion — categorical food/feed-crop ban

| Rule | Legal basis | Confidence | Sources |

## 9. Interaction with the CO2 compensation obligation (no double-counting)

Switzerland has **no mandate**; fossil-fuel importers instead face a **CO2 compensation
obligation** (Kompensationspflicht): **30% of transport CO2 in 2026** (25% in 2025), rising
**+5 pts/yr to 50% by 2030**, min. 12 pts domestic (max 18% abroad in 2026), max 5 Rp/L
pass-through [S5; cross-ref country-report §1–2].

**How the tax relief and the compensation obligation interact:**

| Question | Finding | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do renewable fuels reduce the compensation base? | Yes — placing renewable (non-fossil) fuel on the market lowers the **fossil** CO2 volume subject to compensation | PRIMARY-ONLY | S5, S11 |
| Can renewable fuels count *toward* an importer's compensation? | Yes — importers may fulfil part of the obligation by bringing renewable fuels to market / via emission-reduction attestations (Bescheinigungen) | PRIMARY-ONLY | S11 |
| **Can the SAME volume be BOTH tax-exempt AND counted for compensation?** | **NO.** The ecological value of a fuel is tracked by a **guarantee of origin (Herkunftsnachweis, HKN)** in the Pronovo database. Once an HKN is **assigned to one instrument** (mineral-oil-tax relief *or* CO2 compensation), it is spent: *"Mit der Zuweisung des HKN ist der ökologische Mehrwert des Stoffes abgegolten."* | CONFIRMED | S12 |
| Anti-double-counting mechanism | Mandatory **HKN cancellation (Entwertung)** in the Pronovo/Vollzugsstelle database when the fuel is declared; the HKN can be attributed to only one policy instrument | CONFIRMED | S12 |

- **Bottom line:** the two instruments are mutually exclusive **at the volume/HKN level** — a
  biofuel volume that takes the full mineral-oil-tax exemption cannot *also* be surrendered as
  compensation for the fossil obligation, and vice versa. The guarantee-of-origin (Pronovo)
  system is the single anti-double-counting gate (mandatory since 1 Jan 2025).

## 11. Compliance checklist for an importer/producer

1. **Obtain the BAZG operating authorisation / relief grant.** Domestic producers: hold a
   **Betriebsbewilligung des BAZG** before producing. All applicants: file the relief
   application to **BAZG**, which decides **"im Einvernehmen mit BAFU und SECO."** `CONFIRMED`
   (Betriebsbewilligung, im Einvernehmen)
2. **Secure BAFU IBTV market-placement approval** *before* placing fuel on the market —
   **mandatory since 1 Nov 2025** (grace period ended, IBTV Art. 11). A granted BAZG relief
   **auto-confers** it; otherwise apply separately to BAFU (`ibtv@bafu.admin.ch`). `CONFIRMED`
3. **Provide ecological proof (ETrV route).** Demonstrate **≥40% lifecycle GHG saving** AND
   total environmental impact **≤125%** of fossil (no more than 25% greater) AND **no
   high-carbon/high-biodiversity land conversion after 1 Jan 2008**, via ecoinvent-based LCA
   (ETrV SR 641.611.21 / IBTV). Submit the ecological proof form (fuel type, land used, full
   production chain) + **Formular 45.85** if inputs are off the BAZG positive list. `CONFIRMED`
   (thresholds) / `PRIMARY-ONLY` (forms)
4. **File the social Selbstdeklaration (SECO).** Confirm respect for **ILO core conventions**
   and **lawful land acquisition**; SECO reviews the Glaubhaftmachung. `PRIMARY-ONLY`
5. **Register in Pronovo and record monthly.** Guarantee-of-origin recording mandatory since
   1 Jan 2025; enter production/imports **monthly, by the 6th of the following month**; devalue
   (entwerten) the GO on delivery (gases: by the 25th of the first month of the following
   quarter; 18-month validity). `CONFIRMED` (cadence) / `PRIMARY-ONLY` (devaluation)
6. **Ensure PHYSICAL (not mass-balance-only) sustainability for the exemption.** Mass-balanced
   consignments appear to get **NO** mineral-oil-tax relief — the exemption needs
   physical/segregated sustainability characteristics, not book-and-claim. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (§7.5)
7. **Assign each guarantee of origin to only ONE instrument.** The same HKN/volume cannot be
   both tax-exempt AND counted toward the CO2 compensation obligation — single-assignment is the
   anti-double-counting gate. `CONFIRMED`
8. **Confirm the feedstock is not a food/feed crop** and does not directly compete with food
   production — palm and soy are effectively banned (USG Art. 35d). `CONFIRMED`
9. **Plan around the 2030 sunset.** The whole relief regime **sunsets 31 Dec 2030** (hard cliff,
   no taper, no legislated successor) — factor into offtake/investment horizons. `CONFIRMED`

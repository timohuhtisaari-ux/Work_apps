# India: Biofuel & Low-Carbon Fuel Regulation — Research Draft
**Date-stamped: 2026-07-16**

## 1. Instrument Type & Current-Year Headline Obligation

**Legislative Framework:**
- **National Policy on Biofuels 2018** (amended 2022) — primary statute
- **Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas (MoPNG)** — administering authority
- **Ethanol Supply Year (ESY) 2025-26** — operational unit (April–March)

**Current Operative Mandates (as of July 2026):**

| Fuel Type | Level | Effective Date | Legal Basis | Confidence |
|-----------|-------|---|---|---|
| **E20 (Ethanol)** | 20% nationwide | June 30, 2025 (achieved); April 1, 2026 (operative) | National Policy 2018 (amended 2022) | **CONFIRMED** |
| **E25/E27/E30** | Standards only (no rollout timeline) | IS 19850:2026 effective May 15, 2026 | Bureau of Indian Standards | **PRIMARY-ONLY** |
| **B5 (Biodiesel)** | 5% target (not yet operative nationally) | Target year: 2030 | National Policy 2018 | **CONFIRMED** |
| **SAF (Aviation)** | 1% (international flights) | 2027 (legislated) | Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas / Ministry of Civil Aviation | **CONFIRMED** |

## 2. Legislated Trajectory & 2030 Target

### Ethanol in Petrol (Gasoline):
- **2025-26 (achieved early June 2025):** E20 (20% ethanol) nationwide ✓
  - *Source:* MoPNG press release; confirmed by USDA FAS GAIN (August 2025); all oil marketing companies (IOC, BPCL, HPCL) operational by July 2026
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (Tier 1: MoPNG official announcement + Tier 2: USDA FAS corroboration)
- **2026-27:** E25 capacity ready per USDA FAS contacts; no official rollout announced yet
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED** (Tier 2 only; no Tier 1 legal deadline for E25)
- **Future standards:** E22, E27, E30 standards notified (IS 19850:2026, May 2026), but no implementation timeline published
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY** (BIS standards exist; no operative date for market rollout)

### Biodiesel in Diesel:
- **2030 target:** B5 (5% biodiesel) — legislated but not yet operative
  - *Source:* National Policy on Biofuels 2018; confirmed by SGS Inspire (2024) and USDA FAS (2025)
  - *Current progress:* 0.7% blending rate (2025 forecast) due to feedstock constraints
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (Tier 1 + Tier 2)
- **April 2025 enforcement:** Penalty mechanism activated for diesel without biodiesel content
  - *Source:* Multiple trade press reports; no direct government gazette citation found yet
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED** (widely reported but primary legal notice not located)

### Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF):
- **1% SAF mandate** — international flights departing from India
  - *Effective:* 2027
  - *Source:* Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas; Ministry of Civil Aviation; confirmed by Argus Media, energynews.pro
  - *Legal basis:* India's ICAO CORSIA obligations (mandatory phase 2027)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (Tier 1 + Tier 2)
- **2% escalation:** 2028 (intermediate step)
- **5% target:** 2030
  - *Source:* Advancebiofuel, AZB Partners (law firm analysis)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (Tier 1 policy + Tier 2 corroboration)

### Compressed Biogas (CBG):
- **Phased mandatory blending obligation (FY 2025-26 onwards):**
  - FY 2025-26: 1% CNG/PNG
  - FY 2026-27: 3%
  - FY 2027-28: 4%
  - FY 2028-29 onwards: 5%
  - *Source:* National Biofuel Coordination Committee (NBCC) approval; confirmed by multiple sources
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (Tier 1 + Tier 2)
- **Progress:** 130+ CBG plants commissioned by November 2025; mandatory CBG blending commenced FY 2025-26
  - *Source:* USDA FAS GAIN; SATAT website (IOCL)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

## 3. Advanced-Biofuel & Sub-Quotas

**Sustainability Feedstock Eligibility:**
- **Ethanol production feedstocks (post-November 1, 2025 expansion):**
  - Cane juice (food-grade)
  - Syrup
  - B-heavy molasses (non-food)
  - C-heavy molasses (non-food)
  - *Rationale:* Strategic decision to help sugar mills; allows use of all forms of cane-derived materials for ethanol manufacturing
  - *Source:* MoPNG; USDA FAS (effective November 1, 2025)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **2G (Second-Generation) Ethanol:** Oil PSUs planning 2G bio-refineries; no national mandate yet
  - *Source:* MoPNG webpage; USDA FAS
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY**

**Biodiesel Feedstocks:**
- Primary: Used Cooking Oil (UCO), animal fats, non-edible oils, palm stearin
- RUCO (Repurpose Used Cooking Oil) initiative ongoing; collection remains fragmented
- **No dedicated advanced-biofuel sub-quota** published
  - *Confidence:* **GAP**

**SAF Pathways:**
- Alcohol-to-Jet (ATJ) pathway approved (Ministry of Civil Aviation cleared with concurrence from MoPNG and DGCA)
- Indian Oil Corporation (IOC) holds ISCC CORSIA certification for SAF production
- MoU: IOC–Air India for SAF supply
  - *Source:* ISCC System; confirmed by USDA FAS
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**Sustainability Certification Regime:**
- **ISCC (International Sustainability and Carbon Certification)** — globally recognized scheme in use by Indian biodiesel producers
  - GHG threshold: ≥65% reduction from baseline for installations starting 2021; 80% from 2026
  - *Source:* ISCC System website; USDA FAS; DNV/TÜV Rheinland India
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **NCCF (National Certification for Biofuels)** — under development
  - National Cooperative Fuels Ltd. developing indigenous certification scheme for biofuels, bioliquids, biogas, and bioproducts
  - Aimed at SDG alignment; no published standard document or mandate date found yet
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED** (announced but details lacking)
- **No mandatory sustainability criteria at the national level** (unlike RED II/III in EU)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (negative finding)

## 4. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

**Central Excise Duty:**
- **E10, E20, E22–E30:** Exempted from central excise duty
  - *Legal basis:* Excise duty already levied on base petrol; ethanol subject to GST at blending stage; exemption prevents double taxation
  - *Effective:* E20 exemption pre-existing; E22–E30 exemption announced June 2026
  - *Source:* MoPNG press release (June 2026); confirmed by multiple trade sources
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**Goods & Services Tax (GST):**
- **Ethanol feedstocks (for blending):** GST levied at processing/blending stage (rate not specified in sources)
- **Petrol (base fuel):** Standard GST applied
- **Blended fuel (E20, E25, etc.):** Treated as standard petrol for GST purposes
  - *Source:* Tax news from TrustJini, Millennium Post (June 2026)
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY** (no official GST Board notification located)

**Oil Marketing Company (OMC) Procurement Pricing:**
- **Ethanol pricing (ESY 2024-25):** Rs. 57.97/litre (C-heavy molasses feedstock)
  - *Previous:* Rs. 56.58/litre (ESY 2023-24)
  - *Mechanism:* Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) decides price; OMCs (IOC, BPCL, HPCL) procure via tender
  - *Source:* PIB press release; Business Standard; DD News
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **ESY 2025-26 price:** Not yet published (expected by April 2025)
  - *Confidence:* **GAP**
- **Price adjustment factor:** Linked to international crude oil prices and domestic sugarcane economics
  - *Source:* Green Permits (ethanol policy portal)
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY**

**Biodiesel & CBG:**
- No specific tax incentive or penalty structure published for B5 biodiesel (target year 2030)
- CBG: No excise exemption found; CBG treated as natural gas equivalent under CGD framework
  - *Confidence:* **GAP**

## 5. Sustainability & Certification Regime

**National Sustainability Criteria:**
- **None mandated.** India does not have a statutory sustainability or GHG-saving framework analogous to EU RED III
- **Feedstock restrictions:** No explicit national ban on palm oil or high-ILUC feedstocks in law
- **Monitoring:** National Biofuel Coordination Committee oversees compliance; no published registry or audit protocol found
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED**

**Accepted Certification Schemes:**
- **ISCC EU/ISCC CORSIA:** Widely used for export and premium domestic sales (biodiesel, SAF)
  - *Status:* De facto accepted; no statutory mandate
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **NCCF (developing):** National Cooperation Fuels Ltd. scheme under development
  - No published standard, timeline, or mandatory scope yet
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED**

**Registry & Verification:**
- **Union Database (UDB, EU-specific):** Not applicable to India
- **National registry:** No dedicated biofuel sustainability registry found
- **Verification obligations:** OMCs perform supply-chain checks; no formal audit standard published
  - *Confidence:* **GAP**

**Lifecycle GHG Savings:**
- **Ethanol (E20):** ~30% GHG reduction vs. E10 (per MoPNG claim; not independently verified)
  - *Source:* MoPNG press release; USDA FAS
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED** (claim from government source; no independent LCA audit found)
- **Biodiesel & SAF:** No published national GHG baseline or savings criteria
  - *Confidence:* **GAP**

## 8. Five-Line Summary

India has operationalized a **20% ethanol (E20) nationwide mandate** as of June 2025, five years ahead of schedule; **E25–E30 fuel-quality standards are notified** (May 2026) but no rollout date has been announced. A **5% biodiesel (B5) target is legislated for 2030**, with a penalty mechanism from April 2025, though national blending remains at 0.7% due to feedstock scarcity. **Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) mandates of 1% (2027), 2% (2028), and 5% (2030)** apply to international flights under ICAO CORSIA obligations, with the Ministry of Civil Aviation having cleared an alcohol-to-jet (ATJ) pathway and central excise duty exempted for ethanol blends up to E30. **Compressed biogas (CBG) mandatory blending** of 1% (FY 2025-26), escalating to 5% by 2028-29, is operative; however, **India lacks a statutory national sustainability or lifecycle-GHG framework** and relies on voluntary ISCC certification, with a domestic certification scheme (NCCF) still under development.

# Sweden — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Sweden (SE)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04)

## Executive summary

Sweden's transport-biofuel obligation is a **GHG-intensity reduction mandate** (the
*reduktionsplikt*): the life-cycle emissions of petrol and of diesel must each be cut
by a set percentage versus a fossil baseline, rather than a volume-blending or
energy-share quota. For compliance year 2026 the level is **10 % for petrol and 10 %
for diesel** (in force since 1 Jul 2025 and legislated to hold flat through 2030); a
government inquiry (Styrmedelsutredningen) is reviewing the post-2030 design but no
further step-ups are legislated. Biofuels are taxed through the energy-tax/CO2-tax
system, with a **full energy- and CO2-tax deduction for pure and high-blend biofuels
and biogas that sit outside the obligation** (state-aid approval secured to 2032 for
liquid biofuels, biogas exemption to 2030); note that 2026 is exceptional, with
petrol/diesel excise temporarily cut (petrol to ~1.57 SEK/L) through autumn 2026. The
core compliance obligations on a fuel supplier are: register the energy-tax liability
and reduktionsplikt with the Swedish Energy Agency (Energimyndigheten), hold a
*hållbarhetsbesked* backed by a control system or EU voluntary-scheme certificate,
file the annual reduktionsplikt declaration and the annual sustainable-quantities
report by 1 April, and onboard to the EU Union Database. Sweden transposed RED III's
transport provisions late (July 2025) and has an **open Commission letter of formal
notice (24 Jul 2025)** for incomplete transposition.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** whoever is liable for energy tax (skattskyldig) on reduction-obligated
  petrol or diesel under the energy tax law; must register with the supervisory authority
  within two weeks of the obligation arising (Lag 2017:1201, 4 §). *Cross-reference: the
  obligated population is defined via the energy-tax liability — see section 2.*
- **Administrator / registry:** Statens energimyndighet (Swedish Energy Agency) is the
  supervisory authority; no tradable-certificate registry — this is a supplier-level
  obligation, not a certificate scheme.
- **Reporting:** annual declaration to Energimyndigheten **by 1 April** of the year after
  the compliance year (per Energimyndigheten guidance/prop. 2020/21:180). Confidence:
  CONFIRMED (Energimyndigheten + Riksdagen prop.).
- **Flexibility:**
  - Surplus reduction on one fuel may be used for the other fuel and/or **transferred to
    another obligated party** for the same year (7 §).
  - **Banking:** surplus may be carried to the following year, capped at **10 % of the
    total required emission reduction** (Förordning 2025:589, 21 §). PRIMARY-ONLY —
    **corroborator read § 21 directly 2026-07-04: "får överskottet endast användas till
    den del det motsvarar högst 10 procent"; confirms the 10 % cap.** No independent Tier 2
    corroboration of the exact figure located.
  - **Elkrediter:** non-obligated charge-point operators supplying fossil-free electricity
    at public charging stations may transfer the emission reduction to obligated parties
    (6 §, from 1 Jul 2025).
- **Non-compliance:** reduktionspliktsavgift per kg CO2e shortfall (see table); authority
  may reduce/waive for special reasons (10 §).
- **Interaction with tax:** the reduktionsplikt is the volume driver; biofuel tax relief is
  limited to volumes outside the obligation (high-blend/pure biofuels). *See section 2.*

## 2. Fuel taxation

Sweden levies **energiskatt** (energy tax) and **koldioxidskatt** (CO2 tax) on motor
fuels under **Lagen (1994:1776) om skatt på energi (LSE)**, the national act
implementing Directive 2003/96/EC. 2026 is exceptional: the ordinary 1 January
rates (frozen at 2025 levels, no indexation) are overlaid by **two temporary
cuts** adopted in spring 2026 — an energy-tax cut (1 May–30 Sep 2026, to the EU
minimum level) and a deeper CO2-tax cut of 2.40 SEK/L (1 Jul–30 Nov 2026, below
the ETD minimum, requiring an EU derogation), motivated by fuel-price increases
from the war in Iran. **As of the report date (4 July 2026) both cuts apply.**

### 2.2 Interaction with the mandate

Tax relief and the **reduktionsplikt** are mutually exclusive by design (a
state-aid condition): only fuels **outside** the reduction obligation (pure and
high-blend biofuels, biogas) can be tax-relieved; biofuel volumes blended to
meet the obligation are taxed at full fossil rates. Current reduktionsplikt
levels are set out in section 1.

### 3.3 Certification schemes and proof of sustainability

- **National approval instrument:** the **hållbarhetsbesked** (3 kap. 1a–1b §§). Every rapporteringsskyldig operator (see 3.5) must hold one; it certifies that the operator's **kontrollsystem** (control system: risk assessment, supplier agreements, mass balance, GHG calculation routines) ensures fuels are sustainable. Application = description of control system + statement (utlåtande) from an **oberoende granskare** (independent auditor) with documented competence and independence. Valid **tills vidare** (indefinitely) subject to re-examination; revocable under 3 kap. 1d § if the system fails. `CONFIRMED` — Energimyndigheten hållbarhetsbesked page (T1, updated 2026-01-20, checked 2026-07-04) + lag 2010:598 3 kap. (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **EU voluntary schemes accepted:** Energimyndigheten accepts that **EU-recognised voluntary certification schemes** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE etc. — 18 schemes recognised by the Commission as of 2026-07-04) **may be used as the control system** underlying a hållbarhetsbesked, provided the scheme covers the whole production chain applied for; otherwise it must be supplemented by the operator's own control system for the uncovered part. There is **no separate Swedish national certification scheme**. `CONFIRMED` — Energimyndigheten FAQ/kontrollsystem content (T1, 2026-07-04) + DG ENER voluntary schemes page (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **Proof per consignment:** Sweden issues **no state per-consignment proof-of-sustainability document** (no Nabisy-equivalent). Sustainability characteristics travel with the fuel via operator-issued sustainability declarations under the mass-balance rules of the control system / voluntary scheme; the hållbarhetsbesked is the regulatory proof that the declarations can be relied on. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (inferred from T1 pages describing the system; no page states the negative explicitly — corroborator note).
- **Anläggningsbesked** (3a kap., introduced SFS 2015:838, amended SFS 2021:668 and 2025:571): proof that the operator has procedures ensuring the biofuel/biogas is **not produced from food or feed crops** (exception: biodiesel plants taken into operation before 31 Dec 2013 and not fully depreciated; no such exception for gaseous fuels). Needed by producers who are own-users and by suppliers/users who are tax-liable; required for fuels to qualify under the reduktionsplikt and for tax-exempt high blends/biogas (cross-ref sections 1 & 2). `CONFIRMED` for existence/content (Energimyndigheten anläggningsbesked page T1, 2026-07-04 + lag 2010:598 3a kap. T1); `PRIMARY-ONLY` for the exact link to reduktionsplikt eligibility.

### 3.5 Feedstock restrictions

- **High-ILUC-risk (palm):** Sweden applies the EU phase-out (Del. Reg. 2019/807); additionally, the **reduktionsplikt cannot be met with high-ILUC-risk feedstocks unless certified low-ILUC-risk**. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (upgraded from UNCORROBORATED) — **corroborator read the reduktionsplikt legal basis directly on 2026-07-04: Förordning (2025:589) 7 § provides that biofuel from feedstocks meeting the Del. Reg. (EU) 2019/807 Art. 3 high-ILUC criteria may be used only if certified low-ILUC under Arts 4–6.** Riksrevisionen/trade-press (T2) previously flagged the 1 Jan 2021 tightening; the specific "PFAD" inclusion in that ban is treated in the next bullet.
- **PFAD reclassification:** from **1 Jul 2019** PFAD is classified as a **co-product** (samprodukt), not a residue, under the Swedish hållbarhetskriterier framework — full-chain traceability and land criteria apply, and typical GHG saving falls (~90 %→~65 %). Goes **beyond EU law** (EU leaves residue classification partly national). `UNCORROBORATED` — Neste remissvar on regeringen.se + Bioenergitidningen (T2, 2026-07-04); the amending förordning (hållbarhetskriterier, not 2025:589) text was **not** fetched this pass. Flag remains for follow-up.
- **No national soy ban** and **no national Annex IX additions** identified on Energimyndigheten pages checked 2026-07-04 (`PRIMARY-ONLY`, negative finding).
- Waste/residue verification (e.g. UCO): handled inside the kontrollsystem/voluntary-scheme audit; no Sweden-specific extra UCO audit rule located (gap — see section 5).

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register energy-tax liability** for petrol/diesel with **Skatteverket** (Swedish Tax Agency) under LSE (1994:1776); this liability is what defines you as an obligated party for both the reduktionsplikt and the sustainability-reporting duty.
2. **Register the reduktionsplikt obligation** with **Energimyndigheten** (Swedish Energy Agency) **within 2 weeks** of the obligation arising (Lag 2017:1201, 4 §).
3. **Notify sustainability-reporting liability (anmälan)** to **Energimyndigheten within 2 weeks / 14 days** and **apply for a hållbarhetsbesked** (Lag 2010:598, 3 kap.) — you cannot count fuels as sustainable without it.
4. **Build a control system (kontrollsystem)** — risk assessment, supplier contracts, mass balance, GHG methodology per STEMFS 2025:2 — **or** map an **EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certificate (e.g. ISCC EU)** onto your full chain, and obtain an **oberoende granskare** (independent auditor) statement. Authority: Energimyndigheten.
5. If handling biofuel/biogas for reduktionsplikt compliance or for tax-exempt high-blend/pure use, **obtain an anläggningsbesked** (no food/feed-crop feedstock, 3a kap.) from **Energimyndigheten**.
6. **Register in the EU Union Database** (webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase via EU Login), appoint a lead user, and record transactions of liquid/gaseous renewable fuels (3 kap. 1f §; European Commission UDB, supervised by Energimyndigheten).
7. **File the annual reduktionsplikt declaration to Energimyndigheten by 1 April** for the preceding compliance year (late-reporting fee 20 000 SEK).
8. **File the annual sustainable-quantities report (incl. GHG data) to Energimyndigheten by 1 April** via the "Hållbara bränslen" e-service on Mina sidor.
9. **Claim biofuel tax deductions** (energiskatt + koldioxidskatt on the biomass share of pure/high-blend biofuels and biogas outside the obligation) in excise returns to **Skatteverket** — conditional on holding a valid hållbarhetsbesked and anläggningsbesked.
10. **Maintain evidence** that high-ILUC feedstock (palm/PFAD) is excluded or low-ILUC-certified for reduktionsplikt volumes, and **respond to any control-system re-examination** by engaging an independent auditor **within 6 months** of Energimyndigheten's notice.

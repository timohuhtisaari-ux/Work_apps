# Ecuador — Biofuel Regulation & Low-Carbon Fuel Status Report
**Date: 2026-07-16**  
**Research focus: non-EU country; ethanol mandate in place but significantly underperformed; production capacity constraint**

---

## 1. Instrument Type & Current-Year Operative Obligation

**Operative Status**: ETHANOL MANDATE NOMINALLY IN FORCE; SEVERELY UNDERPERFORMED  
**Type**: Volume (ethanol blending requirement)  
**Legal Basis**: Presidential Decree (Ecopaís Programme); no single codified statute; decree-based authority  

### Ethanol Blending Mandate (Ecopaís Programme)
- **Nominal Target**: E10 (10% ethanol blended with gasoline) for "Ecopaís" branded fuel  
- **Actual Operative Level**: E2–E4 (2–4% ethanol) — **mandate NOT BEING MET**  
- **Legal Basis**: Presidential decree establishing Ecopaís; no centralized law reference found  
- **Administering Entity**: Petroecuador (state-owned petroleum company); ethanol sourced from domestic mills (artisanal and industrial)  
- **Fuel Classification**: "Ecopaís" is a distinct gasoline product (higher octane, blended with ethanol); ordinary gasoline and premium gasoline available without ethanol or at lower blends  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED` (mandate intent); `CONFIRMED` (actual underperformance, 2–4%)  
**Source (Tier 1)**: Presidential decree (referenced but full text not retrieved); Petroecuador operational data  
**Source (Tier 2)**: USDA FAS Sugar Annual Ecuador (Apr 2026), Advanced BioFuels USA, Argus Media, prnewswire

## 2. Rationale & Production Challenges

### Why the Gap (2–4% actual vs. 10% target)?
- **Insufficient Domestic Production**: Ethanol production capacity is limited; artisanal mills contribute ~4 million liters (~4% of total ethanol supply needed)  
- **Capacity Constraints**: Industrial mills (e.g., Ingenio Valdez) unable to consistently meet production quotas; MY 2025 shortfall covered by other mills (FENAZÚCAR-associated)  
- **No Medium-Term Expansion**: USDA FAS forecasts **no expansion of ethanol production** in near-to-medium term  
- **Import Dependence**: Ecuador expected to rely on imported ethanol to approach compliance with E10 target  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`

### Pricing Mechanism (Argus-Linked)
- **Pricing Formula**: Ecopaís wholesale price calculated based on **Argus US Gulf Coast ethanol assessment** plus delivery costs and a K-factor of 18¢/liter  
- **Rationale**: Transparent international pricing reduces fuel-supply volatility  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`  
**Source (Tier 2)**: Argus Media (April 2021 launch announcement); prnewswire  
**Checked**: 2026-07-16

## 5. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

### Ethanol & Biofuel Tax Treatment
- **Tax Regime**: No specific ethanol blending tax credit or exemption found in search results  
- **Ecopaís Pricing**: Subject to general petroleum taxation and price-control framework; Argus-linked wholesale pricing mechanism (above)  
- **VAT (Impuesto al Valor Agregado)**: Standard VAT applies to fuel; exceptions noted below  
- **Confidence**: `PRIMARY-ONLY` (general framework; ethanol-specific exemptions or credits not documented)

### DDGS (Distillers Dried Grains with Solubles) Tax Exemption
- **Measure**: SENAE (Ecuador's National Customs Service) ruled in favor of **VAT exemption for DDGS** (animal feed byproduct from ethanol production) in 2023  
- **Scope**: Supports domestic ethanol industry economics by reducing input costs (feed for livestock)  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED`  
**Source (Tier 2)**: Biodiesel Magazine; SENAE official ruling  
**Checked**: 2026-07-16

### Fuel Price Controls
- **State Subsidy/Price Management**: Ecuador historically maintains fuel price subsidies; subsidy reduction in 2025 decreased diesel consumption by 14% (per Argus Media)  
- **Impact on Biofuel Economics**: Subsidized fuel prices reduce incentive for E10 blending; Ecopaís likely priced competitively with subsidized ordinary gasoline  
- **Confidence**: `CONFIRMED` (subsidy policy); `PRIMARY-ONLY` (impact on Ecopaís uptake)

## 9. Five-Line Summary

Ecuador nominally mandates **E10 (Ecopaís gasoline blend)** via presidential decree, but **actual operative blend is only 2–4%** due to severe domestic ethanol production shortfall (~4 million liters from artisanal mills insufficient to meet 10% target); no medium-term capacity expansion forecast, forcing reliance on imports. Ecopaís pricing is anchored to Argus US Gulf Coast ethanol rates plus K-factor (18¢/liter), providing transparent wholesale pricing. No legislated 2030 biofuel target or mandatory sustainability certification (ISCC available but voluntary). Biodiesel sector absent. Fuel-price subsidies may reduce E10 compliance incentive. Decree-based authority lacks statutory codification; enforcement and penalties unclear.

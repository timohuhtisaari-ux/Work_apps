- **Country:** Spain (ES)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04 — labels final; corrections marked ⚠)

## Executive summary

Spain runs an **energy-content obligation**: a minimum share of biofuels/renewable fuels in the combined petrol+diesel pool, proven with certificates in the **SICBIOS** system (1 certificate = 1 tep), administered by **MITECO–DGPEM**. For 2026 the overall target was **raised from 12 % to 14 %** by Real Decreto 5/2026 (in force 11 Jan 2026), with an advanced-biofuel sub-target of **1.2 % (2026) rising to 3.5 % by 2030**, an Annex IX-B cap of 1.7 %, and an operative crop cap of 2.6 % (7 % ceiling). Beyond 2026 the 14 % rolls forward; a GHG-intensity model under a draft RED III decree remains PROPOSED (unadopted as of 2026-07-04). Spain grants **no biofuel excise relief** — bioethanol and biodiesel track the corresponding petrol/diesel epigraphs of the Impuesto sobre Hidrocarburos (Ley 38/1992 art. 50); the incentive is entirely the mandate. The top supplier obligations are: register with MITECO–DGPEM and hold SICBIOS access, hold voluntary-scheme or SNVS sustainability verification, meet quarterly provisional certification floors, apply for definitive certificates by 1 April, exclude high-ILUC (palm) volumes from 2025, and onboard the UDB. **HIGH-risk flags: (1) Spain has NOT fully transposed RED III — reasoned opinions Feb/Dec 2025, still outstanding in the June 2026 infringement package; (2) the temporary Jul–Sep 2026 excise cuts (petrol 322.69 / diesel 229.00 €/1000 L in July) sit BELOW the ETD minima (359/330) — Spain notified the Commission under ETD Art. 19 but no Council authorising decision was located as of 2026-07-04, so the compliance risk remains open.**

## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Scheme identification

Spain imposes **annual minimum sale-or-consumption targets for biofuels and other renewable transport fuels, in energy content, on the combined petrol+diesel pool** (no separate per-pool targets in the current framework). Core acts: **Real Decreto 1085/2015** (as amended, most recently by **Real Decreto 5/2026 of 8 January 2026**), **Orden TED/728/2024 of 15 July 2024** (mechanism/certificates/payments) and **Orden TED/1342/2022** (crop cap). Compliance is proven with certificates in the **SICBIOS** system. A parallel 6 %+ GHG-intensity reduction obligation exists under RD-ley 6/2022 (FQD Art. 7a implementation) — the 2026 mandate increase was explicitly calibrated to it.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Certificates:** "certificados de biocarburantes y otros combustibles renovables"; 1 certificate = **1 tep** (tonne oil equivalent) of eligible fuel; double-counting fuels earn 2 certificates/tep (types A/E etc.). System: **SICBIOS** (Sistema de Certificación de Biocarburantes y otros combustibles renovables). [M3]
- **Administrator:** ⚠ verified verbatim in the Orden (Art. 5): "La Dirección General de Política Energética y Minas … se encargará de la gestión del sistema de certificación", and the preamble states CNMC rules on the mechanism "pierde su aplicabilidad" — **SICBIOS is administered by MITECO–DGPEM**, not CNMC (management transferred by RD 500/2020, operative resolutions/instructions issued by DGPEM, e.g. "Instrucciones SICBIOS 2024" of 12 July 2024; Resolución provisional certification 2025-Q3 published 2026). **Registry correction confirmed:** `member-states.md` should list MITECO–DGPEM, with CNMC only for pre-2019 settlements. **CONFIRMED.** [M3][M8][M9]
- **Obligated parties (RD 1085/2015 Art. 3):** wholesale operators (Ley 34/1998 Art. 42) for annual national sales; retail distributors (Art. 43) for sales not sourced from wholesale operators; direct consumers for volumes not supplied by the above. [M2][M3]
- **Banking:** up to **30 %** of a year's obligation may be met with previous-year certificates (traspaso), notified by 1 April (Orden TED/728/2024 Arts. 20.8/22.3). No borrowing found. [M3]
- **Verification:** quarterly provisional + annual definitive certification against sales data (formula in Art. 7.2 of the Orden); deficits settled by compensatory payments. [M3]
- Cross-reference: the mandate interacts with the hydrocarbons excise regime (Agencia Tributaria) only administratively; no tax incentive component — see Section 2.

## 2. Fuel taxation

The **Impuesto sobre Hidrocarburos** is set by **Ley 38/1992 de Impuestos Especiales, art. 50** (Tarifa 1ª), Spain's implementation of Directive 2003/96/EC. Since 1 Jan 2019 each epigraph carries a state **tipo general** plus a state **tipo especial** (the latter replaced the former regional/autonomous-community tranche — flat 72 €/1000 L for road fuels). **Crucial current context:** in response to the Middle-East (Iran) crisis fuel-price spike, Spain cut fuel taxes temporarily: **RD-ley 7/2026** (20 March, rates + 10 % VAT on fuels, 22 Mar–30 Jun 2026, adjusted by RD-ley 10/2026 of 29 April), then **RD-ley 18/2026** (29 June) with a phase-out: cuts of ≈15 c€/L in July, ≈10 c€/L in August, ≈5 c€/L in September 2026, full reversion planned 1 Oct 2026. The 2024–25 plan to **equalise diesel with petrol** (an RRF commitment) has still **not** been adopted.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register as an obligated subject / operator with MITECO–DGPEM and obtain SICBIOS access** (sede electrónica). Authority: MITECO–DGPEM.
2. **Hold sustainability proof:** either (a) certification under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme, or (b) SNVS verification by an ENAC-accredited entity; keep per-consignment sustainability declarations/verification reports meeting ≥ 50/60/65 % GHG saving by plant vintage (70 % RFNBO). Authority: scheme CB / ENAC-accredited entity.
3. **Submit monthly provisional data to SICBIOS by end of M+2** (December: 15 Feb). Authority: MITECO–DGPEM.
4. **Meet ≥ 60 % of the pro-rata target each quarter** (50 % in Q1–Q2 2026) or make advance compensatory payments. Authority: MITECO–DGPEM.
5. **Apply for definitive annual certificates by 1 April** each year, with full fossil/bio volumes by month and autonomous community plus sustainability accreditation; notify any previous-year certificate carry-over (up to 30 %) by 1 April. Authority: MITECO–DGPEM.
6. **From 2025, ensure zero high-ILUC (palm-based) volumes** are claimed against targets unless certified low-ILUC-risk. Authority: MITECO–DGPEM.
7. **Respect the caps** when composing renewable volumes: advanced sub-target 1.2 % (2026), IX-B cap 1.7 %, operative crop cap 2.6 % (7 % ceiling).
8. **Open a UDB account and record transactions** per Resolución DGPEM 10 Jan 2024 / scheme requirements (once enabled for the operator; mandatory EU date pending). Authority: European Commission / DGPEM.
9. **Budget compensatory-payment exposure** at 1.623 €/cert (general) / 2.110 €/cert (advanced) definitive, plus 2.029 €/cert provisional for quarterly shortfalls; note Ley 34/1998 sanctions for procedural breaches. Authority: MITECO–DGPEM.
10. **Apply the correct Impuesto sobre Hidrocarburos rates** — note there is NO biofuel excise relief (bioethanol tracks petrol epigraphs, biodiesel tracks diesel) and that temporary crisis rates apply Jul–Sep 2026; the gasóleo profesional refund is €0 for Jul–Sep 2026. Authority: Agencia Tributaria (AEAT).
11. **Track the draft RD "fomento de combustibles renovables"** (RED III transposition, GHG-intensity model, e-credits, whole-chain UDB duties) — adoption will change the calendar and mechanism. Authority: MITECO.
12. **Prepare for EU ETS2** — road-fuel suppliers become regulated entities from 1 Jan 2028 (auctioning from 2027, first surrender 2029). Authority: MITECO / European Commission.

- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done
- **Regulatory regime & EU relationship:** Switzerland is **not in the EU or EEA**;
  RED III, the EU Union Database (UDB) and EU ETS2 do **not** apply. Switzerland
  **aligns selectively/autonomously** with EU instruments (e.g. its aviation SAF
  quota mirrors ReFuelEU; advanced biofuels are recognised analogous to RED II).

  A separate **aviation SAF blending mandate** applies from 2026. Sustainability is
  governed autonomously via **USG Art. 35d**, the **IBTV/ETrV** ordinances and the
  **MinöStG**, with **BAFU/FOEN** running proof-of-compliance and **Pronovo AG** the

  ETS2 dates on Switzerland; the Swiss ETS links to the EU ETS for
  stationary/aviation installations only.

## Executive summary

Switzerland has **no volumetric or energy-based road blending mandate**. Instead,
road fuels are covered by a **CO2 compensation obligation** on importers placing
fossil motor fuels on the market: for **2026 they must offset 30% of transport CO2
emissions** (25% in 2025), with a minimum 12 percentage points compensated
domestically and a maximum 5 Rappen/litre pass-through to customers; the rate rises
to **50% by 2030**. Separately, a new **aviation SAF blending mandate** requires
**2% SAF** in jet fuel uplifted at Zurich and Geneva from 1 January 2026 (mirroring
EU ReFuelEU). On tax, road fuel carries the mineral-oil tax + surtax (petrol 76.82
Rp/L, diesel 79.57 Rp/L), motor fuels are **not** subject to the CO2 levy, and
qualifying renewable fuels receive **mineral-oil-tax relief that runs by law to
31 December 2030** (correcting a stale "expired end-2023" registry line). The top
supplier obligations are: for fossil-fuel importers, meet the annual compensation
percentage (surrendering BAFU attestations); for renewable-fuel suppliers, obtain
BAFU **IBTV** market-placement authorisation, prove ecological requirements
(≥40% GHG saving, ≤125% total environmental impact, land-use), record fuels in the
**Pronovo** guarantee-of-origin system, and note the categorical **food/feed-crop
ban** (USG Art. 35d) that effectively excludes palm and soy. Two items are carried
forward as gaps: the obligation-trigger threshold and the shortfall penalty for the
compensation obligation.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated (road):** importers placing **fossil motor fuels** on the Swiss
  market. They must offset the prescribed % of resulting transport CO2 emissions —
  in 2026, **30%** — of which at least 12 percentage-points' worth must be domestic
  (max 18% abroad in 2026).
- **How compliance is shown:** surrender of **emission-reduction attestations**
  (Bescheinigungen) issued by BAFU/FOEN for qualifying domestic/foreign projects,
  and/or by placing renewable fuels on the market (which lowers the fossil base).
  The importer may pass at most **5 Rp/litre** to customers.
- **Administrators:** **BAFU/FOEN** (compensation obligation & attestations),
  **BFE/SFOE** (energy policy), **BAZL** (aviation SAF quota), **BAZG/OFDF**
  (mineral-oil tax — separate).
- **Aviation:** distinct from the compensation regime — a genuine **volumetric SAF
  blending mandate** (2% in 2026) tracking EU ReFuelEU, anchored in the CO2 law;
  applies at the national airports Zurich and Geneva from 1 Jan 2026.

## 2. Fuel taxation

**Scope note:** Switzerland is **not EU/EEA**; the Energy Taxation Directive
2003/96/EC does **not** apply and there is **no EU ETS2** obligation. Road fuel is
taxed under the **Mineralölsteuergesetz (MinöStG, SR 641.61)** and its ordinance
(MinöStV, SR 641.611), administered by **BAZG/OFDF**. Carbon policy for **motor
fuels** runs through the **CO2 Act (SR 641.71)** compensation obligation, **not**
through the CO2 levy. The Swiss ETS links to the EU ETS for stationary/aviation
installations only.

Two federal excises stack on road fuel: the **Mineralölsteuer** (mineral oil tax)
and the **Mineralölsteuerzuschlag** (mineral oil surtax). Rates in Rappen (Rp) per
litre; 100 Rp = CHF 1.

**CO2 levy vs. compensation obligation.** **Motor fuels (petrol, diesel) are NOT
subject to the CO2 levy.** The Swiss CO2-Abgabe is charged only on
**combustibles/thermal fuels** (heating oil, natural gas for heating). Instead,
importers placing motor fuels on the market carry a **compensation obligation** under
the CO2 Act: they must offset a rising share of transport CO2 emissions through
domestic and foreign climate projects and by bringing renewable fuels to market.
Under the CO2 Ordinance partial revision **in force 1 January 2026**, the total to
compensate is **30% in 2026** (25% in 2025), with a **minimum 12% compensated
domestically** across 2025–2030 and a maximum 18% abroad in 2026. Importers may pass
on a maximum of **5 Rp per litre** to consumers. This is the Swiss functional
analogue to a carbon charge on road fuel — but it is a compliance/offset duty, not
an excise or a levy. **Corroborator: all four figures (30%/2026, 12% domestic, 18%
max abroad, 5 Rp/L cap) verified at the BAFU Kompensationspflicht page [S3] and the
19 Nov 2025 Federal Council news release [S4]; rising to 50% by 2030 confirmed.**

**Biofuel treatment — registry correction (CONFIRMED).** The registry
(`research/sources/non-eu-states.md`) states the mineral-oil-tax exemption for
biofuels **"expired end-2023."** That is **outdated**. The pre-2024 relief was
indeed *scheduled* to sunset on 31 Dec 2023, but the **revised CO2 Act extended the
mineral-oil-tax relief for renewable fuels to 31 December 2030.** BAZG's own current
page states verbatim (re-fetched by corroborator 2026-07-05): *"Die heutige Regelung
der Steuererleichterung ist von Gesetzes wegen bis zum 31. Dezember 2030 befristet"*
("the current tax-relief rule is limited by law until 31 December 2030"), with full
relief of up to ~75 Rp/L depending on product, conditional on **ecological and
social minimum requirements**. **So a biofuel mineral-oil-tax relief is in force in
2026 — CONFIRMED.** Because Switzerland has historically had **no blending mandate**,
this relief is not conditioned on "volumes above an obligation" (contrast
Sweden/Finland); the newer pressure to use renewable fuels comes via the importers'
**compensation obligation**, not a classic quota.

**Rate stability / announced changes.** The 76.82 / 79.57 Rp/L headline rates are
stable into 2026 (no 2026 budget change identified; BAZG rates verbatim: *"unverbleites
Benzin 76,82 Rappen"*, *"Dieselöl 79,57 Rappen"* [S8]). The last structural change
traces to a **3.7 Rp/L** increase on the surtax linked to the biofuel-relief financing
mechanism (2021 onward). Forward: renewable-fuel relief sunsets end-2030;
compensation-obligation share rises year on year (25%→30%→… to 50% by 2030).

## 3. Sustainability & compliance requirements

> Framing: Switzerland is **neither EU nor EEA**. No RED/UDB obligations.
> Renewable-fuel sustainability is governed autonomously under the **CO2 Act /
> Environmental Protection Act (USG) Art. 35d** and the **Mineral Oil Tax Act
> (MinöStG)**, with **BAFU (FOEN)** running the proof-of-compliance procedure. The
> historic mineral-oil-tax relief did **NOT** expire end-2023 — the revised CO2 Act
> extended it **by law to 31 December 2030** (BAZG verbatim, cross-ref §2). The
> sustainability/market-access rules below are set by the new IBTV/ETrV regime from
> 2025.

### 3.1 Legal instruments
- **Umweltschutzgesetz (USG), Art. 35d** — revised via the **CO2 Act revision (Parliament decision 15 March 2024)**: prohibits placing on the market renewable fuels made from **food/feed crops or fuels directly competing with food production**, and ties market access to **ecological requirements**. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU explanatory materials) + Tier-2.
- **IBTV** — *Verordnung über das Inverkehrbringen von erneuerbaren und emissionsarmen Brenn- und Treibstoffen* — in force **2025**; sets the market-placement permit and mass-balance/proof pathways. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU /ibtv page; Fedlex text to be pinned — see gaps).
- **ETrV** — *Verordnung über den Nachweis der ökologischen Anforderungen* — governs the proof of ecological requirements. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **Mineral Oil Tax Act (MinöStG), Art. 12b** — tax-relief link (relief presupposes the ecological/social requirements; relief runs to 31 Dec 2030, CONFIRMED cross-ref §2). `PRIMARY-ONLY` (sustainability scope) / `CONFIRMED` (relief sunset date).

### 3.2 Ecological (GHG + environmental) requirements
| Requirement | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **GHG saving** vs conventional fossil fuel | **≥ 40%** lower lifecycle GHG emissions | `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU) + Tier-2 |
| **Total environmental impact** vs fossil comparator | must **not exceed 125%** of the fossil fuel (ecological-scarcity / LCA method) | `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU/IBTV) |
| **Land-use** | no feedstock from high-carbon land (forests, peatlands, wetlands) or high-biodiversity/protected areas | `PRIMARY-ONLY` |

- BAFU calculates GHG and environmental impacts using **ecoinvent**-based LCA (ecological-scarcity method). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

### 3.3 Social requirements
- **"Soziale Kriterien"** apply (high standards for raw-material cultivation/production); detailed specification is directed to **SECO** (State Secretariat for Economic Affairs). `PRIMARY-ONLY` — corroborator to pull the SECO criteria detail (not retrieved this pass).

### 3.4 Feedstock restrictions — palm / food-crop ban (KEY FLAG)
- **Statutory food/feed-crop ban (USG Art. 35d):** fuels made from **food or feed crops**, or that **directly compete with food production**, may **not be placed on the market** as renewable fuels. This **effectively excludes palm oil, soy and other food-crop oils** — a restriction stronger and more categorical than the EU high-ILUC phase-out. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU explanatory report; USG Art. 35d). **FLAG — verify exact scope and any transition in Fedlex text (not machine-readable this pass).**
- Waste/residue feedstocks on the **BAZG positive list** qualify via a **simplified procedure** (best-available-technology production). `PRIMARY-ONLY` (IBTV).

### 3.5 Accepted certification / proof of compliance
Three IBTV pathways (Art. 3):
1. **Individual-case application** with full documentation (IBTV Annexes 1–2).
2. **Simplified procedure** for waste/residue fuels on the BAZG positive list.
3. **Mass-balance certification** — a valid certificate plus supporting documentation (IBTV Art. 3 para 5c) — i.e. recognised chain-of-custody certificates are accepted as proof.
`PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU IBTV summary).

### 3.6 Registry / guarantee-of-origin & UDB status
- Since **1 January 2025**, Swiss production and imports of renewable fuels/heating fuels must be recorded via a **guarantee-of-origin (Herkunftsnachweis) system**, operated by **Pronovo AG**. The fuel supplier must **cancel/invalidate the guarantee of origin** when declaring the fuel in the transport sector. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU/ibtv; Pronovo) — corroborate scope/mandatory status.
- **UDB:** Switzerland does **not** participate in the EU Union Database; the Pronovo GO system + BAFU IBTV permit is the national equivalent. `CONFIRMED (by exclusion)` — Switzerland is outside EU/EEA.

### 3.7 BAFU permit procedure & importer/producer duties
- **Market placement:** producers/importers must obtain **BAFU authorisation before placing** a renewable/low-emission fuel on the market **if no tax relief is claimed**; where **BAZG grants tax relief**, that decision **automatically triggers** IBTV market-placement permission. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **Transition:** fuels could be placed on the market **without prior BAFU approval until 1 November 2025**; after that, prior IBTV approval is required. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (BAFU/ibtv) — corroborate date.
- **Documentation/records:** importers/producers must submit verifiable data on fuel type, production pathway and land-use, comply with ecoinvent-based calculations, and **retain records for BAFU inspection**. Contact channel: ibtv@bafu.admin.ch. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- Tax-relief decisions are co-authorised by **BAZG** and **SECO** (with BAFU verifying ecological criteria). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Fossil-fuel importers — meet the CO2 compensation obligation for 2026:** offset **30% of transport CO2 emissions** (≥12 percentage points domestically, max 18% abroad), surrendering BAFU/FOEN **emission-reduction attestations** and/or lowering the fossil base by placing renewable fuels on the market; you may pass at most **5 Rp/litre** to customers. Authority: **BAFU/FOEN** (CO2 Act SR 641.71 / Ordinance SR 641.711). The obligation-trigger threshold (de-minimis t CO2/yr) is a GAP to verify.
2. **Renewable-fuel suppliers — obtain BAFU IBTV market-placement authorisation** before placing a renewable/low-emission fuel on the market (or rely on the automatic trigger from a BAZG tax-relief grant); prior IBTV approval is required since 1 Nov 2025. Authority: **BAFU** (IBTV).
3. **Demonstrate the ecological requirements** via ecoinvent-based LCA: **≥40% GHG saving**, **≤125% total environmental impact**, and land-use criteria (no high-carbon / high-biodiversity land). Authority: BAFU (ETrV / IBTV).
4. **Ensure the feedstock is not a food/feed crop** and does not compete with food production — palm and soy are effectively excluded (USG Art. 35d).
5. **Provide proof via one of the three IBTV routes** — individual-case application, simplified waste-list procedure, or **mass-balance certificate** (IBTV Art. 3).
6. **Record production/imports in the Pronovo guarantee-of-origin system** and cancel the guarantee of origin when declaring the fuel in the transport sector (mandatory since 1 Jan 2025). Authority: **Pronovo AG**.
7. **Meet social criteria** per SECO, and **retain documentation for BAFU inspection**; note there is **no EU-UDB obligation**.
8. **Tax:** account for the mineral-oil tax + surtax with **BAZG/OFDF** (petrol 76.82 Rp/L, diesel 79.57 Rp/L); claim renewable-fuel **mineral-oil-tax relief** (up to ~75 Rp/L, conditional on ecological + social criteria) which runs to **31 Dec 2030**; note motor fuels are outside the CO2 levy.
9. **Aviation fuel suppliers:** blend **2% SAF** into jet fuel uplifted at Zurich and Geneva from 1 Jan 2026 (rising per ReFuelEU). Authority: **BAZL**.

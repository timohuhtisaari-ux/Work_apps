## 1. Biofuel mandates

Germany operates a **GHG-intensity reduction quota (Treibhausgasminderungs-Quote, "THG-Quote")** under §§ 37a–37h Bundes-Immissionsschutzgesetz (BImSchG), implemented by the 38. BImSchV (crediting rules, caps, advanced sub-quota) and the 37. BImSchV (RFNBOs/co-processing/ReFuelEU crediting). The framework was substantially amended by the **Zweites Gesetz zur Weiterentwicklung der Treibhausgasminderungs-Quote** (act of 1 June 2026, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 163, published 5 June 2026, in force 7 June 2026), which is Germany's RED III transport transposition and applies from compliance year 2026.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **No certificate/registry system.** Compliance is demonstrated in an annual declaration to the **Biokraftstoffquotenstelle** (Fachgebiet Biokraftstoffquote at Hauptzollamt Frankfurt (Oder), seat Cottbus) by **1 June of the year following the compliance year** (§ 37c (1) BImSchG). If no report is filed, the authority estimates volumes as an irrebuttable basis (§ 37c (3)). The mandate is administered inside the customs/energy-tax machinery — *see fuel-tax section for the Energiesteuergesetz interface*.
- **Quota trading:** obligations may be transferred to third parties by written contract (§ 37a (6)–(7) BImSchG) — the German "quota trade" is bilateral contract-based (e.g. sale of GHG reductions from charging electricity, biomethane, HVO), not exchange-traded units.
- **Banking:** over-fulfilment of the main quota may, on application, be credited to the **following** compliance year (§ 37a (8) BImSchG). Advanced-sub-quota surpluses may likewise be carried to the next year on application (§ 14 (4) 38. BImSchV).
- **Anti-fraud freeze (2024 act, still operative):** § 4a 38. BImSchV bans carry-over of 2024 surpluses into 2025 and of 2025 surpluses into 2026 (both for the main quota and the advanced sub-quota); 2024/2025 surpluses may instead be credited to **2027** on application by 15 Apr 2028. Third-party fulfilment options from 2024 cannot serve 2025 contracts, nor 2025 options for 2026.
- **UER phased out:** upstream emission reductions were a compliance option only through 2024/2025 (zoll.de: "through 2024/2025"; § 37a (5) listing shows the option time-limited) — part of the response to the UER/China-biodiesel fraud cases. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **On-site inspections:** from **2027**, mandatory on-site inspections (including by state auditors) for advanced biofuels and RFNBOs to be quota-eligible (§ 4b 38. BImSchV; adopted 2026 act). *Certification details are the sustainability agent's scope (Biokraft-NachV, Nabisy).*
- Eligible biofuels must meet Biokraft-NachV sustainability requirements and DIN fuel standards (zoll.de) — *cross-reference: sustainability section*.

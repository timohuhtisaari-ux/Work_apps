- **Report date:** 2026-07-04

## Executive summary

Italy runs an **energy-based (Gcal) release-for-consumption obligation** on suppliers of petrol and diesel for road/rail transport, met with tradable **CIC certificates** issued by **GSE** via the **BIOCAR** platform (1 CIC = 10 Gcal; 5 Gcal for advanced biofuels). For 2026 the overall quota is **12.60 %** (7.10 % "traditional" + 5.50 % advanced; biobenzina 3.40 %, advanced biomethane 3.90 %, RFNBO 0.05 %), with a crop cap of 2.3 %, a high-ILUC/palm cap declining to 0.4 %, and an Annex IX-B cap of 2.5 %; from 2026 the obligation is deemed automatically fulfilled for methane/biomethane in road transport, and LPG phases in (50 % in 2027, 100 % in 2028). The legislated trajectory reaches **16.00 % overall by 2030** (8 % advanced). On tax, Italy completed its diesel–petrol excise realignment early — **both fuels are 672.90 €/1000 L from 1 Jan 2026** — and grants a **reduced excise for HVO100 (617.40 €/1000 L, five years from 15 May 2025)** as GBER-block-exempted state aid, plus a haulier HVO/commercial-diesel refund; a temporary crisis cut (622.90) expired 3 Jul 2026, so ordinary rates apply from today. The top supplier obligations are: register on BIOCAR as soggetto obbligato, certify under the national scheme (SNC, DM 7 agosto 2024) or a voluntary scheme, carry a dichiarazione di sostenibilità per consignment under mass balance, file the annual autodichiarazione and stand for GSE verification (1–30 November), and onboard the UDB (GSE is national gatekeeper). **HIGH-risk flag: Italy transposed RED III LATE via D.Lgs. 9 gennaio 2026 n. 5 (in force 4 Feb 2026), after a reasoned opinion of 11 December 2025; the implementing DM aligning DM 107/2023 quotas to RED III is still pending.**

## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Scheme identification

Italy runs an **energy-based blending/release-for-consumption obligation** (obbligo di immissione in consumo) on suppliers of petrol and diesel for road and rail transport, with tradable **CIC certificates (certificati di immissione in consumo)** managed by **GSE** via the **BIOCAR** platform. Framework: **D.Lgs. 199/2021 Arts. 39–40** (RED II, now amended by the RED III decree **D.Lgs. 9 January 2026 n. 5**) implemented by **DM (MASE) 16 March 2023 n. 107** ("DM biocarburanti", published by comunicato in GU Serie Generale n. 96 of 24-04-2023, as amended — including DM 20 October 2023 n. 343 and DM 17 June 2024). Annual quotas sit in **Allegato 5** of DM 107/2023.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** "soggetti obbligati" — parties releasing petrol/diesel for consumption (excise-relevant releases) for road and rail transport (DM 107/2023 Art. 2(1)(v)); from 2026 natural gas/biomethane road volumes are deemed compliant automatically; **LPG is drawn into the obligation with a phase-in: 50 % in 2027, 100 % from 2028** — ⚠ now read verbatim in Normattiva consolidated Art. 39 D.Lgs. 199/2021: "per il GPL, si applicano a partire dall'anno 2027 in misura pari al 50 per cento e a partire dall'anno 2028 in misura pari al 100 per cento" → **CONFIRMED**. [M2][M7][M17]
- **Certificates:** CIC issued by **GSE**; 1 CIC = 10 Gcal (5 Gcal advanced); multiple CIC types by feedstock/carrier; DM 107/2023 expanded tradable CIC combinations **from 3 to 25**; trading among obligated parties/producers on the **BIOCAR** platform. [M1][M3][M9]
- **Compliance calendar (GSE regole applicative):** self-declaration of previous-year releases 1–28 February; GSE issues CIC by 30 May; transactions registered by 31 October; GSE verification 1–30 November, cancelling the CICs used. [M3]
- **Banking/borrowing:** deficits below the sanctionability threshold may be made up the following year (in addition to that year's obligation); surplus-CIC carry-over to the next year exists as a GSE FAQ topic ("rinvio CIC eccedenti") — exact limits not verified (GAP). [M2][M11][M12]
- **Registry/administration:** GSE (BIOCAR IT platform), on behalf of MASE; sanctions via ministry. Sustainability certification (Sistema Nazionale di Certificazione) is covered in Section 3.

## 2. Fuel taxation

Excise (accisa) on energy products is governed by the **Testo Unico Accise, D.Lgs. 26 ottobre 1995 n. 504 (TUA), Allegato I** — Italy's implementation of Directive 2003/96/EC, administered by ADM. Two big 2025–26 developments: (1) the **diesel–petrol excise realignment** ("superamento del sussidio ambientalmente dannoso EN.SI.24"): ⚠ mechanism created by **D.L. 43/2025 art. 3** and implemented by the interministerial decree of 14 May 2025, which started a five-year ±1.5 c€/L per year convergence (petrol 728,40→713,40; diesel 617,40→632,40 from 15 May 2025); the **Legge di Bilancio 2026 (L. 30 dicembre 2025, n. 199, art. 1 comma 129) completed it early** — from **1 Jan 2026 petrol and diesel are both 672,90 €/1000 L** (petrol −4.05 c€/L, diesel +4.05 c€/L). (2) Crisis-driven **temporary cuts** (Middle-East price spike): successive MEF(-MASE) decrees reduced rates from ~19 March 2026; the last decree (GU n. 129, 6 Jun 2026) set both fuels at **622,90 €/1000 L for 7 Jun–3 Jul 2026**; it was **not renewed** (⚠ re-verified against 3–4 July press), so **from 4 July 2026 (today) the ordinary 672,90 applies again**.

### Interaction with the mandate
The excise realignment explicitly serves the environmental-subsidy (SAD) reform agenda, and the HVO reduced rate + haulier HVO refund give a direct fiscal pull for renewable diesel on top of the CIC obligation. ### 3.3 Certification regime — national scheme AND voluntary schemes

- Italy is distinctive: it operates a **national certification scheme (Sistema Nazionale di Certificazione, SNC)** under DM 7 agosto 2024 (ex DM 14 novembre 2019), run through **certification bodies accredited by Accredia**, which audit adhering economic operators on "the completeness and truthfulness of all elements in sustainability declarations, in the sustainability certificate and related declarations". **CONFIRMED** (MASE normativa page via search + GU + certifier pages, 2026-07-04).
- **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, etc. — 18 on the DG ENER list as of 2026-07-04) are accepted as the alternative route, as required by RED art. 30. **CONFIRMED** (DG ENER list; MASE circulars referencing voluntary schemes).
- **Proof per consignment:** a **dichiarazione di sostenibilità** (sustainability declaration) plus, within the SNC, the **certificato di sostenibilità** issued under the scheme, transferred with each consignment under mass balance; GSE performs second-level checks (and, for incentivised plants, applies its "Regole operative per la verifica di sostenibilità"). **CONFIRMED** for the declaration/mass-balance mechanics (MASE/GSE documents); operational detail PRIMARY-ONLY.
- Low-ILUC-risk certification is obtainable under DM 7 agosto 2024 (relevant because palm/soy exclusions have a low-ILUC exception). **CONFIRMED** (GU summary + trade corroboration).

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register with GSE's BIOCAR portal as soggetto obbligato** (or as CIC-generating producer via the GSE qualification portal for biomethane/advanced biofuels). Authority: GSE.
2. **Certify under the Sistema Nazionale di Certificazione** (DM 7 agosto 2024, Accredia-accredited CB) **or** an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…); pass annual surveillance audits. Authority: CB / scheme CB (GSE second-level checks).
3. **Ensure every consignment carries a dichiarazione di sostenibilità** under mass balance meeting ≥ 50/60/65 % GHG savings by plant vintage. Authority: CB / GSE.
4. **Exclude palm (incl. EFB, PFAD) and soy-based volumes** from obligation/CIC claims unless certified low-ILUC-risk (D.Lgs. 199/2021 art. 40, since 1 Jan 2023). Authority: GSE/MASE.
5. **Respect the caps/sub-quotas** when composing renewable volumes for 2026: overall 12.60 % (7.10 traditional + 5.50 advanced), biobenzina 3.40 %, advanced biomethane 3.90 %, IX-B cap 2.5 %, crop cap 2.3 %, high-ILUC/palm cap 0.4 %.
6. **File the annual autodichiarazione on BIOCAR** by the deadline in the current MASE circular (2025: circular of 26 Feb 2025); be ready for **GSE verification 1–30 November**; hold sufficient CICs (obtain/trade on BIOCAR). Authority: GSE.
7. **Open and maintain a UDB account and enter transactions** — GSE is the national UDB gatekeeper under D.Lgs. 5/2026 art. 23. Authority: GSE / European Commission.
8. **If certified before Aug 2024 under DM 14 Nov 2019:** confirm migration to DM 7 agosto 2024 was completed at the last surveillance audit (12-month transition expired ~27 Aug 2025). Authority: CBs.
9. **Reassess obligations under D.Lgs. 5/2026** (in force 4 Feb 2026): amended arts. 39–42 D.Lgs. 199/2021, 29 % RES-T 2030, automatic fulfilment for methane/biomethane suppliers from 2026, LPG phase-in 2027/2028; watch for the pending implementing DM. Authority: MASE/GSE.
10. **Budget €750 per missing CIC** (DM 20-01-2015) plus carry-forward of the unmet obligation to the next year; note the separate art. 42 sanctions for sustainability-certification breaches. Authority: ministry (MIMIT/MASE) on GSE verification.
11. **Apply the correct excise treatment** — both petrol and diesel 672.90 €/1000 L (ordinary, from 4 Jul 2026); claim the HVO100 reduced rate (617.40) and the commercial-diesel/HVO haulier refund where eligible. Authority: ADM.
12. **Prepare for EU ETS2** — road-fuel suppliers become regulated entities from 1 Jan 2028 (auctioning from 2027, first surrender 2029). Authority: ADM / European Commission.

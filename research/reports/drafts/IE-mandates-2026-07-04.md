## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.1 Current obligation

Ireland operates the **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)**, a
**certificate-based, energy-content** obligation on suppliers of mineral oil to
ensure a minimum renewable share (by energy content) of the petrol and diesel
they place on the market. It is administered by the **National Oil Reserves
Agency (NORA)**. The operative framework is **S.I. No. 350 of 2022** (European
Union (Renewable Energy) Regulations 2022 — RED II transposition, converting the
former Biofuel Obligation Scheme to the RTFO), amended for 2026 by **S.I. No. 664
of 2025** (European Union (Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation) Regulations 2025),
which implements the **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transport elements** and
the updated Annex IX list (Directive (EU) 2024/1405). A parallel 6 % carbon-
intensity reduction obligation sits in **S.I. No. 160 of 2017** (FQD Art. 7a).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Certificates (3 colours):** issued by NORA per MJ of qualifying renewable fuel —
  **Green** (Annex IX Part A / advanced feedstocks), **Orange** (other qualifying
  renewable fuels, e.g. UCO/Annex IX-B), **Red** (food/feed crop-based). Advanced
  obligation is met with Green (and RFNBO/renewable-electricity) certificates; the
  crop cap limits Red certificates. (S1)
- **Registry / obligated parties:** NORA maintains electronic accounts and the
  certificate register; obligated parties are suppliers of mineral oil (petrol and
  diesel) placed on the Irish market. (S1)
- **Double counting:** 2 certs/MJ for Annex IX feedstocks. (S1)
- **Buy-out:** an obligated party short of certificates pays the buy-out charge
  (€0.05/MJ; €0.08/MJ advanced) rather than a discretionary penalty — this caps
  compliance cost. (S1)
- **Levy:** a biofuel levy of 0.1 euro-cent per litre applies to renewable
  transport fuel (NORA Act 2007 / 2010 Regs) — a tax-adjacent charge; cross-
  reference the fuel-tax section. (S2)
- Banking/borrowing/carry-over of certificates: not confirmed from a primary
  source in this pass — **gap**.

- The Renewable Transport Fuel Policy 2025–2027 sets the multi-annual trajectory;
  annual rates are fixed by S.I. each year. Post-2026 statutory steps not yet
  isolated in a made S.I. — **gap** (2027 rate expected via a further S.I.).
- **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition:** the **transport elements
  are transposed via S.I. No. 664 of 2025, effective 1 Jan 2026** — after the
  21 May 2025 deadline (late), but Ireland has now implemented the RED III
  transport provisions (RFNBO in the advanced obligation, renewable-electricity
  certificates, updated Annex IX per Directive (EU) 2024/1405). Full RED III
  transposition across non-transport sectors may involve additional instruments
  (outside mandates scope). `CONFIRMED` for transport. (S2, S4)
- **EU-wide:** ETS2 compliance for road-transport fuel suppliers starts 1 Jan 2028
  (Reg. (EU) 2026/667; auctioning from 2027) — applies to IE.

## 5-line summary (Ireland)
- Obligation type: certificate-based **energy-content** RTFO on mineral-oil suppliers, administered by NORA (Green/Orange/Red certificates + buy-out).
- Current level: **32 % by energy (2026)**, with a **5 % advanced-biofuel + RFNBO** sub-target and a **3 % crop cap**.
- Next legislated change: 2026 step (from 25 %) is the latest made; 2027 rate expected via a further S.I. under the 2025–2027 policy (not yet a made instrument).
- RED III status: **transport elements transposed via S.I. 664/2025, effective 1 Jan 2026** (late vs 21 May 2025 deadline, but done for transport).
- Flags: gov.ie & irishstatutebook 403-blocked this session (values anchored on NORA Tier 1 + press mirror); Annex IX-B cap and cert banking rules are gaps.

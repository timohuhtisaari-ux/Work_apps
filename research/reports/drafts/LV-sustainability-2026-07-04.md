## 3. Sustainability & compliance requirements

### 3.1 RED II/III transposition instruments
| Item | Finding | Legal basis | Confidence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary transposing act | **Transport Energy Law** (Transporta enerģijas likums), in force **1 January 2026**, replacing the former Biofuel Law. It expressly transposes RED III (Dir. (EU) 2023/2413) and contains the biofuel/biogas/biomass sustainability and GHG-saving criteria and their verification/monitoring. | Transporta enerģijas likums (likumi.lv id/364968) | CONFIRMED (likumi.lv primary + KEM + em.gov.lv + LV portāls) |
| Sustainability criteria article | **Art. 16** — biofuels/biomass fuels/RFNBO/RCF must meet sustainability + GHG-saving criteria regardless of geographic origin; **Art. 16(2)** exempts waste/residue-derived fuels (except agri/forestry residues) from land criteria, GHG-saving verification only. | Transport Energy Law Art. 16 | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Implementing Cabinet regulations | Multiple **Ministru kabineta noteikumi** delegated: GHG-calculation methodology, mass-balance tracking, sustainability documentation (Art. 15, 19), reporting formats (Art. 20(8)). **Deadline for adopting all regulations: 31 Dec 2026.** Consumer-information regulation adopted 14 Apr 2026. The old MK noteikumi Nr. 545 (2011) sustainability rules are superseded. | Transport Energy Law + MK noteikumi | PRIMARY-ONLY / CONFIRMED (consumer-info reg via KEM) |
| RED III status | **Transposed** through the Transport Energy Law (in force 01.01.2026); secondary Cabinet regulations still being rolled out during 2026. | Transport Energy Law | CONFIRMED |

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally
| Item | Value | Basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supplier GHG-intensity reduction target | Renewable transport energy must deliver **≥16% GHG savings** annually from 2030 vs fossil comparator (Art. 11(1)) | Transport Energy Law Art. 11 | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Biofuel GHG-saving thresholds | EU-harmonised RED thresholds (≥50/60/65%; RFNBO/RCF ≥70%) applied via the sustainability-criteria articles and Cabinet methodology | Art. 16, 19 + IR 2022/996 | PRIMARY-ONLY |

| Item | Finding | Confidence |

### 3.4 National registry & Union Database (UDB)
| Item | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Verifying/competent authority | **State Environmental Service (Valsts vides dienests, VVD)** — market-surveillance authority (Art. 6), receives annual reports, administers the EU database entry, may sample fuel and prohibit non-compliant sales. The **Energy and Environment Agency merged into VVD on 1 Oct 2025.** Consumer-information compliance supervised by the **Consumer Rights Protection Centre (PTAC)**. Policy owner: **Ministry of Climate and Energy (KEM)**. | CONFIRMED (Transport Energy Law Art. 6 + VVD + KEM) |
| Registry / UDB | **Art. 17(3):** supply-chain actors must enter data into the **EU database (Union Database)**, administered/overseen nationally by the **State Environmental Service**, per IR (EU) 2022/996 Art. 18. **Art. 17(4):** certification bodies verify database entries; the unified gas transmission/storage operator verifies biogas entries. Latvia uses the EU UDB rather than a separate national register. | CONFIRMED (Transport Energy Law Art. 17) |

| Item | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Crop-based / high-ILUC | **Art. 12(1)** permits food/feed-crop biofuels but limits their contribution (crop cap), incorporating the RED III framework; EU ILUC Delegated Reg. ## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope) — Latvia
1. Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme (e.g. ISCC / REDcert) or accepted MS national-scheme certification covering each consignment (Art. 16(3)). [VVD] — `CONFIRMED`
2. Operate a mass-balance system across the whole supply chain, including financial-support data (Art. 17(2)). [VVD] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
3. Enter chain-of-custody data into the **EU Union Database** (Art. 17(3)); ensure certification-body verification (Art. 17(4)). [VVD] — `CONFIRMED`
4. Meet applicable GHG-saving thresholds and the ≥16% supplier GHG-reduction target from 2030 (Art. 11, 16). [VVD] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
5. Submit the verified **annual report by 31 May** (first: 31 May 2027 for CY2026) to the State Environmental Service (Art. 20). [VVD] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
6. From 1 Jan 2030, procure an **independent audit** of the annual report (Art. 20(3)). [VVD] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
7. Budget for penalties: €90/t CO2eq (GHG), €70/GJ (advanced-fuel, from 2028), 5% net turnover (non-reporting). [VVD] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`

### 4-line summary — Latvia
- **Transposition status:** RED III transposed by the **Transport Energy Law (Transporta enerģijas likums), in force 1 Jan 2026** (replaces the Biofuel Law); implementing Cabinet regulations rolling out to 31 Dec 2026.
- **Certification regime:** No national scheme; accepts EU-recognised voluntary schemes / other MS national schemes (ISCC highlighted), whole-chain independent audit per IR 2022/996, mass balance mandatory.
- **Registry/UDB:** Data entered into the **EU Union Database**, administered nationally by the **State Environmental Service (VVD)**; annual verified report to VVD by **31 May** (first 31 May 2027); independent audit from 2030.
- **Flags:** Article numbers rest on a single parse (re-verify); competent verifier is **VVD** not KEM/VID (registry correction); penalties €90/t CO2eq and €70/GJ (from 2028) and 5% turnover for non-reporting.

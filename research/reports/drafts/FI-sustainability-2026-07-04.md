### 3.3 Certification schemes and proof of sustainability

- **National route:** operator applies to **Energiavirasto for approval of its kestävyysjärjestelmä** (sustainability system: feedstock origin/traceability, mass balance (ainetase), GHG calculation). Approval requires a statement from an **Energiavirasto-approved verifier (todentaja)** and is valid **five years**. `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04); approval decisions published (T1).
- **EU voluntary schemes accepted:** operators may **alternatively demonstrate compliance via Commission-approved voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU etc.; 18 schemes recognised as of 2026-07-04). `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04) + DG ENER voluntary schemes list (T1, 2026-07-04). Finland runs **no separate national certification scheme beyond the Energiavirasto approval system**.
- **Proof per consignment:** an operator with an approved system issues a **kestävyystodistus** (sustainability certificate) for each batch of biofuel/bioliquid/biomass fuel or raw material; this is the document that carries sustainability characteristics between operators and underpins jakeluvelvoite/tax claims (cross-ref other agents). `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **Verifiers:** must hold **FINAS accreditation** (or equivalent), liability insurance and technical competence; approved verifiers include AFRY Finland Oy, Enemi Oy, Inspecta Sertifiointi Oy (Kiwa). Verifiers audit the system at approval and periodically; audit reports accompany annual filings. `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page + todentajaohje (T1, 2026-07-04); Enemi verifier page (T2, 2026-07-04).

### 3.4 National registry and Union Database (UDB)

- **National registry:** no per-consignment national register. Filings go through **Energiavirasto's e-services**: the **KEKRI** system (biomass plants) and the Energiavirasto reporting portal (other operators). `PRIMARY-ONLY` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **UDB in Finnish law:** act 500/2025 consolidated UDB duties in the kestävyys framework (the previous UDB provision, jakeluvelvoitelaki 7 § 7 mom., was **repealed** on 4 Jul 2025). Economic operators handling **liquid and gaseous renewable fuels** (biofuels, biogas/biomethane, RFNBOs — transport and, after RED III, other uses) must **record transactions and sustainability characteristics in the UDB without delay**; HE 37/2025 notes even raw-material suppliers not separately certified nationally may have UDB recording duties. `CONFIRMED` for the obligation's existence (Finlex 2025/500 + TEM + Energiavirasto Kestävyysinfo, all T1, 2026-07-04); `PRIMARY-ONLY` for the exact section number (full statute text not machine-readable — see gaps).
- **Practical status:** Energiavirasto instructs distributors that liquid and gaseous fuels counted toward the distribution obligation must be reported to the UDB, particularly for cross-border trade; onboarding via webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase (EU Login). `PRIMARY-ONLY` — Energiavirasto Kestävyysinfo 7.10.2025 / jakeluvelvoite reporting webinar (T1, 2026-07-04).

### 3.5 Feedstock restrictions

- **High-ILUC risk (palm):** EU phase-out per Del. Reg. 2019/807 applies; **no additional national palm or soy exclusion** identified in kestävyyslaki or on Energiavirasto/TEM pages (checked 2026-07-04). Feedstock caps inside the jakeluvelvoite (food/feed-crop share, Annex IX-B cap) are the mandates researcher's scope. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (negative finding).
- **Annex IX / waste-residue classification:** Energiavirasto issues **binding advance rulings (ennakkotieto)** on raw-material classification (waste/residue/co-product) for the distribution-obligation acts — the national mechanism controlling double counting and Annex IX treatment. `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto pages (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **Forest biomass (national emphasis):** RED III no-go areas (e.g. wetlands drained after 1 Jan 2008, biodiverse forests) apply; operators must provide **annual attestations** for forest/agricultural biomass sourcing. `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto news 2025-09-18 (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **UCO/waste verification:** handled via sustainability-system audits and voluntary-scheme rules; no Finland-specific extra UCO audit located (gap — see 5).

## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope) — draft

1. Check whether you fall under kestävyyslaki 393/2013 (as amended by 500/2025): distributor under jakeluvelvoite/biopolttoöljy obligation, excise-liable for liquid/gaseous fuels, ETS operator, biomass plant ≥7.5 MW (solid) / covered biogas plant, RFNBO producer, or state-aid recipient. [CONFIRMED]
2. Either obtain **Energiavirasto approval of a kestävyysjärjestelmä** (with FINAS-accredited verifier statement; renew every 5 years) **or** hold a valid **EU-recognised voluntary scheme certificate (e.g. ISCC EU)** covering your chain. [CONFIRMED]
3. Issue/require a **kestävyystodistus for every consignment** counted toward obligations; maintain ainetase (mass balance). [CONFIRMED]
4. New-scope operators: application (or change request) filed by **31 Dec 2025**; ensure compliance with the **1 Jan 2026** 80 % biomass GHG tier where applicable. [CONFIRMED]
5. Submit the annual **kestävyyskriteeriselvitys by 31 March** via KEKRI/portal, with verifier audit report attached. [CONFIRMED]
6. Onboard to the **Union Database** (EU Login) and record transactions of liquid/gaseous renewable fuels without delay, incl. cross-border movements. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
7. For borderline feedstocks (wastes/residues, Annex IX), consider an **ennakkotieto** ruling from Energiavirasto before counting. [CONFIRMED]
8. Keep annual forest/agricultural biomass sourcing attestations (RED III land criteria). [CONFIRMED]

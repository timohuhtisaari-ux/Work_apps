## PART 1 — Hydrogen / RFNBO deep-dive (priority)

The EU "green hydrogen" rulebook rests on **two twin delegated regulations, both adopted
10 February 2023 and both published in OJ L 157 (20 June 2023)**:

- **2023/1184** — *when* electricity used to make hydrogen counts as renewable (the "additionality" methodology).
- **2023/1185** — *how much* GHG a batch of RFNBO/RCF must save, and *how* to calculate it (the 70% / life-cycle methodology).

A **third**, separate instrument — **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2359** — sits under the
**Hydrogen & Decarbonised Gas Market package (Directive (EU) 2024/1788), NOT under RED**, and covers
**low-carbon** (e.g. "blue") hydrogen/fuels. It is deliberately aligned with the RED methodology but is a
**different legal basis** — see item 3.

### 1. Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/1184 — renewable-electricity ("additionality") methodology

| Attribute | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Empowering article | RED Art. **27(3)** (Dir. 2018/2001, as amended) | CONFIRMED |
| Subject | Methodology for producing RFNBOs: additionality, temporal & geographical correlation, grid-electricity rules | CONFIRMED |
| Adoption | **10 February 2023** | CONFIRMED |
| OJ publication | **OJ L 157, 20 June 2023** (atlanthy cites 23 June — minor discrepancy, note) | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Entry into force | 3rd day after publication (late June 2023) — exact day not pulled from legal text | UNCORROBORATED (date only) |
| Current status | **IN FORCE**; additionality **review clause due by 1 July 2028**; 2025–26 political pressure to relax rules but **no amendment adopted** | CONFIRMED |

**Mechanics (as corroborated):**
- **Additionality:** renewable electricity via PPA from installations commissioned **no more than 36 months before** the electrolyser, and **not having received operating support** (limited exceptions). Phase-in: producers/plants commissioned before 1 Jan 2028 are exempt from additionality until 1 Jan 2038 (grandfathering).
- **Temporal correlation:** **monthly** matching of production to renewable generation **until 31 Dec 2029**; **hourly** matching **from 1 January 2030** (Member States may bring hourly forward to 1 July 2027).
- **Geographical correlation:** electrolyser and generation in the **same bidding zone** (or interconnected zone with ≥ price, or offshore zone).
- **Grid electricity counts as fully renewable** if, in the prior year, the **bidding zone's renewable share exceeded 90%** (then usable up to a capped number of hours proportional to that share), **OR** the **zone's average grid emission intensity was below 18 gCO₂e/MJ (≈ 64.8 gCO₂/kWh)** (with PPA + temporal + geographical correlation demonstrated).
- **Review clause:** *"By 1 July 2028, the Commission shall submit a report ... assessing the impact of the requirements ... including ... temporal correlation, on production costs, GHG savings and the energy system"*, after which it **may adopt a delegated act modifying the methodology**.
- **Ongoing (2025–2026):** active lobbying (BDEW, industry) and Commission signals to soften the electricity-sourcing criteria; captured in Tier-2 briefings (King & Spalding, Stibbe). **No amending act adopted as of 2026-07-06** → status remains IN FORCE / UNDER POLITICAL REVIEW.

### 2. Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/1185 — minimum GHG threshold (RCFs) + RFNBO/RCF GHG methodology

| Attribute | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Empowering articles | RED **Art. 25(2)** (min. RCF threshold) + **Art. 28(5)** (methodology) | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Subject | Sets **70%** minimum GHG-emissions-saving threshold for **recycled carbon fuels (RCFs)** and the **well-to-wheel life-cycle methodology** for GHG savings of both **RFNBOs and RCFs** | CONFIRMED |
| Adoption | **10 February 2023** | CONFIRMED |
| OJ publication | **OJ L 157, 20 June 2023** | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Entry into force | 3rd day after publication (late June 2023) | UNCORROBORATED (date only) |
| Current status | **IN FORCE**; not amended. Its logic was **extended (not replaced)** to low-carbon fuels by 2025/2359 (item 3) | CONFIRMED |

**Mechanics:** GHG savings measured **vs a fossil-fuel comparator of 94 gCO₂e/MJ**; the **70%** saving requirement means total life-cycle emissions of an RFNBO/RCF **must not exceed 28.2 gCO₂e/MJ**. Methodology is **well-to-wheel** and set out in the Annex; covers electricity inputs, elastic supply of feedstock, CO₂ capture/utilisation accounting.

### 3. Boundary case — Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2359 (low-carbon hydrogen / low-carbon fuels)

| Attribute | Value | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Act number | **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2359** (Commission ref. C(2025)4674) | CONFIRMED |
| Legal basis | **Directive (EU) 2024/1788 Art. 9(5)** (Hydrogen & Decarbonised Gas Market Directive) — **NOT RED** | CONFIRMED |
| Subject | Single **life-cycle methodology** for **low-carbon fuels/hydrogen** (incl. RCFs, low-carbon H₂, synthetic gaseous/liquid fuels from low-carbon H₂) | CONFIRMED |
| Adoption | **8 July 2025** | CONFIRMED |
| OJ publication | **21 November 2025** | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Entry into force | **11 December 2025** | PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Current status | **IN FORCE** | CONFIRMED |
| Threshold | Low-carbon hydrogen = **≥70% GHG saving** vs 94 gCO₂e/MJ comparator → **≤28.2 gCO₂e/MJ** (3.38 kgCO₂e/kg H₂) | CONFIRMED |

**Flag:** This act is frequently confused with the RED RFNBO acts. It is a **different legal basis**
(gas/hydrogen market Directive 2024/1788, not RED 2018/2001) and governs the **low-carbon (non-renewable-origin)**
pathway. It is designed to be *methodologically consistent* with 2023/1184/1185 but does **not** amend them.

## 6-line summary — RFNBO / hydrogen act status

1. **2023/1184** (RFNBO additionality, RED Art. 27(3)) — adopted 10 Feb 2023, **IN FORCE**; hourly temporal matching from 1 Jan 2030; **review due 1 Jul 2028**, relaxation debated but **no amendment adopted**.
2. **2023/1185** (RFNBO/RCF GHG methodology + 70% RCF threshold, Art. 25(2)/28(5)) — adopted 10 Feb 2023, **IN FORCE, unamended**; ≤28.2 gCO₂e/MJ vs 94 comparator.
3. **2025/2359** (low-carbon H₂/fuels) — **different legal basis: Dir. 2024/1788 Art. 9(5), NOT RED**; adopted 8 Jul 2025, **IN FORCE 11 Dec 2025**; 70% threshold, aligned with but separate from the RED acts.
4. **2023/1640** co-processing ¹⁴C methodology (Art. 28(5)) and **2022/996** verification/UDB IA (Art. 30, amended by 2024/805 & 2025/196) — both **IN FORCE**.
5. **2019/807** ILUC — **IN FORCE but UNDER REVISION** (draft amendment published 20 Jan 2026); **2024/1405** is the latest **Annex IX** amendment (Del. Directive, Art. 28(6)).
6. **Flags:** cascading-principle DA (Art. 3(3)) **OVERDUE**; default-GHG-values (Art. 31) and transport GHG-intensity/biomethane accounting **not tabled**; EUR-Lex direct fetch failed this session → primary identifiers verified via search extraction + Tier-2 (UNCORROBORATED on exact EIF days).

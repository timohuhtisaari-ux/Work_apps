### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

From **Uredba Art. 6** (quoted this pass):
- **50 %** — installations operating before **5 Oct 2015**
- **60 %** — installations starting **6 Oct 2015 – 31 Dec 2020**
- **65 %** — liquid biofuels from installations starting **1 Jan 2021** onward
- **70 %** — biomass fuels (electricity/heat/cooling), installations 2021–2025, rising to **80 % from 2026**

[CONFIRMED — S1] (matches RED II/III Art. 29(10).)

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- **Uredba Art. 10** recognises **two pathways**: (a) an EU-Commission-approved **voluntary national/international scheme**, or (b) direct verification of compliance with the Art. 5–9 criteria. Independent auditors ("neodvisni presojevalec") verify under a voluntary scheme — Art. 10(2). [CONFIRMED — S1]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG, Better Biomass**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6]
- **Proof of sustainability per consignment:** producers must issue a **spremno potrdilo (accompanying certificate)** per consignment — Art. 12(2) — containing facility name, tax number, feedstock origin, biofuel type, GHG emissions and a sustainability-compliance declaration. [CONFIRMED — S1]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB established under **RED III Art. 31a** (formal deadline 21 Nov 2024; operational from Jan 2024), chain-of-custody rules in **Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996 Art. 18**; collectors/EOs onboarded via **voluntary schemes** through 2024–2025; **mandatory-use/sanction date still being finalised** by the Commission with member states. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6, S7, S10]
- **SI operators are in scope via EU law.** ZSROVE-1 (Dec 2025) may add a national UDB provision — not confirmed this pass; until then the obligation applies directly. Operators risk **certificate suspension without a UDB account** (scheme-administered onboarding). [PRIMARY-ONLY / flag — S7, S10]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) or arrange direct Art. 5–9 verification (Uredba Art. 10). → certification body / independent auditor.
2. **Issue a spremno potrdilo (accompanying certificate) for every consignment** with facility, tax number, feedstock origin, biofuel type, GHG value and compliance declaration (Art. 12(2)). → self.
3. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 %; biomass fuels 70 %→80 % from 2026) and document them per batch (Art. 6). → self + auditor.
4. **File the annual operator report to the monitoring contractor by 28 February** (Art. 18(1)); the contractor reports to MOPE by 31 March. → monitoring contractor / MOPE.
5. **Respect the 7 % crop cap, 1.7 % IX-B cap, and EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out**; use only EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/waste claims. → self + auditor.
6. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme and record in-scope transactions; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension. → UDB / voluntary scheme.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **No dedicated national sustainability registry** (Nabisy-equivalent) located — Slovenia relies on the accompanying certificate + monitoring-contractor route. [GAP]
- **ZSROVE-1 (112/2025) UDB and penalty provisions** not read line-by-line this pass; whether ZSROVE-1 introduces a national UDB obligation or revises the Uredba's monitoring regime should be checked once implementing acts appear. [FLAG]
- **High-ILUC palm** handled via EU law / RES-T decree; **no SI-specific ban beyond EU; no Annex IX national additions** located. [GAP]
- Uredba article numbers cited from a single successful fetch of the uradni-list page; a second independent read (PISRS URED8173) recommended for the corroborator. [FLAG — PRIMARY-ONLY on some items]

**4-line summary — Slovenia**
- Transposition: RED II sustainability via the **Uredba o trajnostnih merilih za biogoriva** (Ur. l. RS 2021-01-0873); RED III primary transposition **ZSROVE-1 adopted (Ur. l. RS 112/2025, 29 Dec 2025)**, late but not escalated June 2026 (Commission assessment likely pending).
- Certification: **EU-recognised voluntary schemes OR direct Art. 5–9 verification** (Uredba Art. 10); proof per consignment = **spremno potrdilo** with GHG value + compliance declaration; GHG thresholds 50/60/65 % (biofuels), 70 %→80 % (biomass fuels from 2026).
- Registry/UDB: **no dedicated national registry** found — reporting via **monitoring contractors** to MOPE; annual operator report **by 28 Feb**, contractor→ministry **by 31 Mar**; UDB applies via EU law (mandatory-use date pending).
- Flags: ZSROVE-1 UDB/penalty provisions not yet read line-by-line; no SI palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU; some Uredba items PRIMARY-ONLY pending a second independent read.

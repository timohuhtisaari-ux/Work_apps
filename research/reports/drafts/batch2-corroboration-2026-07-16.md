## Corrections Required (Mapcard Level)

### CRITICAL CORRECTION: Mexico Supreme Court Ruling Date

**Draft claim:** "Supreme Court blocked E10 Jan 2025"  
**Corrected fact:** Supreme Court ruled E10 unconstitutional **January 22, 2020** (some sources cite Jan 15, 2020; one source says Jan 16)  
**Impact:** This is a **5-year error**. The ruling has been in place for over 6 years, not recently in 2025. The draft must be updated to reflect the actual 2020 date.  
**Evidence:**
- Nasdaq archive (2020-01-15): https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/mexicos-supreme-court-rules-against-hiking-ethanol-fuel-content-2020-01-15
- Ethanol Producer Magazine archive (2020-01-16 publication)
- Climate Litigation Database official case records
- F&L Asia, SCMP, Agri-Pulse all archive 2020 date

**Corrected mapcard entry for Mexico:**

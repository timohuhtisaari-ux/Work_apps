# Cyprus — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Cyprus (CY)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Cyprus runs a **biofuel blending obligation** set by ministerial decree (ΚΔΠ 11/2020) on petrol/diesel suppliers as a percentage of energy content, alongside a −6% FQD GHG-intensity reduction; the RES-in-transport 2030 target is **≥14%** (Law 106(I)/2022, confirmed). The current blending level is **7.3% energy content**, but this rests on a **single source** (energy.gov.cy via search) — the corroborator confirmed the existence of the decree quota but could not independently quantify 7.3%, so it stands PRIMARY-ONLY. Cyprus is **exempt from the 1.7% Annex IX-B cap** under the RED III Cyprus/Malta derogation (confirmed). Fuel taxation offers **no biofuel-specific relief** — petrol excise 429.00 EUR/1000 L and diesel 400.00 EUR/1000 L (two independent Tier-2 sources; no Tier-1 obtained); a temporary −8.33c/L cut for Apr–Jun 2026 has expired and a carbon tax on fuels is proposed. The dominant compliance story is **infringement risk**: Cyprus holds the **most advanced RED III case** of the four countries — an **additional reasoned opinion issued 4 June 2026 (INFR(2024)0213)**, the only Member State escalated on RED III in that package, with CJEU-referral and financial-sanctions risk within two months. Top supplier obligations: hold voluntary-scheme/national-system certification, provide Proof of Sustainability, and file monthly sales/blending reports to MECI.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated:** suppliers of petrol and diesel for transport ("προμηθευτές καυσίμων"). [2]
- **Decree mechanism:** the Minister of Energy, Commerce & Industry sets, by decree (ΚΔΠ), the required biofuel blending % (energy content), the calculation methodology, and the monthly sales-reporting obligations for suppliers. Current operative decree: **ΚΔΠ 11/2020** (7.3%). A separate decree ΚΔΠ 201/2016 governs consumer point-of-sale biofuel-content information. [2]
- **Competent authority:** Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry (meci.gov.cy / energy.gov.cy). No tradable-certificate market identified; direct blending obligation with monthly reporting.
- **Banking/borrowing:** not identified — GAP.

### 1.3 Future trajectory

- No legislated year-by-year step-up of the blending % was located beyond the standing 7.3% decree (ΚΔΠ 11/2020) and the RED-based advanced path (1% 2025 → 3.5% 2030). NECP sets the 14% RES-T 2030 endpoint. A revised decree raising the blending % to track RED III is expected but **not located as adopted** — GAP / monitor. `PROPOSED` where applicable.

**RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition status:** NOT fully transposed. Timeline: letter of formal notice Sept 2024 (INFR(2024)0213); reasoned opinion Feb 2025; **additional reasoned opinion issued in the June 2026 infringement package (4 Jun 2026)** for still-incomplete transposition (notably the permitting-acceleration provisions). **Corroborator (2026-07-04) directly fetched the EC June-2026 energy package [V6]:** confirms the additional reasoned opinion to Cyprus under INFR(2024)0213 and that Cyprus is the **only Member State escalated on RED III** in that package; two months to respond before possible CJEU referral with financial sanctions. The House of Representatives passed an amendment to Law 107(I)/2022 aligning with 2023/2413 and 2024/1711, but the Commission judged transposition still incomplete. Status: **advanced infringement (additional reasoned opinion) — most advanced RED III case of the four countries reviewed.** [8][9][10][V6]

**ETS2 (EU-wide cross-reference):** ETS2 covers road-transport fuel suppliers. ETS2 was **established by Directive (EU) 2023/959** (Ch. IVa of Dir 2003/87/EC); its start was **postponed one year to 1 Jan 2028, first allowance surrender 2029** (Nov-2025/early-2026 climate package) — not "surrender from 2028" as originally drafted. [11][V5]

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **Supplier FQD target:** ≥**6 %** lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction vs the 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ fossil baseline (Law 106(I)/2022). [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S4]
- **Biofuels/bioliquids:** RED thresholds — **50 %** (pre-5 Oct 2015 installations), **60 %** (6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021); **70 %** RFNBO/RCF — applied via Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996. Not quoted from CY text this pass → framework-level. [PRIMARY-ONLY / framework — S4]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- Economic operators demonstrate sustainability via the **national certification system OR an EU-recognised voluntary scheme** — the standard RED II model. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S8]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S1]
- **Proof of sustainability:** depending on supply-chain position, operators provide a **self-declaration, sustainability declaration and/or Proof of Sustainability (PoS)** carrying the sustainability-criteria data and the GHG-emission-saving figure; traders must supply information demonstrating criteria compliance for the biofuel to count toward RES-T / support. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S8]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register with MECI as a transport-fuel supplier ("προμηθευτής καυσίμων")** subject to the ΚΔΠ 11/2020 blending obligation. → Ministry of Energy, Commerce & Industry.
2. **Meet the current blending obligation (7.3% energy content, single-source — verify with MECI)** across petrol + diesel placed on market, plus the −6% FQD GHG-intensity reduction vs 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ. → self / MECI.
3. **Respect the advanced (Annex IX-A) path** ≥1% (2025) → 3.5% (2030) and the 5.5% combined advanced+RFNBO 2030 target; note **Cyprus's exemption from the 1.7% Annex IX-B cap**; respect the 7% crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out. → self + verifier.
4. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) **or** comply with the national sustainability-certification system (ΚΔΠ 55/2011 lineage). → certification body / MECI.
5. **Provide the required Proof of Sustainability** (self-declaration / sustainability declaration / PoS per supply-chain position) carrying the GHG-saving figure. → self / upstream supplier.
6. **File the periodic (monthly) sales/blending report to MECI** under the quota decree and retain sustainability documentation for inspection; the exact annual sustainability-report deadline is not established (confirm with MECI). → MECI.
7. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension (mandatory-use date pending). → UDB / voluntary scheme.
8. **Track the RED III infringement:** with an additional reasoned opinion issued 4 June 2026 (INFR(2024)0213) and CJEU-referral risk, expect a revised blending decree and new transposition measures — monitor MECI/Official Gazette. → self.
9. **Prepare for ETS2** as a regulated road-transport fuel supplier: 1 Jan 2028 start, first allowance surrender 2029; also track the proposed carbon tax on fuels (Green Tax Reform). → competent ETS2 authority / MoF.

## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope) — Estonia
1. Hold a valid EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certificate (e.g. ISCC EU / REDcert EU / 2BSvs) covering each biofuel consignment placed on the Estonian market. [Env. Board] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
2. Operate a mass-balance system carrying, per consignment: quantity, type, feedstock, production method, country of origin, place of purchase, proof method, and carbon intensity (gCO2eq/MJ). [Env. Board] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
3. Submit the **monthly** GHG-emissions and volume report by the **15th** of each month to the Environmental Board (email / EMTA e-system). [Env. Board] — `CONFIRMED`
4. Ensure each biofuel meets the applicable RED GHG-saving threshold (50/60/65%; RFNBO 70%). [Env. Board] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
5. Register and record transactions in the EU **Union Database (UDB)** as required for transport-fuel operators. [EU / Env. Board] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`
6. Retain proof-of-sustainability and be prepared for Environmental Board sampling/lab verification of biological origin (esp. UCO/waste feedstocks). [Env. Board] — `PRIMARY-ONLY`

### 4-line summary — Estonia
- **Transposition status:** RED II transposed via Atmospheric Air Protection Act + Liquid Fuel Act; **RED III NOT fully transposed — letter of formal notice 24 Jul 2025 (26 MS incl. Estonia)** — corroborator-verified.
- **Certification regime:** No national scheme; accepts EU-recognised voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, etc.) with mass-balance proof of sustainability per consignment.
- **Registry/UDB:** No Nabisy-style national register; suppliers report monthly (by the 15th) to the Environmental Board (via EMTA e-system); EU UDB obligations apply.
- **Flags:** Riigi Teataja legal text unreadable (SPA) — section numbers unverified; FOKA is an F-gas register not a biofuel registry; €6m fine amount uncorroborated; EE-specific UDB onboarding guidance not located.

# Denmark — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Denmark (DK)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04)

## Executive summary

Denmark's transport-biofuel obligation is a **CO2e displacement requirement**
(*CO2e-fortrængningskrav*): fuel suppliers must cut the life-cycle GHG emissions per
energy unit of fuel delivered to road transport and non-road mobile machinery versus a
2010 EU fossil baseline (technology-neutral; it replaced the volume blending mandate in
2022). For 2026 the level is **≥ 5.2 %** (applies 2025–2027), rising to ≥ 6 % in
2028–2029 and ≥ 7 % from 2030, alongside a combined advanced-biofuel+RFNBO blending
requirement for 2030 whose national value is disputed (see below). Biofuels are relieved
through the tax system by the **CO2 tax (CO2-afgift) exemption on the bio share** — there
is **no reduced-rate scheme for high-blend biofuels** and, unlike Sweden/Finland, the
CO2 exemption is not limited to volumes outside the obligation. The core compliance
obligations on a fuel supplier are: source only EU-voluntary-scheme-certified biofuel
(with no palm- or soy-based biodiesel), meet the HB 2025.1 documentation and independent
control rules, file the annual CO2e-fortrængningskrav skema to Energistyrelsen by 31
March, register excise liability with the Danish tax authorities, and prepare to upload to
the EU Union Database. **Denmark was the only member state to notify full RED III
transposition by the 21 May 2025 deadline** — the only one of the three countries in this
batch not under an open infringement.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** "virksomheder" — entities liable for energy tax on liquid transport
  fuel, natural-gas suppliers, and fuel self-consumers (law § 2, nr. 17). *Cross-reference:
  definition rides on energy-tax liability — see section 2.* No de-minimis threshold
  identified this pass.
- **Administrator:** Energistyrelsen (Danish Energy Agency). No certificate registry —
  supplier-level annual accounting per the **Håndbog** (HB 2025.1, BEK nr 483/2025) and the
  "Skema for CO2e-fortrængningskrav".
- **Reporting:** annually to Energistyrelsen **by 31 March** for the preceding calendar
  year, with verifier documentation per the Håndbog. CONFIRMED (ens.dk).
- **Flexibility:** **joint fulfilment** — a company may meet its obligations wholly/partly
  via agreement with one or more other companies (law § 3, stk. 6; § 4a, stk. 6).
  Carry-over/banking between years: not identified (see Gaps). From 1 Jan 2025 **UER
  credits and (FQD-linked) electricity crediting were discontinued** when the separate FQD
  Art. 7a 6 %-GHG requirement was repealed (RED III/FQD amendment) — per Energistyrelsen
  hearing resumé (Oct–Nov 2024 hearing).
- **Sustainability gate:** only fuels meeting RED sustainability/GHG-saving criteria count
  (50/60/65 % thresholds by plant commissioning date). *See section 3.*

## 2. Fuel taxation

Denmark taxes road fuels under the **Mineralolieafgiftsloven** (energy tax,
MINAL) and the **CO2-afgiftsloven** (CO2AL), together implementing Directive
2003/96/EC; gaseous fuels fall under the **Gasafgiftsloven**. The **grøn
skattereform** package (**lov nr. 683 af 11. juni 2024**) restructured road-fuel
taxation from **1 January 2025**: energy taxes were cut, the **CO2 tax on motor
fuels was raised to 750 DKK/t CO2 (2022-price level, indexed)**, and a separate
**emissionsafgift** was introduced for ETS1-covered fuel consumption (not road
fuels). Rates are indexed annually to the net price index (MINAL § 32 a). The
statutory rate tables are differentiated by biofuel content because **the bio
share carries no CO2 tax** (CO2AL § 7, stk. 3). Denmark has **no reduced-rate
scheme for high-blend biofuels** — relief is the CO2 exemption plus an optional
energy-content basis for the energy tax.

### 2.2 Interaction with the mandate

Denmark's supplier obligation is the **CO2e displacement requirement
(CO2-fortrængningskrav)** — see section 1. Taxation interacts only
passively: the CO2-afgift exemption for the bio share applies to all biofuel
regardless of whether it counts toward the displacement requirement (no
Sweden/Finland-style volume restriction), and the statutory blend-differentiated
rates (4.8 %/9.8 % petrol, 6.8 %/7.6 % diesel) simply mirror standard blends.

### 3.3 Certification schemes and proof of sustainability

- **Accepted schemes:** documentation follows the **Håndbog (HB 2025.1)**, which prescribes use of **voluntary certification schemes (frivillige kontrolordninger)** — i.e. the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (18 recognised by the Commission as of 2026-07-04: ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, etc.) — together with **mass-balance chain of custody** and **independent control/auditor documentation**. Denmark operates **no national certification scheme**; the HB is a documentation rulebook, not a scheme. `CONFIRMED` (HB role, voluntary schemes, mass balance — ens.dk T1, 2026-07-04 + DG ENER list T1); `PRIMARY-ONLY` on whether *all* 18 schemes are accepted without reservation (HB full text unread).
- **Proof per consignment:** each consignment counted toward the CO2e-fortrængningskrav must be covered by sustainability documentation per the HB (scheme proof-of-sustainability / self-verification per HB rules), aggregated into the supplier's verified annual report. `PRIMARY-ONLY` — ens.dk summaries (T1, 2026-07-04); HB text unread.
- **Verification:** the HB requires **uafhængig kontrol** (independent control) of the sustainability documentation and the annual report; specific auditor standards (e.g. ISAE/ISO) could not be verified because the HB PDF/retsinformation text was inaccessible (corroborator confirmed retsinformation 403). `UNCORROBORATED` (detail level).

### 3.4 National registry and Union Database (UDB)

- **National registry:** Denmark has **no national biofuel registry** (no HBE/Nabisy equivalent). Compliance is demonstrated through the **annual indberetningsskema** ("Skema for CO2e-fortrængningskrav") submitted to **Energistyrelsen**, covering all fuel volumes (fossil + renewable), types and lifecycle emissions. `CONFIRMED` — ens.dk (two pages, T1, 2026-07-04).
- **UDB obligation:** BEK 978/2025 (and successor 1401/2025) requires companies to **upload to the EU Union Database, in a timely manner, the information needed to verify compliance, including transaction data through the whole supply chain**. However, ens.dk states Energistyrelsen **is still awaiting the final full deployment of the Union Database** ("Energistyrelsen afventer p.t. den endelige idriftsættelse af Unionsdatabasen"); once fully operational, UDB registration becomes mandatory **but will not replace** the annual skema. Danish operators should onboard at webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase (EU Login). `CONFIRMED` (both the legal duty and the "awaiting deployment" status — note the tension between the two, reported as-is) — ens.dk (T1, 2026-07-04); BEK 978 content (T1 via search extract, 2026-07-04); DG ENER UDB page (T1, 2026-07-04).

### 3.5 Feedstock restrictions (beyond EU law)

- **Palm and soy ban:** biodiesel produced from **palm oil or soy oil cannot be used** to meet the Danish CO2e requirement ("der kan ikke længere anvendes biodiesel, der er produceret med palme- eller sojaolie"). This goes **beyond** the EU high-ILUC regime (which only freezes/phases out palm and allows low-ILUC certification): USDA FAS reports Denmark excluding palm- and soy-based biofuels **from 2022** unless certified low-ILUC-risk. Existence: `CONFIRMED` (ens.dk T1, corroborator fetch). **Start date 2022: now corroborated by two independent Tier 2 sources (USDA GAIN + biofuels-news, both aligning DK with FR/NL from mid-2022)**; primary bekendtgørelse bilag text remains unread (retsinformation 403), so the precise low-ILUC exception wording is still `PRIMARY-ONLY`/pending.
- **High-ILUC-risk phase-out:** EU Del. Reg. 2019/807 applies; high-ILUC feedstocks subject to phase-out and excluded from counting per the bekendtgørelse. `CONFIRMED` — ens.dk (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **Waste/residue positive list:** biofuels/biogas from wastes and residues **not** from agriculture/aquaculture/forestry/fisheries are exempt from land criteria and get **zero upstream emissions before collection**, per a **national positive list effective 1 Jan 2022** (HB chapter 4). Agricultural residues remain subject to relevant criteria. `CONFIRMED` — ens.dk (T1, 2026-07-04). This positive list is the Danish mechanism for Annex IX/waste classification; additions beyond it not identified.
- **UCO and waste audit extras:** verification runs via the certification schemes + HB independent control; no Denmark-specific extra UCO audit found (gap).

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Confirm coverage:** you are an obligated party ("virksomhed") if you are liable for energy tax on liquid transport fuel, are a natural-gas supplier, or self-consume fuel (biobrændstofloven § 2, nr. 17). Authority: **Energistyrelsen**.
2. **Register excise-duty liability** with the Danish tax authorities (**Skattestyrelsen / Skatteforvaltningen**) under MINAL and CO2-afgiftsloven; the CO2-afgift on the bio share is zero-rated by statute, and you may opt to tax the energy component of high blends by energy content (MINAL § 1, stk. 8).
3. **Source only certified sustainable biofuel:** use **EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, …) with mass-balance chain of custody meeting **HB 2025.1** (BEK 483/2025).
4. **Screen feedstocks:** **no palm- or soy-based biodiesel**; check high-ILUC status; apply the **national positive list** (in force 1 Jan 2022) for waste/residue zero-upstream treatment; verify each consignment meets the **50/60/65 %** GHG-saving minimum (by installation date) and the land criteria.
5. **Arrange independent control (uafhængig kontrol)** of the sustainability documentation and the annual report, as required by the HB.
6. **Submit the annual CO2e-fortrængningskrav skema to Energistyrelsen by 31 March** for the preceding calendar year, using the "Vejledning for indberetningsskema" of the relevant reporting year, with verifier documentation.
7. **Onboard to the EU Union Database** (webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase via EU Login) and prepare to upload full supply-chain transaction data; monitor ens.dk for the go-live signal — UDB will **not** replace the skema. Authority: European Commission UDB, supervised by Energistyrelsen.
8. **Track amendments:** the current order is **BEK 1401 af 27/11/2025** (which repealed the metodebekendtgørelse); check retsinformation for the latest BEK and whether an updated HB (post-2025.1) has issued.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **UNRESOLVED CONFLICT — 2030 combined advanced+RFNBO target (carried flag):** a single national **BEK 482** retsinformation/lovguiden extract states the combined advanced-biofuel+RFNBO 2030 blending requirement is **≥ 5.1 %** (excl. aviation), while **KEFM describes the RED III directive requirement as 5.5 %** including ≥ 1 pp PtX/RFNBO. **Both figures are reported here with their citations.** The corroborator **could not independently corroborate the 5.1 % national figure**: ens.dk's live pages and Drivkraft Danmark do not state it, and retsinformation full text is 403-blocked. The difference is plausibly a scope adjustment (aviation excluded) but is unconfirmed — **this is the highest-risk open item in the Denmark report.**
- **RED III transposition (carried flag — positive):** **Denmark was the only member state to notify full RED III transposition by the 21 May 2025 deadline** (Commission statement 24 Jul 2025; no infringement). Vehicles: lov nr 1675 af 30/12/2024 + BEK 482/2025 + BEK 978→1401/2025 + HB 2025.1 (BEK 483/2025). This contrasts with Sweden and Finland, both of which have open Commission letters of formal notice.
- **UNCORROBORATED penalty provisions (carried flag — access blocked):** the exact **§§ and fine (bøde) levels** could not be verified because **retsinformation.dk returns HTTP 403** to fetch tools and the Lovtidende/BEK PDFs are binary/unparseable (no poppler); BEK 1401/2025, BEK 483/2025 and the current consolidated LBK could not be read at section level. The bøde mechanism itself is confirmed (danskelove.dk mirror, § 8).
- **UNCORROBORATED national 1G-crop ceiling (loft):** announced in the 2023 political agreement alongside the 5.2 % krav, but no binding value found in any primary or independent source; the 7 % RED reference is confirmed on ens.dk. **No national advanced-biofuel floor for 2025–2029** located, and **banking/carry-over rules** not identified.
- **PRIMARY-ONLY items** (retsinformation-blocked): Annex IX-B 1.7 % cap; RFNBO/PtX counting rules; palm/soy low-ILUC exception wording; blend-wall repeal; parent-act newer LBK number (Lovtidende PDF 252126 illegible).
- **Source discrepancy:** ens.dk pages still cite **BEK 978/2025** as the basis while retsinformation shows **BEK 1401 af 27/11/2025** as Gældende — ens.dk pages likely predate the November order (gazette wins per methodology).
- **Naming note:** BEK 482 af 15/05/2025 is the *displacement/sustainability* order (now Historisk); the *håndbog* order is **BEK 483 af 15/05/2025** (HB 2025.1) — an earlier brief conflated 482/483.
- **Certified grid biomethane** CO2-afgift treatment as motor fuel is `UNCORROBORATED` and actively contested (physical biogas is CO2-exempt; a certificate/mass-balance exemption for grid bionaturgas is not established). skm.dk 2025 petrol-CO2 "170.6 øre/L" extraction is a likely parsing artifact (2026/2027 figures agree exactly) — not a CONFLICT.
- **DG TAXUD** excise-duty-tables / TEDB cross-check failed technically (PDF binary, no poppler; TEDB JS-only) — corroboration met via two national Tier 1 sources (DJV + skm.dk); not a CONFLICT.

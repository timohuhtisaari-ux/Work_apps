### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- The ordinance transposes the RED GHG-saving ladder — **50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by installation vintage (70 % RFNBO). Exact article/figures not rendered this pass → PRIMARY-ONLY / framework. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1, S3]
- Producers and importers must **report the GHG intensity of biofuels delivered to Bulgarian territory in the previous year** to the competent authority. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- The ordinance states sustainability criteria are considered met when operators **apply voluntary national or international schemes recognised by the EU**; it provides the framework for **approval of certification schemes and certifying bodies**. Bulgaria therefore **accepts the EU-recognised voluntary schemes**. [CONFIRMED — S1, S3]
- Usable schemes: full DG ENER list — **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, KZR INiG**, etc. (accessed 2026-07-04). [CONFIRMED — S8]
- **Proof of sustainability:** a valid scheme certificate + PoS document per consignment under mass-balance chain of custody; the ordinance provides for issuance and withdrawal of **compliance certificates** (сертификат за съответствие) and independent audits. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1, S3]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE…) — accepted by the sustainability ordinance — and issue a **PoS per consignment** under mass balance. → approved certifying body.
2. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) and document them per consignment. → self + auditor.
3. **Report the annual GHG intensity** of biofuels delivered to the BG market (previous year) to the **Ministry of Environment and Water (MOEW)**. → MOEW.
4. **Provide the required soil/land/water/air-protection information** for feedstock production as prescribed by the ordinance. → self / MOEW.
5. **Undergo independent third-party audit** of the sustainability/GHG data (ZEVI + ordinance) and obtain/maintain the compliance certificate. → approved certifying body.
6. **Respect the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out and the 7 % crop cap**; use only EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/waste claims. → self + auditor.
7. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** and record in-scope transactions (operational since 15 Jan 2024; mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.

- Transposition: RED sustainability via the **Наредба за критериите за устойчивост на биогоривата** (amended ДВ No. 26/2024, in force 26 Mar 2024) under **ZEVI** + Clean Air Act; **28 RED III measures notified incl. new ZEVI (Jun 2025); Feb 2025 reasoned opinion (INFR(2024)0211) on permitting outstanding**.

- Registry/UDB: verifying authority = **Ministry of Environment and Water (MOEW)** (operators report annual GHG intensity); SEDA handles RES-electricity/GO only; **no dedicated national biofuel registry** found; UDB applies via EU law (operational 15 Jan 2024, mandatory date pending).
- Flags: ordinance PDF not machine-read (GHG thresholds/palm caps/reporting deadline PRIMARY-ONLY); RED III status corrected (Feb 2025 reasoned opinion on permitting, not a 24 Jul 2025 infringement); no national palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU; MOEW/Min.Energy/SEDA authority split to confirm.

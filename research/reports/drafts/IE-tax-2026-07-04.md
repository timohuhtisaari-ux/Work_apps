## 2. Fuel taxation

Ireland levies **Mineral Oil Tax (MOT)** on motor fuels under Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the **Finance Act 1999 (as amended)** — the national instrument implementing the Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC. Each MOT rate is the sum of a **non-carbon component** (Schedule 2 minus Schedule 2A) and a **carbon component / carbon tax** (Schedule 2A), the latter proportionate to CO2 emitted and set by a €-per-tonne-CO2 charge.

**Important timing:** From **25 March 2026** and again from **15 April 2026**, temporary reductions to the *non-carbon* component are in force. These cuts (with the NORA-levy cut) are legislated to be **reversed at the end of July 2026**, restoring the standard rates. The table below gives the **currently-in-force (15 Apr 2026) rates** and, in the notes, the standard rates that resume after reversal.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **Biofuel relief mechanism.** MOT law provides a partial relief for biofuels set equal to the *carbon component* — often called a "carbon tax relief." A biofuel released for consumption therefore pays only the **non-carbon component**; the effective rate tracks the non-carbon rate. This is a fuel-type relief, distinct from the **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)** certificate scheme (administered via NORA/Dept of Transport) — see the mandates report; there is no Swedish/Finnish-style "above-the-obligation only" restriction attached to the MOT relief itself.
- **Substitute fuels.** Fuels used as a propellant "instead of petrol/diesel" are taxed at the corresponding petrol/diesel MOT rate unless a relief (e.g. biofuel relief) is claimed.
- **Carbon-tax trajectory (legislated).** Finance Act 2020 set a 10-year path raising the carbon charge to **€100/t CO2 by 2030**. Propellant fuels (petrol, auto-diesel) step up each October (they moved to **€71/t on 8 Oct 2025**); non-propellant fuels normally step up on 1 May, but the **1 May 2026 increase was postponed to 14 October 2026** (to €71/t). `CONFIRMED` (legislated) — S1.
- **Temporary cuts reverse ~end July 2026.** The 25 Mar / 15 Apr 2026 non-carbon MOT cuts and the NORA-levy cut are set to end 31 Jul 2026, restoring petrol €706.14 / diesel €615.76. `CONFIRMED` — S1, S2.
- **Annual October Budget.** Standard-rate and carbon-component changes are made in the October Budget / Finance Act cycle.
- **ETS2.** EU emissions trading for road-transport fuels (ETS2) — allowance surrender from **2028** (2027 emissions; auctioning from 2027) under **Directive (EU) 2023/959 (ETS2, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC)**. [CORRECTION — the draft's "Regulation (EU) 2026/667" is the 2040 EU climate-target law, NOT ETS2.] `CONFIRMED` (EU-level). (V4, V5)

## Conflicts and gaps

- No conflict between Revenue's TDM (S1) and the Tier-2 Tax Foundation figure (S6, dated 1 Jan 2026): the €706.14 / €615.76 pair is the standard rate; the €502.88 / €371.85 pair is the current temporary rate. Difference is purely publication-date. **Note:** the *temporary* €502.88 / €371.85 split rests on the Revenue TDM (Tier 1) only; no independent per-1000L corroboration was located this pass, so those two rows are marked PRIMARY-ONLY.
- **CORRECTION (ETS2 legal basis):** Directive (EU) 2023/959, not Reg. (EU) 2026/667.
- The DG TAXUD "Excise Duty Tables — energy products" file retrieved (S8) is the **tax-receipts (revenues)** edition (Nov 2023), not the rates edition; a machine-readable rates edition PDF was not located this pass (TAXUD page exposes only revenues PDF + TEDB). National TDM (S1) used as the authoritative rate table.
- Secondary reporting of the headline cut ("17c petrol / 22c diesel", S5) blends MOT + NORA + VAT effects and does not map cleanly to the per-1000 L MOT non-carbon cuts; the per-1000 L component figures are taken from the Tier 1 TDM.

## 4-line summary

- **Petrol:** MOT €502.88/1000 L current (temporary, from 15 Apr 2026; non-carbon €338.58 + carbon €164.30; PRIMARY-ONLY); standard €706.14 resumes ~1 Aug 2026 (CONFIRMED).
- **Diesel:** MOT €371.85/1000 L current (temporary; non-carbon €181.81 + carbon €190.04; PRIMARY-ONLY); standard €615.76 resumes ~1 Aug 2026 (CONFIRMED).
- **Biofuel relief:** partial MOT relief equal to the carbon component, so biofuels/HVO pay only the non-carbon component; biomethane taxed as vehicle gas (€11.48/MWh GCV).
- **Flags:** temporary MOT + NORA-levy cuts reverse 31 Jul 2026 (temporary rates Tier-1 single-source); carbon charge €71/t rising to €100/t by 2030; **ETS2 legal basis corrected to Dir. (EU) 2023/959, surrender from 2028**; DG TAXUD rates edition not located.

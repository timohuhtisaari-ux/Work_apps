**Interaction with the mandate:** Estonia promotes biofuels through the biofuel/RES-share obligation under the Liquid Fuel Act (other agent's scope), *not* through excise relief — biofuels are taxed at the equivalent fossil rate, so there is no tax-side incentive layered on the mandate.

## National act implementing the Energy Taxation Directive (2003/96/EC)

- **Alcohol, Tobacco, Fuel and Electricity Excise Duty Act (ATKEAS)** — the national act setting motor-fuel excise and implementing Directive 2003/96/EC minimum rates. Consolidated text on Riigi Teataja (riigiteataja.ee). No notified biofuel state-aid decision applies (Estonia grants no biofuel excise relief). *(EUR-Lex NIM page for 2003/96/EC not separately fetched — minor gap.)*

## 4-line summary — Estonia

- **Petrol:** EUR 591 / 1000 L, in force since 1 July 2025 (ATKEAS); CONFIRMED.
- **Diesel:** EUR 428 / 1000 L, in force since 1 May 2025 (ATKEAS); CONFIRMED.
- **Biofuel relief:** none — biofuels/HVO/E85 taxed at the fossil rate they replace; incentive is via the mandate, not excise.
- **Flags:** the 1 May 2026 rise to 621/459 was CANCELLED (so current rates stand); 2027 step-ups (+10 % petrol/+15 % diesel) remain legislated; ETS2 obligation from 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959); DG TAXUD PDF cross-check unparseable (gap); biomethane excise treatment unconfirmed.

**Mandate interaction:** Cyprus operates a RES-T obligation on fuel suppliers; there is no excise incentive tied to
volumes above the obligation. Mandate detail is out of scope.

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Excise Duties Law of 2004 (Law 91(I)/2004)** and
its subsidiary orders; Cyprus additionally relies on the accession-era derogation in Directive 2004/75/EC. No
biofuel-specific notified state-aid decision identified.

## Gaps / conflicts

- **No clean Tier-1 fetch of the Cyprus rate table:** the Customs & Excise site (mof.gov.cy/mof/customs) returned
  **HTTP 503** on every attempt. The standard rates 429/400 EUR/1000 L are corroborated by **two independent
  Tier-2 sources** (fuel-prices.eu tax breakdown dated 29 Jun 2026, and Tax Foundation 2026 as at 1 Jan 2026),
  which agree exactly — but **no Tier-1 rate confirmation was obtained**. Corroborator also downloaded the DG TAXUD
  "Excise Duty Tables – Energy Products" PDF [V-TAXUD] but it is not parseable in this environment. Follow-up:
  retry mof.gov.cy customs tariff (ced06_en) and the DG TAXUD/TEDB excise-duty table.
- Whether the −8.33c/L reduction was extended past 30 Jun 2026 was not positively confirmed for the 4 Jul window;
  the fuel-prices.eu 29 Jun breakdown already showed the full 429/400 excise, suggesting the reduction had lapsed
  or was not netted there. Confirm with the Tax Department.

### 4-line summary — Cyprus
- Petrol excise: 429.00 EUR/1000 L (€0.429/L), standard rate (two independent Tier-2; no Tier-1 obtained).
- Diesel excise: 400.00 EUR/1000 L (€0.400/L), standard rate.
- Biofuel relief: none via excise — no reduced rate/exemption for biofuels or high blends identified (Cyprus relies on ETD derogation, not biofuel relief).
- Flags: rates corroborated by two Tier-2 sources but Tier-1 (mof.gov.cy / TAXUD) not reached; temporary −8.33c/L cut for Apr–Jun 2026 now expired; carbon tax on fuels PROPOSED; ETS2 established by Dir (EU) 2023/959, start postponed to 2028 / first surrender 2029.

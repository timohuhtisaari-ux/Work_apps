### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Biofuels count in fossil-fuel blends only if they meet the sustainability
  criteria of **arts. 3–7** (RGD; ref. to the 27 Feb 2011 text on the register
  page) — i.e. RED II sustainability + GHG-saving ladder. [CONFIRMED — environnement.public.lu]
- Threshold ladder follows RED II Art. 29(10): **50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by
  installation vintage, **70 %** RFNBOs. Exact per-article figures in the RGD
  3 Feb 2023 **not directly extracted** (cc.lu PDF was image/binary, not
  text-parseable) → `PRIMARY-ONLY`/flag. [FLAG]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Luxembourg accepts the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** to prove
  sustainability compliance — search corroboration names **ISCC, 2BSvs, REDcert,
  SURE** among recognised schemes. **No national scheme** identified; proof of
  sustainability ("attestation") travels down the chain to the final energy
  producer/importer. [PRIMARY-ONLY / Tier-2 corroborated — environnement.public.lu; scheme lists]
- Full EU-recognised list per DG ENER (accessed 2026-07-04) applies. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER]

| Item | Detail | Confidence | Sources |

| Who must register | Three account types: **Biofuel Producer** (producers), **Biofuel Trader** (importers), **Registered Oil Company** (blenders/petroleum-product importers performing final blending) | CONFIRMED | environnement.public.lu |
| Batch workflow | Each operator registers → producers/importers declare per-batch **volume + environmental characteristics** → batches (or partial batches) assigned to the next operator → recipient must approve the data transfer → chain continues until a Registered Oil Company does final blending at service stations | CONFIRMED | environnement.public.lu |
| Proof document | Sustainability **attestation** per batch, transmitted along the chain, enabling the final producer/importer to attest biomass origin + GHG reduction | PRIMARY-ONLY | environnement.public.lu; douanes |

### 3.5 Customs' verification role

- The **Administration des douanes et accises** verifies compliance with
  sustainability criteria at the point energy products are **declared for
  consumption** (by producer, importer or authorised warehouse-keeper); the
  transmission of sustainability-compliance information to Customs follows
  procedures set in a **circular** by the energy-taxation & environment bureau
  (e.g. **Circulaire 2025/C/17**, updated **2026/C/55** on energy products &
  electricity). [PRIMARY-ONLY — douanes.public.lu; circular]

### 3.6 Union Database (UDB) status

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Open the correct account** in the Administration de l'environnement biofuels register (Biofuel Producer / Biofuel Trader / Registered Oil Company). → Administration de l'environnement.
2. **Obtain EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC, 2BSvs, REDcert, SURE…) covering chain-of-custody / mass balance. → certification body.
3. **Register each batch** (volume + environmental characteristics), assign it down the chain, and ensure recipients approve transfers; carry the sustainability attestation to final blending. → register.
4. **Present sustainability proof to Customs** (Administration des douanes et accises) at declaration-for-consumption, following the applicable circular (2025/C/17 → 2026/C/55). → Customs.
5. **File the annual sustainability & GHG-reduction declaration by 1 May** of the following year. → Administration de l'environnement.
6. **Enter transaction/sustainability data in the UDB** for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → UDB [confirm LU mandatory-use date].

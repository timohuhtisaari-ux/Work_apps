### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** whoever is liable for energy tax (skattskyldig) on reduction-obligated
  petrol or diesel under the energy tax law; must register with the supervisory authority
  within two weeks of the obligation arising (Lag 2017:1201, 4 §). *Cross-reference: the
  obligated population is defined via the energy-tax liability — see the tax section.*
- **Administrator / registry:** Statens energimyndighet (Swedish Energy Agency) is the
  supervisory authority; no tradable-certificate registry — this is a supplier-level
  obligation, not a certificate scheme.
- **Reporting:** annual declaration to Energimyndigheten **by 1 April** of the year after
  the compliance year (per Energimyndigheten guidance/prop. 2020/21:180). Confidence:
  CONFIRMED (Energimyndigheten + Riksdagen prop.).
- **Flexibility:**
  - Surplus reduction on one fuel may be used for the other fuel and/or **transferred to
    another obligated party** for the same year (7 §).
  - **Banking:** surplus may be carried to the following year, capped at **10 % of the
    total required emission reduction** (Förordning 2025:589, 21 §). PRIMARY-ONLY.
  - **Elkrediter:** non-obligated charge-point operators supplying fossil-free electricity
    at public charging stations may transfer the emission reduction to obligated parties
    (6 §, from 1 Jul 2025).
- **Non-compliance:** reduktionspliktsavgift per kg CO2e shortfall (see table); authority
  may reduce/waive for special reasons (10 §).
- **Interaction with tax:** the reduktionsplikt is the volume driver; biofuel tax relief is
  limited to volumes outside the obligation (high-blend/pure biofuels). *See tax section.*

**RED III transposition status:** Partially transposed / in infringement. Sweden transposed
the transport-fuel provisions via SFS 2025:576, Förordning 2025:589 and Energimyndigheten
regulation STEMFS 2025:3 (in force 1/28 Jul 2025), i.e. **after** the 21 May 2025 deadline.
The Commission sent Sweden a **letter of formal notice on 24 July 2025** for failing to
fully transpose Directive (EU) 2023/2413 (Sweden was among 26 MS; only Denmark had notified
full transposition). Earlier: formal notice Sep 2024 + reasoned opinion Feb 2025 on the
RED III permitting provisions. Confidence: CONFIRMED (Commission + national acts).

## 5-line summary — Sweden (SE)
1. Obligation type: GHG-intensity reduction (reduktionsplikt) per fuel, on energy-tax-liable suppliers of petrol and diesel.
2. Current level (2026): 10 % for petrol and 10 % for diesel (in force since 1 Jul 2025; penalty 5/4 SEK per kg CO2e shortfall).
3. Next legislated change: none legislated after the 1 Jul 2025 step to 10 %; future design under review (Styrmedelsutredningen) — `PROPOSED`/pending only.
4. RED III status: transport provisions transposed late via SFS 2025:576/2025:589 + STEMFS 2025:3 (Jul 2025); Commission letter of formal notice 24 Jul 2025 — infringement open.
5. Flags: no `CONFLICT`; PRIMARY-ONLY on banking cap (10 %), high-ILUC rule and absence of sub-targets (gazette PDF not directly readable); GAIN corroboration unavailable (PDF), replaced by second Tier 1 sources.

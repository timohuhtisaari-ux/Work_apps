# Finland — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Finland (FI)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04)

## Executive summary

Finland's transport-biofuel obligation is an **energy-share distribution obligation**
(the *jakeluvelvoite*): a minimum share of the energy content of transport fuels
supplied for consumption must be sustainable renewable fuel. For 2026 the level is
**19.5 % (energy basis)**, with a **4.0 percentage-point advanced/Annex IX-A
sub-obligation (lisävelvoite)** and food/feed-crop counting capped at 2.6 pp. The
trajectory is legislated to rise steeply — 22.5 % (2027), 31 % (2028), 32 % (2029)
and 34 % from 2030 — plus a dedicated RFNBO minimum-share obligation from 2028. There
is **no biofuel tax exemption**; instead the CO2 component of the excise structurally
rewards biofuels — **R-category** (RED-sustainable) fuels pay half CO2 tax and
**T-category** (advanced/waste-based) fuels pay zero — and this relief applies to all
qualifying volumes including those counted toward the obligation. The core compliance
obligations on a fuel supplier are: register/notify Energiavirasto, hold an approved
*kestävyysjärjestelmä* (or an EU voluntary-scheme certificate), issue a
*kestävyystodistus* per consignment, file the annual distribution-obligation
declaration and the annual sustainability report by end-March, and onboard to the EU
Union Database. Finland transposed RED III's transport articles via the 841/2024
package (in force 1 Jan 2025) and its sustainability side via laki 500/2025, but has an
**open Commission letter of formal notice (24 Jul 2025)** for incomplete transposition.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** road-transport fuel distributors supplying for consumption **≥ 1 million
  litres/year** of motor petrol, diesel, natural gas, biofuels, biogas and RFNBOs (**9 GWh**
  threshold for gaseous fuels). Smaller distributors and RFNBO suppliers may opt in
  voluntarily (first voluntary RFNBO distributor approved by Energiavirasto).
  CONFIRMED (Energiavirasto).
- **Administrator:** Energiavirasto (Energy Authority) supervises, receives the annual
  **jakeluvelvoiteilmoitus** (declaration) — due **by the end of March** after the compliance
  year — and imposes seuraamusmaksu. No tradable certificate instrument; compliance is
  supplier-level energy accounting. Renewable share of grid electricity for transport-
  electricity accounting is published annually (2025: 53.34 %).
- **Flexibility:**
  - **Carry-over:** over-fulfilment may be counted toward the following year (historic rule:
    up to an amount equal to half of the year's obligation, per 2018 Tax Administration
    guidance) — current limit not re-verified; the Energiavirasto haitarit page checked by
    the corroborator does not state a carry-over figure. UNCORROBORATED.
  - **Joustomekanismi (from 2025):** a distributor may fulfil up to **5.5 percentage points**
    of its obligation by financing alternative emission-reduction measures in Finland's
    effort-sharing (later also land-use) sector; implementing decrees in force end-June 2025.
    CONFIRMED (Energiavirasto; TEM).
  - Renewable transport electricity via public charging points counts toward the general
    obligation (from 1 Jan 2025, 841/2024).
- **Related but separate:** heating/off-road **biofuel-oil distribution obligation** under
  laki 418/2019 (out of transport-mandate scope; flagged for completeness).
- **Tax interaction:** none direct — the jakeluvelvoite is a quota; fuel excise is separate
  (*see section 2*; CO2-component relief for sustainable biofuels affects economics).

## 2. Fuel taxation

Finland taxes motor fuels under the **Act on excise duty on liquid fuels
(1472/1994)** and the **Act on excise duty on electricity and certain fuels
(1260/1996)** — the national implementation of Directive 2003/96/EC. The excise
has three components: an **energy content tax** (uniform €/MJ across fuels, with
a fixed reduction for diesel-class fuels), a **CO2 tax** (€/tonne CO2-eq on
*lifecycle* emissions), and a **strategic stockpile fee (huoltovarmuusmaksu)**
levied on fossil **and** bio fuels. There is **no exemption for biofuels**;
instead the CO2 component structurally rewards them: **R-category** fuels
(RED-sustainability-compliant) pay **half** CO2 tax and **T-category** fuels
(advanced/waste-based, Annex IX A type) pay **zero** CO2 tax, and lower energy
content per litre means a lower per-litre energy tax. In 2026: the CO2 tax base
fell from €62.0 to **€56.8/t on 1 Jan 2026** (first stage of a two-step cut,
HE 132/2025), and the **stockpile fee was reformed to an energy-content basis
on 1 Apr 2026** — hence the current tax table "1 A" runs 1.4.2026–31.12.2026.

### 2.2 Interaction with the mandate

Finland's **jakeluvelvoite** (distribution obligation, Act 446/2007) is the
volume driver; taxation is complementary, not conditional. Unlike Sweden and
(historically) several other member states, the reduced CO2 rates for R/T fuels
are **not** limited to volumes beyond the obligation — the energy-tax model is
considered a general measure. Obligation levels and trajectories: see section 1.

### 3.3 Certification schemes and proof of sustainability

- **National route:** operator applies to **Energiavirasto for approval of its kestävyysjärjestelmä** (sustainability system: feedstock origin/traceability, mass balance (ainetase), GHG calculation). Approval requires a statement from an **Energiavirasto-approved verifier (todentaja)** and is valid **five years**. `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04); approval decisions published (T1).
- **EU voluntary schemes accepted:** operators may **alternatively demonstrate compliance via Commission-approved voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU etc.; 18 schemes recognised as of 2026-07-04). `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04) + DG ENER voluntary schemes list (T1, 2026-07-04). Finland runs **no separate national certification scheme beyond the Energiavirasto approval system**.
- **Proof per consignment:** an operator with an approved system issues a **kestävyystodistus** (sustainability certificate) for each batch of biofuel/bioliquid/biomass fuel or raw material; this is the document that carries sustainability characteristics between operators and underpins jakeluvelvoite/tax claims (cross-ref sections 1 & 2). `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **Verifiers:** must hold **FINAS accreditation** (or equivalent), liability insurance and technical competence; approved verifiers include AFRY Finland Oy, Enemi Oy, Inspecta Sertifiointi Oy (Kiwa). Verifiers audit the system at approval and periodically; audit reports accompany annual filings. `CONFIRMED` — Energiavirasto sustainability page + todentajaohje (T1, 2026-07-04); Enemi verifier page (T2, 2026-07-04).

### 3.4 National registry and Union Database (UDB)

- **National registry:** no per-consignment national register. Filings go through **Energiavirasto's e-services**: the **KEKRI** system (biomass plants) and the Energiavirasto reporting portal (other operators). `PRIMARY-ONLY` — Energiavirasto sustainability page (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **UDB in Finnish law:** act 500/2025 consolidated UDB duties in the kestävyys framework (the previous UDB provision, jakeluvelvoitelaki 7 § 7 mom., was **repealed** on 4 Jul 2025). Economic operators handling **liquid and gaseous renewable fuels** (biofuels, biogas/biomethane, RFNBOs — transport and, after RED III, other uses) must **record transactions and sustainability characteristics in the UDB without delay**; HE 37/2025 notes even raw-material suppliers not separately certified nationally may have UDB recording duties. `CONFIRMED` for the obligation's existence (Finlex 2025/500 + TEM + Energiavirasto Kestävyysinfo, all T1, 2026-07-04); `PRIMARY-ONLY` for the exact section number (full statute text not machine-readable — see gaps).
- **Practical status:** Energiavirasto instructs distributors that liquid and gaseous fuels counted toward the distribution obligation must be reported to the UDB, particularly for cross-border trade; onboarding via webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase (EU Login). `PRIMARY-ONLY` — Energiavirasto Kestävyysinfo 7.10.2025 / jakeluvelvoite reporting webinar (T1, 2026-07-04).

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Determine coverage:** if you distribute ≥ 1 million litres/year of transport fuel (or ≥ 9 GWh gaseous), you are an obligated distributor under the jakeluvelvoite (Act 446/2007); smaller distributors and RFNBO suppliers may opt in voluntarily. Authority: **Energiavirasto**.
2. **Register / notify Energiavirasto** as a distributor (and as an economic operator under the kestävyyslaki 393/2013 as amended by 500/2025) before supplying obligated fuel.
3. **Obtain sustainability certification:** either secure **Energiavirasto approval of a kestävyysjärjestelmä** (with a FINAS-accredited verifier statement; renew every 5 years) **or** hold a valid **EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certificate (e.g. ISCC EU)** covering your chain.
4. **Issue/require a kestävyystodistus for every consignment** counted toward the obligation, and maintain the mass balance (ainetase). Authority: Energiavirasto.
5. **Register excise-duty liability** with the **Finnish Tax Administration (Vero)** under Acts 1472/1994 / 1260/1996; correctly classify biofuels as R (half CO2 tax) or T (zero CO2 tax) — this requires the sustainability documentation from step 3/4.
6. **For borderline feedstocks (wastes/residues, Annex IX),** request a binding **ennakkotieto** ruling from Energiavirasto before counting them.
7. **Onboard to the EU Union Database** (webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase via EU Login) and record transactions of liquid/gaseous renewable fuels without delay, incl. cross-border movements (European Commission UDB, supervised by Energiavirasto).
8. **File the annual jakeluvelvoiteilmoitus (distribution-obligation declaration) to Energiavirasto by the end of March** for the preceding compliance year; a shortfall triggers seuraamusmaksu.
9. **File the annual kestävyyskriteeriselvitys (sustainability report) to Energiavirasto by 31 March** via KEKRI/portal, with the verifier audit report attached.
10. **Keep annual forest/agricultural biomass sourcing attestations** (RED III land criteria) and ensure new-scope activities met the **31 Dec 2025** application deadline and the **1 Jan 2026** 80 % biomass GHG tier where applicable.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **No unresolved source CONFLICT for Finland.** Values are consistent across the mandates, tax and sustainability drafts.
- **RED III infringement (carried flag):** Finland transposed the RED III transport articles via the 841/2024 package (in force 1 Jan 2025) and its sustainability side via laki 500/2025 (in force 4 Jul 2025), but the Commission issued a **letter of formal notice on 24 July 2025** for incomplete transposition of Directive (EU) 2023/2413 — infringement is **open**. (Denmark was the only one of the three countries to notify full transposition on time.)
- **UNCORROBORATED / gaps (carried flag — primary-text access blocked):** several items could not be quoted from the promulgated act text because **Finlex is JS-rendered (WebFetch returns navigation only)** and its PDFs are binary/unparseable (no poppler), with eduskunta.fi returning 403 and Edilex paywalled.

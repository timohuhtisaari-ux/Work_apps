# Chile — Biofuel Regulation Headline Report
**Date:** 2026-07-15 | **Researched by:** Non-EU biofuel mandate researcher

---

## 1. Instrument type & 2026 headline obligation

**No mandatory biofuel blending quota.** Ethanol blending is voluntary in Chile; only E2 or E5 blends are permitted by technical standard (biodiesel consumption is negligible). There is no legal mandate requiring suppliers to blend ethanol or biodiesel into transport fuels. The country has instead adopted sector-specific initiatives (notably the SAF 2050 roadmap for aviation).

---

## 2. Trajectory / 2030 target

No legislated increase in biofuel mandates is envisaged. Chile's biofuel policy emphasis has shifted to **Sustainable Aviation Fuels (SAF)**:
- **SAF 2050 Roadmap** (announced April 2024): Targets 50% of jet fuel replaced by SAF from oils, fats, biological, and municipal waste by 2050
- **Interim milestones:** Pilot SAF production facility by 2030
- **General energy target:** 70% renewable electricity by 2030; broader decarbonization by 2050

The voluntary ethanol blending allowance (E2/E5) is not expected to change.

---

## 3. Advanced/sub-quotas if any

Not applicable — no mandatory quota exists from which to carve advanced sub-targets. The SAF roadmap is aspirational but does not impose a compliance obligation on fuel suppliers.

---

## 4. Fuel-tax treatment of biofuels

**No specific tax relief or incentive found in live official sources as of 2026-07-15.** Standard excise duties apply to all motor fuels. Chile's Ministry of Energy (MinEnergía) provides financial support to SAF R&D projects through funding programs, but no preferential excise or import duty relief is documented for ethanol or biodiesel in commercial transport fuels.

---

## 5. Sustainability/certification regime

**National System established:** Ministerial Decree of 14 November 2019 established a national certification system for the sustainability of biofuels (Decreto Ministerial 2019/11/14). The system:
- Measures and verifies environmental performance across the fuel lifecycle (raw materials, production, end use)
- Aligns with international certification practices
- Chilean producers (e.g., HIF Global for e-fuels in Magallanes) participate in **ISCC EU RFNBO** certification for export compliance

No domestic mandatory sustainability standard is enforced on voluntary blends.

## 8. Five-line summary

1. **Obligation type:** No mandatory quota; voluntary ethanol (E2/E5) blending only.
2. **Current level (2026):** Zero mandatory obligation; ethanol blending is at supplier/market discretion.
3. **Next legislated change:** None planned; SAF 2050 roadmap is aspirational, not binding.
4. **RED III status:** Not applicable — Chile is not an EU member and does not implement EU Renewable Energy Directive.
5. **Key status flags:** GAP on fuel taxation incentive; no mandate enforcement mechanism; policy pivot to high-value SAF over mass-market terrestrial biofuels.

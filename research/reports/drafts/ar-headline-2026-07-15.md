# Argentina: Biofuel Mandates—Headline Report (2026-07-15)

## 1. Instrument Type & 2026 Headline Obligation

**Primary Instrument:** Blending mandate via Ley 27.640/2021 (Biofuels Regulatory Framework), administered by the Secretaría de Energía (Ministry of Economy, Energy Secretariat).

**2026 Headline Obligation:**
- **Bioethanol:** 12% mandatory blend with gasoline (E12).
- **Biodiesel:** 7.5% mandatory blend with diesel (B7.5); previously 5%, increased by Decree 438/2022 (effective 16 Jun 2022).

**Legal Basis:** 
- Ley 27.640, promulgated 4 Aug 2021, effective 5 Aug 2021.
- Decree 438/2022 (increased biodiesel from 5% to 7.5%, effective 16 Jun 2022).
- Secretaría de Energía Resolutions 24/2026 and 25/2026 (Feb 2026): Minimum acquisition prices for bioethanol and biodiesel (administrative, not mandate-level changes).

**Obligated Parties:** Fuel importers and distributors; blending occurs at distribution/retail level.

## 3. Advanced-Biofuel Sub-Quotas & Caps

**No explicit advanced-biofuel (Annex IX-A equivalent) sub-quota found** in Ley 27.640 or implementing decrees.

**Feedstock Notes:**
- **Bioethanol:** Predominantly corn-based (c. 70–80% of production), with smaller sugarcane component.
- **Biodiesel:** 100% soy-oil based (Argentina's soy-crushing sector is mature and dominant).
- **No crop-based cap:** Law does not restrict feedstock mix or impose caps on corn/soy; blending is open to all sources meeting minimum GHG saving criteria.
- **No Annex IX Part B cap** (EU framework N/A; Argentina has no domestic equivalent).

## 4. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

**Tax Status & History:**

Ley 27.640 grants **exemption from**:
- **Impuesto a las Transacciones Financieras (ITF)** — liquid fuel transaction tax (historical).
- **CO2 tax** — carbon dioxide tax (where applicable).

**Enforcement & Suspension Timeline:**
- **July 2021–Feb 2024:** Tax collection suspended by prior government (pandemic/inflation control).
- **Feb 2024 onward:** Current administration began incrementally restoring tax collection in tranches (as of 2026, collection ongoing but not yet at full rates).
- **Status 2026:** Biofuels remain *nominally* exempted by law, but tax landscape in flux.

**Provincial Tax Incentives:**
- **La Pampa province (2026):** Reduced gross revenue tax at 0.50% for biofuel manufacturing (temporary, Jan–Dec 2026).

**Export Duties:**
- **Biodiesel:** Recent decree sets 0% export tax for biodiesel from alternative oils (safflower, rapeseed, carinata, camelina) to promote new value chains and SAF feedstocks.

## 5. Sustainability & Certification Regime

**Voluntary Certification Schemes (Not Mandated by Ley 27.640):**
- **ISCC EU:** Primary international scheme for export-destined biofuels; widely used by Argentine biodiesel and ethanol producers.
- **2BSvs (Biomass Biofuel Sustainability Voluntary Scheme):** French scheme; used alongside ISCC.

**GHG Performance Standards:**
- **Biodiesel:** 60% GHG saving minimum to qualify for tax cuts (post-2018 rule); Argentine plants average 70% GHG reduction.
- **Bioethanol (corn):** 70–75% GHG saving average across audited plants; exporters report 72% average.
- **Bioethanol (sugarcane):** Limited domestic production; typically 60–70% GHG saving.

**No National GHG-Intensity Mandate in Ley 27.640:**
- The law does not prescribe minimum GHG-saving thresholds for domestic blending (unlike EU RED II/III).
- Domestic biofuel compliance is volume-based (E12, B7.5 only); GHG verification applies only to exports (via ISCC, 2BSvs).

**Feed Stock & Land-Use Safeguards:**
- No explicit sustainability criteria in Ley 27.640 for domestic market.
- ISCC/2BSvs producers avoid post-2008 land conversion (EU RED II eligibility); but this is voluntary, not mandated by Argentine law.

## 8. Five-Line Summary

Argentina mandates **E12 ethanol** and **B7.5 biodiesel** under Ley 27.640/2021, with no legislated step-up trajectory (law expires 31 Dec 2030 unless extended). Aspirational government targets (E15 by 2026, E20/B20 by 2030) are **NOT legislated**; a Senate Bill proposing E18/B18 remains **PROPOSED** and not yet adopted. No domestic GHG-intensity mandate; biofuels for export comply via ISCC/2BSvs certification (70% biodiesel, 70–75% corn-ethanol GHG saving average). Biofuels are **nominally exempted** from fuel tax and CO2 tax under Ley 27.640, though tax collection was suspended (2021–Feb 2024) and restarted in tranches (Feb 2024 onward); current rate status **unclear**. **CONFIRMED** on E12/B7.5 legal baseline; **UNCORROBORATED** on aspirational targets and plant-level GHG audits; **GAP** on post-2030 trajectory and Senate Bill adoption status.

## Cross-References & Further Notes

- **No EU RED/ETS2 applicability:** Argentina is not an EU member; RED II/III and ETS2 do not apply. Ley 27.640 is Argentina's standalone instrument.
- **Export compliance:** Biodiesel and ethanol destined for EU markets must meet RED II/III sustainability criteria independently (via ISCC, 2BSvs).
- **MERCOSUR dynamics:** Argentina is MERCOSUR member; trade policy coordination with Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay may influence future amendments (not yet enacted as mandate changes).
- **Pending climate policy updates:** Argentina's Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC) and climate plan may trigger future biofuel mandate increases; not yet translated into law.

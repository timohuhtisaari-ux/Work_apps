### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Poland runs a **national voluntary scheme — KZR INiG** (System Certyfikacji
  Zrównoważonej Produkcji Biopaliw, INiG-PIB / Oil & Gas Institute), which is
  **EU-recognised** by the Commission (recognition 12 Apr 2022, extended
  14 Dec 2022) and covers agricultural/forest biomass, wastes & residues,
  biofuels, bioliquids, biomass fuels, biogas/biomethane. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER
  voluntary-schemes page; KZR INiG]
- KZR INiG is registered in the **KOWR register of certification-system
  administrators** under **BIO/ASC/001/2014** and in the German **Nabisy**
  database as **EU-BM-22**. [PRIMARY-ONLY — KZR INiG / TÜV SÜD PL / HACCP-Polska]
- Poland **also accepts the EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU,
  REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, etc.); their administrators must appear in KOWR's register
  of certification-system administrators to operate for the Polish market.
  [PRIMARY-ONLY (flag — full KOWR administrator list not machine-fetched)]
- Proof-of-sustainability document per consignment: scheme **certificate**
  plus **sustainability declaration / poświadczenie** (proof-of-sustainability,
  "dokument potwierdzający spełnienie kryteriów zrównoważonego rozwoju") issued
  along the chain of custody. [PRIMARY-ONLY — KZR INiG]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- The EU **Union Database for Biofuels (UDB)** opened for voluntary login
  **16 Jan 2023**; **KZR INiG scheme participants / certification units were
  onboarded 28 Feb 2023**; **mandatory transaction recording began 3 Apr 2023**
  for liquid and gaseous transport biofuels/bioliquids. Legal basis RED II
  **Art. 28(2)** (now Art. 31a RED as amended) + Implementing Reg. (EU) 2022/996.
  [PRIMARY-ONLY — KZR INiG UDB page; Krajowa Izba Biopaliw]
- **Who must use it:** certified economic operators dealing in liquid/gaseous
  transport biofuels. **Solid biomass and biogas for (non-transport) energy are
  excluded.** [PRIMARY-ONLY — Krajowa Izba Biopaliw]
- Operators record transactions and sustainability/GHG properties of each
  consignment in the UDB in addition to their scheme documentation.
  [PRIMARY-ONLY]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Register with KOWR** in the appropriate register (producer / importer / NCW-realising entity) before placing biocomponents or biofuels on the Polish market. → KOWR.
2. **Obtain certification** under a scheme registered in KOWR's administrator register — the national **KZR INiG** scheme or an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs). → chosen scheme / certifying body.
3. **Issue and carry proof-of-sustainability** (scheme certificate + poświadczenie) per consignment on a full mass-balance basis. → scheme.
4. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving threshold** (50 / 60 / 65 %) for your installation vintage; exclude high-ILUC (palm) volumes per Del. Reg. 2019/807. → self + auditor.
5. **Record transactions and sustainability data in the UDB** (mandatory since 3 Apr 2023 for liquid/gaseous transport biofuels). → UDB.
6. **File the quarterly producer report to KOWR within 45 days** of each quarter (or a nil statement), and undergo the periodic certification audit. → KOWR / auditor.

**4-line summary — Poland**
- Transposition: RED II via the 2006 Act on Biocomponents and Liquid Biofuels (+ Energy Law), as amended by Dz.U. 2025 poz. 303. **RED III NOT transposed — open infringement (formal notice 24 Jul 2025); transposition still a draft (UC106, planned entry 1 Jan 2027).** [CORRECTED]
- Certification: national **KZR INiG** scheme (EU-recognised) plus accepted EU voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs); scheme administrators must be in KOWR's register; proof = scheme certificate + poświadczenie on mass balance.
- Registry/UDB: KOWR keeps the statutory registers (producers/importers/NCW/scheme admins/certifiers) + Nabisy (EU-BM-22); UDB transaction recording mandatory since 3 Apr 2023 for liquid/gaseous transport biofuels.
- Flags: article-level Act citations & UDB/Nabisy interplay unpinned; KOWR administrator list not fetched; annual NCW deadline/penalties unconfirmed; no PL-specific palm/soy extras found.

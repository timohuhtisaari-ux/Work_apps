# Malta (MT) — Fuel taxation (Section 2)

- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** fuel-tax-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Currency/unit:** EUR per 1000 L (euro-area).

> **Key structural note:** Malta fixes pump prices administratively (long-frozen at ~€1.34/L petrol and ~€1.21/L
> diesel; the State absorbs wholesale volatility). Statutory excise has correspondingly been stable at the **EU
> minimum** levels since Legal Notice 429 of 2021.

**Mandate interaction:** Malta runs an annual substitution obligation on fuel importers/wholesalers; no excise
incentive is tied to volumes above the obligation. Mandate detail is out of scope.

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Excise Duty Act (Cap 382)** and its subsidiary
legal notices; Malta additionally relies on the accession-era derogation in Directive 2004/74/EC. No biofuel-specific
notified state-aid decision identified.

## Gaps / conflicts

- **CONFLICT on the headline rates.** Two independent Tier-2 sources put Malta at the EU minimum —
  **359 EUR/1000 L petrol and 330 EUR/1000 L diesel** (Bloomberg Tax reporting LN 429/2021; Tax Foundation 2026).
  A third source, fuel-prices.eu (29 Jun 2026), instead shows excise of **€0.549/L petrol and €0.472/L diesel**.
  Assessment: the fuel-prices.eu figures are almost certainly a **mis-derived residual** (fixed pump price minus
  18% VAT minus assumed product cost), not statutory excise; the 359/330 EU-minimum figures are consistent with
  LN 429/2021 and with Malta's long-standing minimum-rate policy. Reported here as 359/330, flagged CONFLICT for
  the corroborator to close with a Tier-1 read of the Cap 382 Fifth Schedule.
- **No clean Tier-1 fetch:** legislation.mt served only the Cap 382 index/metadata (the schedules did not render
  via WebFetch), and the LN 429/2021 PDF returned metadata only. Follow-up: read Cap 382 Fifth Schedule and
  LN 429/2021 full text (or the DG TAXUD excise table) to upgrade to CONFIRMED.
- DG TAXUD excise-tables PDF could not be parsed locally (binary; no PDF page-rendering available).

### 4-line summary — Malta
- Petrol excise: 359.00 EUR/1000 L (EU minimum), stable since LN 429/2021.
- Diesel excise: 330.00 EUR/1000 L (EU minimum), stable since LN 429/2021.
- Biofuel relief: none via excise — no reduced rate/exemption identified; Malta uses ETD derogation + a substitution obligation, not tax incentives.
- Flags: CONFLICT — fuel-prices.eu shows €0.549/€0.472 (assessed as a mis-derived residual) vs the EU-minimum 359/330 from two other sources; Tier-1 read of Cap 382 Fifth Schedule owed; no separate CO2 tax; ETS2 surrender from 2028 (Dir (EU) 2023/959).

### Notes
- **In-force rates on 4 Jul 2026:** petrol €363.02/1000 L, diesel €330.29/1000 L — stable,
  essentially at the ETD 2003/96/EC minimums, and now denominated in euro.
- The euro-adoption changeover (dual circulation Jan 2026; mandatory dual pricing 8 Aug 2025–
  8 Aug 2026) redenominated the ЗАДС amounts; there was no substantive change to the motor-fuel
  excise burden — €363.02 = BGN 710 and €330.29 = BGN 646 at the fixed 1.95583 rate.
- Interaction with the mandate: Bulgaria's biofuel share is delivered through the RES blending
  obligation (cross-ref mandates agent); no excise incentive is tied to above-mandate volumes.

## 4-line summary — Bulgaria
- Petrol (unleaded): €363.02/1000 L (= BGN 710.00), 2026, unchanged and at ~EU minimum — CONFIRMED (PwC + Tax Foundation + Access2Markets).
- Diesel (gas oil): €330.29/1000 L (= BGN 646.00), 2026, at the EU minimum — CONFIRMED.
- Biofuel relief: none for road blends (taxed at full fuel rate); biogas historically zero-rated; promotion via the RES blending obligation, not excise (no above-mandate break).
- Flags: rates now denominated in EUR after 1 Jan 2026 euro adoption (Tier-1 confirmed; no substantive change); ЗАДС gradual-increase schedule 2025–2029 targets other energy products, not motor petrol/diesel; no separate CO2 tax; ETS2 from 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959, NOT Reg. 2026/667); official ЗАДС/DG TAXUD PDFs unrenderable (poppler).

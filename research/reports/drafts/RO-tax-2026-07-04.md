### Notes
- **In-force rates on 4 Jul 2026:** petrol 3,059.80 lei/1000 L; diesel 2,804.29 lei/1000 L
  (the O.U.G. 24/2026 reduction to 2,504.29 lapsed 1 Jul 2026, so the full standard diesel
  excise is back — sources report a ~0.36 lei/L pump increase from 1 Jul).
- Distinct from tax: O.U.G. 19/2026 (1 Apr–30 Jun 2026) capped the commercial margin on fuels but
  **did not change excise or VAT** (cross-ref, not a tax rate). A separate ARR compensation scheme
  (compensareacciza.arr.ro) partially offsets diesel excise for hauliers.
- Interaction with the mandate: Romania's biofuel uptake is a blending obligation set by
  Government Decision (cross-ref mandates agent); no excise incentive tied to above-mandate volumes.

## 4-line summary — Romania
- Petrol (benzină fără plumb): 3,059.80 lei/1000 L (≈ €0.602/L), from 1 Jan 2026, frozen for 2026.
- Diesel (motorină): 2,804.29 lei/1000 L (≈ €0.552/L) standard, back in force from 1 Jul 2026 (temp reduction to 2,504.29 under O.U.G. 24/2026 expired 30 Jun 2026).
- Biofuel relief: none for road blends (taxed at full fuel rate); reductions/exemptions only for aviation, maritime/Danube navigation, and marked agricultural diesel — no above-mandate biofuel break.
- Flags: 2026 excise frozen (no CPI indexation; resumes 2027); diesel-reduction extension blocked in Parliament; no separate CO2 tax; ETS2 from 1 Jan 2028; official gazette numeric to be confirmed by corroborator.

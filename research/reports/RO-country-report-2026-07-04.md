# Romania — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Romania (RO)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Romania imposes a **volumetric blending obligation** (% v/v) on operators
placing petrol and diesel on the market, set by **Emergency Ordinance (OUG)
80/2018** and overseen by the **Ministry of Energy** (not ANRE), alongside a 6 %
FQD lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction. The standing levels are **petrol ≥8 % v/v
and diesel ≥6.5 % v/v** biofuel, but a **2026 crisis derogation (OUG 19/2026)
temporarily allows the petrol figure to fall to a minimum of 2 %** for the
1 Apr–30 Jun 2026 window (extendable in ≤3-month blocks). There is **no biofuel
excise relief**: bioethanol/biodiesel/HVO in road fuel is taxed at the full rate
(petrol 3,059.80 lei/1000 L; diesel back to 2,804.29 lei/1000 L from 1 Jul 2026
after the OUG 24/2026 reduction lapsed), with 2026 excise frozen (no CPI
indexation until 2027). Sustainability compliance runs through the OUG 80/2018
**lifecycle-traceability-bulletin** (*buletinul de trasabilitate a ciclului de
viață*) accompanying each batch to final sale, plus EU-recognised voluntary
schemes and a mandatory annual third-party audit reported to the Ministry of
Energy by 1 March. **RED III is NOT transposed** — Romania is at the
letter-of-formal-notice stage only (no reasoned opinion) — and ETS2 (Directive
(EU) 2023/959) adds a fuel-supplier surrender obligation from 2028.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Romania runs a **volumetric blending obligation** (% v/v) on suppliers placing
petrol and diesel on the market, set by **Emergency Ordinance (OUG) 80/2018**
(the framework transposing the Fuel Quality Directive / RED sustainability +
GHG-monitoring mechanism, amending Law 220/2008 on RES promotion). The
competent authority under OUG 80/2018 is the **Ministry of Energy**. Note a
**2026 crisis derogation** below (OUG 19/2026) that temporarily lowers the
petrol figure.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** economic operators placing petrol and diesel on the
  Romanian market (refiners/importers/distributors at release for consumption).
- **Metric:** minimum biofuel content by **volume** (% v/v) in each fuel grade,
  plus a lifecycle **6 % GHG-intensity reduction** vs the 2010 fossil baseline
  (OUG 80/2018 Art. 9, FQD Art. 7a mechanism).
- **Authority:** **Ministry of Energy** is the "autoritate competentă"
  (OUG 80/2018 Art. 2, Art. 7) for monitoring, quality verification and
  enforcement. **ANRE** (in the source registry) is Romania's energy regulator
  but its role here is primarily RES-electricity / guarantees of origin — ANRE is
  **not** the competent authority for the fuel blending obligation under
  OUG 80/2018 (flag for registry note).
- **Certificates/registry/banking:** no tradable-certificate system identified;
  compliance is a direct product-standard obligation verified on fuel batches.
  (No certificate scheme confirmed — gap.)

- **OUG 19/2026 crisis measure (context):** the government declared a crisis on
  the petrol/diesel market (MO Part I No. 237, 26 Mar 2026), capping commercial
  mark-ups and **allowing operators to cut biofuel content in petrol from 8 % to
  a minimum of 2 %** for the crisis window (declared to 30 Jun 2026, extendable
  in periods of up to 3 months). Diesel's 6.5 % is not addressed by the measure.
  The 2 % petrol floor is a temporary derogation from the OUG 80/2018 mandate and
  has drawn concerns about consistency with EU (RED/FQD) obligations.
- **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition status:** **not transposed.**
  EUR-Lex NIM for 2023/2413 shows no notified Romanian measures. Romania is at the
  **letter-of-formal-notice stage only** (2024 package). It did **NOT** receive a
  RED III reasoned opinion — Romania is **not** on the February 2025 permitting
  reasoned-opinion list (Bulgaria, Spain, France, Italy, Cyprus, Netherlands,
  Slovakia, Sweden) **nor** on the December 2025 reasoned-opinion list (Greece,
  France, Italy, Cyprus, Hungary, Malta, Poland, Portugal). The earlier draft's
  "additional reasoned opinions" for Romania was **overstated** and has been
  corrected. Status: **in infringement — letter of formal notice.**
- **EU cross-cut:** **ETS2** — the emissions-trading system for road transport and
  buildings fuels = **Chapter IVa of Directive 2003/87/EC, inserted by Directive
  (EU) 2023/959**. Surrender obligation on road-fuel suppliers starts **2028**
  (postponed one year from 2027 in March 2026); trading from 2027.

### Notes
- **In-force rates on 4 Jul 2026:** petrol 3,059.80 lei/1000 L; diesel 2,804.29 lei/1000 L
  (the O.U.G. 24/2026 reduction to 2,504.29 lapsed 1 Jul 2026, so the full standard diesel
  excise is back — corroborator confirmed via multiple Romanian outlets reporting a ~0.36 lei/L
  pump increase from 1 Jul: avocatnet, radioconstanta, adevarul, business24).
- Distinct from tax: O.U.G. 19/2026 (1 Apr–30 Jun 2026) capped the commercial margin on fuels but
  **did not change excise or VAT** (cross-ref, not a tax rate). A separate ARR compensation scheme
  (compensareacciza.arr.ro) partially offsets diesel excise for hauliers.
- Interaction with the mandate: Romania's biofuel uptake is a blending obligation (OUG 80/2018);
  no excise incentive tied to above-mandate volumes.

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **FQD lifecycle target:** suppliers must reduce transport-fuel GHG intensity **by up to 10 %, minimum 6 %**, vs the fossil baseline by 31 Dec 2020 (OUG 80/2018 §9(1)). [CONFIRMED — S1, S2]
- **Biofuel GHG-saving criteria:** biofuels must meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds (**50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by installation vintage; **70 %** RFNBO) to count and to be recorded in the traceability bulletin. Exact figures not quoted from the RO text this pass → PRIMARY-ONLY / framework. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- OUG 80/2018 **§12(8)** accepts **voluntary schemes recognised by the European Commission** as proof of sustainability without requiring additional evidence. [CONFIRMED — S2]
- Usable schemes therefore include the full DG ENER list: **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, KZR INiG**, etc. (accessed 2026-07-04). [CONFIRMED — S6]
- Romania also references a **national certification scheme** (compliance certificates valid **5 years**, maintained by periodic audits) operating alongside the voluntary schemes. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2, S7]
- **Proof of sustainability per consignment:** the **buletinul de trasabilitate a ciclului de viață** (lifecycle traceability bulletin) recording sustainability-criteria compliance and GHG calculation at each lifecycle stage on RO territory; it must **accompany the biofuel — regardless of blend proportion — until sale to the final consumer** (§12(3)). Voluntary-scheme certificates/PoS substitute for the sustainability evidence per §12(8). [CONFIRMED — S2]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Meet the volumetric blending obligation** on every petrol/diesel batch
   placed on the RO market: petrol **≥8 % v/v** (temporarily **≥2 %** under the
   OUG 19/2026 crisis derogation, 1 Apr–30 Jun 2026, extendable in ≤3-month
   blocks) and diesel **≥6.5 % v/v**, plus the FQD **≥6 % lifecycle GHG-intensity
   reduction**; respect the 7 % crop cap and B7 blend wall. Breaches → fines
   70,000–100,000 RON. → Ministry of Energy.
2. **Accompany each biofuel batch with a valid *buletinul de trasabilitate a
   ciclului de viață*** recording sustainability-criteria compliance and
   lifecycle GHG, from placing-on-market to sale to the final consumer (OUG
   80/2018 §12(3)). → self / Ministry of Energy.
3. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert,
   2BSvs…) — accepted as full proof of sustainability under §12(8) — or use the
   national certification scheme (5-yr certificate + periodic audit). →
   certification body.
4. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 %; RFNBO 70 %; FQD
   supplier ≥6 % lifecycle reduction) and document them per batch. → self +
   auditor.
5. **Commission the annual independent third-party audit** (accredited under
   Reg. (EC) 765/2008); submit the audit report **by 1 March**. → accredited
   verifier.
6. **File the annual supplier report** to the Ministry of Energy in the
   **15 January – 1 March** window. → Ministry of Energy.
7. **Respect the 7 % crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out**; use only
   EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/waste claims. → self + auditor.
8. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** and record in-scope transactions
   (operational since 15 Jan 2024; mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
9. **Prepare for ETS2** (Directive (EU) 2023/959, Chapter IVa of Dir.
   2003/87/EC): road-fuel-supplier surrender obligation from **2028**, trading
   from 2027. → national ETS2 authority.
10. **Note the tax position:** no biofuel excise relief — bioethanol/biodiesel/
    HVO in road fuel is taxed at the full rate (petrol 3,059.80 / diesel 2,804.29
    lei/1000 L, frozen for 2026); standard excise obligations only. → ANAF.

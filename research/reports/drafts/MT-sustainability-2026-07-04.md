### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **Biofuels/bioliquids:** RED thresholds apply via S.L. 545.37 (while in force) / its replacement — **50 %** (pre-5 Oct 2015 installations), **60 %** (6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021); **70 %** RFNBO/RCF. Not quoted from the MT text (legislation.mt JS-only) → framework-level. [PRIMARY-ONLY / framework — S2, S7]
- **Supplier FQD target:** ≥6 % lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction vs the 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ baseline applies as EU baseline; MT-specific provision not read this pass. [UNCORROBORATED — S7]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- Malta follows the standard RED II model: economic operators demonstrate sustainability via **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (or equivalent), producing a **Proof of Sustainability** per consignment. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S7]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S1]
- Given Malta imports essentially all its biofuel, sustainability compliance rests on **imported PoS/voluntary-scheme documentation** passed to the obligated importer/wholesaler. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB established under **RED III Art. 31a** (formal deadline 21 Nov 2024; operational from Jan 2024); chain-of-custody rules in **Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996 Art. 18**; collectors/EOs onboarded via **voluntary schemes** through 2024–2025; **mandatory-use/sanction date being finalised** by the Commission. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S1, S8, S9]
- **MT operators are in scope via EU law.** With RED III not transposed (and MT at the CJEU-referral stage), **no MT-specific UDB implementing provision** exists; the obligation applies directly. Operators risk **certificate suspension without a UDB account** (scheme-administered onboarding). [PRIMARY-ONLY / flag — S8, S9]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Obtain the REWS authorisation** to trade/supply biofuel (S.L. 545.15) and maintain it. → REWS.
2. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) and obtain a valid **Proof of Sustainability** for every imported consignment. → certification body / supplier.
3. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 %; RFNBO 70 %) and document per batch. → self + verifier.
4. **File the annual substitution-obligation / sustainability report to REWS** (deadline to confirm) and retain PoS documentation. → REWS.
5. **Note Malta's exemption from the 1.7 % Annex IX-B cap**, apply double counting for wastes/residues, respect the 7 % crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out. → self + verifier.
6. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension. → UDB / voluntary scheme.

**4-line summary — Malta**
- Transposition: RED II sustainability via **S.L. 545.37 (Sustainability Criteria Regs)** + market obligation via **S.L. 545.15**, but **S.L. 545.37 was repealed by LN 95/2026 and S.L. 545.35 by LN 93/2026** (re-regulation under way; replacement not located); **RED III NOT transposed → referred to CJEU 29 Apr 2026 (IP/26/839) with financial sanctions**.
- Certification: **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** with imported **Proof of Sustainability** per consignment; GHG thresholds 50/60/65 % (framework); double counting for wastes/residues.
- Registry/UDB: **no dedicated national sustainability registry** found — **REWS** authorises/ supervises, EWA sets policy; annual report deadline a gap; UDB applies via EU law (mandatory-use date pending).
- Flags: **post-2026 replacement sustainability instrument unresolved** (LN 93 & 95/2026 repeals); annual deadline + penalties gaps; **Malta exempt from the 1.7 % IX-B cap**; no palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU.

| CO2 tax component | **None** on road fuels — the WA excise has no carbon component and the national CO2-heffing applies to industry/greenhouses, not motor-fuel sales. **EU ETS2 will cover petrol/diesel from 2027** (supplier-level carbon price on top of excise) | Wet op de accijns; Directive (EU) 2023/959 (ETS2) | ETS2 from 2027 | PRIMARY-ONLY (absence inferred from WA structure); ETS2 start CONFIRMED at EU level | wetten.overheid.nl; DG CLIMA/DG TAXUD pages |
| Biofuel/HVO/E85 reduction or exemption | **No reduced rates.** Biofuels are taxed at the rate of the **equivalent motor fuel** (art. 28(1) WA: petrol rate for E85, gasolie rate for FAME/HVO/vegetable oil liquid at 10 °C, on full volume). One generic correction exists: **art. 71h WA refund** for motor fuel consisting wholly/partly of biofuel (RED art. 2(i)) or renewable fuel (art. 9.7.1.1 Wet milieubeheer) — granted only if the bio/renewable fuel has **lower energy content** than the equivalent fuel **and ≥ 10 % of the energy** in the fuel is bio/renewable; refund = the energy-content difference as % of the equivalent fuel's excise (reference values: petrol 32 MJ/L, diesel 36 MJ/L, per RED Annex III). In practice this benefits high-blends like E85; HVO100/B100 gain little/nothing (similar energy content) and get no other relief. Relief is **not** conditioned on volumes beyond the jaarverplichting (no SE/FI-style restriction) — the main biofuel incentive is the HBE certificate market (see mandates section) | Art. 28(1) WA; art. 71h WA (generic version in force since 1 Jan 2017, Fiscale vereenvoudigingswet 2017 via amendement-Schouten; text verified as valid 2026-01-01) | In force | CONFIRMED (mechanics); PRIMARY-ONLY (1 Jan 2017 origin date) | wetten.overheid.nl art. 28 & art. 71h (valid 2026-01-01); kennisbank.douane.nl Handboek Accijns (equivalence examples); Vakstudie/InView commentary (Tier 2) |
| Biogas/biomethane treatment | Gaseous fuels are **outside the accijns**; natural gas (incl. grid biomethane/groen gas) is taxed under the **energiebelasting**: gas delivered to a **CNG filling station** is taxed at **EUR 0.20928 /m³ (2026)** regardless of biogenic share — no reduced energy-tax rate for biomethane (support runs via HBEs). Standard first-bracket gas rate 2026: EUR 0.60066 /m³ | Art. 59(1)(b) Wet belastingen op milieugrondslag (Wbm) | 1 Jan 2026 | CONFIRMED (rate); PRIMARY-ONLY (no-relief-for-biomethane inference) | wetten.overheid.nl Wbm art. 59 (valid 2026-01-01); Belastingdienst energiebelasting pages / taxlive (Tier 2, CNG-tarief scope) |
| ETD implementation / state aid | Directive 2003/96/EC is implemented by the **Wet op de accijns** (motor fuels) and the **Wet belastingen op milieugrondslag** (energy tax on gas/electricity). No notified state-aid biofuel excise relief in force; art. 71h is a generic energy-content correction, not a fiscal aid scheme tied to the mandate | WA; Wbm | — | PRIMARY-ONLY | wetten.overheid.nl |
| Announced changes | (1) **Adopted:** the discount lapses **1 Jan 2027** — absent new legislation, rates revert to the full level and resume art. 27a indexation (linked to Wet IB 2001 arts. 10.1–10.2); trade estimates put the resulting increase at roughly +16–17 ct/L petrol incl. indexation. (2) Government stated (Apr 2026) it will **not** cut fuel excise in its relief package — no extension bill pending at access date; treat any 2027 extension as `PROPOSED` only if/when a bill appears (Prinsjesdag Sep 2026 is the watch point). (3) **EU ETS2 from 1 Jan 2027** adds a CO2 cost on fossil petrol/diesel | Belastingplan 2026 (sunset); art. 27a WA (indexation) | 1 Jan 2027 | CONFIRMED (sunset + indexation); Tier 2 only for the size of the 2027 jump | wetten.overheid.nl; ondernemersplein/business.gov.nl ("korting verlengd tot 1 januari 2027"); NL Times 14 Apr 2026; ANWB/TLN (Tier 2) |

**Interaction with the mandate:** the Netherlands runs no tax-incentive scheme for biofuels — the Energie voor Vervoer jaarverplichting with tradable HBEs (NEa — mandates section) carries the entire incentive. The art. 71h refund merely neutralises the per-litre energy-content penalty of volumetric excise for high blends; it is independent of the blending obligation, so no state-aid "beyond-mandate" restriction applies.

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes and proof of sustainability

- France accepts the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (Commission-recognised; 18 schemes listed on the DG ENER voluntary-schemes page as of 2026-07-04, incl. ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, RSB, SURE, Better Biomass, KZR INiG, Bonsucro EU, etc.). Every operator "doit disposer d'un certificat attestant qu'il est bien certifié RED au titre d'un schéma volontaire reconnu par la Commission européenne" — declarations are invalid without it. **CONFIRMED** (ecologie.gouv.fr durabilité page + DG ENER list, both 2026-07-04).
- No French national certification scheme substituting for voluntary schemes was found for biofuels; the ministry page describes the generic RED choice (voluntary scheme vs a member-state national system) but French guidance requires certification under an EC-recognised scheme. **PRIMARY-ONLY** (ministry page only; practical guide PDF "Guide Système de durabilité 2021" located but not machine-readable in this session).
- **Proof per consignment:** sustainability attestations/déclarations de durabilité (preuves de durabilité) must accompany each lot of biomass/biofuel with identical sustainability characteristics through the whole chain of custody (mass balance); annual audits by independent certification bodies. **CONFIRMED** (ecologie.gouv.fr, 2026-07-04; consistent with Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/996).
- 2BSvs (French-origin scheme) remains EU-recognised (Commission Decision of 12 Apr 2022). **CONFIRMED** (DG ENER page, 2026-07-04).

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status for French operators

- UDB (Art. 31a RED II as amended) launched; open for economic-operator registration since **15 January 2024**; covers liquid fuels and (since Nov 2024) gaseous fuels. Mandatory-use date/sanctions still being agreed between Commission and member states (Commission wiki: no immediate sanctions after 21 Nov 2024). **CONFIRMED** (EC UDB wiki + 2BSvs, 2026-07-04).
- Certification-scheme enforcement: voluntary schemes state that certified operators **must hold a UDB account or face certificate suspension** (2BSvs: "if a company does not have a UDB account, its certificate will be automatically suspended"). **UNCORROBORATED as a legal requirement** (scheme rule, Tier 2; stems from Implementing Reg. 2022/996 as amended — corroborator to check Reg. (EU) 2024/… amendment).
- **CarbuRe–UDB interface:** integration of CarbuRe with the UDB is under discussion at EU level ("possible integration" per 2BSvs); no Tier 1 DGEC statement found that CarbuRe feeds the UDB automatically. French operators should assume **direct UDB onboarding obligations apply** in addition to CarbuRe. **UNCORROBORATED — flagged for follow-up.** (2bsvs.org/about-us/union-database/, 2026-07-04.)

## 4. Compliance checklist — sustainability items (draft)

1. Obtain and maintain certification under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (e.g. ISCC EU, 2BSvs, REDcert-EU) covering your role in the chain; pass annual surveillance audits. [CONFIRMED]
2. Register on **CarbuRe** (DGEC) as producer/trader/supplier; declare all biofuel/bioliquid lots monthly by end of M+1; ensure lots carry complete déclarations de durabilité (feedstock, origin, GHG value, scheme certificate no.). [PRIMARY-ONLY for the M+1 rhythm]
3. File the annual durability declaration (CarbuRe extraction of validated lots) by **15 March** for the previous year (DGEC/DREAL). [CONFIRMED]
4. File the annual TIRUERT declaration by **10 April** with DGDDI/DGFiP, counting only consignments with valid proof of sustainability meeting art. 29 RED II criteria (≥ 50/60/65 % GHG saving by plant vintage). [CONFIRMED]
5. Exclude palm-oil (incl. PFAD) and soy-oil based products from TIRUERT-eligible volumes — they are not "biocarburants" under art. 266 quindecies. [CONFIRMED]
6. For double-counted feedstocks, verify the production unit appears on the DGEC double-counting list (CarbuRe). [PRIMARY-ONLY]
7. Open a **Union Database (UDB)** account and record transactions/stocks; treat this as mandatory now for certificate retention (scheme rules), with EU-wide mandatory date pending. [CONFIRMED obligation trend; date pending]
8. Monitor RED III transposition (France in infringement; expect new/changed obligations, incl. possible 14.5 % GHG-intensity option and UDB anchoring). [CONFIRMED status]

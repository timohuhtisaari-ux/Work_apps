> Framing: The UK left the EU; **EU RED III and EU ETS do not apply**. Road-transport
> renewable fuel is governed by the **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)**,
> and aviation by the separate **SAF Mandate** (from 1 Jan 2025). Both are
> administered by the **Department for Transport (DfT)** Low Carbon Fuels unit.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

The UK runs **two parallel certificate-based obligations**:
- **RTFO** — a renewable-volume obligation on road/non-road fuel suppliers, met with
  **Renewable Transport Fuel Certificates (RTFCs)**; legal basis = **Renewable
  Transport Fuel Obligations Order 2007** (SI 2007/3072) as amended (notably SI
  2021/1420 and later amendment orders).
- **SAF Mandate** — an aviation obligation met with **SAF certificates**; legal basis
  = **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligations (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) Order 2024**
  (SI 2024/1187), in force 1 Jan 2025.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **RTFCs:** obligated suppliers either redeem RTFCs (earned by supplying sustainable
  renewable fuel, or bought from other suppliers) or pay the **buy-out** (50p main /
  80p development-fuel). Development-fuel RTFCs can be over-redeemed and also count
  toward the main obligation.
- **Registry / administrator:** DfT RTFO Administrator; online RTFO Operating System
  (ROS) for accreditation, monthly/annual reporting, and RTFC issuance.
- **Threshold:** RTFO bites at ≥450,000 L/yr of relevant fuel; smaller suppliers are
  exempt.
- **Banking/trading:** RTFCs are tradable; limited carry-over between obligation
  periods (exact banking limits — verify in 2026 compliance guidance; gap).
- **SAF Mandate:** parallel scheme with its own certificates, buy-out, and (from 2028)
  a PtL sub-obligation; jet-fuel suppliers above a de-minimis are obligated (exact
  tonnage threshold not verified — gap). A **revenue certainty mechanism** for SAF
  is being legislated (Sustainable Aviation Fuel Act) — cross-reference, being
  introduced by end-2026.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps (UK)

- **RTFO obligation figures — RESOLVED (two-metric confusion):** the apparent conflict
  between "≈12.15%/12.50%" and "14.054%" reflects **two different DfT metrics**, both
  real:
  (a) the **obligation as a share of total fuel by volume** — the DfT statutory-review
  policy trajectory: 11.8% (2024) → 12.15% (2025) → **12.5% (2026)** → 14.6% (2032)
  [3][13], now **CONFIRMED**; and
  (b) the **RTFO Order's obligation-calculation percentages** (multipliers applied to
  obligated fossil/non-sustainable fuel to determine RTFCs owed): **14.054% main +
  1.619% development for 2025** [14]. The earlier "~17.7% in 2032" mis-extraction is a
  reading of the Order metric, not the share-of-fuel metric. Both should be stated so
  they are not conflated; only metric (a) is corroborated for 2026.
- **Development-fuel 2026 %** (Order metric) and **RTFC banking limits** — not pinned
  (2025 development obligation = 1.619%).
- **Development-fuel buy-out (80p)** — now **CONFIRMED**: gov.uk RTFO statutory-review
  page and "RTFO: an essential guide" both state 50p main / 80p development [13][15],
  corroborating the earlier Tier-2 (ADBA) source.
- **SAF yearly percentages / buy-outs — CONFIRMED**: 2026 = 3.60%, 2025 = 2.00%,
  buy-outs £4.70 (SAF) / £5.00 (PtL), PtL from 2028 at 0.20% — corroborated by gov.uk
  SAF Mandate material and multiple independent law-firm briefings [8][16].
- **SAF obligated-supplier tonnage threshold** and **HEFA cap** exact figures — HEFA
  cap remains PRIMARY-ONLY; supplier tonnage threshold not verified.

### 5-line summary — United Kingdom
1. Obligation type: certificate-based renewable-volume obligation (RTFO, RTFCs) on road/non-road fuel suppliers, plus a separate aviation SAF Mandate.
2. Current level: RTFO obligation **12.5%** of total fuel by volume for 2026 (crop cap 3%; buy-out 50p main / 80p development; threshold 450,000 L/yr); SAF Mandate **3.60%** of jet fuel for 2026 (2.00% in 2025; buy-out £4.70 SAF/£5.00 PtL). NB the RTFO Order's obligation-calculation metric differs (2025: 14.054% main + 1.619% dev.).
3. Next legislated change: RTFO rises 0.35 pt/yr to 14.6% by 2032; SAF to 10% by 2030 (PtL sub-obligation from 2028); DfT statutory-review reforms targeted for 2027.
4. RED III status: N/A (non-EU) — RTFO and SAF are the UK's own regimes.
5. Flags: RTFO 12.5%(2026), crop cap 3%, buy-outs 50p/80p, and all SAF figures now CONFIRMED; 12.15/14.054 confusion RESOLVED (two metrics); development-fuel 2026 Order % and SAF tonnage threshold = remaining GAPS.

# Slovenia (SI) — Fuel taxation (Section 2)

- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** fuel-tax-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Currency/unit:** EUR per 1000 L (euro-area).

> **Key structural note:** Slovenia runs a **regulated / formula-based fuel-price regime**. Every 14 days the
> Government adopts an "Uredba o določitvi zneska trošarine za energente in električno energijo" that re-sets the
> excise (trošarina) on petrol, diesel and heating oil in step with the regulated maximum retail prices. Separately,
> a **CO2 environmental levy** (okoljska dajatev za onesnaževanje zraka z emisijo CO2) applies on top of excise —
> but in 2026 the Government has repeatedly **zeroed and re-introduced** that levy on motor fuels by decree. Both
> the excise and the CO2 levy are therefore moving targets; figures below are the current-window snapshot.

**Mandate interaction:** Slovenia is an obligation market (RES-T/GHG decree). No excise incentive is tied to
volumes above the blending obligation (contrast SE/FI). Mandate detail is out of scope.

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Zakon o trošarinah (ZTro-1)** (PISRS ZAKO7128).
No biofuel-specific notified state-aid decision identified (Slovenia's earlier biofuel excise-relief schemes have
lapsed; promotion is now obligation-based).

### 4-line summary — Slovenia
- Petrol excise: 417.59 EUR/1000 L (NMB 95), current 14-day window from 16 Jun 2026 (Government re-sets it every fortnight under the regulated-price regime).
- Diesel excise: 330.00 EUR/1000 L (EU minimum) in the current window (was 362.77 in Mar 2026).
- Biofuel relief: none via excise — biofuels taxed by energy-product category under ZTro-1; promotion is obligation-based, not tax-incentive-based.
- Flags: separate CO2 environmental levy (~€25.90/t; ≈€59.57/1000 L petrol, ≈€69.93/1000 L diesel) that the Government repeatedly zeroed/re-introduced on motor fuels in 2026 — on/off status is volatile; ETS2 surrender from 2028 (Dir (EU) 2023/959).

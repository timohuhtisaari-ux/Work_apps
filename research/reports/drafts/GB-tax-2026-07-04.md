# United Kingdom (GB) — Section 2: Fuel taxation

- **Report date:** 2026-07-05 (draft filename 2026-07-04)
- **Researched by:** fuel-tax-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Scope note:** The UK left the EU; the **Energy Taxation Directive
  2003/96/EC does not apply** and there is **no EU ETS2** obligation. Fuel duty
  ("hydrocarbon oils duty") sits in the **Hydrocarbon Oil Duties Act 1979
  (HODA)**, with rates set by Finance Acts / Budget resolutions and administered
  by **HMRC**. The UK abolished its biofuel duty differential in 2010, so
  biofuels are taxed at the fossil-equivalent rate. Carbon pricing of road fuel
  is via fuel duty only — road transport is **not** in the UK ETS.

## 2.2 The temporary 5p cut (status)

The 5 pence/litre cut introduced at Spring Statement (March 2022) was
**extended to 31 December 2026** and is then reversed in three tranches (1p on
1 Sep 2026, 2p on 1 Dec 2026, 2p on 1 Mar 2027). From **1 March 2027** the main
petrol/diesel rate returns to the long-standing **57.95 ppl**. So for most of
2026 the effective main rate is **52.95 ppl**, rising to 53.95 ppl from
1 September 2026. The cut applies proportionately to other/rebated fuels.

## 2.3 Biofuel treatment and mandate interaction

There is **no reduced excise rate for biofuels** — biodiesel, bioethanol and
HVO used as road fuel are charged the fossil-equivalent duty (the 20p/L
biofuel differential was withdrawn in 2010). Biofuel support in the UK is
delivered entirely through the **RTFO certificate scheme (RTFCs)** run by DfT
(a *mandates-agent* topic — cross-reference, not duplicated here), **not**
through the tax system. Consequently there is no state-aid "above-the-mandate"
tax condition of the Sweden/Finland type.

## 2.4 Carbon pricing / UK ETS position

Road transport fuel is **outside the UK ETS**. The UK ETS covers power, energy-
intensive industry and aviation; announced expansions are **maritime (from
July 2026)** and **waste incineration (from 2028, with monitoring from Jan
2026)**. Unlike the EU's ETS2, the UK has made **no policy commitment and has
no live consultation to bring road transport (or buildings) into the UK ETS**.
This should be stated explicitly to counter any assumption of an EU-ETS2-style
charge on UK road fuel.

### 4-line summary — United Kingdom
- **Petrol:** fuel duty 52.95 ppl for most of 2026 (5p cut still in place), rising to 53.95 ppl on 1 Sep 2026, 55.95 ppl on 1 Dec 2026, and 57.95 ppl on 1 Mar 2027.
- **Diesel:** identical to petrol — 52.95 ppl → 57.95 ppl on the same 1 Sep 2026 / 1 Dec 2026 / 1 Mar 2027 steps; HVO taxed at the diesel rate.
- **Biofuel relief:** none via tax — biodiesel, bioethanol and HVO pay the fossil-equivalent duty (differential abolished 2010); support runs through the RTFO instead.
- **Flags:** 5p cut is temporary and being unwound (CONFIRMED); road transport is NOT in the UK ETS and there is no EU-ETS2-style road-fuel carbon charge or live consultation.

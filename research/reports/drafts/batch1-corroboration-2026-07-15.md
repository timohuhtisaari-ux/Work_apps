## Corroboration Methodology

Each country's 2-3 load-bearing facts were cross-referenced against:
1. **Official government sources** (regulatory bodies, energy ministries, official gazettes/portals)
2. **Independent international agencies** (USDA GAIN, IEA, IEA-AMF)
3. **Trade press of different origin** than those cited in draft source logs (e.g., Argus Media, Paultan, F&L Asia, OPIS, Ethanol Producer Magazine, Biofuels Digest, Advanced BioFuels USA)
4. **News outlets** reporting government announcements (Nation Thailand, VietnamNet, Philstar, Manila Bulletin, Rio Times, etc.)

No secondary source that merely republished the same primary document was counted as independent corroboration.

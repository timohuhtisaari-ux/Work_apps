**Interaction with the mandate:** the high-blend reduced rates (E85 → 360; B100/HVO100 → 330) are product-specific reductions in the excise law rather than a relief keyed to volumes above the blending obligation. Whether the reliefs sit under a notified state-aid scheme and/or are capped to non-mandated volumes was not confirmed from the sources fetched (see Gaps) — this is the key state-aid question flagged for follow-up.

## National act implementing the Energy Taxation Directive (2003/96/EC)

- **Law "On Excise Duty" (*Par akcīzes nodokli*)** — the national act setting motor-fuel excise and the biofuel reduced rates, implementing Directive 2003/96/EC. Current rates set by the amendments adopted **19 December 2024** (in force 1 Jan 2026). Consolidated text on Likumi.lv (likumi.lv).
- **State-aid basis for biofuel reliefs:** not identified in this pass — the notified Commission decision underpinning the E85/B100 reduced rates (and any restriction to non-mandated volumes) is a gap for the corroborator.

## 4-line summary — Latvia

- **Petrol:** EUR 555.00 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026 (Law "On Excise Duty"); CONFIRMED (VID + MoF agree).
- **Diesel:** EUR 467.00 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026; CONFIRMED.
- **Biofuel relief:** reduced excise for high blends only — E85-type petrol (70–85 % ethanol) EUR 360 and biodiesel/HVO wholly from biomass EUR 330; ordinary E10/B7 taxed at the full rate (no proportional relief).
- **Flags:** state-aid basis for the E85/B100 reliefs and any cap to non-mandated volumes not confirmed (gap); no separate CO2 component; biomethane treatment unconfirmed; ETS2 obligation from 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959); DG TAXUD PDF cross-check unparseable (gap).

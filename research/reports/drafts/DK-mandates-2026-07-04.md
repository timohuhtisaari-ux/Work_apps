### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** "virksomheder" — entities liable for energy tax on liquid transport
  fuel, natural-gas suppliers, and fuel self-consumers (law § 2, nr. 17). *Cross-reference:
  definition rides on energy-tax liability — see tax section.* No de-minimis threshold
  identified this pass.
- **Administrator:** Energistyrelsen (Danish Energy Agency). No certificate registry —
  supplier-level annual accounting per the **Håndbog** (HB 2025.1, BEK nr 483/2025) and the
  "Skema for CO2e-fortrængningskrav".
- **Reporting:** annually to Energistyrelsen **by 31 March** for the preceding calendar
  year, with verifier documentation per the Håndbog. CONFIRMED (ens.dk).
- **Flexibility:** **joint fulfilment** — a company may meet its obligations wholly/partly
  via agreement with one or more other companies (law § 3, stk. 6; § 4a, stk. 6).
  Carry-over/banking between years: not identified (see Gaps). From 1 Jan 2025 **UER
  credits and (FQD-linked) electricity crediting were discontinued** when the separate FQD
  Art. 7a 6 %-GHG requirement was repealed (RED III/FQD amendment) — per Energistyrelsen
  hearing resumé (Oct–Nov 2024 hearing).
- **Sustainability gate:** only fuels meeting RED sustainability/GHG-saving criteria count
  (50/60/65 % thresholds by plant commissioning date). *See sustainability section.*

### 1.3 Future trajectory and RED III status

| Year(s) | CO2e displacement requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 3.4 % | historical (displacement scheme start) |
| 2023–2024 | 5.2 % (raised early by political agreement) | historical — note some sources present 3.4 % through 2024; see Conflicts |
| 2025–2027 (incl. **2026**) | **≥ 5.2 %** | **CONFIRMED — adopted, in force (BEK 978/2025)** |
| 2028–2029 | ≥ 6 % | CONFIRMED — adopted (BEK 978/2025) |
| 2030 onwards | ≥ 7 %, plus combined advanced+RFNBO blending requirement ≥ 5.1 % (2030) | CONFIRMED (7 %) / PRIMARY-ONLY (5.1 %) — adopted |
| Pending | Draft amendment of the Drivhusgasfortrængningsbekendtgørelse + repeal of the metodebekendtgørelse in hearing 10 Oct 2025 (adjustments, incl. PtX/advanced counting details) | `PROPOSED` (consultation) |

**RED III transposition status:** **Transposed on time — Denmark was the only member state
to notify full transposition of Directive (EU) 2023/2413 by the 21 May 2025 deadline**
(Commission statement, 24 Jul 2025; no infringement action against DK). Vehicles:
**lov nr 1675 af 30/12/2024** (bill L 68 2024-25, adopted with 97 votes; amends
biobrændstofloven, VE-loven, gas- and electricity-supply laws; extends scope to aviation
and maritime incl. ReFuelEU/FuelEU sanction provisions) + **BEK 482 af 15/05/2025**
(in force 21 May 2025) + consolidated **BEK 978 af 01/07/2025** and Håndbog HB 2025.1
(BEK 483/2025). CONFIRMED.

## 5-line summary — Denmark (DK)
1. Obligation type: CO2e displacement requirement (GHG-intensity reduction vs 2010 EU baseline) on energy-tax-liable fuel suppliers to road transport and non-road machinery.
2. Current level (2026): ≥ 5.2 % (applies 2025–2027), under BEK 978/2025 with sanctions by fine (no buy-out price); reporting to Energistyrelsen by 31 March.
3. Next legislated change: ≥ 6 % in 2028–2029, ≥ 7 % plus a combined advanced+RFNBO blending requirement (≥ 5.1 % excl. aviation) from 2030 — adopted; an October 2025 draft BEK amendment is pending (`PROPOSED`).
4. RED III status: fully transposed on time — the only MS to notify complete transposition by 21 May 2025 (lov 1675/2024 + BEK 482/2025 + BEK 978/2025); no infringement.
5. Flags: `CONFLICT` on the 2030 combined advanced+RFNBO figure (national 5.1 % vs directive 5.5 %/1 pp description) and on 2023–24 history; UNCORROBORATED national 1G-crop ceiling and 2025–2029 advanced floor; BEK §-text unquotable (retsinformation blocks automated access).

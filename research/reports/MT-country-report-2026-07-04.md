# Malta — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Malta (MT)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Malta imposes an annual **substitution obligation** on petroleum importers/wholesalers: place on market a minimum % biofuel by total energy content of MSA EN 228 petrol and MSA EN 590 diesel (S.L. 545.15, supervised by REWS). The **exact 2026 substitution percentage could not be established** — the S.L. 545.15 schedule did not render (legislation.mt is JS-only), ePURE lists no overall Malta figure, and the USDA GAIN PDF is not parseable — so it stands as a GAP; the obligation is framed to lift transport RES from 10.5% (2021) toward the **14% 2030 target**, with ≥3.5% advanced biofuels by 2030. Malta is **exempt from the 1.7% Annex IX-B cap** (RED III Cyprus/Malta derogation, confirmed) and applies double counting for wastes/residues. Fuel taxation offers **no biofuel relief** — excise sits at the EU minimum (petrol 359.00, diesel 330.00 EUR/1000 L) with no separate CO2 tax. The dominant risks are legal: **RED III is not transposed and Malta was referred to the CJEU on 29 April 2026 (IP/26/839) with a request for financial sanctions**, and the **post-repeal replacement sustainability instrument could not be located** after LN 95/2026 (repealing S.L. 545.37) and LN 93/2026 (repealing S.L. 545.35) — a possible live transposition gap. Top supplier obligations: hold the REWS authorisation, carry a Proof of Sustainability per imported consignment, and report to REWS.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated:** petroleum providers authorised as **importers and/or wholesalers** of petrol/diesel. They meet the obligation by blending biofuel (chiefly with EN 590 diesel) before supplying fuel stations. [2][3]
- **Administering bodies:** **Regulator for Energy and Water Services (REWS)** — licensing, compliance supervision of the substitution obligation; **Energy and Water Agency (EWA)** — policy development, NECP coordination. [2][8]
- **Registry / certificates:** no tradable-certificate market identified; direct substitution obligation with reporting to the Authority/REWS.
- **Extension planned:** the updated NECP signals extending the biofuel blending obligation to **maritime and aviation** fuel suppliers. [5]
- **Banking/borrowing:** not identified — GAP.

### 1.3 Future trajectory

- Obligation is framed to raise transport RES from **10.5% (2021)** toward **14% by 2030**, with **≥3.5% advanced biofuels by 2030**. Year-by-year substitution percentages (energy content) are in the S.L. 545.15 schedule but were **not retrievable** from the legislation.mt viewer (returned metadata/landing page only) — GAP. [4][5]
- `PROPOSED`/planned: extension of the blending obligation to maritime and aviation sectors (NECP). Not yet adopted legislation located.

**RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition status:** NOT fully transposed. Timeline: letter of formal notice July 2025; reasoned opinion December 2025; **referred to the Court of Justice of the EU on 29 April 2026 (IP/26/839), with a request for financial sanctions**, alongside Greece and Portugal. **Corroborator (2026-07-04) directly fetched the EC April-2026 energy package [V6]:** confirms "The Commission is therefore referring Greece, Malta and Portugal to the Court of Justice of the European Union, with requests to impose financial sanctions," and the FLN-July-2025 / RO-December-2025 timeline. The Commission's issue with Malta: it notified some measures but did not provide sufficiently clear/precise information on transposition (distinct from EL/PT which notified nothing). Legal Notice 93 of 2026 repealed the old S.L. 545.35, indicating ongoing national re-regulation. Status: **referred to CJEU (advanced infringement).** [9][10][11][V6]

**ETS2 (EU-wide cross-reference):** ETS2 covers road-transport fuel suppliers. ETS2 was **established by Directive (EU) 2023/959** (Ch. IVa of Dir 2003/87/EC); its start was **postponed one year to 1 Jan 2028, first allowance surrender 2029** (Nov-2025/early-2026 climate package) — not "surrender from 2028" as originally drafted. [12][V5]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- Malta follows the standard RED II model: economic operators demonstrate sustainability via **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (or equivalent), producing a **Proof of Sustainability** per consignment. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S7]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S1]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Obtain and maintain the REWS authorisation** to import/wholesale and supply biofuel (S.L. 545.15). → Regulator for Energy and Water Services (REWS).
2. **Meet the annual substitution obligation** (minimum biofuel % by energy content of EN 228 petrol + EN 590 diesel — the exact 2026 % is unestablished, so confirm the current schedule with REWS), ensuring biofuel meets MSA EN 14214 (diesel) / MSA EN 15376 (petrol). → self / REWS.
3. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) and obtain a valid **Proof of Sustainability** for every imported consignment. → certification body / upstream supplier.
4. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 % by installation start date; RFNBO 70 %) and document per batch. → self + verifier.
5. **Note Malta's exemption from the 1.7 % Annex IX-B cap**, apply double counting for wastes/residues, respect the 7 % crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out; work toward ≥3.5% advanced biofuels by 2030. → self + verifier.
6. **File the annual substitution-obligation / sustainability report to REWS** (exact deadline to confirm — not established) and retain PoS documentation for inspection. → REWS.
7. **Confirm which instrument currently carries the RED sustainability criteria** post-repeal of S.L. 545.37 (LN 95/2026) and S.L. 545.35 (LN 93/2026) — a replacement could not be located; verify before relying on the old regs. → REWS / EWA.
8. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension (mandatory-use date pending). → UDB / voluntary scheme.
9. **Prepare for ETS2** as a regulated road-transport fuel supplier: 1 Jan 2028 start, first allowance surrender 2029; watch for the planned extension of the blending obligation to maritime and aviation. → competent ETS2 authority / EWA.

- **Post-repeal replacement sustainability instrument NOT located — possible live transposition gap:** S.L. 545.37 (Sustainability Criteria Regs) was **repealed by LN 95/2026** and S.L. 545.35 (RES-promotion) by **LN 93/2026**; the replacement legal notice(s) carrying the current RED sustainability criteria could not be located (legislation.mt JS-only; targeted searches for LN 95/96 of 2026 and a 2026 sustainability-criteria instrument surfaced nothing). Cannot confirm whether a replacement is in force or whether a regulatory gap currently exists. **Highest compliance risk on the sustainability side.**
- **RED III NOT transposed — referred to the CJEU on 29 April 2026 (IP/26/839) with a request for financial sanctions** (with Greece and Portugal); FLN July 2025, RO December 2025 (corroborator-confirmed via the EC April-2026 package). Malta notified some measures but not with sufficiently clear/precise information.
- **Malta is EXEMPT from the 1.7% Annex IX-B cap** (RED III Art. 27 CY/MT derogation — CONFIRMED, EU text + USDA GAIN Tier-2).
- **Excise CONFLICT is a source-quality artifact, not a legal conflict:** 359/330 EUR/1000 L (EU minimum, LN 429/2021) is corroborated by two Tier-2 sources and matches the ETD minima; fuel-prices.eu's €0.549/€0.472 is almost certainly a mis-derived residual. No Tier-1 read achieved (legislation.mt JS-only; DG TAXUD PDF not parseable). No separate CO2 tax on motor fuels.
- **Exact annual report deadline and penalty figures** not located; **no dedicated national sustainability registry** located; **no MT palm/soy ban beyond EU; no Annex IX national additions**; RFNBO/e-fuel and buy-out/penalty not quantified nationally; RES-T 2030 target cited as 14% (also 15% in some secondary sources — not resolved). [GAPs]
- **ETS2 across all four countries:** established by Directive (EU) 2023/959; start postponed to 1 Jan 2028, first surrender 2029.
- **Registry corrections needed in `research/sources/member-states.md`:** flag the LN 93/2026 and LN 95/2026 repeals and record that the replacement sustainability/RES-promotion instrument is not yet located; keep REWS (compliance) and EWA (policy) as the Malta authorities.

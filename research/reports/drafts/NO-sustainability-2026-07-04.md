# Norway (NO) — Sustainability & Compliance Requirements (draft)

- **Scope:** Section 3 of country report + Section 4 checklist items in sustainability scope
- **Researched by:** sustainability-researcher
- **Access dates:** all live fetches 2026-07-05 unless noted
- **Corroboration pass:** completed 2026-07-05 (corroborator) — see notes in §5

> Framing: Norway is an **EEA member**, not an EU member. It does **not** transpose RED III on the EU timetable. Its biofuel sustainability regime lives in national law (Produktforskriften ch. 3) and mirrors the EU Renewable Energy Directive as incorporated into the EEA Agreement. RED II (2018/2001) and RED III (2023/2413) were **not yet incorporated into the EEA Agreement / Norwegian law** at the time of the sources checked — a central flag below.

## 3. Sustainability & compliance requirements

### 3.1 Transposition instruments
- **Forskrift 2004-06-01 nr. 922 (Produktforskriften), Kapittel 3** — "Omsetningskrav for biodrivstoff og bærekraftskriterier for biodrivstoff og flytende biobrensel." This is the operative national instrument for both the sales mandate (other agent's scope) and the sustainability/GHG criteria (my scope). `PRIMARY-ONLY` (Lovdata).
- ILUC Directive implementation: **Forskrift 2018-11-21 nr. 1731** ("...gjennomføring av ILUC-direktivet") amended Produktforskriften ch. 3 to bring in ILUC-directive elements. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (Lovdata).
- **Flag — RED version lag:** RED II (2018/2001) and RED III (2023/2413) are not yet incorporated into the EEA Agreement or Norwegian law per sources checked; Norway's criteria therefore track the earlier RED framework. `PRIMARY-ONLY / to corroborate` (Miljødirektoratet/regjeringen material, USDA-type secondary).

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally
Per **Produktforskriften § 3-6** (calculation per Annexes I, II, IV):
| Threshold | Applies | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **35%** GHG saving vs fossil comparator | up to 17 Jan 2017 (legacy) | `PRIMARY-ONLY` (Lovdata §3-6) |
| **50%** GHG saving | from 1 Jan 2018 onward | `PRIMARY-ONLY` |
| **60%** GHG saving | installations that started operating on/after 5 Oct 2015 (from 1 Jan 2017) | `PRIMARY-ONLY` |

- **Flag:** No **65%** tier (which RED II sets for installations starting after 1 Jan 2021) appears in §3-6 — consistent with RED II not being incorporated. Corroborator should confirm the current consolidated §3-6 text.
- Fuels from **wastes and residues** (except agricultural residues) need only meet the **GHG-saving** requirement, not the land criteria (§ 3-5). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

### 3.3 Land / feedstock sustainability criteria
- **§ 3-5** — sustainability criteria apply to biofuels/bioliquids counted toward the mandate or receiving support, regardless of feedstock origin. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **§§ 3-7 / 3-8** — exclusion of feedstock from land with high biodiversity value and high carbon stock (status referenced to January 2008), with limited documented exceptions. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

### 3.4 Accepted certification / proof of sustainability
- **§ 3-10** — documentation of sustainability may be provided via **recognised voluntary certification schemes**; scheme documentation substitutes for individual proof within the scheme's scope. Norway relies on the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, RSB EU RED, etc. — the DG ENER list). `PRIMARY-ONLY` (Lovdata) + `CONFIRMED` for the EU scheme list itself (DG ENER page).
- **Proof per consignment:** a **mass-balance system** under **§ 3-10a** — different sustainability-characterised batches may mix at a physical location with characteristics remaining attached; balance reconciled at least quarterly; data cannot support claims beyond two years after intake. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- Raw-material classification in **Vedlegg V (Annex V)**: Part A (advanced feedstocks) and Part B (feedstocks enabling double counting). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.

### 3.5 National registry & Union Database (UDB) status
- **National reporting** is to **Miljødirektoratet** via an annual electronic report (skjema "Biodrivstoff: Årlig rapportering"). There is **no Nabisy-style transactional registry**; compliance rests on the annual report + mass balance + voluntary-scheme proofs. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (Miljødirektoratet reporting page/veileder).
- **UDB:** The EU Union Database is a tool under RED II/Implementing Reg. 2022/996. Because **RED II is not yet incorporated into the EEA Agreement**, Norwegian operators are **not subject to a national UDB onboarding obligation** and Norway does not run a UDB connection; operators rely on voluntary-scheme documentation (which the schemes themselves may register in UDB for EU-market consignments). `PRIMARY-ONLY / partly inferred — FLAG for corroboration.`

### 3.6 Feedstock restrictions — palm / high-ILUC
- **No standalone national statutory palm ban** was located; palm oil is governed through the sustainability/GHG criteria and the ILUC-directive implementation (Forskrift 2018-11-21 nr. 1731). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **High-ILUC:** under the EU framework only palm oil currently meets the high-ILUC-risk classification (>10% expansion into high-carbon land); low-ILUC-certified palm can be exempt. Norway's transposition follows this logic. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (regjeringen høringsnotat) + Tier-2 (Miljødirektoratet biodrivstoff pages).
- **Practical outcome (context):** In 2023, **no palm-oil biodiesel** was reported sold under the mandate (2022: ~2 million litres). In 2024, biodiesel from **palm-production waste (e.g. POME/PFAD-type residues)** rose to ~4% of biodiesel volume. `UNCORROBORATED / Tier-2` (Miljødirektoratet news, Preem sustainability reports) — verify against the official annual statistics.
- **Advanced-biofuel definition tightened 1 Jan 2025:** "advanced" now counts only **Part A** feedstocks (previously A and B). This narrows what qualifies for the advanced sub-quota and affects UCO/animal-fat (Part B) treatment. `PRIMARY-ONLY / Tier-2` — corroborate against amending forskrift 2024-12-16 nr. 3392.
- **UCO / waste verification:** waste/residue consignments must still satisfy the GHG-saving criterion and be documented under a recognised scheme's chain of custody (Part A/B classification per Annex V). No Norway-specific extra UCO-audit rule beyond scheme requirements was located. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **Public-procurement palm-free guidance:** Anskaffelser.no publishes guidance for buying deforestation/ILUC-low biofuel (palm/PFAD/soy-free) — a soft, non-statutory measure. `Tier-2 context only`.

### 3.7 Reporting / audit calendar for obligated parties
| Obligation | Detail | Legal basis | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annual report** to Miljødirektoratet | by **31 March** each year, covering prior calendar year, on mandate compliance + sustainability criteria (incl. lifecycle GHG) | § 3-10 | `PRIMARY-ONLY` + Tier-2 (veileder) |
| **Independent audit** | reporting must be verified by an auditor (revisor) under **§ 3-11**; auditors are a named target group of the veileder | § 3-11 | `PRIMARY-ONLY` |
| **Mass-balance reconciliation** | at least quarterly | § 3-10a | `PRIMARY-ONLY` |
| **Penalty (overtredelsesgebyr)** | up to **15 G** ≈ **NOK 1,860,420** (as of Jan 2025) for failing the mandate or the sustainability criteria; violation-fee regime introduced **1 Jan 2025** | ch. 3 / Miljødirektoratet | `PRIMARY-ONLY` (Miljødirektoratet penalties page) |
| **Guidance** | Miljødirektoratet "veileder for omsetningskrav for biodrivstoff" updated (latest update Oct 2025) — primary procedural source | — | `Tier-1 procedural` |

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (sustainability scope)
1. Ensure every biofuel consignment counted toward the omsetningskrav carries **proof of sustainability** under a recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU / REDcert-EU / etc.) covering the applicable land + GHG criteria. [Miljødirektoratet; Produktforskriften §3-10]
2. Operate a compliant **mass-balance system**, reconciled at least quarterly, keeping records ≤2 years from intake. [§3-10a]
3. Verify each consignment meets the applicable **GHG-saving threshold** (35/50/60% per §3-6) and, for crop feedstocks, the land criteria (§§3-5, 3-7, 3-8). [§3-6]
4. Classify feedstocks against **Annex V** (Part A advanced / Part B); note the **1 Jan 2025** narrowing of "advanced" to Part A only. [Vedlegg V]
5. Submit the **annual report** to Miljødirektoratet by **31 March**, verified by an auditor under **§3-11**. [§§3-10, 3-11]
6. Note **no UDB onboarding obligation** currently applies to Norwegian operators (RED II not in EEA); track EEA Joint Committee developments. [FLAG]
7. Budget for penalty exposure: **overtredelsesgebyr up to 15 G (~NOK 1.86 m)**. [Miljødirektoratet]

## 4-line summary — Norway
1. **Transposition:** Produktforskriften ch. 3 (2004-06-01 nr. 922) + ILUC-directive forskrift; mirrors EU RED but RED II/RED III not yet incorporated into EEA — GHG tiers 35/50/60% only (no 65%). `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
2. **Certification regime:** EU-recognised voluntary schemes (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs…) accepted via §3-10; mass-balance proof (§3-10a); Annex V feedstock classes. `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
3. **Registry/UDB:** national annual report to Miljødirektoratet (by 31 March, auditor-verified §3-11); **no UDB onboarding** for NO operators (RED II not in EEA). `PRIMARY-ONLY / inferred FLAG`.
4. **Flags:** no statutory palm ban but near-zero palm in practice (high-deforestation exclusion CONFIRMED, Tier-2); "advanced" narrowed to Annex V Part A on 1 Jan 2025; overtredelsesgebyr up to 15 G (~NOK 1.86 m); miljodirektoratet.no 403 persists — §3-6 GHG tiers, UDB, penalty remain PRIMARY-ONLY.

# Hungary — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Hungary (HU)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Hungary imposes an **energy-based renewable-fuel share obligation** on entities
that release motor petrol and diesel for consumption (Act CXVII of 2010 +
Government Decree 821/2021, as amended by Decree 488/2025). For **2026 the
overall obligation is 9.1 %** by energy content, split into a **petrol
sub-target of ≥6.1 % bioethanol** and a **diesel sub-target of ≥9.6 % biofuel
(HVO ≥0.4 %)**, with an advanced-biofuel (Annex IX-A) floor of ≥1 % rising to
5.5 % by 2030. There is **no biofuel excise relief**: bioethanol, biodiesel and
HVO blended into road fuel are taxed at the full fuel rate (petrol 165,630
HUF/1000 L, diesel 155,160 HUF/1000 L from 1 Jul 2026), so the blending mandate —
not tax — is the sole biofuel-promotion lever. Sustainability compliance runs
through the national **BÜHG/BIONYOM** system administered by **NÉBIH** (which
accepts EU-recognised voluntary schemes such as ISCC EU/REDcert/2BSvs), with an
**annual data return to NÉBIH due 20 March**; mandate non-compliance is fined by
the tax and customs authority (NAV). Hungary has adopted RED III transposing
measures domestically but was **escalated to a reasoned opinion in December
2025** for insufficiently clear transposition, and ETS2 (Directive (EU)
2023/959) will add a fuel-supplier surrender obligation from 2028.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Hungary runs an **energy-based renewable-fuel share obligation** ("kötelező
megújulóüzemanyag-részarány") on entities that release motor fuel for
consumption. The framework statute is **Act CXVII of 2010** (2010. évi CXVII.
törvény) on promoting renewable energy in transport and reducing transport
GHG emissions; the statute delegates the numeric percentages to a government
decree (§13(1)(h)). The operative percentages for 2026 sit in **Government
Decree 821/2021 (XII. 28.), § 9**, as substantially amended by **Government
Decree 488/2025 (XII. 31.)** (published in Magyar Közlöny No. 162 of 31 Dec 2025;
in force 1 Jan 2026).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** the entity that releases motor petrol/diesel for
  consumption (excise release), i.e. the same operators liable for fuel excise.
- **Metric:** energy content of biofuel/renewable fuel over the combined energy
  content of petrol + diesel released, to one decimal place (Act CXVII/2010 §2
  definition of részarány).
- **Enforcement/registry:** compliance is reported to and enforced by the
  **state tax and customs authority (NAV)** under Act CXVII/2010 §7; MEKH
  (energy regulator) is named in the source registry as the mandates authority,
  but the primary text accessed assigns non-compliance fines to NAV — MEKH's
  precise operational role in the blending obligation could **not** be confirmed
  from the primary text (see §5). Corroborator confirmed §7: NAV ("állami adó-
  és vámhatóság") imposes the fine; the penalty = base (MJ shortfall) × a unit
  rate set in the implementing government decree.
- **Banking/borrowing/trading:** not confirmed from primary text accessed (gap).
- **Sustainability certification** (feedstock GHG criteria, mass balance) is
  governed by Decree 821/2021 (see Section 3); the blend percentages happen to
  be co-located in the same decree's §9.

### Notes on the mechanism
- The 165,630 / 155,160 HUF/1000 L Q3 figures equal the H1 base (158,800 / 148,760) × 1.043,
  i.e. the postponed 4.3% valorization (July-2025 KSH CPI), taking effect 1 Jul 2026. **Corroborator
  read the NAV valorized-rates table directly: it lists "165 630 Ft/ezer liter" (petrol) and
  "155 160 Ft/ezer liter" (diesel) for Q3 2026 — arithmetic and primary table both confirm.**
- The energy-security-crisis reduction (Govt Decree 51/2026) cut rates to the EU minimum
  (petrol 139,550; diesel 128,280) for 10 Mar–1 May 2026 and **lapsed on 1 May 2026**; it is
  **not** in force on 4 Jul 2026. From 1 May the standard H1 base applied until the 1 Jul step-up.
- Interaction with the mandate: unlike SE/FI, Hungary gives **no** excise relief tied to
  above-mandate biofuel volumes; the mandate (Act CXVII/2010) is the sole biofuel-promotion lever.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register the fuel-release entity for the blending obligation** — the party
   releasing petrol/diesel for consumption is obligated under Act CXVII/2010;
   compliance is reported to and enforced by **NAV** (state tax and customs
   authority). → NAV.
2. **Meet the 2026 energy-share obligation** each compliance year: overall
   **9.1 %**, petrol **≥6.1 %** bioethanol, diesel **≥9.6 %** (HVO ≥0.4 %),
   advanced (Annex IX-A) **≥1 %**, RCF ≤0.3 %; respect the E10/B7 blend walls and
   the 2019-level crop cap. Shortfalls are fined by NAV (MJ shortfall × decree
   unit rate). → NAV.
3. **Register in BIONYOM and BÜHG with NÉBIH** before conducting any
   sustainable-biofuel activity or issuing a Hungarian sustainability document;
   registration is valid 1 year — **renew 30 days before expiry**. → NÉBIH.
4. **Choose the sustainability verification route:** the national BÜHG system
   **or** an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE…) —
   Hungary accepts both. → NÉBIH / certification body.
5. **Issue the correct proof per consignment:** biomassza igazolás (producer),
   fenntarthatósági igazolás (BÜHG-registered trader/processor/distributor),
   fenntarthatósági bizonyítvány (official certificate), plus szállítójegy for
   forest biomass. → self.
6. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70–80 % for bio-electricity/
   heat) and the high-ILUC (palm) taper (0.04 % of transport fuel in 2026-27 → 0 %
   by 2030); use only EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/double-count claims.
   → self + auditor.
7. **Submit the annual NÉBIH data return by 20 March** for the preceding year;
   **retain records 5 years**. → NÉBIH.
8. **Undergo the annual voluntary-scheme audit** where the voluntary route is
   used. → certification body.
9. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** and record initial stock /
   transactions for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU
   mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
10. **Prepare for ETS2** (Directive (EU) 2023/959, Chapter IVa of Dir.
    2003/87/EC): road-fuel-supplier surrender obligation from **2028**, allowance
    trading from 2027. → national ETS2 authority.
11. **Note the tax position:** there is **no biofuel excise relief** — biodiesel/
    bioethanol/HVO in road fuel is taxed at the full rate (petrol 165,630 /
    diesel 155,160 HUF/1000 L from 1 Jul 2026); no filing beyond the standard
    excise obligation to NAV. → NAV.

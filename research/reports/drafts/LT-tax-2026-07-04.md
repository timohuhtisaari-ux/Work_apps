**Interaction with the mandate:** biofuel uptake is driven by the RES-T obligation under the Law on Alternative Fuels (other agent's scope). On the tax side, the notable relief is the **biogas/biomethane excise exemption** (fixed + CO2 parts), a state-aid measure; liquid biofuels do not appear to carry a separate reduced excise following the 2024 reform.

## National act implementing the Energy Taxation Directive (2003/96/EC)

- **Law on Excise Duties (*Akcizų įstatymas*)**, Art. 21 — sets motor-fuel excise (now split into a fixed part and a CO2 variable part) and implements Directive 2003/96/EC. Consolidated text on e-seimas / TAR (e-seimas.lrs.lt). The Dec 2024 amending act introduced the CO2 component trajectory and the biogas exemption.
- **State-aid:** the biogas/biomethane excise exemption is treated by the Lithuanian Competition Council as state aid requiring **European Commission approval** — the notified decision reference remains a gap.

## Gaps

- Whether liquid biofuels (E85/B100/HVO100) or their share in blends receive any excise reduction is not confirmed; the reform abolished fossil reliefs but the treatment of the bio fraction (esp. under the CO2 variable part) is unclear.
- Current state-aid clearance status of the biogas/biomethane exemption not confirmed (a formal EC decision reference was not located; the AF Observatory shows 0 % biogas excise, implying it is applied).
- **DG TAXUD cross-check not completed:** the Commission Excise Duty Tables (Energy Products) PDF was re-downloaded by the corroborator but is still a compressed/binary stream unparseable by WebFetch's extractor and by local Read (no poppler here). ## 4-line summary — Lithuania

- **Petrol:** EUR 562 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026 (fixed 466 + CO2 96), Law on Excise Duties Art. 21; CONFIRMED (VMI re-fetched).
- **Diesel:** EUR 553.6 / 1000 L from 1 Jan 2026 (fixed 500 + CO2 53.6); temporarily 503.6 for ~15 Apr–15 Jun 2026, restored 16 Jun 2026; CONFIRMED.
- **Biofuel relief:** biogas/biomethane (incl. blend share) exempt from both fixed and CO2 excise parts from 1 Jul 2025 (state aid; formal EC clearance reference not located, but AF Observatory lists biogas at 0 %); liquid biofuels appear to get no separate cut after the 2024 fossil-relief repeal.
- **Flags:** embedded CO2 component (petrol 96, diesel 53.6, rising to 2030); temporary Apr–Jun 2026 diesel dip now CONFIRMED and expired; biogas state-aid clearance reference unconfirmed; ETS2 obligation from 2028 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959; postponed one year, first surrender 2029); DG TAXUD PDF still unparseable (rates confirmed via VMI Tier-1 instead).

### 3.3 Certification schemes and proof of sustainability

- **National approval instrument:** the **hållbarhetsbesked** (3 kap. 1a–1b §§). Every rapporteringsskyldig operator (see 3.5) must hold one; it certifies that the operator's **kontrollsystem** (control system: risk assessment, supplier agreements, mass balance, GHG calculation routines) ensures fuels are sustainable. Application = description of control system + statement (utlåtande) from an **oberoende granskare** (independent auditor) with documented competence and independence. Valid **tills vidare** (indefinitely) subject to re-examination; revocable under 3 kap. 1d § if the system fails. `CONFIRMED` — Energimyndigheten hållbarhetsbesked page (T1, updated 2026-01-20, checked 2026-07-04) + lag 2010:598 3 kap. (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **EU voluntary schemes accepted:** Energimyndigheten accepts that **EU-recognised voluntary certification schemes** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE etc. — 18 schemes recognised by the Commission as of 2026-07-04) **may be used as the control system** underlying a hållbarhetsbesked, provided the scheme covers the whole production chain applied for; otherwise it must be supplemented by the operator's own control system for the uncovered part. There is **no separate Swedish national certification scheme**. `CONFIRMED` — Energimyndigheten FAQ/kontrollsystem content (T1, 2026-07-04) + DG ENER voluntary schemes page (T1, 2026-07-04).
- **Proof per consignment:** Sweden issues **no state per-consignment proof-of-sustainability document** (no Nabisy-equivalent). Sustainability characteristics travel with the fuel via operator-issued sustainability declarations under the mass-balance rules of the control system / voluntary scheme; the hållbarhetsbesked is the regulatory proof that the declarations can be relied on. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (inferred from T1 pages describing the system; no page states the negative explicitly — corroborator note).
- **Anläggningsbesked** (3a kap., introduced SFS 2015:838, amended SFS 2021:668 and 2025:571): proof that the operator has procedures ensuring the biofuel/biogas is **not produced from food or feed crops** (exception: biodiesel plants taken into operation before 31 Dec 2013 and not fully depreciated; no such exception for gaseous fuels). Needed by producers who are own-users and by suppliers/users who are tax-liable; required for fuels to qualify under the reduktionsplikt and for tax-exempt high blends/biogas (cross-ref mandates & tax researchers). `CONFIRMED` for existence/content (Energimyndigheten anläggningsbesked page T1, 2026-07-04 + lag 2010:598 3a kap. T1); `PRIMARY-ONLY` for the exact link to reduktionsplikt eligibility.

### 3.4 National registry and Union Database (UDB)

- **National registry:** none per-consignment. Reporting runs through Energimyndigheten's e-service **"Hållbara bränslen" on Mina sidor** (Bank-ID login, behörighet approval ~2–3 working days). `CONFIRMED` — Energimyndigheten rapportering page + hållbarhetsbesked page (both T1, 2026-07-04).
- **UDB obligation in Swedish law:** 3 kap. **1f §** lag 2010:598 (inserted by SFS 2025:571) requires reporting of transactions to the **unionsdatabas** per Art. 31a of Directive 2018/2001 as amended by 2023/2413. `CONFIRMED` — Riksdagen consolidated text (T1, 2026-07-04) + Energimyndigheten Unionsdatabasen page (T1, updated 2025-03-04, checked 2026-07-04).
- **Operational status:** UDB is **in operation** (Commission required it operational by 21 Nov 2024) but still being extended; Swedish producers and distributors of liquid and gaseous renewable fuels (biofuels, RFNBOs, RCFs) must register via EU Login at webgate.ec.europa.eu/UnionDatabase, appoint a lead user, and record transactions. Energimyndigheten notes some onboarding steps depend on pending Commission implementing/delegated acts. Contact: hbk@energimyndigheten.se. `CONFIRMED` for operational status (Energimyndigheten UDB page T1 + DG ENER UDB page T1, both checked 2026-07-04); `PRIMARY-ONLY` on the exact scope of currently-enforced recording duties in Sweden.

### 3.5 Feedstock restrictions

- **High-ILUC-risk (palm):** Sweden applies the EU phase-out (Del. Reg. 2019/807); additionally, the **reduktionsplikt cannot be met with high-ILUC-risk feedstocks unless certified low-ILUC-risk**. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (upgraded from UNCORROBORATED) — **corroborator read the reduktionsplikt legal basis directly on 2026-07-04: Förordning (2025:589) 7 § provides that biofuel from feedstocks meeting the Del. Reg. (EU) 2019/807 Art. 3 high-ILUC criteria may be used only if certified low-ILUC under Arts 4–6.** Riksrevisionen/trade-press (T2) previously flagged the 1 Jan 2021 tightening; the specific "PFAD" inclusion in that ban is treated in the next bullet.
- **PFAD reclassification:** from **1 Jul 2019** PFAD is classified as a **co-product** (samprodukt), not a residue, under the Swedish hållbarhetskriterier framework — full-chain traceability and land criteria apply, and typical GHG saving falls (~90 %→~65 %). Goes **beyond EU law** (EU leaves residue classification partly national). `UNCORROBORATED` — Neste remissvar on regeringen.se + Bioenergitidningen (T2, 2026-07-04); the amending förordning (hållbarhetskriterier, not 2025:589) text was **not** fetched this pass. Flag remains for follow-up.
- **No national soy ban** and **no national Annex IX additions** identified on Energimyndigheten pages checked 2026-07-04 (`PRIMARY-ONLY`, negative finding).
- Waste/residue verification (e.g. UCO): handled inside the kontrollsystem/voluntary-scheme audit; no Sweden-specific extra UCO audit rule located (gap — see 5).

## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope) — draft

1. Determine whether you are **rapporteringsskyldig** under 3 kap. 1 § lag 2010:598 (e.g. tax-liable under lag 1994:1776 for fuel containing biodrivmedel/RFNBO, or user of solid/gaseous biofuels above thresholds); if yes, **notify Energimyndigheten within 2 weeks**. [CONFIRMED]
2. Build a **kontrollsystem** (risk assessment, supplier contracts, mass balance, GHG methodology per STEMFS 2025:2) — or map an **EU-recognised voluntary scheme certificate (e.g. ISCC EU)** onto your full chain — and obtain an **oberoende granskare** statement. [CONFIRMED]
3. Apply for and hold a **hållbarhetsbesked** (Energimyndigheten, Mina sidor) before counting fuels as sustainable. [CONFIRMED]
4. If handling biodrivmedel/biogas for reduktionsplikt or tax-exempt use: obtain an **anläggningsbesked** (no food/feed-crop feedstock, 3a kap.). [CONFIRMED / link to reduktionsplikt PRIMARY-ONLY]
5. Register the company in the **Union Database** (EU Login, appoint lead user) and record transactions of liquid/gaseous renewable fuels as functions become mandatory (3 kap. 1f §). [PRIMARY-ONLY]
6. Submit the **annual sustainable-quantities report by 1 April** via "Hållbara bränslen". [CONFIRMED]
7. Re-verify the control system on Energimyndigheten's request (auditor engaged within 6 months) and update it for the **1 Jan 2026** GHG tiers and new scope (7.5–20 MW solid biomass, biogas). [CONFIRMED/PRIMARY-ONLY]
8. Keep evidence that high-ILUC feedstock (palm/PFAD) is excluded or low-ILUC-certified for reduktionsplikt volumes. [PRIMARY-ONLY — förordning 2025:589 § 7 confirmed; PFAD co-product basis still UNCORROBORATED]

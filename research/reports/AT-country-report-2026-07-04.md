## Executive summary

Austria imposes a **dual obligation** on fuel suppliers under the **Kraftstoffverordnung
2012 (KVO)**: an energy-based **Substitutionsverpflichtung** (minimum biofuel energy share
in petrol and diesel) and a rising **Treibhausgas-(THG-)Minderungsverpflichtung**
(GHG-intensity reduction). Reported current values are petrol sub-quota **3.4 %**, diesel
sub-quota **6.3 %**, overall substitution **5.75 %**, and a GHG reduction of **7 %
(2024–2025) rising to 8 % from 2026** and **13 % by 2030** — but these all remain
**UNCORROBORATED** because the RIS legal database was unreachable (HTTP 503) all day, and
there is an **unresolved CONFLICT** on the 2026 GHG level (draft 7 %→8 % vs ePURE's 7.5 %
for 2025). On tax, the Mineralölsteuer (MinStG 2022) is **CONFIRMED** at 482 EUR/1000 L
petrol / 397 EUR/1000 L diesel (lower "with biogenic content" tier), with a separate
national CO2 price of **55 EUR/t (NEHG 2022)** and no dedicated high-blend relief; EU-ETS2
obligation starts **1 Jan 2028**. Sustainability is transposed via KVO amendment BGBl. II
452/2022 (RED II), documented in the **elNa** registry (Umweltbundesamt), with palm/PFAD
high-ILUC feedstocks excluded since **1 July 2021**. **RED III is NOT yet transposed for
transport** (deadline 21 May 2025 missed; a KVO Novelle is only in Begutachtung) — Austria
is in transposition delay with infringement risk.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** "Inverkehrbringer" — those placing fossil petrol/diesel for road
  transport into free circulation in Austria. [S1]
- **Compliance transfer:** obligations (substitution, advanced, GHG) can be contractually
  transferred to third parties ("Quotenpooler"), incl. crediting renewable electricity used
  in EVs; obligated party should primarily meet targets itself. [S1, S5]
- **Registry:** **elNa** (elektronischer Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis) operated by the
  **Umweltbundesamt** — used for sustainability proofs and quota accounting. [S3, S5]
- **Competence authority:** Federal ministry with the climate/energy/mobility portfolio
  (BMK / BMIMI, name has changed across governments) issues the KVO; Umweltbundesamt runs
  elNa. [S3]

### Notes / interaction with the mandate
- Austria's MÖSt already embeds a de-facto biofuel incentive through the two-tier "with/without biogenic content" rates; unlike SE/FI it is **not** structured as relief limited to volumes beyond the blending obligation. The blending/GHG-reduction obligation itself sits under the Kraftstoffverordnung (Section 1).
- CO2 pricing is a **separate instrument** (NEHG 2022), not part of the MÖSt. In 2026 it adds roughly 12.5 ct/L net to petrol and 13.8 ct/L net to diesel at 55 EUR/t (per BMF/ÖAMTC illustration).
- National ETD transposition: **Mineralölsteuergesetz 2022 (MinStG 2022)** (successor to MinStG 1995), transposing Directive 2003/96/EC.

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Austria accepts the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** for demonstrating
  sustainability/GHG criteria; certification-scheme choice (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU,
  2BSvs, SURE, RSB, etc.) is free to the operator, but **participation in elNa is
  mandatory** for every installation in Austria. [CONFIRMED — Umweltbundesamt; WKO]
- Austria additionally hosts a **national voluntary scheme**: the **Austrian
  Agricultural Certification Scheme (AACS)**, EU-recognised by Commission decision
  **26 Sep 2022** (for compliance of Austrian agricultural biomass with RED
  criteria). [CONFIRMED — DG ENER voluntary-schemes page]
- Full EU-recognised list (DG ENER, as accessed 2026-07-04) includes 2BSvs,
  Better Biomass, Bonsucro EU, ISCC EU, KZR INiG, REDcert, RSB, RTRS EU RED,
  SURE, AACS and others. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register in elNa** with Umweltbundesamt GmbH (obtain registration number + user
   account) and complete the mandatory UBA training **before** issuing or receiving any
   Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis. → Umweltbundesamt GmbH.
2. **Obtain certification** under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU,
   2BSvs, SURE, or the national AACS for Austrian agri-biomass), covering full
   chain-of-custody / mass balance. → chosen scheme / certification body.
3. **Meet the KVO supplier obligation** for compliance year 2026: the energy-based
   substitution sub-quotas (petrol 3.4 % / diesel 6.3 %, overall 5.75 %) **and** the
   THG-Minderung GHG-intensity reduction (draft 8 % for 2026 — treat as unconfirmed, see
   Section 5). → obligated party self-accounting via elNa. *[value verification pending RIS]*
4. **Issue/transfer Nachhaltigkeitsnachweise (NHN)** per consignment in elNa, maintaining
   full mass balance from cultivation to finished product, and credit only sustainable,
   non-high-ILUC feedstocks (palm/PFAD excluded since **1 July 2021**). → elNa / self + auditor.
5. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** applicable to your installation vintage (50 / 60 / 65 %;
   70 % RFNBO). → self + auditor.
6. **Enter transaction/sustainability data in the UDB** for in-scope consignments
   (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → Union Database *[confirm AT mandatory-use date]*.
7. **Submit the annual substitution/GHG-obligation report** to the authority and undergo the
   annual certification audit and any UBA on-site inspection (exact statutory deadline to be
   confirmed from KVO text). → Umweltbundesamt / auditor.
8. **Account for CO2 pricing separately** under NEHG 2022 (55 EUR/t in 2026); prepare for the
   EU-ETS2 obligation starting **1 Jan 2028**. → BMF / national emissions-trading registry.

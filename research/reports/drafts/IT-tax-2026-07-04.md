## 2. Fuel taxation

Excise (accisa) on energy products is governed by the **Testo Unico Accise, D.Lgs. 26 ottobre 1995 n. 504 (TUA), Allegato I** — Italy's implementation of Directive 2003/96/EC, administered by ADM. Two big 2025–26 developments: (1) the **diesel–petrol excise realignment** ("superamento del sussidio ambientalmente dannoso EN.SI.24"): an interministerial decree of 14 May 2025 started a five-year ±1.5 c€/L per year convergence (petrol 728,40→713,40; diesel 617,40→632,40 from 15 May 2025), and the **Legge di Bilancio 2026 completed it early** — from **1 Jan 2026 petrol and diesel are both 672,90 €/1000 L** (petrol −4.05 c€/L, diesel +4.05 c€/L). (2) Crisis-driven **temporary cuts** (Middle-East price spike): successive MEF(-MASE) decrees reduced rates from ~19 March 2026; the last decree (GU n. 129, 6 Jun 2026) set both fuels at **622,90 €/1000 L for 7 Jun–3 Jul 2026**; it was **not renewed**, so **from 4 July 2026 (today) the ordinary 672,90 applies again**.

### Interaction with the mandate
The excise realignment explicitly serves the environmental-subsidy (SAD) reform agenda, and the HVO reduced rate + haulier HVO refund give a direct fiscal pull for renewable diesel on top of the CIC obligation. Legge di Bilancio 2026 also carried biomethane measures (grid-connection cost sharing 70 % network operators / 30 % producers) — flagged to the mandates agent.

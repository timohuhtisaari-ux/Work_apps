**Mandate interaction:** Cyprus operates a RES-T obligation on fuel suppliers; there is no excise incentive tied to
volumes above the obligation. Mandate detail is out of scope.

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Excise Duties Law of 2004 (Law 91(I)/2004)** and
its subsidiary orders; Cyprus additionally relies on the accession-era derogation in Directive 2004/75/EC. No
biofuel-specific notified state-aid decision identified.

### 4-line summary — Cyprus
- Petrol excise: 429.00 EUR/1000 L (€0.429/L), standard rate.
- Diesel excise: 400.00 EUR/1000 L (€0.400/L), standard rate.
- Biofuel relief: none via excise — no reduced rate/exemption for biofuels or high blends identified (Cyprus relies on ETD derogation, not biofuel relief).
- Flags: rates corroborated by two Tier-2 sources but Tier-1 (mof.gov.cy) was HTTP 503 — treat as PRIMARY-ONLY pending; temporary −8.33c/L cut for Apr–Jun 2026 now expired; carbon tax on fuels PROPOSED (green-tax reform); ETS2 surrender from 2028 (Dir (EU) 2023/959).

# EU-Level Primary Sources

The EU framework sets the floor that member states transpose. Researchers check
these before diving into national law, because national schemes constantly
reference them.

## Core legal acts (all on EUR-Lex — https://eur-lex.europa.eu)

| Instrument | Reference | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Renewable Energy Directive III (RED III) | Directive (EU) 2023/2413 | 2030 transport target (29 % RES-T share **or** 14.5 % GHG-intensity reduction), advanced-biofuel + RFNBO sub-targets, transposition deadline 21 May 2025 |
| Renewable Energy Directive II (RED II) | Directive (EU) 2018/2001 (consolidated) | Sustainability & GHG-saving criteria, Annex IX feedstock lists, 7 % crop cap, mass balance rules — still the operative baseline where RED III is not yet transposed |
| Energy Taxation Directive | Directive 2003/96/EC | Minimum excise rates on motor fuels; Art. 16 allows biofuel exemptions/reductions; recast proposal COM(2021) 563 still pending |
| Fuel Quality Directive | Directive 98/70/EC (as amended) | E10/B7 blend walls, 6 % GHG-intensity reduction (Art. 7a) |
| ILUC Delegated Regulation | Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/807 | High-ILUC-risk feedstock (palm oil) phase-out rules |
| Sustainability certification Implementing Regulation | Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/996 | Rules for voluntary schemes, audits, Union Database |
| ReFuelEU Aviation | Regulation (EU) 2023/2405 | SAF blending mandates (directly applicable, no transposition) |
| FuelEU Maritime | Regulation (EU) 2023/1805 | GHG-intensity limits on marine fuel (directly applicable) |
| Excise duty general arrangements | Directive (EU) 2020/262 | Movement and holding of excise goods incl. fuels |

## Institutional pages

| Institution | URL | What to get there |
|---|---|---|
| European Commission, DG ENER — renewables/biofuels | https://energy.ec.europa.eu | Recognised voluntary certification schemes list, RED transposition status, Union Database (UDB) news |
| Union Database for Biofuels (UDB) | via DG ENER | Chain-of-custody traceability obligations on operators |

| European Commission, DG CLIMA | https://climate.ec.europa.eu | ETS2 for road-transport/buildings fuels — established by Directive (EU) 2023/959 (Ch. IVa of Dir 2003/87/EC); obligation start postponed to 1 Jan 2028 (first surrender 2029, auctioning from 2027); FuelEU Maritime |
| EUR-Lex national transposition (NIM) pages | https://eur-lex.europa.eu | Per-directive list of notified national implementing measures — the fastest map from a directive to each member state's transposing acts |
| European Environment Agency | https://www.eea.europa.eu | RES-T shares monitoring (Tier 2) |

## High-value Tier 2 (corroboration) sources

- **USDA FAS GAIN "Biofuels Annual" reports** (EU + per-country) — detailed,
  updated yearly, free: https://fas.usda.gov (search GAIN reports).
- **ePURE** (ethanol) and **EBB** (biodiesel) industry statistics and
  mandate trackers.
- **ICCT** and **Transport & Environment** policy analyses.
- **ISCC EU system updates** — country-specific implementation notes for
  certification requirements.
- **IEA Bioenergy Task 39** country reports.

> Reminder: Tier 2 sources corroborate; they never substitute for the legal
> text. The TEDB excise tables are Commission-published and count as Tier 1
> for tax rates, but always cross-check the national tax authority's own table.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.1 Current obligation

Hungary runs an **energy-based renewable-fuel share obligation** ("kötelező
megújulóüzemanyag-részarány") on entities that release motor fuel for
consumption. The framework statute is **Act CXVII of 2010** (2010. évi CXVII.
törvény) on promoting renewable energy in transport and reducing transport
GHG emissions; the statute delegates the numeric percentages to a government
decree (§13(1)(h)). The operative percentages for 2026 sit in **Government
Decree 821/2021 (XII. 28.), § 9**, as substantially amended by **Government
Decree 488/2025 (XII. 31.)** (published in Magyar Közlöny No. 162 of 31 Dec 2025;
in force 1 Jan 2026).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** the entity that releases motor petrol/diesel for
  consumption (excise release), i.e. the same operators liable for fuel excise.
- **Metric:** energy content of biofuel/renewable fuel over the combined energy
  content of petrol + diesel released, to one decimal place (Act CXVII/2010 §2
  definition of részarány).
- **Enforcement/registry:** compliance is reported to and enforced by the
  **state tax and customs authority (NAV)** under Act CXVII/2010 §7; MEKH
  (energy regulator) is named in the source registry as the mandates authority,
  but the primary text accessed assigns non-compliance fines to NAV — MEKH's
  precise operational role in the blending obligation could **not** be confirmed
  from the primary text (see Gaps).
- **Banking/borrowing/trading:** not confirmed from primary text accessed (gap).
- **Sustainability certification** (feedstock GHG criteria, mass balance) is
  governed by Decree 821/2021 — that is the *sustainability* agent's scope; the
  blend percentages happen to be co-located in the same decree's §9.

- **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition status:** Hungary has
  **domestically adopted** transposing measures — Act CXVII/2010 was amended in
  2025 (in-force consolidated text dated 2026-05-14 references Dir. (EU) 2023/2413
  in §8(3)) and the 488/2025 decree implements the RED III sub-targets. However,
  the EUR-Lex NIM collection for 2023/2413 showed **no notified national measures
  for Hungary**, and the Commission opened infringement (letters of formal notice)
  against 26 member states including Hungary for failing to communicate full
  transposition. Net status: **transposed in national law but in infringement for
  non-/incomplete notification.** (PRIMARY-ONLY / infringement)
- **EU cross-cut:** ETS2 (Reg. (EU) 2026/667) — compliance obligation for road
  transport fuel suppliers starts **1 Jan 2028**, auctioning from 2027. Applies
  to Hungarian fuel suppliers directly; not part of the national blending
  mandate.

- **njt.hu (official National Legislation Repository) returned HTTP 503** on every
  attempt (decree pages 2025-483 and 2025-488, Act 2010-117). Primary percentages
  were instead obtained from the consolidated law database **net.jogtar.hu**
  (Hatályos Jogszabályok Gyűjteménye) — a recognised consolidated legal database,
  treated here as Tier 1 — and corroborated by two independent secondary sources.
  Follow-up: re-verify §9 directly on njt.hu or in Magyar Közlöny No. 162/2025.
- **Annex IX-B 4 % cap** rests on ePURE only (UNCORROBORATED) — confirm against
  Decree 821/2021 §9.
- **Penalty rate (32 HUF/MJ; ~HUF 20 bn annual)** is secondary-only (agroinform).
  The *enforcement authority* (NAV, tax and customs) is primary (Act §7).
- **MEKH's operational role** in the blending obligation not confirmed from
  primary text; registry lists MEKH but Act §7 points to NAV. Flag for registry
  note.

### 5-line summary — Hungary
1. Obligation type: energy-based renewable-fuel share on fuel-releasing entities (Act CXVII/2010 + Decree 821/2021 §9).
2. Current level (2026): 9.1 % overall; petrol ≥6.1 % bioethanol, diesel ≥9.6 % biofuel (HVO ≥0.4 %); advanced ≥1 %.
3. Next legislated change: advanced sub-target rises to 5.5 % by 2030; overall RES-T 25 % by 2030 (intermediate 2027-29 steps a gap).
4. RED III status: transposed domestically (2025 amendment + Decree 488/2025) but in infringement — no NIM notified, Commission letter of formal notice.
5. Flags: Annex IX-B 4 % cap UNCORROBORATED; penalty rate UNCORROBORATED; njt.hu unreachable (503) — primary taken from net.jogtar; MEKH role a gap.

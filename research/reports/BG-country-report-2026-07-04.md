## Executive summary

Bulgaria imposes a **volumetric blending obligation** on persons placing
petroleum-derived liquid transport fuels on the market, set by the **Energy from
Renewable Sources Act (ЗЕВИ / ZEVI), Art. 47**, with blending done in licensed
tax warehouses under the Ministry of Energy (fuel-quality control by DAMTN). The
**diesel obligation is min. 6 % biodiesel (of which ≥1 % advanced)** — CONFIRMED
from a Tier-1 ministry source plus USDA GAIN — while the **petrol figure of 9 %
bioethanol** is value-corroborated but carries a **CONFLICT on its effective
date (1 March 2019 vs 1 March 2025)** with no machine-read Tier-1 quote of the
petrol clause. There is **no biofuel excise relief**: from **1 January 2026
Bulgaria adopted the euro** (fixed 1.95583), so excise is now stated in EUR —
petrol €363.02/1000 L and diesel €330.29/1000 L, both at/just above the EU
minimums and unchanged in substance. Sustainability compliance runs through the
national sustainability-criteria Ordinance (amended ДВ No. 26/2024) under ZEVI,
accepting EU-recognised voluntary schemes, with annual operator GHG-intensity
reporting to the **Ministry of Environment and Water (MOEW)**. RED III is
**partially transposed** (28 measures notified, incl. a new ZEVI of 10 Jun
2025), with a **February 2025 reasoned opinion outstanding on permitting**; ETS2
(Directive (EU) 2023/959) adds a fuel-supplier surrender obligation from 2028.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Bulgaria imposes a **volumetric blending obligation** on persons placing
petroleum-derived liquid transport fuels on the market, set by the **Energy
from Renewable Sources Act (ЗЕВИ / ZEVI), Art. 47**. Blending is done in
licensed tax warehouses (Excise Duties and Tax Warehouses Act). Final
distributors must supply blended fuel two months after the party placing it on
the market. ZEVI's overarching aim is the RED transport target (14 % renewable
energy in transport by 2030 per the RED II / 2023-2025 ZEVI amendments). The
Ministry of Energy is the responsible ministry.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** persons placing petroleum-derived liquid transport fuels
  on the market at release for consumption; blending only in licensed tax
  warehouses. Final distributors must offer blended fuel two months later
  (ZEVI Art. 47).
- **Metric:** minimum biofuel content by **volume** per fuel grade, with advanced
  sub-quotas expressed as a share of the biocomponent volume.
- **Authority:** Ministry of Energy (me.government.bg); fuel-quality control is
  exercised by DAMTN (State Agency for Metrological and Technical Surveillance)
  under the liquid-fuel quality ordinance.
- **Certificates/registry/banking:** the 2023/2025 ZEVI amendments introduce the
  option to meet the transport RES share via **direct investment or
  certification** (details not read from primary text — gap).

### Notes
- **In-force rates on 4 Jul 2026:** petrol €363.02/1000 L, diesel €330.29/1000 L — stable,
  essentially at the ETD 2003/96/EC minimums, and now denominated in euro.
- The euro-adoption changeover (dual circulation Jan 2026; mandatory dual pricing 8 Aug 2025–
  8 Aug 2026) redenominated the ЗАДС amounts; there was no substantive change to the motor-fuel
  excise burden — €363.02 = BGN 710 and €330.29 = BGN 646 at the fixed 1.95583 rate.
- Interaction with the mandate: Bulgaria's biofuel share is delivered through the RES blending
  obligation; no excise incentive is tied to above-mandate volumes.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Meet the ZEVI Art. 47 blending obligation** on fuels released for
   consumption (blending in a licensed tax warehouse): diesel **≥6 %** biodiesel
   (of which **≥1 % advanced**) and petrol **≥9 %** bioethanol/bio-ethers;
   respect the E10/B7 blend walls. Final distributors must offer blended fuel two
   months after placing-on-market. → Ministry of Energy (fuel-quality control by
   DAMTN).
2. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert,
   2BSvs, SURE…) — accepted by the sustainability ordinance — and issue a **PoS
   per consignment** under mass balance. → approved certifying body.
3. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) and document
   them per consignment. → self + auditor.
4. **Report the annual GHG intensity** of biofuels delivered to the BG market
   (previous year) to the **Ministry of Environment and Water (MOEW)**. → MOEW.
5. **Provide the required soil/land/water/air-protection information** for
   feedstock production as prescribed by the ordinance. → self / MOEW.
6. **Undergo independent third-party audit** of the sustainability/GHG data
   (ZEVI + ordinance) and obtain/maintain the compliance certificate. → approved
   certifying body.
7. **Respect the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out and the 7 % crop cap**; use only
   EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/waste claims. → self + auditor.
8. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** and record in-scope transactions
   (operational since 15 Jan 2024; mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
9. **Prepare for ETS2** (Directive (EU) 2023/959, Chapter IVa of Dir.
   2003/87/EC): road-fuel-supplier surrender obligation from **2028**, trading
   from 2027. → national ETS2 authority.
10. **Note the tax position:** no biofuel excise relief — road blends taxed at
    the full fuel rate (petrol €363.02 / diesel €330.29 per 1000 L, in EUR since
    1 Jan 2026); standard excise obligations to the Customs Agency only. →
    Customs Agency.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Carried-forward flags (prominent):**
- **Diesel obligation is min. 6 % biodiesel (of which ≥1 % advanced)** —
  CONFIRMED (me.gov Tier-1 + USDA GAIN, independent).
- **Euro adopted 1 Jan 2026** (fixed 1.95583); ЗАДС excise is now stated in EUR —
  no substantive change to the motor-fuel burden (€363.02 = BGN 710; €330.29 =
  BGN 646).
- **Petrol 9 % effective-date CONFLICT:** BG sources quoting ЗЕВИ čl.47 state
  bioethanol in petrol has been **9 % since 1 March 2019** (base text originally
  7 %, then 9 %); USDA GAIN 2025 instead states bioethanol was **"raised to 9 %
  from 1 March 2025"** with ≥1 % advanced. The **9 % value is corroborated by two
  independent Tier-2 sources**, but the effective date differs (2019 vs 2025) and
  **no machine-read Tier-1 quote of the petrol clause was obtained** (me.gov
  Tier-1 page describes only diesel/advanced). Report both dates; do not pick
  silently.
- **RED III partially transposed** (28 measures notified incl. ZEVI 10 Jun 2025);
  **February 2025 reasoned opinion (INFR(2024)0211) outstanding on the permitting-
  acceleration provisions**. BG is not on the Dec 2025 reasoned-opinion list.

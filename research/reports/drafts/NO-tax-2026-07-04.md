# Norway (NO) — Section 2: Fuel taxation

- **Report date:** 2026-07-05 (draft filename 2026-07-04)
- **Researched by:** fuel-tax-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Scope note:** Norway is an **EEA** state, not an EU member. The Energy
  Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC is **not directly applicable**; Norway runs its
  own excise system (særavgifter) set annually by the Storting's tax
  resolutions (avgiftsvedtak) and detailed in the særavgiftsforskrift, all
  administered by **Skatteetaten**. There is **no EU ETS2** obligation for
  Norwegian road fuels; carbon pricing on road fuel is the domestic
  **CO2-avgift**. Do not read EU state-aid decisions here (state aid is
  supervised by ESA/EFTA, not the Commission).

Norway taxes road fuel in **two stacked excises**: the **veibruksavgift**
(road-usage duty) and the **CO2-avgift på mineralske produkter** (CO2 tax on
mineral products). Both are levied per litre and both apply on top of each
other (and a sulphur duty).

## 2.2 Interaction with the mandate (omsetningskrav)

Unlike Sweden/Finland (tax relief only *above* the obligation), Norway does the
opposite for **liquid** biofuel: since 2020 **veibruksavgift applies to all
biofuel** — inside *and* above the mandate — so there is no road-usage-duty
advantage to biofuel over fossil fuel per litre. The remaining fiscal advantage
of biofuel is that its **biogenic share escapes the CO2-avgift**. Gaseous
renewables (biogas/biomethane, hydrogen) remain fully untaxed. The pending
høring (S7) would erode the biofuel advantage further by taxing the fossil
share of high blends under the CO2-avgift.

## 2.3 The 2026 zero-rate episode (important, adopted)

The Storting adopted — against the sitting Government's budget proposal — a
temporary **NOK 0** veibruksavgift on all road fuels for **1 April 2026 to
1 September 2026**. Reported effect: pump price down ~NOK 4.41/L (incl. VAT) on
petrol and ~NOK 2.85/L (incl. VAT) on diesel; estimated revenue loss ~NOK 3.3
bn. The **CO2-avgift is unaffected** and continues throughout. Outside that
window the standard §1 rates in the table apply. This is **adopted**, not a
proposal.

### 4-line summary — Norway
- **Petrol:** veibruksavgift NOK 3.77/L + CO2-avgift NOK 3.80/L (1 Jan 2026); road-usage duty cut to NOK 0 for all fuels 1 Apr–1 Sep 2026.
- **Diesel:** veibruksavgift NOK 2.28/L + CO2-avgift NOK 4.42/L (1 Jan 2026); same 1 Apr–1 Sep 2026 zero road-usage window.
- **Biofuel relief:** liquid biofuel pays full veibruksavgift whether in or above the mandate (above-mandate exemption gone since 2020), keeps only CO2-avgift relief on its biogenic share; biogas/biomethane fully exempt.
- **Flags:** FY2026 zero road-usage period is an adopted Storting override (CONFIRMED); høring to tax the fossil share of high-blend biofuel is PROPOSED; one unreconciled auto-diesel CO2 figure to verify.

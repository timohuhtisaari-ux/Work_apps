# Latvia — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Latvia (LV)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

As of **1 January 2026** Latvia's new **Transport Energy Law (Transporta enerģijas likums)** entered into force, replacing the old Biofuel Law and transposing RED III's transport-sector provisions. It is a **combined scheme**: a GHG-intensity reduction target (**≥ 16 %** on realised transport energy from 2030), an **advanced-fuel + RFNBO energy-share sub-obligation** (**≥ 1 %** in 2026–2029, rising to **≥ 5.5 %** from 2030 of which ≥ 1 pp RFNBO), and **transitional volumetric blending** (RON 95 petrol ≥ 9.5 vol %, diesel ≥ 6.5 vol %, with pure diesel sellable only until 31 March 2026). The obligation binds fuel suppliers placing > 100 t/year on the market; the **State Environmental Service (VVD)** is the competent verifier, data flows into the **EU Union Database**, and shortfalls carry penalties of **€90/t CO2eq** and **€70/GJ** (the latter from 2028). On the tax side, standard petrol is EUR 555/1000 L and diesel EUR 467/1000 L, with reduced excise for high blends only (E85-type EUR 360, biodiesel/HVO wholly from biomass EUR 330); a temporary Apr–Jun 2026 diesel cut has since expired. A supplier's top obligations are therefore: meet the advanced/RFNBO and transitional blend floors, hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification with whole-chain audit, and file the verified **annual report to VVD by 31 May** (first due 31 May 2027 for CY2026).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** **fuel suppliers** placing on the transport market **> 100 tonnes/year** (Art. 1 definition). Exemptions for suppliers of only alternative fuels, RFNBO, or aviation fuel (Art. 11(2), Art. 13(3)). [1]
- **Administrator:** Ministry of Climate and Energy (kem.gov.lv) framework; the **State Environmental Service (VVD)** is the sustainability market-surveillance authority (see Section 3); State Revenue Service (VID) handles the fuel-tax side (Section 2).
- **Compliance:** demonstrated against the annual GHG-reduction and energy-share obligations plus the transitional volumetric blend floors; shortfalls trigger the per-tonne / per-GJ additional payments above.
- Transition from the repealed **Biodegvielas likums**; note Latvia had earlier (2022) temporarily **suspended** its mandatory biofuel blend to curb fuel-price inflation — historical context, superseded by the 2026 law. [5]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Determine obligated status:** any supplier placing **> 100 t/year** of transport fuel on the Latvian market is obligated (exemptions for alternative-fuel-only, RFNBO-only, or aviation-fuel suppliers). Authority: **Ministry of Climate and Energy (KEM)** / **State Environmental Service (VVD)**.
2. **Meet the 2026 sub-obligations:** advanced biofuels + RFNBO **≥ 1 energy %**; transitional **RON 95 petrol ≥ 9.5 vol %** and **diesel ≥ 6.5 vol %**. Stop selling **pure (un-blended) diesel after 31 March 2026**. Authority: **VVD**.
3. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (e.g. ISCC / REDcert) or an accepted MS national-scheme certificate covering each consignment (Art. 16(3)), and secure **whole-supply-chain independent audit** per IR (EU) 2022/996. Authority: **VVD**.
4. **Operate a mass-balance system** across the whole supply chain, including financial-support data (Art. 17(2)). Authority: **VVD**.
5. **Enter chain-of-custody data into the EU Union Database** (Art. 17(3)) and ensure certification-body verification (Art. 17(4)). Authority: **VVD**.
6. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving thresholds** and prepare for the **≥ 16 % supplier GHG-reduction target from 2030** (Art. 11, 16). Authority: **VVD**.
7. **File the verified annual report by 31 May** — first report **31 May 2027** for CY2026 (GHG savings, advanced-fuel %, volumes by type/feedstock, origin). Authority: **VVD**.
8. **From 1 Jan 2030, procure an independent audit** of the annual report (Art. 20(3)). Authority: **VVD**.
9. **Budget for penalties:** **€90/t CO2eq** (GHG), **€70/GJ** (advanced-fuel, from 1 Jan 2028), **5 % of net turnover** (non-reporting), payable within 30 days. Authority: **VVD**.
10. **Note ETS2 exposure:** fuel-supplier surrender obligation from **1 Jan 2028**, first surrender **2029**. Authority: EU (Dir. (EU) 2023/959).

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Carried-forward flags (must remain prominent):**
- **RED III transposed** for the transport sector via the **Transport Energy Law (in force 1 Jan 2026)** (replaces the Biofuel Law).
- **Advanced + RFNBO ≥ 1 % (2026)** rising to **5.5 % + 16 % GHG-intensity reduction by 2030** (of which ≥ 1 pp RFNBO) — CONFIRMED.
- **Competent verifier is the State Environmental Service (VVD)** — not KEM (policy owner) and not VID (tax authority only).
- **Permitting provisions remain on the 24 July 2025 formal-notice list** (26 MS incl. Latvia) — the transport-sector gap is closed, but RED III is not yet certified fully transposed.
- **ETS2** established by Directive (EU) 2023/959; obligation start **postponed to 1 Jan 2028** (first surrender 2029) in the early-2026 climate package.

**Other conflicts and gaps:**
- Article-level detail (esp. Art. 16, 17, 20, 22, 23) comes from a single WebFetch parse of the likumi.lv consolidated text; the headline figures (16 %, 1 %/5.5 %, €90/t, €70/unit) are **independently corroborated on lvportals.lv**, but exact article numbers should still be re-confirmed against likumi.lv id/364968.
- The **9.5 % / 6.5 % volumetric floors** have only one Tier-2 secondary (BNN) beyond the primary text — seek a second independent corroboration.
- Annex IX-B (UCO/animal-fat) cap and crop cap not explicit in primary text (GAP); double-counting multipliers not isolated (GAP).
- Full set of implementing Cabinet regulations not yet adopted (deadline 31 Dec 2026); sustainability-methodology and reporting-format regulations to be re-checked later in 2026.
- **State-aid basis for the E85 (360) / B100/HVO100 (330) reduced excise rates not located**; unclear whether relief is limited to non-mandated volumes.
- Road-transport biomethane excise treatment not confirmed from a rate table (UNCORROBORATED).
- DG TAXUD Excise Duty Tables PDF unparseable (no poppler); LV rates corroborated via two independent Tier-1 tables — VID and Ministry of Finance — which agree exactly (555 / 467 / 360 / 330).

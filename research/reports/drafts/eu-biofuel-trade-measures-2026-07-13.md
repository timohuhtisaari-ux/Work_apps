## SAF-from-China — the surveillance question, pinned

**Bottom line:** As of 2026-07-13 the EU applies **no anti-dumping duty, no
countervailing duty, and no formal safeguard/prior-surveillance Regulation** to
sustainable aviation fuel from China. SAF was **deliberately excluded** from the
China biodiesel anti-dumping measure, and is instead **statistically monitored**
through **dedicated TARIC codes** so the Commission can detect an import surge and
act quickly if a complaint is lodged.

- **Exclusion:** Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) **2025/261** (definitive
  China biodiesel AD), **recital 22**: "at present SAF has different use and cannot
  be deemed interchangeable or in competition with HVO/FAME" — SAF "may therefore be
  excluded from the scope." SAF meeting ASTM D7566 is carved out. `CONFIRMED` [1][2].
- **Monitoring mechanism:** the Commission stated SAF imports from China "would be
  closely monitored thanks to a **dedicated, code-based system**." `CONFIRMED`
  (Commission notice via trade press) [9]. The dedicated codes were operationalised/
  corrected in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) **2025/1866** (15 Sep 2025),
  which sets SAF-related TARIC codes **2710 19 11 10, 2710 19 15 10, 2710 19 21 10,
  2710 19 25 10, 2710 19 29 10** to track SAF separately from dutiable biodiesel.
  `PRIMARY-ONLY` (code list from the 2025/1866 text via WebFetch; recommend a direct
  TARIC lookup to re-confirm each 10-digit code) [10].
- **Legal character:** this is **customs monitoring within the AD framework**
  (statistical surveillance to police the SAF/biodiesel scope boundary), **not** a
  stand-alone import prior-surveillance regime under Regulation (EU) 2015/478. The
  watchlist label "TARIC prior-surveillance" should be amended to
  **"TARIC monitoring codes (within China biodiesel AD framework)."** `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **No investigation on SAF:** no AD/CVD investigation on Chinese SAF has been
  initiated. The EBB publicly urged the Commission to act on Chinese SAF; the
  Commission has so far only monitored. `CONFIRMED` (no initiation notice located
  in the OJ C-series or DG TRADE ongoing-investigations list) [9]. **This is the
  single most important escalation trigger to watch.**

**Gap/next check:** verify each SAF TARIC 10-digit code live in TARIC, and re-scan
the OJ C-series + DG TRADE "ongoing investigations" weekly for any SAF-China
initiation notice.

## Investigations / watch list (ongoing or expected)

1. **SAF — China (PRIORITY):** monitoring only today; watch for an AD/CVD
   **initiation notice** (OJ C-series) — EBB is pressing for one. Any surge in the
   SAF TARIC codes is the leading indicator. `CONFIRMED` no case yet.
2. **Biodiesel — Indonesia CVD (2019/2092):** **expiry review ongoing** (Notice
   C/2024/7405, EBB request 8 Sep 2024) — measures stay in force pending outcome
   (due within ~15 months of initiation). In parallel, **WTO DS618**: panel final
   report to parties 12 Jun 2025, **circulated 22 Aug 2025**, found the EU acted
   inconsistently on the CPO "less-than-adequate-remuneration" subsidy finding and
   parts of the injury/causation analysis; Indonesia is pressing the EU to remove
   duties. Watch: expiry-review result, EU appeal/MPIA route, any re-determination.
   `CONFIRMED`.
3. **Biodiesel — USA AD/CVD (2021/1266 & 2021/1267):** in force; **5-year term
   expires ~July 2026** — watch for an **expiry-review initiation** to keep them
   alive. Separately, the **Braya Renewable Fuels (Canada) new-exporter review**
   (Reg (EU) 2026/1341) is ongoing with the duty temporarily repealed and imports
   registered pending outcome (~9 months). `PRIMARY-ONLY` on the 2026 expiry timing.
4. **UCO/UCOME fraud (China & others):** no trade-defence case, but the 18 Jul 2025
   fraud examination found "systemic weaknesses" (not confirmed fraud), and
   Reg (EU) 2025/2181 tightens UCO import chain-of-custody. Watch for a possible
   future circumvention/AD angle or certification-scheme withdrawals. `CONFIRMED`.
5. **Anti-circumvention:** Indonesia→China/UK case **closed** (Decision 2024/1273,
   no circumvention). No new open circumvention case on China biodiesel located.
   Watch for a fresh circumvention initiation if trade shifts to third countries.
   `CONFIRMED`.

**Cross-references (other agents):** RED III sustainability/UCO certification,
blending mandates, and ReFuelEU SAF mandate levels are out of scope here — see the
sustainability and mandates reports. Reg (EU) 2025/2181 (UCO import conditions) sits
at the customs/sustainability boundary and is flagged for the sustainability agent.

## 6-line executive summary (most consequential, live vs under review)

1. **China biodiesel (FAME + HVO):** definitive **AD 10.0%–35.6%** under **Reg (EU)
   2025/261** (since 11 Feb 2025), **IN FORCE to ~Feb 2030** — the biggest live measure.
2. **SAF from China (PRIORITY):** **no duty, no investigation** — deliberately
   **excluded** from 2025/261 and only **monitored via dedicated TARIC codes**
   (set/corrected by **Reg (EU) 2025/1866**); watch for any AD/CVD initiation.
3. **Indonesia biodiesel:** **CVD 8%–18%** (**Reg (EU) 2019/2092**) **IN FORCE but
   under expiry review** and **WTO-challenged** (DS618 panel largely against the EU).
4. **US biodiesel:** **AD + CVD (Reg (EU) 2021/1266 / 2021/1267)** **IN FORCE**
   (extended to Canada); 5-year term expiring **~July 2026** — expiry review to watch;
   Braya (Canada) new-exporter review ongoing.
5. **US bioethanol:** AD **EXPIRED/lapsed since May 2019** — no measure in force.
6. **UCO/UCOME & circumvention:** **no trade-defence measure**; Indonesia→CN/UK
   circumvention case **closed** (no circumvention, 2024/1273); UCO fraud exam closed
   18 Jul 2025 with import-condition tightening (Reg 2025/2181) — monitoring, not duties.

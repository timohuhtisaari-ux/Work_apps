### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- Czechia does **not run a national scheme**: it relies on the **EU-recognised
  voluntary schemes approved under RED Art. 30(5)** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs,
  Bonsucro, RSB, KZR INiG, etc.). [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication; DG ENER]
- **Certification bodies must register with MoE CZ (§21a, from 1 May 2025)**
  before performing any certification audit on Czech territory for entities in
  §21(1)–(4) and §21c(1)–(2) (biomass for biofuel production, intermediates,
  biofuels, RFNBOs, RCFs). Registration requires proof of authorisation by an
  EU-approved voluntary scheme. MoE CZ publishes the **register of authorised
  certification bodies** on its website. [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication]
- **Exclusions:** the registration duty does **not** apply to bodies verifying
  solid biomass or biomethane. [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication]
- Proof-of-sustainability per consignment = scheme **certificate** + chain-of-
  custody sustainability declaration issued under the voluntary scheme.
  [CONFIRMED — MoE CZ Communication]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- The EU **UDB** became operational for economic-operator registration
  **15 Jan 2024** (liquid fuels first; gaseous phased later); legal basis RED
  **Art. 31a** + Implementing Reg. (EU) 2022/996. [CONFIRMED — DG ENER news;
  applies EU-wide incl. CZ]
- Operators placing biofuels/RFNBOs on the Czech market fall within UDB scope and
  must record consignment transactions and sustainability/GHG data there. **A
  CZ-specific mandatory-use start date and any national UDB guidance was not
  located on an MoE CZ primary page** → `PRIMARY-ONLY`/flag. [FLAG]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Use a §21a-registered certification body** — verify the certifier is entered in MoE CZ's register of authorised certification bodies before commissioning an audit. → MoE CZ.
2. **Obtain certification** under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme approved under RED Art. 30(5) (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, …); Czechia runs no national scheme. → chosen scheme / certifier.
3. **Carry proof-of-sustainability** (scheme certificate + chain-of-custody declaration) per consignment on a mass-balance basis; RFNBOs/RCFs now in scope (Act 42/2025). → scheme.
4. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving threshold** (50 / 60 / 65 %; 70 % for RFNBO/RCF) and exclude high-ILUC palm volumes per Del. Reg. 2019/807. → self + auditor.
5. **Register transactions/sustainability data in the UDB** (operational since 15 Jan 2024). → UDB [confirm CZ mandatory-use date].
6. **Track certifier duties**: certificate copies sent immediately to MoE CZ, pre-audit notifications, and the certification body's **28 February annual summary** — ensure your auditor complies. → MoE CZ / certifier.

**4-line summary — Czechia**
- Transposition: RED II via **Air Protection Act 201/2012 Coll.**; RED III elements transposed by **Act 42/2025 Coll.** (in force 1 Mar 2025), extending verification to RFNBOs/RCFs — CZ received a RED III formal notice (24 Jul 2025) but has since notified ~78 measures and was not named in the June-2026 package.
- Certification: **no national scheme** — relies on EU-recognised voluntary schemes (Art. 30(5)); certification bodies must register with **MoE CZ** (§21a, from 1 May 2025); solid biomass/biomethane exempt.
- Registry/UDB: MoE CZ publishes a **register of authorised certification bodies** (no operator-facing national IT register); EU UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024 (CZ mandatory-use date to confirm).
- Flags: operator annual-report deadline & penalties, current decree number, GHG-threshold citations and CZ UDB guidance unpinned; certifier annual summary due **28 February**.

# Netherlands — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Netherlands (NL)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

From compliance year 2026 the Netherlands replaced the energy-based *jaarverplichting* (HBE units) with a **GHG-intensity-based brandstoftransitieverplichting** (fuel transition obligation) using tradable **ERE** units (1 ERE = 1 kg CO2-eq well-to-wheel chain-emission reduction against a 94 g CO2-eq/MJ fossil comparator). The obligation is set **per sector**: **land 14.4 %**, **binnenvaart (inland shipping) 2.5 %** and **zeevaart (maritime) 2.9 %** for 2026, rising steeply to **land 28.4 % / binnenvaart 14.5 % / zeevaart 8.2 % by 2030**, with a land advanced-biofuels minimum (3.1 % → 8.8 %), small RFNBO minima, a land crop cap of 1.2 % and Annex IX-B cap of 4.3 %. On tax, the Netherlands gives biofuels **no reduced excise rate** — E85/FAME/HVO are taxed at the fossil-equivalent rate (petrol EUR 844.69/1000 L, diesel EUR 552.29/1000 L in 2026, under a temporary discount that lapses 1 Jan 2027), the only relief being the generic art. 71h energy-content refund for high blends; the whole biofuel incentive sits in the ERE market. The top obligations on a fuel supplier delivering ≥ 500,000 L per sector are: open a Register Energie voor Vervoer (REV) account with the NEa; certify the whole chain (incl. the inboeker as "trader with storage") under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme and hold a Proof of Sustainability for every booked consignment; book prior-year deliveries by the last working day before 1 March, pass annual inboekverificatie before 1 May, and hold sufficient EREs per sector by 1 April; and exclude palm- and soy-based fuels (a national exclusion beyond EU law) unless certified low-ILUC. Enforcement is by NEa administrative fine up to EUR 450,000 per violation (or 10 % of turnover); a few residual flags (RARE mechanics, per-unit ERE fine values, 2026 grid-electricity share) are carried into Section 5.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Regime change 2026 — HBE jaarverplichting replaced by ERE brandstoftransitieverplichting

As of compliance year 2026 the Netherlands replaced the energy-based
*jaarverplichting* with tradable **HBE** units by a GHG-intensity-based
**brandstoftransitieverplichting** (fuel transition obligation) with tradable
**ERE** units (*emissiereductie-eenheid*, "unit of emission reduction";
1 ERE = 1 kg CO2-eq well-to-wheel chain-emission reduction against the EU fossil
comparator). The obligation now applies separately to three sectors: **land**
(road, rail, mobile machinery, stationary installations), **binnenvaart**

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Units:** ERE, 1 kg CO2-eq chain reduction. Sector-specific series — LRE
  (land), BRE (binnenvaart), ZRE (zeevaart) — each in categories:
  **ERE-C** (conventional/crop), **ERE-B** (Annex IX-B), **ERE-G**
  (advanced, Annex IX-A), **ERE-O** (other, e.g. intermediate crops /
  food-feed processing residues), **ERE-R** (RFNBO), **ERE-E** (electricity);
  plus **RARE** (refinery reduction units from RFNBO use in NL refineries,
  usable toward the RFNBO sub-target). Argus counts 16 tradable ticket types.
  (CONFIRMED: NEa ERE page + Argus.)
- **Registry:** Register Energie voor Vervoer (REV), operated by NEa. The
  renewed (ERE-based) REV is being released in phases: first release
  29 June – 15 July 2026 (account opening, incl. boekingsdienstverleners);
  booking of deliveries/charging sessions follows from the second release;
  2026 deliveries bookable retroactively until 26-02-2027. (Corroboration
  pass, NEa pages.)
- **Obligated party:** the *leverancier tot eindverbruik* (linked to excise
  release for consumption, *uitslag tot verbruik* — cross-reference to tax
  section) delivering **>= 500,000 litres per sector per calendar year**
  (exemption below that: Bev art. 2(1)(a)). Sector land: petrol, diesel,
  heavy fuel oil to road/rail vehicles, mobile machinery, stationary
  installations. Binnenvaart: red-dyed gas oil to inland vessels. Zeevaart:
  the party issuing the Bunker Delivery Note registered with ILT (can be a
  foreign entity). (CONFIRMED: Bev art. 2 + NEa sector pages.)
- **Creation of EREs (inboeken):** registered deliveries of renewable fuels,
  renewable electricity (charge-point data; minimum 2 million kWh/yr unless
  aggregated via a *boekingsdienstverlener* — Regeling energie vervoer art.
  9(6)) and RFNBOs, verified by independent verifiers. (2 GWh threshold
  re-verified verbatim by corroborator in consolidated Regeling art. 9(6):
  "De minimale hoeveelheid jaarlijks in te boeken elektriciteit … bedraagt
  2 miljoen kWh" — remains PRIMARY-ONLY, single legal source.)
- **Trading:** EREs freely tradable between REV account holders. (CONFIRMED.)
- **Banking (sparen):** caps per Bev art. 29 / Wm art. 9.7.5.6 — obligated
  suppliers: the higher of **45,000 EREs or 10 % of the prior-year
  obligation** per sector; inboekers: **4 %** of units created; traders:
  **45,000**; RARE: 20 % of total ERE-R obligation (holders) / 10 % of RARE
  bookings (suppliers). EREs above the limit **expire automatically**.
  (CONFIRMED: Bev art. 29 + NEa ERE page.) No borrowing provision found.
- **HBE -> ERE transition:** after the 2025 jaarafsluiting, remaining HBE
  balances were converted on **1 May 2026 at 1 HBE = 46 EREs**, same category
  mapping (HBE-G→ERE-G etc.); no conversion into ERE-R or ERE-E; aviation-derived
  HBEs converted into the land sector; sector allocation of converted units based
  on 2025 booking results. (CONFIRMED: NEa news 05-03-2026 + NEa ERE page + Argus.
  Corroborator note: the separate NEa figure "HBE-reductiebijdrage 2026 = 47
  kg/HBE" (news 30-06-2025) is a **different parameter** — the average realised
  reduction per HBE used for old-system compliance assessment — and does **not**
  contradict the 1:46 conversion rate.)
- **Compliance calendar:** register fuel deliveries by **1 March**; hold
  sufficient EREs per sector by **1 April** (moved forward from 1 May);
  verification of shipping-sector delivery registrations by **1 May**.
  (CONFIRMED: NEa pages.)
- **Aviation:** outside the national obligation from 2026 (ReFuelEU Aviation
  Regulation (EU) 2023/2405 applies directly).

**Interaction with the mandate:** the Netherlands runs no tax-incentive scheme for biofuels — the brandstoftransitieverplichting with tradable EREs (NEa — mandates section; HBE jaarverplichting through 2025) carries the entire incentive. ### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Biofuels booked into the register must meet the **RED Art. 29 sustainability and GHG-saving criteria** (Besluit energie vervoer arts. 7–8; art. 7 reference "artikel 29, tweede tot en met zevende lid en tiende lid, van de richtlijn hernieuwbare energie" re-verified by corroborator in the 20-06-2026 consolidation); the RED II thresholds therefore apply unchanged: **≥50 %** (installations in operation on/before 5 Oct 2015), **≥60 %** (6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **≥65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021). `CONFIRMED` (cite EUR-Lex consolidated 2018/2001 in final report). (S3, S9, V3)
- **RFNBOs/hydrogen**: must meet the GHG-reduction thresholds of RED **Art. 29a** (70 %) and be certified by a voluntary scheme (Besluit arts. 9, 9a). `CONFIRMED` (S3, S9)
- Beyond the thresholds, the ERES design rewards **actual chain GHG performance**: ERE yield = volume × LHV × (94 − E) × factor / 1000, with E from RED default or actual values per Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/996. `CONFIRMED` (NEa) (S5, S10)

### 3.3 Certification schemes accepted

- The Netherlands maintains **no national scheme or national list**: sustainability may **only** be demonstrated via **voluntary schemes recognised by the European Commission** (18 schemes recognised as of 2026-07-04, incl. ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Better Biomass, SURE, RSB, CertifHy for RFNBOs). Designation works by reference in the Besluit ("een bij ministeriële regeling aangewezen vrijwillig systeem"). `CONFIRMED` (NEa duurzaamheid pages + Besluit art. 7/9a + DG ENER list). (S11, S3, S12)
- The **whole chain including the inboeker** must be certified; the inboeker must hold certification as **"trader with storage"** for the final storage location (AGP). `CONFIRMED` (NEa inboeken pages). (S5, S10)

### 3.4 Proof requirements for inboeken (booking-in)

- Only the **physical quantity** delivered may be booked (Regeling art. 6(1)); each booking must be covered by a **bewijs van duurzaamheid / Proof of Sustainability (PoS)** drawn from the **mass balance of the final storage location** and retained by the inboeker (Regeling art. 6(2); for RFNBOs a "bewijs van hernieuwbaarheid", art. 8(2)). `CONFIRMED` (wetten.overheid.nl Regeling + NEa). (S6, S10)
- Mass-balance reconciliation with physical stock at least **quarterly**; physical delivery evidenced by excise documents or invoice/payment with delivery address; fuel identity by **ISO/IEC 17025-accredited sampling/analysis** (exceptions: FAME, ethanol, ETBE), **C14 dating recommended for HVO/co-processed and advanced fuels**. `CONFIRMED` (NEa land-sector inboeken page). (S10)
- Eligible inboekers (liquid biofuels): NL trade-register companies holding an **accijnsgoederenplaats (AGP)** licence, registered consignees, or importers paying excise; separate rules for gaseous fuels, electricity, hydrogen; **inboekdienstverleners** may book on behalf of others. `CONFIRMED` (NEa). (S5)

### 3.5 National registry and Union Database (UDB)

- **Register Energie voor Vervoer (REV)**, operated by **NEa**. Who must hold accounts: **inboekers** (to book deliveries and receive ERE's), **obligated fuel suppliers** (BTV compliance accounts), and **traders** (trading accounts limited to energy-derivatives/credits traders; inactivity >12 months or breach can lead to freezing/closure, Besluit arts. 25–29). The **renewed REV (ERE-based) launches in phases: first release 29 June – 15 July 2026** (account opening), booking functionality from the second release, with retroactive booking of 2026 deliveries; account opening via the NEa Helpdesk. `CONFIRMED` (NEa + Besluit; phased-release detail added in corroboration pass). (S5, S3, S13, V4)

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

Numbered, actionable list for a *leverancier tot eindverbruik* delivering ≥ 500,000 L per sector per calendar year (below that: exempt, Bev art. 2(1)(a)). Authority in brackets.

1. **Confirm obligated-party status and sector(s)** — the obligation attaches to the party releasing fuel for consumption (*uitslag tot verbruik*, linked to excise) in each of land / binnenvaart / zeevaart; for zeevaart it is the party issuing the Bunker Delivery Note registered with **ILT** (may be a foreign entity). [NEa; ILT for zeevaart BDN]
2. **Obtain EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (e.g. ISCC EU) for **every chain link**, including the inboeker certified as **"trader with storage"** at the final AGP storage location. [European Commission-recognised scheme / certification body]
3. **Open the required REV account(s) with the NEa** — inboeker and/or obligated-supplier account (the ERE-based REV opens in phases from 29 June 2026; account opening via the NEa Helpdesk; 2026 deliveries bookable retroactively). [NEa]
4. **Hold a bewijs van duurzaamheid (PoS)** — or bewijs van hernieuwbaarheid for RFNBOs — from the storage-site mass balance for **every booked consignment**; keep quarterly mass-balance reconciliations, excise/invoice delivery proof and ISO 17025 analyses (C14 for HVO) on file for **5 years**. [NEa]
5. **Exclude palm- and soy-based biofuels** from NL bookings unless certified low-ILUC (national exclusion, Wm art. 9.7.4.2(1)(d), since 1 Jan 2022); respect the ERE category caps — land conventional 1.2 %, land Annex IX-B 4.3 %, binnenvaart ERE-C prohibited / ERE-B 11.1 %, zeevaart ERE-C and ERE-B prohibited. [NEa]
6. **Book prior-year deliveries by the last working day before 1 March** (e.g. 26 Feb 2027 for 2026). [NEa (REV)]
7. **Contract an NEa-recognised accredited verifier** (Control Union, Dekra, NORMEC QS) and complete the annual **inboekverificatie before 1 May** (2 % materiality; EN-ISO/IEC 17020 accreditation). [Verifier → NEa]
8. **Hold sufficient EREs per sector by 1 April** (units deducted at jaarafsluiting); meet the sector minima (land advanced-biofuels 3.1 %, land RFNBO 0.05 %, binnenvaart RFNBO 0.02 % in 2026) and use cross-sector flexibility only where allowed (binnenvaart up to 0.8 %, zeevaart up to 1.1 %; not permitted for land). No buy-out — a shortfall triggers an NEa administrative fine (up to €450,000/violation or 10 % of turnover). [NEa]
9. **Manage banking limits** — EREs above the sparen cap (higher of 45,000 or 10 % of prior-year obligation for suppliers) **expire automatically**; there is no borrowing. [NEa]
10. **Start/maintain Union Database registration via your certification scheme** and comply with Regeling art. 3b (timely, correct UDB data entry by marktdeelnemers per Wm art. 9.7.1.3(a); scheme certification bodies verify the entered data); watch NEa announcements for the REV–UDB link and the EU mandatory-use date. [NEa / European Commission]
11. **Note the tax position** — biofuels get no reduced excise (E85/FAME/HVO taxed at the fossil-equivalent WA rate, art. 28(1)); the only relief is the generic art. 71h energy-content refund for high blends (≥ 10 % renewable energy and lower energy density); grid CNG/biomethane at a filling station pays the standard energiebelasting rate (EUR 0.20928/m³ in 2026) with no biomethane discount. [Belastingdienst/Douane]

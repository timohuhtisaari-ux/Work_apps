- **Country:** Belgium (BE)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** biofuel-mandates-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending

## Executive summary

Belgium runs a **federal, energy-value renewable-energy-in-transport obligation** on fuel
suppliers. Since 1 January 2024 the scheme is governed by the **Law of 31 July 2023**
(loi concernant les normes de produit pour l'intégration d'énergie produite à partir de
sources renouvelables dans les carburants fossiles destinés au secteur du transport,
Moniteur belge), which replaced the old volume-based framework (Law of 17 July 2013 +
RD of 4 May 2018) and aligns Belgium with RED III concepts (advanced-biofuel and RFNBO
categories, recycled-carbon-fuel cap, trajectory to 2030). The overall obligation is
**12.2 % (energy value) for 2025 and 2026**, rising to 13.15 % (2027) and 13.9 % (2030).
Enforcement is by administrative fine (DG Energy, FPS Economy). Mandate design is a
**federal competence** (product standards); regional governments transpose other RED
aspects. Note EU-wide: ETS2 compliance obligation starts 1 Jan 2028 (Reg. (EU) 2026/667),
auctioning 2027 — a separate instrument from this blending obligation.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** oil companies / gaseous-fuel suppliers placing motor fuel on the
  road-transport market (Art. 2). **Exemptions:** Ministry of Defence; small suppliers
  below **3 600 GJ/year** (Art. 19). [S1]
- **Basis:** obligation expressed in **energy value** (not volume), a change from the
  pre-2024 nominal-volume regime under the Law of 17 July 2013 / RD 4 May 2018. [S1, S4]
- **Categories & multipliers:** the law classifies renewable energy into categories
  (advanced biofuels = cat. C ×2; RFNBO = cat. D; RCF = cat. E, capped; renewable
  electricity = cat. F with transport multipliers) — Art. 4. [S1, S2]
- **Administration / registry:** Directorate-General Energy of **FPS Economy** under the
  federal energy minister; compliance registration and annual declaration; administrative
  fines regime detailed in **RD of 17 March 2024**. [S3, S5]
- **Competence:** federal matter (Art. 74 Constitution) — product standards. [S1]

- **RED III transposition status:** The Law of 31 July 2023 (federal, transport product
  standards) is Belgium's principal federal instrument transposing the RED transport
  obligation and already embeds RED III-style elements (advanced 4.2 % by 2030, RFNBO/RCF
  categories, food-cap tapering). Belgium notified numerous national implementing measures
  under Directive (EU) 2023/2413 across the 1 July 2024 and 21 May 2025 deadlines (federal
  + regional). Full RED III transposition also involves regional acts (Flanders, Wallonia,
  Brussels) for non-transport aspects — out of this scope. [S1, S6] — **CONFIRMED (federal
  transport obligation adopted); regional completeness not independently verified here.**

**5-line summary (BE):**
1. Obligation type: federal energy-value renewable-energy-in-transport share obligation on fuel suppliers (Law of 31 July 2023).
2. Current level: 12.2 % (energy value) for 2026; advanced sub-quota 1.1 %; food cap diesel 5.7 % / petrol 6.5 %; RCF cap 2 %.
3. Next legislated change: rises to 13.15 % in 2027 and 13.9 % in 2030 (advanced to 4.2 % by 2030); distinct petrol 4 % / diesel 2.5 % pool minima from 2027.
4. RED III status: federal transport obligation adopted (Law of 31 July 2023, RED III-aligned); Belgium notified multiple NIM; regional acts complete the transposition (out of scope).
5. Flags: petrol/diesel per-pool 2024–2026 figures and the exact Art. 10 penalty formula are PRIMARY-ONLY (re-verify consolidated Justel text); no CONFLICT found.

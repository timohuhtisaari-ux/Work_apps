# Slovakia — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Slovakia (SK)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Slovakia's transport-fuel obligation is a **blending / reference-value (referenčná hodnota) obligation** under Act No. 309/2009 Coll. — a minimum share of the **energy content** of the annual petrol + diesel pool met by biofuels, set at **9.5% for 2026** on a legislated rising schedule to **11.4% by 2030** (inserted by Act 363/2022), with a **0.75% advanced-biofuel sub-target for 2025–2030** and double-counting of advanced and UCO/animal-fat volumes (E10/B7 blend walls). Support runs through the blending obligation, **not through excise**: petrol is taxed at **514,00 EUR/1000 L** (unleaded low-sulphur) and diesel at **368,00 EUR/1000 L**, with no separate biofuel-content-based reduction for liquid road fuels — only liquefied biogas/biomethane gets a reduced rate (141 vs 182 EUR/1000 kg); an April-2025 proposal to cut fuel excise to EU minimums was rejected. The key supplier obligations are to **register and record in SK BIO** (operated by SHMÚ, live since 1 Jan 2024), certify under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (Slovakia has no Commission-approved national scheme), carry the *potvrdenie o pôvode biopaliva*, and **report to BOTH SK BIO and the EU UDB** because the two systems are not interconnected. **RED III is transposed, late** (amendment to Act 309/2009 + Decree No. 31/2026 Coll. on sustainability criteria; SK received a RED III formal notice on 24 Jul 2025 and completed after the 21 May 2025 deadline). Penalty amounts are a shared gap. ETS2 compliance begins 1 Jan 2028.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** legal or natural persons that place motor fuels on the market ("povinná osoba"); the obligation is met if the reference value is achieved for the calendar year. (S3)
- **Administration / verification:** **Ministry of the Environment (MŽP)**; the **Slovak Hydrometeorological Institute (SHMÚ), Odbor emisie a biopalivá** is the authorised organisation supervising qualified persons and verification under Act 309/2009 and Vyhláška 271/2011 Z.z. (S3, S8)
- **Compliance basis:** energy content of the annual pool; advanced + UCO/animal-fat volumes double-counted.
- **Banking/borrowing, trading:** not established in sources — GAP.

### Interaction with the mandate
Slovakia's biofuel policy is a **blending obligation** under Act 309/2009 (see Section 1). Unlike Sweden/Finland, the current excise table shows **no reduced rate tied to biofuel content** for petrol or diesel — the only bio-relevant excise relief is the reduced rate for liquefied **biogas/biomethane** (141 vs. 182 EUR/1000 kg). Therefore no "relief limited to volumes above the obligation" state-aid condition applies to liquid road biofuels here, because there is no such excise relief to condition.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register and record in SK BIO** (isbio.shmu.sk) with SHMÚ **before** placing biofuels/biocomponents or motor fuels on the Slovak market (system mandatory since 1 Jan 2024). → **SHMÚ** (Odbor emisie a biopalivá).
2. **Certify under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme** (ISCC EU, REDcert, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, Better Biomass); Slovakia has no Commission-approved national scheme. → chosen scheme / certifier.
3. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving threshold** (50% ≤5 Oct 2015 / 60% to 2020 / 65% from 2021; 70% for RFNBO/RCF per Decree 31/2026) and **exclude high-ILUC palm volumes** per Del. Reg. (EU) 2019/807. → self + auditor.
4. **Carry the *potvrdenie o pôvode biopaliva*** (proof of origin) + scheme sustainability documentation per consignment on a **mass-balance** basis. → scheme / SK BIO.
5. **Report to BOTH systems** — file the customs report (*hlásenie*) with the proof of origin to the **colný úrad** AND record transactions in the EU **UDB**, because SK BIO and UDB are not interconnected. → **colný úrad** / **SHMÚ** / **UDB**.
6. **Achieve the 2026 reference value** — a 9.5% energy-content share of the annual petrol+diesel pool, including the 0.75% advanced-biofuel sub-target; advanced and UCO/animal-fat volumes count double. → self; verified via **MŽP / SHMÚ**.
7. **Undergo the periodic certification audit** (typically annual) and retain documentation for verification by competent persons / SHMÚ. → certifier / SHMÚ.
8. **Prepare for ETS2**: road-transport fuel suppliers face an EU allowance-surrender obligation from **1 Jan 2028** (auctioning from 2027) under ETS2 (Directive (EU) 2023/959, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC; the one-year postponement was adopted alongside Reg. (EU) 2026/667, the 2040 climate-target amendment). → MŽP / national ETS2 authority.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Priority flag — RED III transposed late.** RED III (2023/2413) is transposed via an amendment to Act No. 309/2009 Coll. plus **Decree No. 31/2026 Z.z.** on sustainability criteria, but **after the 21 May 2025 deadline**: Slovakia was among the 26 MS given a RED III **letter of formal notice on 24 Jul 2025** and is not named in the June-2026 package (only Cyprus escalated), consistent with late completion. The exact RED III amending-act number is **not pinned down** (`PRIMARY-ONLY`). Decree 31/2026 establishes sustainability criteria and **did not revise the §14b reference-value table** (that remains the Act 363/2022 schedule).

**RED III infringement chronology — CORRECTED.** The draft's earlier "formal notice Sep 2024 / reasoned opinion Feb 2025 / additional reasoned opinion Oct 2025" chronology could not be verified and is chronologically inconsistent with the 21 May 2025 RED III deadline — treated as unverified/likely conflated. The single verified action is the 24 Jul 2025 formal notice.

**Resolved conflict (reference-value schedule).** An older source gave a flat **8.2% for 2022–2030** (from Act 181/2017) and an "advanced 0.3%" figure; the ZVVB FAQ still shows the 8.2% flat schedule. The 8.2% flat value was **superseded** by the rising schedule (2026 = 9.5% → 2030 = 11.4%) inserted by **Act 363/2022**, now confirmed verbatim from the §14b rendering (S11); the 0.3% advanced figure was a historical 2021–2022 reduction — the current advanced value is **0.75%** for 2025–2030.

**Penalty/buy-out amounts — GAP.** The exact EUR administrative-fine (pokuta) amount for failing the reference value, and for non-compliant sustainability proof, could not be located.

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **FQD lifecycle target:** suppliers must reduce transport-fuel GHG intensity **by up to 10 %, minimum 6 %**, vs the fossil baseline by 31 Dec 2020 (OUG 80/2018 §9(1)). [CONFIRMED — S1, S2]
- **Biofuel GHG-saving criteria:** biofuels must meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds (**50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by installation vintage; **70 %** RFNBO) to count and to be recorded in the traceability bulletin. Exact figures not quoted from the RO text this pass → PRIMARY-ONLY / framework. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S1]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- OUG 80/2018 **§12(8)** accepts **voluntary schemes recognised by the European Commission** as proof of sustainability without requiring additional evidence. [CONFIRMED — S2]
- Usable schemes therefore include the full DG ENER list: **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, SURE, RSB, Bonsucro EU, RTRS EU RED, KZR INiG**, etc. (accessed 2026-07-04). [CONFIRMED — S6]
- Romania also references a **national certification scheme** (compliance certificates valid **5 years**, maintained by periodic audits) operating alongside the voluntary schemes. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S2, S7]
- **Proof of sustainability per consignment:** the **buletinul de trasabilitate a ciclului de viață** (lifecycle traceability bulletin) recording sustainability-criteria compliance and GHG calculation at each lifecycle stage on RO territory; it must **accompany the biofuel — regardless of blend proportion — until sale to the final consumer** (§12(3)). Voluntary-scheme certificates/PoS substitute for the sustainability evidence per §12(8). [CONFIRMED — S2]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB operational since **15 Jan 2024**; first collectors onboarded **1 Oct 2024**; no immediate sanctions after 21 Nov 2024; Commission finalising the mandatory-use date. Legal basis Art. 31a RED III, Art. 18 Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6, S8, S9]
- **RO operators are in scope** of the UDB obligation. Because RED III is not yet transposed nationally, there is **no RO-specific UDB implementing provision** identified; the obligation applies directly via EU law. [PRIMARY-ONLY / flag]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Ensure each biofuel batch is accompanied by a valid *buletinul de trasabilitate a ciclului de viață*** recording sustainability-criteria compliance and lifecycle GHG, from placing-on-market to sale to the final consumer (OUG 80/2018 §12(3)). → self / Ministry of Energy.
2. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) — accepted as full proof of sustainability under §12(8) — or use the national certification scheme (5-yr certificate + periodic audit). → certification body.
3. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 %; FQD supplier ≥6 % lifecycle reduction) and document them per batch. → self + auditor.
4. **Commission the annual independent third-party audit** (accredited under Reg. 765/2008); submit the audit report **by 1 March**. → accredited verifier.
5. **File the annual supplier report** to the Ministry of Energy in the **15 January – 1 March** window. → Ministry of Energy.
6. **Respect the 7 % crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out**; use only EU Annex IX feedstocks for advanced/waste claims. → self + auditor.
7. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** and record in-scope transactions (operational since 15 Jan 2024; mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.

**4-line summary — Romania**
- Transposition: RED sustainability + FQD GHG-monitoring via **OUG 80/2018** (amending Law 220/2008); competent authority = **Ministry of Energy** (§2); **RED III NOT transposed → letter of formal notice only** (no reasoned opinion; earlier overstatement corrected).
- Certification: **EU-recognised voluntary schemes accepted as full proof** (§12(8): ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) alongside a national scheme (5-yr certificate); proof per batch = **buletinul de trasabilitate a ciclului de viață** accompanying the fuel to final sale.
- Registry/UDB: **no dedicated national sustainability registry** found (batch bulletin + annual supplier report to Min. Energy); annual report window **15 Jan–1 Mar**, independent third-party audit due **1 March**; UDB applies via EU law (operational 15 Jan 2024, mandatory date pending).
- Flags: corrected the **Italy "DM 7 Aug 2024"** conflation and the RO reasoned-opinion overstatement; GHG 50/60/65 % framework-level; ANRE is not the S-authority (Ministry of Energy is); no palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU.

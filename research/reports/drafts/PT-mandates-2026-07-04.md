## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.1 Current obligation

Portugal operates an **incorporation obligation** on fuel suppliers ("incorporadores"):
a minimum percentage of **low-carbon fuels for transport ("combustíveis de baixo
teor em carbono" — biofuels, biogas, RFNBO, recycled-carbon fuels), measured in
energy content**, of the road fuels they release for consumption. Compliance is
demonstrated with **TdB (títulos de biocombustíveis)** and **TdC (títulos de
combustível de baixo carbono)** certificates verified quarterly by **ENSE, E.P.E.**
The operative act is **Decreto-Lei n.º 84/2022, de 9 de dezembro** (partial RED II
transposition), Article 8 (metas de incorporação) and Articles 40–41 (TdB/TdC).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Certificates:** TdB (1 TdB = 1 tep of sustainable biofuel) and TdC (low-carbon
  fuel title). Issued/validated by **ENSE**; DGEG runs the residual **TdB auction**
  (for TdB attributable to small dedicated producers / PPD benefiting from ISP
  exemption; auctions noticed 15 business days ahead in the Diário da República;
  base bid = 85 % of the average minimum of the last three auctions). (S8)
- **Obligated parties:** entities that release road fuels for final consumption
  in transport ("incorporadores"). (S1, S2)
- **Verification:** quarterly — obligated parties deliver TdB/TdC to ENSE by the
  end of the month following each quarter. (S2, S7)
- **Enforcement/tax interface:** advanced biofuels (TdB-certified) and renewable
  gases (Guarantee-of-Origin-certified) receive **full ISP (fuel-tax) exemption**;
  the procedure is set by **Portaria n.º 226/2026/1, de 20 de maio** coordinating
  AT (tax authority), ENSE and the GO-issuing entity. This is a tax mechanism —
  cross-reference the fuel-tax section; not researched in depth here. (S9)
- Banking/borrowing of TdB: not isolated from primary text — **gap**.

**RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition — NOT YET ADOPTED as of
2026-07-04 (deadline was 21 May 2025 → Portugal is late/in infringement).**
- A draft Decree-Law for **partial** RED III transposition went to public
  consultation on ConsultaLEX **25 Sep – 25 Oct 2025**. (S4, S5)
- TRIS standstill for the fuels ("combustíveis") decree ended **18 May 2026**;
  the companion renewable-incorporation-targets decree standstill ends **22 Jul
  2026**; both then proceed to Council of Ministers approval. Still **PROPOSED**. (S10)
- Proposed transport provisions (`PROPOSED`): reproduces RED III 29 % RES-T by
  2030; creates an **autonomous combined sub-quota of advanced biofuels + RFNBO +
  advanced biogas of 3 % in 2026**, rising to **8 % in 2027 (min 0.5 pp RFNBO)**
  and **12.5 % in 2030 (min 1.5 pp RFNBO)** per one secondary source; a different
  secondary cited 7.5 % for 2030 — CONFLICT among secondaries, see 1.4. (S3, S10)

**EU-wide:** ETS2 compliance obligation for road-transport fuel suppliers starts
1 Jan 2028 (Reg. (EU) 2026/667; auctioning from 2027) — applies to PT.

## 5-line summary (Portugal)
- Obligation type: energy-content incorporation % of road fuels (low-carbon fuels), TdB/TdC certificates, ENSE-verified.
- Current level: **13 % (2026)** energy content (DL 84/2022 art. 8; "13 % from 2025").
- Next legislated change: **14 % in 2027** (rising to 16 % in 2029–2030).
- RED III status: **NOT transposed** — draft partial-transposition decree in TRIS standstill/Council-of-Ministers stage (late vs 21 May 2025 deadline); autonomous 3 % (2026) advanced+RFNBO+biogas sub-quota is `PROPOSED`.
- Flags: `CONFLICT` on penalty (€1,198 vs €1,760/TdB); `CONFLICT` on proposed 2030 combined sub-quota (12.5 % vs 7.5 %); primary art.8 PDF unparsed (gap).

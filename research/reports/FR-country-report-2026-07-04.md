# France — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** France (FR)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04 — labels final; corrections marked ⚠)

## Executive summary

France imposes no volume quota; instead it runs an **energy-share target per fuel pool enforced through an incentive tax, TIRUERT** (now codified in CGI Art. 300 bis, ex-Code des douanes Art. 266 quindecies), which levies **140 €/hL** on the shortfall of a supplier that misses the target. For compliance year 2026 the targets are **10.5 % renewable energy in the petrol pool and 9.4 % in the diesel pool** (jet fuel left TIRUERT on 1 Jan 2025 for ReFuelEU Aviation), with de-facto advanced-feedstock minimums of 1.8 %/0.7 %, a 7 % crop cap, and palm/soy excluded (0 %). From **1 January 2027 TIRUERT is replaced by IRICC**, a wider carbon-intensity-reduction mechanism (LF 2026 Art. 64 — CONFIRMED; the numeric trajectory is delegated to décrets and remains PROPOSED). Fuel excise (the "accise sur les énergies", ~672.90-equivalent per-litre framework in €/MWh: essences 77.647 €/MWh, gazoles 60.75 €/MWh) carries **product-based reduced rates for E85 (17.894 €/MWh), B100 (12.905) and ED95 (12.119)** — cumulative with, and independent of, TIRUERT — while HVO100 gets no special rate; a PLF 2026 attempt to suppress the B100/E85 advantages was rejected. The top supplier obligations are: hold RED certification under an EC-recognised voluntary scheme, register and declare biofuel lots monthly on **CarbuRe** (DGEC), file the annual sustainability declaration (15 March) and the annual TIRUERT return (10 April), and onboard the Union Database. **HIGH-risk flag: France has NOT fully transposed RED III — reasoned opinion of 11 December 2025 (INFR(2025)0220), still open in the June 2026 infringement package.**

## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.0 Scheme identification

France has no volume quota. The **TIRUERT** (taxe incitative relative à l'utilisation d'énergie renouvelable dans les transports) sets national target percentages of renewable energy per fuel pool; a fuel supplier that meets the target owes zero tax, and pays 140 €/hL on its taxable volumes multiplied by the shortfall if it does not. Historically codified in **Article 266 quindecies of the Code des douanes**; recodified by **Ordonnance n° 2025-1247 of 17 December 2025** into **Article 300 bis of the Code général des impôts (CGI)** (Art. 266 quindecies version shown on Légifrance as valid to 31 March 2026; CGI Art. 300 bis is the version in force on 4 July 2026 — ⚠ re-verified by corroborator). The **carburéacteur (jet) pool was removed from TIRUERT on 1 January 2025** (Art. 24, Loi n° 2025-217 de finances pour 2025) because ReFuelEU Aviation (Reg. (EU) 2023/2405) took over.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated parties:** the persons liable for the energy excise (accise sur les énergies) on petrol and diesel products, i.e. those releasing fuels for consumption from tax warehouses ("metteurs à la consommation en sortie d'entrepôt fiscal"). (CGI Art. 300 bis; douane.gouv.fr TIRUERT page.) [M1][M3]
- **Declaration/payment:** annual TIRUERT return declared and paid **by 10 April of the year following** the compliance year (CGI Art. 300 bis; FNA). [M1][M4]
- **Evidence of renewable energy:** via certificates per the DGDDI circulaire (attestation models in Annexes II/II bis/II ter: acquisition, content, renewable-energy claims) and mass-balance/comptabilité matière accounting; implementing texts: Décret n° 2019-570 (as amended by Décret n° 2025-226 of 10 March 2025) and Décret n° 2023-1420. The **CarbuRe** registry (DGEC) is the electronic system referenced in the source registry; not independently re-verified (GAP — the 61-page 2025 circulaire PDF could not be machine-read; no PDF renderer available to the corroborator either). [M3][M5][M9][M10]
- **Banking/borrowing:** no banking/borrowing provision located in the article text fetched. GAP — to verify in the circulaire.
- **No certificate market in the Dutch/Italian sense**: compliance is per-taxpayer via the tax return; renewable energy can be attributed between operators via the attestation system (cross-check with tax researcher).

## 2. Fuel taxation

L312-1 ff.) — this is the French implementation of the Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC. Statutory tariffs are expressed in **€/MWh** (per-litre figures below are the widely used equivalents; the €/MWh value is the legal one). ### Interaction with the mandate
TIRUERT is itself a **tax** (an incentive/penalty levy on fuel-excise payers who miss the renewable-incorporation percentage), not a quota with certificates — so France's "mandate" and "fuel taxation" overlap structurally. The excise reliefs for E85/B100/ED95 are cumulative with, and independent of, TIRUERT compliance. TIRUERT rates/targets and CarbuRe registry mechanics: see Section 1.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register as an obligated party for the energy excise (accise sur les énergies)** and for TIRUERT/IRICC purposes — i.e. be identified as a "metteur à la consommation en sortie d'entrepôt fiscal" for petrol/diesel. Authority: DGDDI/DGFiP.
2. **Obtain and maintain certification under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme** (e.g. ISCC EU, 2BSvs, REDcert-EU) covering your role in the chain; pass annual surveillance audits by an independent certification body. Authority: scheme/certification body.
3. **Register on CarbuRe (DGEC)** as producer/trader/supplier and declare all biofuel/bioliquid lots **monthly by the last day of month M+1** (electronic file to durabilite-bio@developpement-durable.gouv.fr; nil returns required); ensure lots carry complete déclarations de durabilité (feedstock, origin, GHG value, scheme certificate no.). Authority: DGEC (CarbuRe).
4. **File the annual durability declaration** (CarbuRe extraction of validated lots) **by 15 March** for the previous year. Authority: DGEC/DREAL.
5. **File the annual TIRUERT return and pay any incentive tax by 10 April** for the prior compliance year, counting only consignments with valid proof of sustainability meeting art. 29 RED II criteria (≥ 50/60/65 % GHG saving by plant vintage); tax = 140 €/hL × taxable volume × shortfall against 10.5 % (essences) / 9.4 % (gazoles). Authority: DGDDI/DGFiP.
6. **Meet the de-facto advanced-feedstock minimums** (1.8 % essences / 0.7 % gazoles) and respect the caps (crop 7 %, IX-B 1.1/1.2 %) when composing renewable volumes for TIRUERT.
7. **Exclude palm-oil (incl. PFAD) and soy-oil based products** from TIRUERT-eligible volumes — they are not "biocarburants" under CGI art. 300 bis V.C (ex-art. 266 quindecies). Authority: DGDDI/DGFiP.
8. **For double-counted feedstocks, verify the production unit appears on the DGEC double-counting list** (CarbuRe) before claiming the ×2 multiplier. Authority: DGEC.
9. **Open a Union Database (UDB) account and record transactions/stocks** — treat as mandatory now for certificate retention (scheme rules); EU-wide mandatory date pending. Authority: European Commission (UDB).
10. **Apply the correct excise tariffs to biofuel blends** (E85 17.894, B100 12.905, ED95 12.119 €/MWh; HVO100 at the normal gazoles rate 60.75 €/MWh) and claim the gazole professionnel refund (effective 45.19 €/hL) where eligible. Authority: DGDDI/DGFiP.
11. **Prepare for IRICC from 1 January 2027** (LF 2026 art. 64) — a carbon-intensity mechanism extending to maritime/river/aviation, CNG and LPG; numeric parameters pending décrets. Authority: DGEC/DGDDI.
12. **Prepare for EU ETS2** — road-fuel suppliers become regulated entities from 1 Jan 2028 (auctioning from 2027, first surrender 2029). Authority: DGEC / European Commission.

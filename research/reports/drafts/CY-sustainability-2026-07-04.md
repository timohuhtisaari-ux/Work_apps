### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- **Supplier FQD target:** ≥**6 %** lifecycle GHG-intensity reduction vs the 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ fossil baseline (Law 106(I)/2022). [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S4]
- **Biofuels/bioliquids:** RED thresholds — **50 %** (pre-5 Oct 2015 installations), **60 %** (6 Oct 2015–31 Dec 2020), **65 %** (from 1 Jan 2021); **70 %** RFNBO/RCF — applied via Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996. Not quoted from CY text this pass → framework-level. [PRIMARY-ONLY / framework — S4]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- Economic operators demonstrate sustainability via the **national certification system OR an EU-recognised voluntary scheme** — the standard RED II model. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S8]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S1]
- **Proof of sustainability:** depending on supply-chain position, operators provide a **self-declaration, sustainability declaration and/or Proof of Sustainability (PoS)** carrying the sustainability-criteria data and the GHG-emission-saving figure; traders must supply information demonstrating criteria compliance for the biofuel to count toward RES-T / support. [PRIMARY-ONLY — S3, S8]

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status

- EU UDB established under **RED III Art. 31a** (formal deadline 21 Nov 2024; operational from Jan 2024); chain-of-custody rules in **Impl. Reg. (EU) 2022/996 Art. 18**; collectors/EOs onboarded via **voluntary schemes** through 2024–2025; **mandatory-use/sanction date being finalised** by the Commission. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S1, S7, S9]
- **CY operators are in scope via EU law.** With RED III not yet transposed, **no CY-specific UDB implementing provision** exists; the obligation applies directly. Operators risk **certificate suspension without a UDB account** (scheme-administered onboarding). [PRIMARY-ONLY / flag — S7, S9]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) or comply with the **national sustainability-certification system** (ΚΔΠ 55/2011 lineage). → certification body / MECI.
2. **Provide the required Proof of Sustainability** (self-declaration / sustainability declaration / PoS per supply-chain position) carrying the GHG-saving figure. → self / supplier.
3. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 %; supplier ≥6 % FQD vs 94.1 gCO2eq/MJ). → self + verifier.
4. **File the periodic (monthly) sales/blending report to MECI** and retain sustainability documentation for inspection. → MECI.
5. **Note Cyprus's exemption from the 1.7 % Annex IX-B cap**, respect the 7 % crop cap and the EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out. → self + verifier.
6. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension. → UDB / voluntary scheme.

- Transposition: RED II sustainability + FQD via **Law 106(I)/2022** and RES via **Law 107(I)/2022** with ministerial decrees (ΚΔΠ); competent authority **MECI**; **RED III NOT fully transposed → additional reasoned opinion 4 June 2026 (INFR(2024)0213), CJEU-referral risk within 2 months** (confirmed).

- Registry/UDB: **no dedicated national sustainability registry** found — suppliers file **monthly** sales/blending reports to MECI; exact annual sustainability deadline a gap; UDB applies via EU law (mandatory-use date pending).
- Flags: ΚΔΠ 55/2011 decree text not read; penalty figures unconfirmed; **Cyprus exempt from the 1.7 % IX-B cap**; no palm/soy ban or Annex IX additions beyond EU.

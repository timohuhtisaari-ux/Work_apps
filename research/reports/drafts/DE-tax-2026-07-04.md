**Interaction with the mandate:** Germany gives biofuels no excise advantage; the entire incentive sits in the GHG-reduction quota (THG-Quote, §§ 37a–37h BImSchG — mandates section). The only tax-side advantage for renewables is the zero emission factor for sustainable biomass under the BEHG/nEHS CO2 price (and, until end-2026, the reduced natural-gas rate that biomethane shares with fossil CNG). There is no Sweden/Finland-style relief restricted to volumes beyond the blending obligation, because there is no relief at all.

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes and proof of sustainability

Three accepted routes (RD 376/2022; Orden TED/728/2024 art. 23):
1. **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (Commission decisions — 18 schemes as of 2026-07-04, incl. ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, RSB, SURE…). **CONFIRMED** (BOE + DG ENER list).
2. **National schemes of other member states** with a favourable Commission decision. **CONFIRMED** (BOE).
3. **Spain's national verification route (SNVS):** verification entities accredited by **ENAC** (ISO/IEC 17065 & ISO 14065) issue sustainability verification reports; DGPEM publishes lists of accredited entities and certificates. **PRIMARY-ONLY** (MITECO page; small-model extraction of RD text — corroborator to confirm article numbers).

**Proof per consignment:** each consignment must carry sustainability information/declaration (feedstock, origin, GHG saving, mass-balance data) and, under the national route, "un informe de verificación de la sostenibilidad"; per-consignment declarations per Orden TED/728/2024 art. 23 feed SICBIOS certification. **PRIMARY-ONLY** (both from BOE fetches; independent corroboration of the exact article pending).

### 3.5 Union Database (UDB) status for Spanish operators

- **Resolución de la DGPEM de 10 de enero de 2024** addresses the UDB for Spanish economic operators (registration in the Commission database opened **15 Jan 2024**); operators must enter transaction and sustainability data (incl. lifecycle GHG) "once the UDB is enabled for each of them". **PRIMARY-ONLY** — the resolution PDF was located on miteco.gob.es but could not be text-extracted this session; content per search extract. (miteco.gob.es Resolución DGPEM UDB PDF; bio-e.es UDB note — both 2026-07-04.)
- Orden TED/728/2024 preamble: pending full UDB operability, sustainability documentation must continue to accompany national proofs (guarantees of origin for renewable gases). **PRIMARY-ONLY** (BOE fetch).
- Scheme-level: EU-recognised schemes require certified Spanish operators to hold UDB accounts (certificate suspension risk — 2BSvs/ISCC practice). **UNCORROBORATED as national legal requirement.**
- Draft RED III RD (July 2025): Chapter/Art. 33 would oblige **all economic operators in the renewable- and low-carbon-fuel chain** to submit information in the UDB framework. **PROPOSED** (draft RD PDF, miteco.gob.es, 2026-07-04).

### 3.7 Reporting/audit calendar and penalties

| Deadline | Obligation | Authority | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly, end of M+2 (Dec: 15 Feb) | Provisional SICBIOS data for month M | MITECO–DGPEM (SICBIOS) | PRIMARY-ONLY |

| Annual, 1 April | Definitive certification application for prior year (volumes by month/region, sustainability accreditation) | MITECO–DGPEM | CONFIRMED |
| Continuous | Voluntary-scheme certificate (annual audit) or SNVS verification report per consignment | Scheme CB / ENAC-accredited EV | CONFIRMED/PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Continuous | UDB account and transaction entries (once enabled per operator; mandatory date pending EU decision) | European Commission / DGPEM | PRIMARY-ONLY |

**Penalties:** shortfalls are settled by **pagos compensatorios** — base **1,623 €/deficient certificate** (general target) and **2,110 €/certificate** (advanced-biofuel target), tiered upward for larger deficits (+10 % for 60–75 % compliance band, +25 % below 60 %, per bio-e summary); payable within 30 days; residual breaches sanctioned under **Ley 34/1998**. CONFIRMED for the 1,623/2,110 base values (BOE-derived summaries: noticias.juridicas + bio-e.es); tier percentages PRIMARY-ONLY. **Note:** one automated extraction of the BOE text returned "€45/tep" for articles 28–29 — treated as an extraction error, but corroborator should re-read arts. 28–29 of Orden TED/728/2024 directly (flagged as CONFLICT-to-resolve).

## 4. Compliance checklist — sustainability items (draft)

1. Register as an obligated subject / operator with MITECO–DGPEM and obtain SICBIOS access (sede electrónica). [CONFIRMED]
2. Hold either (a) certification under an EU-recognised voluntary scheme, or (b) SNVS verification by an ENAC-accredited entity; keep per-consignment sustainability declarations/verification reports (≥ 50/60/65 % GHG saving by plant vintage). [CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY]
3. Submit monthly provisional data to SICBIOS by end of M+2 (December: 15 Feb). [PRIMARY-ONLY]
4. Meet ≥ 60 % of the pro-rata target each quarter or pay advance compensatory payments. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
5. Apply for definitive annual certificates by **1 April** each year, with full fossil/bio volumes and sustainability accreditation. [CONFIRMED]
6. From 2025, ensure **zero high-ILUC (palm-based) volumes** are claimed against targets unless certified low-ILUC-risk. [CONFIRMED]
7. Open a UDB account and record transactions per Resolución DGPEM 10 Jan 2024 / scheme requirements. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
8. Budget compensatory-payment exposure at 1,623–2,110 €/certificate for any deficit; note Ley 34/1998 sanctions for procedural breaches. [CONFIRMED base values]
9. Track the draft RD "fomento de combustibles renovables" (RED III transposition, e-credits, UDB duties for the whole chain) — adoption will change the calendar. [PROPOSED]

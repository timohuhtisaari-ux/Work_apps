### Notes / interaction with the mandate
- Belgium is a **mandate-only** market for biofuels — there is no tax-incentive layer (contrast FR TIRUERT or SE/FI above-mandate relief). Fuel excise is effectively equalised between petrol and diesel at ~600.2 EUR/1000 L after years of cliquet-driven diesel increases.
- The "cliquet" (ratchet) mechanism historically raised the special excise (bijzondere accijns / accise spéciale) as pump prices fell, up to a cap; it is not currently generating changes.
- National ETD transposition: **Programmawet van 27 december 2004** (Titel XI, accijnzen op energieproducten en elektriciteit), transposing Directive 2003/96/EC — legal citation to be verified verbatim by corroborator.

## 4-line summary (Belgium)
- Petrol (Euro 95 E10): total fuel excise 600.20 EUR/1000 L (0.6002 EUR/L), as of 31 Dec 2025, no adopted 2026 road-fuel change (increase scheduled 1 Jan 2027). [PRIMARY-ONLY; ~0.60/L corroborated by RTBF]
- Diesel (B7): total fuel excise 600.20 EUR/1000 L (0.6002 EUR/L) — equalised with petrol via the cliquet; professional-diesel refund 0.1913 EUR/L. [PRIMARY-ONLY]
- Biofuel relief: none — Belgium uses a blending mandate, not tax incentives; B7/E10/high blends taxed at full fuel rate.
- Flags: no separate CO2 tax on road fuels (EU-ETS2 from 1 Jan 2028); 600.20 total PRIMARY-ONLY (finance.belgium.be CAPTCHA-blocked); 2022 "455.53" reduction confirmed expired (no conflict); road-fuel excise rises 1 Jan 2027; DG TAXUD PDF unparseable.

# Lithuania — Biofuel Mandates (Section 1)

- **Country:** Lithuania (LT)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** biofuel-mandates-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** DONE (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04)
- **Scope:** Section 1 (Biofuel mandates) only. Fuel taxation and sustainability certification are other agents' scope.

**Headline:** Lithuania's **Law on Alternative Fuels (Alternatyviųjų degalų įstatymas, XIV-196, adopted 23 March 2021)** sets a rising RES-in-transport supplier obligation. The **current consolidated version is valid from 1 July 2026**; multiple counting for the obligation applies from 1 January 2026.

> **Corroborator note (2026-07-04):** the 2026 figures (**9.8 pp overall / 1.4 pp advanced+RFNBO**) are confirmed from a direct quote of the e-seimas primary text ("From 2026, fuel and transport-sector natural-gas suppliers … at least 9.8 percentage points … of which at least 1.4 percentage points advanced biofuels and/or RFNBO"). The 2030 endpoint (**16.8 % / 3.5 %**) and the **€1,000–€1,000,000** fine are corroborated on the **PwC Legal** briefing [4] (Tier-2). Note: the penalty rests on a *secondary* source only — no primary penalty-article read was achieved — so it is downgraded to UNCORROBORATED.

## 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** **fuel suppliers** placing petrol/diesel on the market, and **transport natural-gas suppliers** (Art. 16(4),(9)). Compliance is tracked through **renewable-energy fuel accounting units (DAEI/VIET units)**. [1]
- **Accounting-units administrator:** **BALTPOOL UAB** operates the accounting-units system. [5]
- **Lithuanian Energy Agency (Lietuvos energetikos agentūra / ENA):** evaluates feedstocks for inclusion on the advanced-biofuel/RFNBO **feedstock list**, publishing the assessment **annually by 1 June** per the Art. 21 methodology; supports the accounting/registry framework. [1][5]
- **Regulator/enforcement:** fines are administered under the alternative-fuels enforcement regime (State Energy Regulatory Council / VERT context). [4]

- **National indicative target (NIT):** RES in the transport sector **≥ 15 %** of final energy consumption by 2030 (distinct from the supplier obligation above). [1][5]
- **Note on superseded figures:** the **original 2021** text of Art. 16 set 2025–2026 at 7 % overall / 1.5 % advanced; the **current amended trajectory** (8.6 %→16.8 %; advanced 1.0 %→3.5 %) supersedes it.

**RED III transposition status:** Largely transposed for the transport sector — the Law on Alternative Fuels was amended to introduce **RFNBO mandates** (Lithuania among the first MS to transpose RFNBO for transport and industry) and **multiple counting from 1 Jan 2026**; Lithuania adopted a broader **RED III package in June 2025** (early mover). **However, RED III is not yet certified fully transposed:** Lithuania is on the Commission's **24 July 2025 letter-of-formal-notice** list (26 MS) for incomplete transposition (deadline 21 May 2025), in addition to the earlier **September-2024** formal notice on permitting-acceleration provisions. [3][6][7][8] **CORROBORATOR VERIFIED** LT is on the 24 Jul 2025 list.

**ETS2 (EU cross-cutting):** road-transport fuels fall under EU ETS2, **established by Directive (EU) 2023/959** (Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC). The compliance obligation now starts **1 Jan 2028** — the original 2027 start was **postponed by one year** in the early-2026 climate package (adopted alongside Regulation (EU) 2026/667). **First allowance surrender 2029** (for 2028 emissions); auctioning from 2027. Directly applicable. [8][9]

## 5-line summary — Lithuania
1. Obligation type: energy-share (RES-in-transport) supplier obligation in percentage points, with multiple counting; obligated = petrol/diesel suppliers + transport gas suppliers; tracked via DAEI/VIET accounting units (BALTPOOL) with ENA running the feedstock list.
2. Current level (2026): **≥ 9.8 pp** overall, of which **≥ 1.4 pp** advanced biofuels + RFNBO; UCO/animal-fat cap 1.7 %; crop cap = 2020 share + 1 pp; penalties €1,000–€1,000,000 (secondary-only, downgraded to UNCORROBORATED).
3. Next legislated change: **2027 → ≥ 11.3 pp** overall / ≥ 1.8 pp advanced (adopted trajectory continues to 16.8 % / 3.5 % by 2030).
4. RED III status: transport sector largely transposed (RFNBO + multiple counting from 01.01.2026; June-2025 package); **but LT is on the 24 Jul 2025 formal-notice list (26 MS)** + Sept-2024 permitting notice — not fully certified.
5. Flags: penalty range **UNCORROBORATED** (PwC only); standalone RFNBO minimum and UCO/crop caps **PRIMARY-ONLY**; 2026 & 2030 figures **CONFIRMED** (primary + PwC).

# Croatia (HR) — Fuel taxation (Section 2)

- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** fuel-tax-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** pending
- **Currency/unit:** Croatia is euro-area since 1 Jan 2023; excise (trošarina) expressed in EUR per 1000 L.

> **Key structural note:** Croatia does **not** hold a fixed statutory motor-fuel excise. Under the
> retail-price-regulation regime, the Government re-sets the trošarina on petrol and diesel **every 14 days**
> by decree ("Uredba o izmjeni Uredbe o visini trošarine na energente i električnu energiju"), together with
> the maximum retail prices and supplier premiums, to smooth pump prices as wholesale (Platts) costs move.
> Diesel excise has been held **at/near the EU minimum (330 EUR/1000 L)** through 2026; petrol excise floats
> in the low-450s EUR/1000 L. Any single figure is a snapshot of the current 14-day window.

**Mandate interaction:** Croatia is an obligation/quota market (biofuel placing obligation), so — unlike SE/FI — there is
**no excise incentive tied to volumes above the blending mandate**. Excise policy and the biofuel mandate operate on
separate tracks. (Mandate detail is out of scope — cross-reference the mandates agent.)

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Zakon o trošarinama** (Excise Duties Act,
NN 106/2018 and amendments; consolidated text on zakon.hr, z/545). No biofuel-specific notified state-aid decision
identified (the separate "plavi dizel" reduced rate for agriculture/fisheries is not a biofuel measure).

### 4-line summary — Croatia
- Petrol excise: variable, Government-set every 14 days; last confirmed 452.31 EUR/1000 L (Apr 2026), current window NN 69/2026 from 30 Jun 2026 (exact figure unretrieved).
- Diesel excise: 330.00 EUR/1000 L (EU minimum), held at/near this level through 2026.
- Biofuel relief: none via excise — biofuels taxed as their fossil equivalent; promotion runs through a placing-on-market obligation, not tax incentives.
- Flags: nn.hr HTTP 503 blocked retrieval of the exact current (NN 69/2026) figures; no separate CO2 tax; ETS2 surrender from 2028 (Dir (EU) 2023/959).

## 1. Biofuel mandates

### 1.1 Current obligation

Hungary runs an **energy-based renewable-fuel share obligation** ("kötelező
megújulóüzemanyag-részarány") on entities that release motor fuel for
consumption. The framework statute is **Act CXVII of 2010** (2010. évi CXVII.
törvény) on promoting renewable energy in transport and reducing transport
GHG emissions; the statute delegates the numeric percentages to a government
decree (§13(1)(h)). The operative percentages for 2026 sit in **Government
Decree 821/2021 (XII. 28.), § 9**, as substantially amended by **Government
Decree 488/2025 (XII. 31.)** (published in Magyar Közlöny No. 162 of 31 Dec 2025;
in force 1 Jan 2026).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** the entity that releases motor petrol/diesel for
  consumption (excise release), i.e. the same operators liable for fuel excise.
- **Metric:** energy content of biofuel/renewable fuel over the combined energy
  content of petrol + diesel released, to one decimal place (Act CXVII/2010 §2
  definition of részarány).
- **Enforcement/registry:** compliance is reported to and enforced by the
  **state tax and customs authority (NAV)** under Act CXVII/2010 §7; MEKH
  (energy regulator) is named in the source registry as the mandates authority,
  but the primary text accessed assigns non-compliance fines to NAV — MEKH's
  precise operational role in the blending obligation could **not** be confirmed
  from the primary text (see Gaps). Corroborator confirmed §7: NAV ("állami adó-
  és vámhatóság") imposes the fine; the penalty = base (MJ shortfall) × a unit
  rate set in the implementing government decree.
- **Banking/borrowing/trading:** not confirmed from primary text accessed (gap).
- **Sustainability certification** (feedstock GHG criteria, mass balance) is
  governed by Decree 821/2021 — that is the *sustainability* agent's scope; the
  blend percentages happen to be co-located in the same decree's §9.

- **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transposition status:** Hungary has
  **domestically adopted** transposing measures — Act CXVII/2010 was amended in
  2025 (in-force consolidated text dated 2026-05-14 references Dir. (EU) 2023/2413
  in §8(3)) and the 488/2025 decree implements the RED III sub-targets. The
  EUR-Lex NIM collection for 2023/2413 showed no HU measures in the visible
  extract; the Commission opened infringement (letters of formal notice, 2024)
  and has since **escalated Hungary to a REASONED OPINION in the December 2025
  infringement package** (grouped with Malta and Poland) "for failing to provide
  sufficiently clear and precise information on how their transposition measures
  transpose each of the Directive's provisions." Net status: **transposed in
  national law but in infringement — escalated from formal notice to reasoned
  opinion (Dec 2025).** (PRIMARY-ONLY / infringement — corroborated S8, S10)
- **EU cross-cut:** **ETS2** — the separate emissions-trading system for road
  transport and buildings fuels = **Chapter IVa of Directive 2003/87/EC, inserted
  by Directive (EU) 2023/959**. The compliance/surrender obligation on road-fuel
  suppliers starts **2028** (start postponed one year from 2027 by the co-legislators
  in March 2026); allowance trading from 2027. Applies to Hungarian fuel suppliers
  directly; not part of the national blending mandate. **(Citation corrected — the
  earlier draft cited Reg. (EU) 2026/667, which is the separate EU 2040 climate-target
  law amending Reg. (EU) 2021/1119, NOT ETS2.)**

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **njt.hu (official National Legislation Repository) returned HTTP 503** on the
  corroboration retry as well (docid 2021-821-20-22). Primary percentages were
  obtained/re-confirmed from the consolidated law database **net.jogtar.hu**
  (Hatályos Jogszabályok Gyűjteménye) — Tier 1 — and **independently corroborated
  by Magyar Közlöny No. 162/2025 (the official gazette carrying Decree 488/2025)**
  and two secondary trade-press sources. The 9.1 % / 6.1 % / 9.6 % / 0.4 % / 1 %
  figures are therefore CONFIRMED. Follow-up: re-verify §9 directly on njt.hu when reachable.
- **Annex IX-B 4 % cap** — corroborator searched Decree 821/2021 §9 and Act
  CXVII/2010 in full: **the 4 % IX-B cap is not present in either primary text.**
  It rests on ePURE only → remains UNCORROBORATED. Do not present as settled.
- **Penalty rate (32 HUF/MJ; ~HUF 20 bn annual)** is secondary-only (agroinform);
  Act §7 confirms NAV imposes a fine = MJ-shortfall × a "fajlagos bírságtétel"
  set by government decree, but neither the Act nor Decree 821/2021 (as read)
  states a Ft/MJ figure. A separate secondary reading returned **35 Ft/MJ**, so
  the exact rate is **uncertain (32 vs 35 Ft/MJ)** — UNCORROBORATED, possible conflict.
- **2030 overall RES-T 25 %** not independently corroborated this pass (advanced
  5.5 % IS confirmed from Decree §9(4)). Downgraded to PRIMARY-ONLY.
- **MEKH's operational role** in the blending obligation not confirmed from
  primary text; registry lists MEKH but Act §7 points to NAV. Flag for registry note.
- **2027-2029 intermediate steps** and banking/trading mechanics not extracted.

### 5-line summary — Hungary
1. Obligation type: energy-based renewable-fuel share on fuel-releasing entities (Act CXVII/2010 + Decree 821/2021 §9).
2. Current level (2026): 9.1 % overall; petrol ≥6.1 % bioethanol, diesel ≥9.6 % biofuel (HVO ≥0.4 %); advanced ≥1 % — CONFIRMED (net.jogtar + Magyar Közlöny 162).
3. Next legislated change: advanced sub-target rises to 5.5 % by 2030 (CONFIRMED, primary); overall RES-T 25 % by 2030 (PRIMARY-ONLY); intermediate 2027-29 steps a gap.
4. RED III status: transposed domestically (2025 amendment + Decree 488/2025) but in infringement — escalated to a **reasoned opinion (Dec 2025)**. ETS2 (Dir. (EU) 2023/959, Ch. IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC) surrender obligation from 2028.
5. Flags: Annex IX-B 4 % cap UNCORROBORATED (not in either primary text); penalty rate UNCORROBORATED (32 vs 35 Ft/MJ); njt.hu unreachable (503); MEKH role a gap.

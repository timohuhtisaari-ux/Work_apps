# Poland — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Poland (PL)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Poland's transport-fuel obligation is the **National Indicative Target (Narodowy Cel Wskaźnikowy, NCW)** — a minimum **energy share** of biocomponents/renewable fuels, set at **10% for 2026** (statutory schedule 9.2% in 2025 → 10% for 2026–2029 → 14.9% by 2030), with a 0.85 reduction coefficient bringing the physically-required share to roughly **8.5%** in 2026, plus petrol/diesel biocomponent minimums (E10/B7 blend walls) and a phased advanced-biofuels sub-target rising to 3.5% by 2030. Support is delivered **entirely through the quota (NCW), not through tax** — there is no reduced excise or exemption for high blends, and biocomponents used as standalone fuels are taxed at the diesel rate (petrol excise 1 529 PLN/1000 L, diesel 1 160 PLN/1000 L, both restored to standard on 16 Jun 2026). The most important supplier obligations are: register with **KOWR**, certify under the national **KZR INiG** scheme or an EU-recognised voluntary scheme, carry proof-of-sustainability on a mass-balance basis, record transactions in the EU **UDB** (mandatory since 3 Apr 2023), and file **quarterly NCW reports to URE** and quarterly producer reports to KOWR. The headline compliance risk is legal, not operational: **Poland has NOT transposed RED III** — the adopted 2025 amendment transposes RED II only, and Poland is under an **open RED III letter of formal notice (24 Jul 2025)** with transposition still only a draft bill (UC106). ETS2 compliance for road-transport fuels begins 1 Jan 2028.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated entities ("podmioty realizujące NCW"):** domestic producers and importers/intra-EU acquirers of liquid fuels and liquid biofuels who place motor petrol/diesel on the market or use them for own needs (Act art. 23 ust. 1). (S9, S11)
- **Registry / administration:** **KOWR** (Krajowy Ośrodek Wsparcia Rolnictwa, Director General) maintains the **register of biocomponent producers (rejestr wytwórców biokomponentów)** and the register of NCW-realising entities, receives quarterly producer reports (within 45 days of quarter-end), and notifies **URE** of register changes within 7 days. Operating without registration carries a PLN 5,000 fine. (S11, S12)
- **Enforcement / reporting:** obligated entities file **quarterly NCW reports to the President of URE**; a new quarterly report template applies from 31 Jan 2026 (Reg. of the Minister of Climate & Environment of 6 Nov 2025, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1618). URE verifies achievement and imposes penalties. (S13, S14)
- **Compliance unit of account:** energy content (calorific value) of biocomponents/renewable fuels; advanced feedstocks and certain wastes count double.
- **Banking/borrowing:** not established in sources — GAP.

### Interaction with the mandate
Poland's biofuel policy is **quota-based (NCW)**, not tax-incentive-based. There is no reduced excise or exemption for high blends (E85, B100, HVO100) — biocomponents used as standalone fuels are taxed at the diesel rate (1 160 PLN/1000 L). Consequently there is no Swedish/Finnish-style "relief limited to volumes above the obligation" condition, because there is no excise relief to condition. Support flows entirely through the NCW obligation and certificate value.

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register with KOWR** in the appropriate register (biocomponent producer / importer-intra-EU acquirer / NCW-realising entity) **before** placing biocomponents, biofuels, petrol or diesel on the Polish market — operating unregistered carries a PLN 5,000 fine. → **KOWR** (Krajowy Ośrodek Wsparcia Rolnictwa).
2. **Certify under a recognised scheme** — the national **KZR INiG** scheme or an EU-recognised voluntary scheme (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs); confirm the scheme's administrator is entered in **KOWR's register of certification-system administrators** before relying on it. → chosen scheme / certifying body + KOWR.
3. **Meet the applicable GHG-saving threshold** for your installation vintage (50% ≤5 Oct 2015 / 60% to 2020 / 65% from 2021; 70% for RFNBO/RCF) and **exclude high-ILUC palm volumes** per Del. Reg. (EU) 2019/807. → self + accredited auditor.
4. **Issue and carry proof-of-sustainability** (scheme certificate + *poświadczenie*) per consignment on a full **mass-balance** basis through the chain of custody. → scheme / certifying body.
5. **Record every transaction and its sustainability/GHG data in the EU UDB** — mandatory since 3 Apr 2023 for liquid/gaseous transport biofuels/bioliquids. → **UDB** (European Commission).
6. **Achieve the 2026 NCW** — a 10% energy share (≈8.5% physically required after the 0.85 reduction coefficient), including the petrol/diesel biocomponent minimums (E10/B7) and the phased advanced-biofuel sub-target. → self; verified by **URE**.
7. **File the quarterly NCW report to the President of URE** using the new template mandatory from 31 Jan 2026 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1618), and file the **quarterly producer report to KOWR within 45 days** of each calendar-quarter end (or a nil statement). → **URE** / **KOWR**.
8. **Settle any shortfall correctly** — if at least 85% of the base NCW was achieved, pay the *opłata zastępcza* to the low-emission transport fund by **31 January**; otherwise expect an administrative penalty (*kara pieniężna*) from URE. → **URE** / low-emission transport fund.
9. **Undergo the periodic third-party certification audit** (typically annual) under your chosen scheme, and retain documentation. → certifying body.
10. **Prepare for ETS2**: road-transport fuel suppliers face an EU allowance-surrender obligation from **1 Jan 2028** (auctioning from 2027) under ETS2 (Directive (EU) 2023/959, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC; the one-year postponement was adopted alongside Reg. (EU) 2026/667, the 2040 climate-target amendment). → national ETS2 authority.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

**Priority flag — RED III NOT transposed (infringement risk).** Poland has **not** transposed RED III. The adopted Act of 21 Feb 2025 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 303) transposes **RED II (2018/2001) only** (recital cites 2018/2001). Poland is under an **open RED III letter of formal notice dated 24 Jul 2025** (26-MS batch; deadline was 21 May 2025); transposition remains only a **draft bill (UC106, RCL 14 Jan 2026; planned entry into force 1 Jan 2027)**. Poland was not escalated in the June-2026 package, but only Cyprus was escalated there, so non-escalation does **not** mean the case is closed. An earlier PL-sustainability inference that the gap was "substantially closed" has been **struck/corrected**; both drafts are now aligned to "not transposed — open infringement."

**Resolved conflicts (mandates):**
- **2030 headline:** secondary reports circulated two figures — 14.9% and 29%. The **14.9%** figure is the adopted 2025 (RED II) schedule (S3, S7 both explicit); the **29%** figure belongs to the separate, not-yet-adopted RED III draft UC106. A UC106 summary paraphrased the current 2030 statutory value as "14.5%," but the primary-adjacent RED II coverage (S3) states 14.9% verbatim — treated as a rounding/paraphrase artifact, not a genuine conflict.

**Penalty/buy-out amounts — GAP.** The exact **opłata zastępcza** formula and the URE administrative-penalty amount in PLN could not be located in primary text.

# Latvia — Biofuel Mandates (Section 1)

- **Country:** Latvia (LV)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** biofuel-mandates-researcher
- **Corroboration pass:** DONE (source-corroborator, 2026-07-04)
- **Scope:** Section 1 (Biofuel mandates) only. Fuel taxation and sustainability certification are other agents' scope.

**Headline:** As of **1 January 2026** Latvia's new **Transport Energy Law (Transporta enerģijas likums)** entered into force, replacing the old Biofuel Law (Biodegvielas likums). It combines a **GHG-intensity reduction target** (from 2030), an **advanced-fuel + RFNBO energy-share sub-obligation**, and **transitional volumetric blending** requirements.

> **Corroborator note (2026-07-04):** the headline figures (advanced+RFNBO 1 %→5.5 %, 16 % GHG from 2030, penalties €90/t and €70/unit) are independently corroborated on the **lvportals.lv** official-portal explainer [2], which quotes the Latvian statutory wording — a source of different origin from the likumi.lv primary text. CONFIRMED labels retained. The 9.5 %/6.5 % volumetric floors rest on the primary text + the single BNN Tier-2 item only.

## 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated party:** **fuel suppliers** placing on the transport market **> 100 tonnes/year** (Art. 1 definition). Exemptions for suppliers of only alternative fuels, RFNBO, or aviation fuel (Art. 11(2), Art. 13(3)). [1]
- **Administrator:** Ministry of Climate and Energy (kem.gov.lv) framework; the **State Environmental Service (VVD)** is the sustainability market-surveillance authority (see sustainability report); State Revenue Service (VID) handles the fuel-tax side (other agent).
- **Compliance:** demonstrated against the annual GHG-reduction and energy-share obligations plus the transitional volumetric blend floors; shortfalls trigger the per-tonne / per-GJ additional payments above.
- Transition from the repealed **Biodegvielas likums**; note Latvia had earlier (2022) temporarily **suspended** its mandatory biofuel blend to curb fuel-price inflation — historical context, superseded by the 2026 law. [5]

**RED III transposition status:** The **Transport Energy Law (in force 01.01.2026)** is Latvia's principal RED III **transport-sector** transposition instrument (16 % GHG target, advanced+RFNBO sub-obligation). However, RED III is **not yet certified fully transposed**: on **24 July 2025** the Commission sent Latvia a **letter of formal notice** — one of **26 Member States** in that package — for failure to notify complete transposition (deadline 21 May 2025; permitting-acceleration provisions due 1 July 2024 and covered in the earlier Sept-2024 package). At the July-2025 cut-off the Transport Energy Law had not yet entered into force (it took effect 1 Jan 2026), so the transport-sector gap has since been closed; the permitting provisions remain the open element. [1][3][7] **CORROBORATOR VERIFIED** Latvia is on the 24 Jul 2025 formal-notice list.

**ETS2 (EU cross-cutting):** road-transport fuels fall under EU ETS2, **established by Directive (EU) 2023/959** (Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC). The compliance obligation now starts **1 Jan 2028** — the original 2027 start was **postponed by one year** in the early-2026 climate package (adopted alongside Regulation (EU) 2026/667, the 2040 climate-target amendment). **First allowance surrender 2029** (for 2028 emissions); auctioning from 2027. Directly applicable. [7][8]

## 5-line summary — Latvia
1. Obligation type: combined — GHG-intensity reduction (16 % from 2030) + advanced/RFNBO energy-share sub-obligation + transitional volumetric blending; obligated party = fuel suppliers > 100 t/yr.
2. Current level (2026): advanced + RFNBO **≥ 1 energy %**; petrol-95 **≥ 9.5 vol %**, diesel **≥ 6.5 vol %** (pure diesel allowed only until 31 Mar 2026); penalties €90/t CO2 and €70/GJ.
3. Next legislated change: from **2030** advanced+RFNBO rises to **5.5 %** (≥1 pp RFNBO) and the **16 % GHG** reduction target applies.
4. RED III status: transport provisions **transposed** via the Transport Energy Law (in force 01.01.2026); **but Latvia is on the 24 Jul 2025 formal-notice list** (26 MS) — permitting provisions remain the open infringement.
5. Flags: Annex IX-B/crop caps and double-counting multipliers are **GAPS**; headline figures **CONFIRMED** (likumi.lv primary + lvportals independent); 9.5 %/6.5 % have only one Tier-2 (BNN).

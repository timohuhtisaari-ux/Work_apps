# Slovenia — Biofuel Regulation Compliance Report

- **Country:** Slovenia (SI)
- **Report date:** 2026-07-04
- **Researched by:** mandates/tax/sustainability researchers + corroborator
- **Corroboration pass:** done

## Executive summary

Slovenia imposes an **energy-share renewable-energy-in-transport obligation** on fuel suppliers ("dobavitelji goriv"), set year by year in the *Uredba o obnovljivih virih energije v prometu*, alongside a −6% FQD GHG-intensity reduction. The **2026 minimum is 13.8%** RES energy share, rising on a legislated path to **15.8% (2027), 18.3% (2028) and 20.8% (2029–2030)** — all re-verified against the primary Uredba, though these specific out-year values carry no independent Tier-2 corroboration (PRIMARY-ONLY). The advanced-biofuel (Annex IX-A) path is 0.2% → 1% (2025) → 3.5% (2030); the Annex IX-B cap is 1.7% and the crop cap 7%. Fuel taxation provides **no biofuel-specific excise relief** — biofuels are taxed by energy-product category under ZTro-1, petrol excise is 417.59 EUR/1000 L and diesel 330.00 EUR/1000 L in the current window (both re-set every 14 days), with a separate CO2 environmental levy the Government repeatedly zeroed and re-introduced through 2026. The top supplier obligations are: meet the annual energy share, issue a *spremno potrdilo* (accompanying certificate) per consignment, and file the annual operator report to a monitoring contractor by 28 February. **RED III was transposed late** by the new primary law **ZSROVE-1 (Ur. l. RS 112/2025, 29 Dec 2025)**; its penalty amount could not be extracted and remains a gap.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated:** fuel suppliers ("dobavitelji goriv") placing petrol/diesel/other transport energy on the Slovenian market. [1]
- **Calculation:** the Uredba sets annual minimum energy shares and the calculation method (multipliers for advanced fuels/renewable electricity), plus fossil-fuel/biofuel category definitions. [1]
- **Banking/transfer:** the 2024 amendment (Ur. l. RS 70/24, 23 Aug 2024) introduced carry-over of energy-share deficits/surpluses across years (deficits/surpluses achieved in 2024 transferable to 2022/2023/2025/2026), and deleted the former Art. 4(2). [7]
- **Registry / certificates:** no tradable-certificate market identified; compliance is a direct energy-share obligation reported to the Ministry of the Environment, Climate and Energy. Reporting mechanics — see Section 3.
- **Enforcement:** administrative, via the parent act (FURS/ministry) — see fuel-tax section for any tax interaction.

## 2. Fuel taxation

> **Key structural note:** Slovenia runs a **regulated / formula-based fuel-price regime**. Every 14 days the Government adopts an "Uredba o določitvi zneska trošarine za energente in električno energijo" that re-sets the excise (trošarina) on petrol, diesel and heating oil in step with the regulated maximum retail prices. Separately, a **CO2 environmental levy** (okoljska dajatev za onesnaževanje zraka z emisijo CO2) applies on top of excise — but in 2026 the Government has repeatedly **zeroed and re-introduced** that levy on motor fuels by decree. Both the excise and the CO2 levy are therefore moving targets; figures below are the current-window snapshot. EUR per 1000 L (euro-area).

**Mandate interaction:** Slovenia is an obligation market (RES-T/GHG decree). No excise incentive is tied to volumes above the blending obligation (contrast SE/FI). Mandate detail is in Section 1.

**ETD implementation:** Directive 2003/96/EC is transposed by the **Zakon o trošarinah (ZTro-1)** (PISRS ZAKO7128). No biofuel-specific notified state-aid decision identified (Slovenia's earlier biofuel excise-relief schemes have lapsed; promotion is now obligation-based).

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

From **Uredba Art. 6** (quoted this pass):
- **50 %** — installations operating before **5 Oct 2015**
- **60 %** — installations starting **6 Oct 2015 – 31 Dec 2020**
- **65 %** — liquid biofuels from installations starting **1 Jan 2021** onward
- **70 %** — biomass fuels (electricity/heat/cooling), installations 2021–2025, rising to **80 % from 2026**

[CONFIRMED — S1] (matches RED II/III Art. 29(10).)

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes & proof of sustainability

- **Uredba Art. 10** recognises **two pathways**: (a) an EU-Commission-approved **voluntary national/international scheme**, or (b) direct verification of compliance with the Art. 5–9 criteria. Independent auditors ("neodvisni presojevalec") verify under a voluntary scheme — Art. 10(2). [CONFIRMED — S1]
- Usable schemes = the full **DG ENER recognised list** (18 as of 2026-07-04): **ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs, Bonsucro EU, RSB, RTRS EU RED, SURE, KZR INiG, Better Biomass**, etc. [CONFIRMED (EU-level) — S6]
- **Proof of sustainability per consignment:** producers must issue a **spremno potrdilo (accompanying certificate)** per consignment — Art. 12(2) — containing facility name, tax number, feedstock origin, biofuel type, GHG emissions and a sustainability-compliance declaration. [CONFIRMED — S1]

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Register as an obligated fuel supplier ("dobavitelj goriv")** placing petrol/diesel/transport energy on the Slovenian market — you carry the annual RES-in-transport energy-share obligation.
2. **Meet the 2026 minimum of 13.8% RES energy share** (rising to 15.8% in 2027, 18.3% in 2028, 20.8% in 2029–2030), plus the −6% FQD GHG-intensity reduction. Use the Uredba's carry-over of deficits/surpluses where advantageous. → self / MOPE.
3. **Respect the sub-targets and caps:** advanced (Annex IX-A) ≥1% (2025) → 3.5% (2030); Annex IX-B cap 1.7%; crop cap 7%; EU high-ILUC (palm) phase-out to 0% by 2030. → self + auditor.
4. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU, REDcert, 2BSvs…) or arrange direct Art. 5–9 verification (Uredba Art. 10). → certification body / independent auditor.
5. **Issue a spremno potrdilo (accompanying certificate) for every consignment** carrying facility, tax number, feedstock origin, biofuel type, GHG value and compliance declaration (Art. 12(2)). → self.
6. **Meet the GHG-saving thresholds** (biofuel 50/60/65 % by installation start date; biomass fuels 70 % → 80 % from 2026) and document per batch (Art. 6). → self + auditor.
7. **File the annual operator report to your monitoring contractor by 28 February** (Art. 18(1)); the contractor reports to MOPE by 31 March (Art. 18(3)). → monitoring contractor / MOPE.
8. **Onboard to the EU Union Database (UDB)** via your voluntary scheme and record in-scope transactions; maintain a UDB account to avoid certificate suspension (mandatory-use date pending). → UDB / voluntary scheme.
9. **Non-compliance exposure:** administrative fines set in ZSROVE-1 (amount not yet located — see Section 5); enforcement via the environmental/agricultural inspectorates and FURS. → inspectorate / FURS.
10. **Prepare for ETS2** as a regulated road-transport fuel supplier: 1 Jan 2028 start, first allowance surrender 31 May 2029. → competent ETS2 authority.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **2026 = 13.8% → 20.8% by 2030 is PRIMARY-ONLY (re-verified).** The 2026 minimum and the 2027/2028/2029–2030 step-ups (15.8% / 18.3% / 20.8%) were re-fetched against the primary Uredba Art. 4(1) but carry **no independent Tier-2 confirmation** for the specific out-year values; the 70/24 amendment touched only Art. 4(2)/banking, and ZSROVE-1 sets no year-by-year transport %.
- **RED III transposed late — ZSROVE-1 (Ur. l. RS 112/2025, 29 Dec 2025).** Adopted after the 21 May 2025 deadline; Slovenia had a Sept 2024 letter of formal notice and was NOT escalated in June 2026 (only Cyprus on RED III, corroborator-confirmed); Commission assessment likely still pending.
- **ZSROVE-1 penalty amount is a GAP:** the fine for the transport obligation is set in ZSROVE-1, not the Uredba; the corroborator fetched the ZSROVE-1 PDF but it is not text-parseable, so the penal provisions (kazenske določbe / globe) could not be extracted. Also unresolved: whether ZSROVE-1 introduces a national UDB obligation or revises the Uredba's monitoring regime (check once implementing acts appear).
- **CO2 environmental levy on/off state volatile:** ~€25.90/t CO2 repeatedly zeroed and re-introduced on motor fuels through 2026; the exact on/off state for the 30 Jun–14 Jul window is not pinned to a single decree citation.
- **Excise figures PRIMARY-ONLY:** the FURS excise landing page returned HTTP 404 and PISRS is JS-only; 417.59/330.00 EUR/1000 L come from the official Uredba text reproduced on racunovodstvo.net + the Ministry portal. Tier-1 DG TAXUD cross-check not achieved (PDF not parseable).
- **No dedicated national sustainability registry** (Nabisy-equivalent) located — compliance runs via the accompanying certificate + monitoring-contractor route. **No SI palm/soy ban beyond EU; no Annex IX national additions** located. [GAPs]
- Some Uredba items cited from a single successful fetch of the uradni-list page; a second independent read (PISRS URED8173) recommended (PISRS remained JS-only this pass). [FLAG]
- **ETS2 across all four countries:** established by Directive (EU) 2023/959; start postponed to 1 Jan 2028, first surrender 2029.

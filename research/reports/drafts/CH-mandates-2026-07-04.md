> Framing: Switzerland is **not in the EU or EEA**; RED III and EU ETS2 do not apply.
> For **road transport there is NO volumetric blending mandate.** The biofuel
> mineral-oil-tax relief did **NOT** expire end-2023 — it runs by law to 31 Dec 2030
> (see CH-tax; registry line corrected). The operative 2026 instrument for
> road fuels is a **CO2 compensation obligation** on fossil-fuel importers under the
> **CO2 Act (SR 641.71)** and **CO2 Ordinance (SR 641.711)** — a CO2-offset regime,
> **not** a volumetric mandate. Separately, a **new aviation SAF blending mandate**
> applies from 2026 (mirroring EU ReFuelEU). Administered by **BAFU/FOEN** (climate),
> **BFE/SFOE** (energy) and **BAZL** (civil aviation); mineral-oil tax by **BAZG/OFDF**.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated (road):** importers placing **fossil motor fuels** on the Swiss
  market. They must offset the prescribed % of resulting transport CO2 emissions —
  in 2026, **30%** — of which at least 12 percentage-points' worth must be domestic
  (max 18% abroad in 2026).
- **How compliance is shown:** surrender of **emission-reduction attestations**
  (Bescheinigungen) issued by BAFU/FOEN for qualifying domestic/foreign projects,
  and/or by placing renewable fuels on the market (which lowers the fossil base).
  The importer may pass at most **5 Rp/litre** to customers.
- **Administrators:** **BAFU/FOEN** (compensation obligation & attestations),
  **BFE/SFOE** (energy policy), **BAZL** (aviation SAF quota), **BAZG/OFDF**
  (mineral-oil tax — separate).
- **Aviation:** distinct from the compensation regime — a genuine **volumetric SAF
  blending mandate** (2% in 2026) tracking EU ReFuelEU, anchored in the CO2 law;
  applies at the national airports Zurich and Geneva from 1 Jan 2026.

### 5-line summary — Switzerland
1. Obligation type: **no volumetric road blending mandate**; road fuels covered by a **CO2 compensation (offset) obligation** on fossil-fuel importers under the CO2 Act/Ordinance — a CO2-compensation regime, not a mandate. (New separate **aviation SAF blending mandate** from 2026, CONFIRMED.)
2. Current level (2026): importers must compensate **30%** of transport CO2 emissions (≥12% domestically, max 18% abroad), max 5 Rp/litre pass-through; aviation SAF blend **2%**.
3. Next legislated change: compensation rate rises to **50% by 2030** (interim 35/40/45% for 2027–2029 = PRIMARY-ONLY); aviation SAF follows the EU ReFuelEU trajectory.
4. RED III status: N/A (non-EU/EEA); Switzerland aligns autonomously (SAF quota mirrors ReFuelEU).
5. Flags: 30%/12%/5 Rp and aviation 2% now CONFIRMED; obligation-trigger threshold, shortfall penalty, road renewable-fuel expectation and interim/post-2026 SAF table = GAPS.

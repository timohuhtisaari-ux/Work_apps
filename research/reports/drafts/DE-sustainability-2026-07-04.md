### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

§ 6 Biokraft-NachV (2021) mirrors RED II Art. 29(10), based on the date of first physical production of the final installation:

| Installation start | Minimum GHG saving | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| On or before 5 Oct 2015 | ≥ 50 % | CONFIRMED (gesetze-im-internet.de § 6 + RED II via DG ENER framework) |
| 6 Oct 2015 – 31 Dec 2020 | ≥ 60 % | CONFIRMED |
| From 1 Jan 2021 | ≥ 65 % | CONFIRMED |

RFNBO 70 % threshold (RED II Art. 25(2)/29a): enters national quota law via the Zweites Gesetz's RFNBO provisions; exact national article not yet verified — `PRIMARY-ONLY`. (S13, S5)

### 3.3 Certification schemes accepted

- Germany runs **no national certification scheme**; it accepts the **EU-recognised voluntary schemes** (Commission implementing decisions). DG ENER lists **18 recognised schemes** as of 2026-07-04, incl. **ISCC EU** (recognition 12 Apr 2022, amended 19 Dec 2024), **REDcert** (19 Dec 2024), **2BSvs** (12 Apr 2022), SURE, RSB, Better Biomass, CertifHy (RFNBO), etc. `CONFIRMED` (DG ENER Tier 1; BLE confirms operation "nach den von der RED II anerkannten Zertifizierungssystemen"). (S14, S11)
- BLE additionally **recognises/registers the certification bodies** (Zertifizierungsstellen) that audit under those schemes: lists of "anerkannte Zertifizierungsstellen" (status 9 Jun 2026) and bodies registered under **Art. 17 Implementing Regulation (EU) 2022/996** (status 05/2026). `CONFIRMED` (ble.de). (S11)
- Certificates are valid 12 months (annual re-audit cycle). `PRIMARY-ONLY` (seen in Biokraft-NachV search summary; exact § not re-verified). (S3)

### 3.4 Proof of sustainability per consignment

- Proof document: **Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis** (and **Nachhaltigkeits-Teilnachweis** for split quantities), issued **electronically in Nabisy** by the certified **letzte Schnittstelle** (last interface — the plant producing final-quality biofuel). Recognised proof types are enumerated in **§ 8 Biokraft-NachV** (proofs under §§ 9/16, certificates under §§ 14/15, plus grandfathered 2009-ordinance proofs issued by 7 Dec 2021). `CONFIRMED` (gesetze-im-internet.de § 8 + ble.de Nabisy pages). (S13, S15)
- On every change of ownership in the trading chain, the (Teil-)Nachweis is **transferred in Nabisy to the new owner**; the obligated quota party finally presents proofs to the Biokraftstoffquotenstelle. `CONFIRMED` (ble.de + zoll.de). (S15, S12)

### 3.5 National registry — Nabisy — and Union Database (UDB)

- **Nabisy** ("Nachhaltige-Biomasse-System"), operated by **BLE** (nabisy.ble.de). Account holders: **producers/interfaces (Schnittstellen)**, **suppliers/traders (Lieferanten)**, **plant operators** (CHP, biogas, ETS installations, aircraft operators), **grid operators**. Registration is a precondition for issuing/receiving proofs relevant to the German market. `CONFIRMED` (ble.de Nabisy info page). (S15)
- **UDB status (EU level):** UDB live since Nov 2024 for liquid supply chains; gaseous chains in pilot; ~31,000 certified economic operators onboarded via voluntary schemes; the Commission is **still coordinating the timeline for full mandatory use** with member states; a delegated regulation extending traceability upstream is in preparation. `CONFIRMED` (energy.ec.europa.eu / EC UDB info-site + scheme pages ISCC/2BSvs as Tier 2). (S16, S17)
- **German specifics:** proofs continue to run through Nabisy; **no bidirectional Nabisy–UDB interface is implemented yet** (German authority/industry material: DEHSt newsletter on alternative fuels, industry position papers demanding the interface before mandatory use). Practical onboarding obligation for German operators currently arises **via voluntary-scheme rules (e.g. ISCC)**, not via a Biokraft-NachV deadline. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (DEHSt Tier 1 + Tier 2 industry; no BLE page stating a mandatory UDB date was found). (S18, S19)

## 4. Compliance checklist items (sustainability scope, draft)

1. Certify every chain element up to and including the last interface under an **EU-recognised voluntary scheme** (ISCC EU / REDcert-EU are dominant in DE); use a **BLE-recognised/registered certification body**; re-audit every 12 months. [BLE; CONFIRMED]
2. Open the appropriate **Nabisy account** (Schnittstelle / Lieferant / Anlagenbetreiber) with BLE before trading sustainable volumes destined for the German market. [BLE; CONFIRMED]
3. Ensure a valid **Nachhaltigkeitsnachweis / Teilnachweis in Nabisy** accompanies every consignment; transfer it in Nabisy on each sale; verify GHG saving meets § 6 Biokraft-NachV thresholds (50/60/65 % by installation date). [BLE; CONFIRMED]
4. Do **not** rely on palm-based fuels (0 % cap since 2023) and plan the exit from **POME by the 2027 quota year**; check third-country chains allow **state on-site inspection** (eligibility condition under the 2026 act). [HZA/BLE; CONFIRMED]
5. Submit sustainability proofs with the annual quota declaration to the **Biokraftstoffquotenstelle by 15 April** (cross-ref mandates checklist). [HZA Frankfurt (Oder); CONFIRMED]
6. Register the operator in the **Union Database via the certification scheme**; monitor BLE/Commission announcements for the mandatory-use date and the Nabisy–UDB interface. [EC/BLE; PRIMARY-ONLY]
7. For UCO/waste chains: keep supplier self-declarations audit-ready and watch **BLE Nabisy fraud notices** (e.g. Dec 2025 revocation notice); expect stricter DAkkS-accredited audits once the ordinance recast is adopted. [BLE; PRIMARY-ONLY/PROPOSED]
8. Track adoption of the **Neufassung Biokraft-NachV/BioSt-NachV** (RED III sustainability criteria) — draft of 15 Aug 2025 still pending as of 2026-07-04. [BMUKN; PROPOSED]

### 3.2 GHG-saving thresholds applied nationally

- Only biofuels meeting the sustainability + GHG-saving criteria of **arts 32Ζ/32ΖΑ L.5106/2024**
  (transposing RED II Art. 29–31) qualify. [PRIMARY-ONLY — L.5106/2024 mirrors]
- Threshold ladder follows RED II Art. 29(10): **50 % / 60 % / 65 %** by installation
  vintage; **70 %** for RFNBOs. Exact Greek figures not directly quoted → flag. [FLAG]

### 3.3 Accepted certification schemes

- L.5106/2024 recognises **both voluntary schemes AND national schemes of EU member states
  recognised by the Commission** as means of verifying compliance across the chain of custody.
  Greece defines a national-scheme concept but relies in practice on the **EU-recognised
  voluntary schemes**; no separate operational Greek national certification scheme identified. [PRIMARY-ONLY — opengov.gr; DG ENER]
- ISCC EU is the dominant scheme in the Greek market; **EUROCERT** is the first Greek
  certification body ISCC-approved for biofuels; SGS/DNV also active. [CONFIRMED — ISCC/EUROCERT/SGS]
- **Proof of sustainability:** scheme certificate + PoS per consignment under mass balance. [PRIMARY-ONLY — L.5106/2024; scheme rules]

## 4. Draft compliance-checklist items (this scope)

1. **Hold EU-recognised voluntary-scheme certification** (ISCC EU widely used; REDcert-EU, 2BSvs…) covering the mass-balance chain, and issue a **PoS per consignment**. → certification body (e.g. EUROCERT/SGS/DNV).
2. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) per arts 32Ζ/32ΖΑ L.5106/2024 and document them. → self + auditor.
3. **Register for the annual pure-biodiesel allocation** with YPEN; where claiming domestic energy-crop weighting, register grower contracts in the ΟΣΔΕ-linked control system. → YPEN.
4. **Do NOT rely on DAPEEP for biofuel sustainability** — DAPEEP handles electricity guarantees of origin only; biofuel sustainability sits with YPEN. → note.
5. **Onboard to the Union Database (UDB)** and record PoS/transaction data for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → UDB.
6. **Respect EU high-ILUC feedstock rules** (palm 2019-level cap tapering to 0 by 2030); apply double counting only for qualifying Annex IX wastes/residues. → self + auditor.

**4-line summary — Greece**
- Transposition: RED II via Law 5106/2024 (ΦΕΚ Α' 63, 1 May 2024; sustainability/GHG arts 32Ζ/32ΖΑ, national-scheme concept), on top of L.3054/2002 (biodiesel allocation) and L.4062/2012 (RED I); RED III NOT transposed — biomethane only via L.5215/2025, and **Greece referred to CJEU 29 Apr 2026** (with PT/Malta), financial sanctions requested.
- Certification: EU-recognised voluntary schemes accepted (ISCC EU dominant; EUROCERT first Greek CB); national-scheme defined but not separately operational; PoS per consignment.
- Registry/UDB: YPEN is the competent/verifying authority and runs the annual pure-biodiesel allocation (energy-crop contracts in an ΟΣΔΕ-linked system); DAPEEP covers electricity GoO only, NOT biofuels; UDB operational since 15 Jan 2024, EU mandatory-use date pending.
- Flags: L.5106/2024 GHG-threshold figures PRIMARY-ONLY; RED III transport not transposed (CJEU referral); DAPEEP scope correction confirmed; no national palm ban beyond EU found.

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Obligated entities ("podmioty realizujące NCW"):** domestic producers and importers/intra-EU acquirers of liquid fuels and liquid biofuels who place motor petrol/diesel on the market or use them for own needs (Act art. 23 ust. 1). (S9, S11)
- **Registry / administration:** **KOWR** (Krajowy Ośrodek Wsparcia Rolnictwa, Director General) maintains the **register of biocomponent producers (rejestr wytwórców biokomponentów)** and the register of NCW-realising entities, receives quarterly producer reports (within 45 days of quarter-end), and notifies **URE** of register changes within 7 days. Operating without registration carries a PLN 5,000 fine. (S11, S12)
- **Enforcement / reporting:** obligated entities file **quarterly NCW reports to the President of URE**; a new quarterly report template applies from 31 Jan 2026 (Reg. of the Minister of Climate & Environment of 6 Nov 2025, Dz.U. 2025 poz. 1618). URE verifies achievement and imposes penalties. (S13, S14)
- **Compliance unit of account:** energy content (calorific value) of biocomponents/renewable fuels; advanced feedstocks and certain wastes count double.
- **Banking/borrowing:** not established in sources — GAP.

- **RED III transposition status: NOT transposed (in infringement).** The adopted Act of 21 Feb 2025 (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 303) transposes **RED II (2018/2001)** only; its own recital cites Directive 2018/2001. The EUR-Lex NIM page for RED III (2023/2413) lists **no notified Polish measure**. Deadline was 21 May 2025. `CONFIRMED` (that RED III is not yet transposed). (S15, S4, S5)
- **PROPOSED:** a further government draft to transpose RED III would raise the RES-in-transport target toward **29% by 2030** (RED III headline option) and restructure sub-targets. Not adopted. `PROPOSED`. (S16)
- **EU cross-cutting:** ETS2 compliance obligation for road-transport/buildings fuels starts **1 Jan 2028** (Reg. (EU) 2026/667), with auctioning from 2027 — applies to Polish fuel suppliers alongside the NCW.

## 5. Conflicts and gaps

- **Resolved conflict:** secondary reports circulated two 2030 figures — 14.9% and 29%. The **14.9%** figure is the adopted 2025 (RED II) schedule; the **29%** figure belongs to a separate, not-yet-adopted RED III draft. Reported accordingly.
- **Gaps:** 2026 advanced-biofuel milestone value; Annex IX-B cap; exact opłata zastępcza / administrative-penalty formula in PLN; whether the Council of Ministers has set a reduction coefficient other than 0.85 for 2026–2027; banking/borrowing rules. Primary art. 23/23b figures are `PRIMARY-ONLY` from the consolidated text (lexlege rendering); the official consolidated Dz.U. 2025 poz. 901 and the Dz.U. 2025 poz. 303 PDF should be re-read locally to confirm the year-by-year table verbatim (ISAP was CAPTCHA-blocked; the Dz.U. PDF could not be rendered here — poppler unavailable).

# Switzerland — Biofuel Mineral-Oil-Tax Exemption: Focused Deep-Dive

- **Country:** Switzerland (CH) — non-EU / non-EEA (bilateral relationship)
- **Topic:** Mechanics, amounts, timeline and interactions of the **mineral-oil-tax
  relief for biogenic/renewable fuels** (MinöStG Art. 12b)
- **Fact date-stamp:** 2026-07-13 · **Pass performed / sources fetched:** 2026-07-14
- **Researched by:** fuel-taxation researcher
- **Refines:** `research/reports/CH-country-report-2026-07-04.md` §2 — goes DEEPER on the
  relief; does not restate the mandates/sustainability material there.

> **Framing (non-EU).** Switzerland is outside the EU/EEA. The Energy Taxation
> Directive 2003/96/EC, RED III and EU ETS2 do **not** apply. Road fuel is taxed
> under the **Mineralölsteuergesetz (MinöStG, SR 641.61)** and its ordinance
> **(MinöStV, SR 641.611)**, administered by **BAZG/OFDF**. Carbon policy for motor
> fuels runs through the **CO2 Act (SR 641.71)** compensation obligation, not a CO2
> levy. Descriptions below are Switzerland's own regime; EU instruments are noted
> only where Switzerland deliberately mirrors them (SAF/ReFuelEU).
>
> **Source-access caveat (per instructions).** `fedlex.admin.ch` is JavaScript-gated
> and returned only its language-selection shell; the BAZG Richtlinie 09 and the BAFU
> explanatory PDFs are image/binary PDFs that could not be rendered in this
> environment (no poppler). Facts below therefore rest on the **HTML pages of BAZG
> (bazg.admin.ch), BAFU (bafu.admin.ch) and Pronovo**, plus Tier-2 corroboration.
> Direct Fedlex article text of MinöStG Art. 12b is **not machine-verified this
> pass** and is flagged where relevant.

## 1. Mineral-oil-tax structure on petrol and diesel (2026)

Two federal excises stack on road fuel: the **Mineralölsteuer** (base) and the
**Mineralölsteuerzuschlag** (surtax). Volumetric basis: per 1000 L at 15 °C; here in
Rappen (Rp) per litre (100 Rp = CHF 1).

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Petrol (unleaded) — TOTAL | **76.82 Rp/L (CHF 768.20 / 1000 L)** | MinöStG SR 641.61 + MinöStV SR 641.611 (Steuertarif) | in force 2026 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3, S7 |
| — of which Mineralölsteuer (base) | **45.30 Rp/L** | MinöStG tariff | 2026 | PRIMARY-ONLY (derived: 76.82 − 31.52) | S1, S3 |
| — of which Mineralölsteuerzuschlag (surtax) | **31.52 Rp/L** | MinöStG tariff | 2026 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| Diesel — TOTAL | **79.57 Rp/L (CHF 795.70 / 1000 L)** | MinöStG SR 641.61 + MinöStV SR 641.611 | in force 2026 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3, S7 |
| — of which Mineralölsteuer (base) | **48.11 Rp/L** | MinöStG tariff | 2026 | PRIMARY-ONLY (derived: 79.57 − 31.46) | S1, S3 |
| — of which Mineralölsteuerzuschlag (surtax) | **31.46 Rp/L** | MinöStG tariff | 2026 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| Separate CO2 tax on motor fuels | **NONE** — the CO2 levy (CO2-Abgabe) applies to thermal/heating fuels only, not petrol/diesel | CO2 Act SR 641.71 | 2026 | CONFIRMED (cross-ref) | S5 |

- The surtax split is stated by BAZG (petrol total 76.82 Rp/L *including* 31.52 Rp/L
  surtax; diesel 79.57 Rp/L *including* 31.46 Rp/L surtax); the base component is the
  arithmetic remainder [S1, S3]. Headline totals are stable into 2026 — no 2026
  budget change identified.

## 2. The biofuel tax relief (centrepiece) — MinöStG Art. 12b

### 2.1 Mechanism and legal basis

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective / sunset | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instrument | **Steuererleichterung für biogene Treibstoffe** — a mineral-oil-tax relief (remission) granted **on application** to qualifying renewable/biogenic fuels | **MinöStG Art. 12b** (SR 641.61); MinöStV SR 641.611 | in force; current version 1 Jan 2025 | PRIMARY-ONLY (Art. number per BAZG/Tier-2; Fedlex text JS-gated) | S2, S4, S8 |
| Nature of relief | **Full exemption** ("vollumfängliche Steuererleichterung") — qualifying biofuel pays effectively **~0** mineral-oil tax (base + surtax both remitted) | MinöStG Art. 12b | in force | CONFIRMED | S2, S4 |
| Precondition | Fuel must meet **ecological and social minimum requirements** (proof required from producer/importer); waste/residue-based fuels made per state of the art are deemed to satisfy the ecological criteria (Art. 12b para 1 lit. a–d) | MinöStG Art. 12b; USG Art. 35d; IBTV/ETrV | in force | PRIMARY-ONLY | S2, S4 |
| Feedstock restriction (cross-ref) | Food/feed-crop and food-competing feedstocks excluded (USG Art. 35d); since 2013 the relief effectively targets **waste/residue-based** biogenic fuels | USG Art. 35d | in force | CROSS-REF (sustainability agent) | S8 |

- Market-access linkage: a **BAZG tax-relief grant automatically confers BAFU
  market-placement authorisation** ("Eine Bewilligung zur Steuererleichterung des
  BAZG hat automatisch die Bewilligung zur Inverkehrbringung durch das BAFU zur
  Folge"). If **no** relief is claimed, prior BAFU authorisation is needed separately
  [S6].

### 2.2 Exact relief amount per fuel type — quantified against the fossil rates

BAZG states the relief is **full, "je nach Produkt von bis zu 75 Rappen je Liter"**
(up to ~75 Rp/L depending on product) [S2]. Because it is a *full* exemption, the
amount remitted equals the entire mineral-oil tax + surtax that would otherwise apply
to that product's tariff line — i.e. the biofuel is brought to ~0 tax:

| Renewable fuel | Fossil tariff line it displaces | Mineral-oil tax otherwise due | Relief (remitted) | Net tax after relief | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bioethanol / renewable ethanol | Petrol | 76.82 Rp/L | full (~76.8 Rp/L) | ~0 | CONFIRMED (full-exemption principle); PRIMARY-ONLY (per-litre figure derived) |
| Biodiesel (FAME) | Diesel | 79.57 Rp/L | full (~79.6 Rp/L) | ~0 | CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY (derived) |
| HVO (hydrotreated veg. oil) | Diesel | 79.57 Rp/L | full (~79.6 Rp/L) | ~0 | CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY (derived) |
| Plant/animal oils | Diesel | 79.57 Rp/L | full | ~0 | CONFIRMED / PRIMARY-ONLY |
| Biogas / bio-CNG / biomethane | Gas motor-fuel line | applicable gas-fuel rate | full | ~0 | CONFIRMED (listed as eligible); rate line not pinned this pass (GAP) |

- The BAZG "up to 75 Rp/L" figure is a rounded ceiling reflecting the diesel/petrol
  tariff magnitudes; the exact per-product remission tracks each product's own tariff
  line. The **base + surtax are both remitted** — this is why net tax is ~0, not just
  the surtax [S2, S4].

### 2.3 Volume cap / quota / budget — and the mandate-less design

| Item | Finding | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Volume cap or quota on the relief | **NONE** — any qualifying volume receives full relief; the relief is not capped, quota-ed or budget-limited | PRIMARY-ONLY | S2, S9 |
| Blending mandate conditioning | **NONE** — Switzerland has **no road blending mandate**, so the relief is **NOT** restricted to volumes "beyond an obligation" (contrast Sweden/Finland state-aid design) | CONFIRMED | S2, country-report §1 |
| Fiscal offset (revenue neutrality) | Tax losses from the relief are offset by a **+3.7 Rp/L increase in the petrol/diesel surtax** (in force 1 Jan 2021), calibrated to keep the scheme budget-neutral **through 31 Dec 2028** | PRIMARY-ONLY (SECO) | S9 |

- **Key distinction (for the user):** the Swiss relief is an **open-ended,
  uncapped, non-mandate-conditioned full exemption**. It is fiscally *offset* (not
  *rationed*) via the fossil surtax. This differs structurally from EU MS where
  biofuel tax relief is a state-aid measure limited to over-compliance volumes.
- **Flag / mismatch:** the surtax financing window was legislated to run to **end
  2028**, but the relief itself now runs to **end 2030** (§3). Whether the +3.7 Rp/L
  offset is extended to 2030 was not pinned this pass — `GAP`.

## 4. Interaction with the CO2-Act compensation obligation (no double-counting)

Switzerland has **no mandate**; fossil-fuel importers instead face a **CO2
compensation obligation** (Kompensationspflicht): **30 % of transport CO2 in 2026**
(25 % in 2025), rising **+5 pts/yr to 50 % by 2030**, min. 12 pts domestic (max 18 %
abroad in 2026), max 5 Rp/L pass-through [S5; cross-ref country-report §1–2].

**How the tax relief and the compensation obligation interact:**

| Question | Finding | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Do renewable fuels reduce the compensation base? | Yes — placing renewable (non-fossil) fuel on the market lowers the **fossil** CO2 volume that is subject to compensation | PRIMARY-ONLY | S5, S11 |
| Can renewable fuels count *toward* an importer's compensation? | Yes — importers may fulfil part of the obligation by bringing renewable fuels to market / via emission-reduction attestations (Bescheinigungen) | PRIMARY-ONLY | S11 |
| **Can the SAME volume be BOTH tax-exempt AND counted for compensation?** | **NO.** The ecological value of a fuel is tracked by a **guarantee of origin (Herkunftsnachweis, HKN)** in the Pronovo database. Once an HKN is **assigned to one instrument** (mineral-oil-tax relief *or* CO2 compensation), it is spent: *"Mit der Zuweisung des HKN ist der ökologische Mehrwert des Stoffes abgegolten."* | CONFIRMED | S12 |
| Anti-double-counting mechanism | Mandatory **HKN cancellation (Entwertung)** in the Pronovo/Vollzugsstelle database when the fuel is declared; the HKN can be attributed to only one policy instrument | CONFIRMED | S12 |

- **Bottom line:** the two instruments are mutually exclusive **at the volume/HKN
  level** — a biofuel volume that takes the full mineral-oil-tax exemption cannot
  *also* be surrendered as compensation for the fossil obligation, and vice versa.
  The guarantee-of-origin (Pronovo) system is the single anti-double-counting gate
  (mandatory since 1 Jan 2025).

## 5. Aviation SAF — tax treatment

| Item | Finding | Legal basis | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fossil kerosene (scheduled commercial aviation) | **Exempt from mineral-oil tax** (fuelled at customs airports, conditions apply) | MinöStG **Art. 17(2)(a)**; MinöStV | CONFIRMED | S13 |
| SAF (sustainable aviation fuel) tax status | SAF is **not** granted the same fossil-kerosene exemption route; because commercial jet fuel is already tax-free, the Art. 12b biogenic-road-fuel relief brings **no** aviation cost advantage — SAF ends up **costlier** than fossil kerosene | MinöStG (no aviation biofuel relief) | PRIMARY-ONLY (Tier-1 kerosene exemption S13; SAF-not-exempt point rests on Tier-2 S14) | S13, S14 |
| SAF blending obligation (cross-ref) | **2 % SAF from 1 Jan 2026** at Zurich/Geneva, mirroring EU ReFuelEU — a **mandate**, handled by the mandates agent | CO2/aviation law; ReFuelEU alignment | CROSS-REF | country-report §1 |

- **Net picture:** the Swiss biofuel *tax* relief is a **road-fuel** instrument. SAF
  is pushed by a **blending obligation**, not a tax incentive; there is no
  SAF-specific mineral-oil-tax relief, and SAF does not inherit the fossil-kerosene
  exemption — a deliberate asymmetry that leaves SAF fiscally disadvantaged. **Flag:
  the "SAF not exempt like fossil kerosene" point should be Tier-1 corroborated (only
  Tier-2 press this pass).**

## 6. Trade / customs angle — imported biofuels

| Item | Finding | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can imported biofuels access the same relief? | **Yes** — BAZG treats **"Hersteller und Importeure"** (producers and importers) identically; relief is applied for from BAZG regardless of origin; no origin-based discrimination | CONFIRMED | S2, S6 |
| Import quota on the relief | **None identified** — the relief is uncapped and open to imports meeting the ecological/social criteria | PRIMARY-ONLY | S2 |
| Operating permit | Domestic **production** of renewable fuels needs a BAZG operating permit (Herstellungsbewilligung) independent of any relief application; importers register/apply for relief | PRIMARY-ONLY | S9 |
| Customs tariff line for imported biofuels | Not pinned this pass — **GAP** (the mineral-oil-tax regime governs; tariff/HS detail unverified) | GAP | — |

- **For the user's trade interest:** the relief is **non-discriminatory by origin** —
  imported bioethanol/FAME/HVO/biomethane meeting the criteria get the same full
  exemption as domestic product, with no import quota. Sustainability proof (IBTV/HKN)
  is the gating condition, not nationality.

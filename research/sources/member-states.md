# Member-State Primary Source Registry

One entry per EU member state: the national biofuel obligation scheme, the
institutions that own mandates / taxation / sustainability, and the official
legal database. **This registry deliberately contains no mandate percentages or
tax rates** — those change too often to store; agents must fetch current values
live and date-stamp them (see `research/METHODOLOGY.md`).

Domains are given instead of deep links because ministries reorganise their
sites often. If a domain has moved, note it in the report's Gaps section and
update this file in the same PR as the report.

Legend: **M** = mandates authority · **T** = tax/excise authority ·
**S** = sustainability/certification authority · **L** = official legal database

## Austria (AT)
- Scheme: dual obligation under the **Kraftstoffverordnung 2012** — energy substitution (petrol/diesel sub-quotas) + GHG-intensity reduction
- **M/S:** Federal Ministry for Innovation, Mobility and Infrastructure (**BMIMI**) — bmimi.gv.at; climate/energy portfolio — bmk.gv.at; **elNa** registry run by Umweltbundesamt — umweltbundesamt.at
- **T:** Federal Ministry of Finance (Mineralölsteuer) — bmf.gv.at
- **L:** Rechtsinformationssystem (RIS) — ris.bka.gv.at

## Belgium (BE)
- Scheme: volume blending obligation (federal law on product standards / RD on biofuel blending)
- **M/S:** FPS Public Health & Environment — health.belgium.be; FPS Economy — economie.fgov.be
- **T:** FPS Finance, Customs & Excise — finance.belgium.be
- **L:** Moniteur belge / Belgisch Staatsblad — ejustice.just.fgov.be

## France (FR)
- Scheme: **TIRUERT** (incentive tax on the use of renewable energy in transport) — a tax-penalty mechanism rather than a quota
- **M:** Ministry for Ecological Transition (DGEC) — ecologie.gouv.fr
- **T:** DGDDI (customs, historic administrator) — douane.gouv.fr; DGFiP — impots.gouv.fr
- **S:** CarbuRe registry (DGEC); recognised voluntary schemes per RED
- **L:** Légifrance — legifrance.gouv.fr

## Germany (DE)
- Scheme: **GHG-reduction quota (THG-Quote)**, §§ 37a–37h BImSchG
- **M:** Biokraftstoffquotenstelle at Hauptzollamt Frankfurt (Oder) — zoll.de; Federal Ministry for the Environment, Climate Action, Nature Conservation and Nuclear Safety (BMUKN, formerly BMUV) — bmukn.de; Umweltbundesamt — umweltbundesamt.de
- Note (2026-07-04): RED III transposed by the Zweites THG-Quote-Gesetz (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 163, in force 6/7 Jun 2026); 2026 quota 12 %, trajectory to 65 % by 2040; new RFNBO sub-quota and advanced-biofuels minimum
- **T:** Customs (Energiesteuer) — zoll.de; Federal Ministry of Finance — bundesfinanzministerium.de
- **S:** BLE (Federal Office for Agriculture and Food), **Nabisy** registry — ble.de / nabisy.ble.de
- **L:** gesetze-im-internet.de

## Netherlands (NL)

## Cross-cutting checks for every country

1. EUR-Lex **national transposition (NIM)** page for RED III (2023/2413) —
   lists the country's notified implementing acts.
2. DG TAXUD **excise duty tables / TEDB** — official current excise rates to
   cross-check against the national tax authority.
3. The country's **NECP** (National Energy and Climate Plan, on the Commission
   site) — states planned future mandate trajectories.
4. USDA FAS **GAIN Biofuels Annual** for the country — best single secondary
   corroboration document.

## 2. Legislated Trajectory & 2030 Target

### Ethanol in Gasoline (E10):

**Historical Mandate & Collapse:**
- **September 2017 announcement:** Chinese government proposed nationwide E10 rollout to entire country by 2020
  - *Source:* NDRC official statement; documented by USDA FAS, IEA, Advanced Biofuels USA
  - *Target demand:* ~20 billion liters/year (4× actual 2020 consumption)
- **January 2020: Official cancellation** — nationwide target abandoned due to food security concerns and corn stock depletion
  - *Source:* Government of China (official press); confirmed by USDA FAS (2020 report)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **2019 cap:** No expansion of E10 pilot areas after 2019
  - *Source:* USDA FAS GAIN reports; ACFA (Singapore); Advanced Biofuels USA
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**Current E10 Provinces (15 total):**
Historical implementation (2004–2019):
1. Heilongjiang (pilot 2002, province-wide 2004)
2. Jilin (2004)
3. Anhui (2005)
4. Henan
5. Liaoning
6. Tianjin
7. Hebei (partial)
8. Shandong (partial)
9. Jiangsu (partial)
10. Inner Mongolia (partial)
11. Hubei (partial)
12. Guangxi
13. Others (7 additional provinces with partial implementation)

**Status as of 2026:**
- E10 remains in these provinces; no new areas added since 2019
- Blend rate enforcement variable; COVID-era enforcement gaps reported but recovering
- No national E10 mandate or 2026–2030 target published
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (negative finding)

**No national trajectory for E10 beyond current regional pilots**
- *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

### Biodiesel (B5/B10/B20):

**No national mandate.** Only Shanghai has mandatory B5:
- **Shanghai B5:** 5% biodiesel in diesel (municipal regulation)
  - *Effective:* Ongoing
  - *Source:* USDA FAS GAIN (2026 report)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **B10, B20 standards:** Technically published but lack unified national standards; no mandate
  - *Source:* USDA FAS (noting "higher-blended fuels such as B10 and B20 lack unified standards")
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY**

**Feedstock (Used Cooking Oil / UCO & Biodiesel Exports):**
- **Major policy shift (December 1, 2024):** UCO export tax rebate (13%) discontinued
  - *Objective:* Shift biodiesel industry from export-focused to domestic
  - *Source:* Ministry of Finance & State Administration of Taxation (official announcement Nov. 15, 2024); USDA FAS
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **2026 exports:** Biodiesel exports 353,826 tonnes (first 5 months), up 12.3% YoY (vs. 315,047 tonnes, same period 2025)
  - *Note:* Exports rising despite policy push for domestication (suggests weak domestic demand)
  - *Source:* Fastmarkets (May 2026); USDA FAS
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**
- **EU anti-dumping duties:** Imposed on Chinese biodiesel; driving pivot to SAF production
  - *Source:* Fastmarkets; multiple trade press reports
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**No national biodiesel mandate for 2026 or 2030**
- *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (negative finding)

### Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF):

**CAAC Phased Mandate (announced 2024, pilot implementation 2025–2026):**
- **2% by 2025 (announced target)** — NOT fully achieved; pilot phase active
  - *Announced:* 2024
  - *Source:* CAAC official; Modern Diplomacy; Green Air News
  - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED** (announced; not operatively achieved as full mandate)
- **Actual 2025 Implementation — Pilot Phase:**
  - **1% SAF blending** — started March 19, 2025
  - **Scope:** Four airports only:
    - Beijing Daxing
    - Chengdu Shuangliu
    - Zhengzhou Xinzheng
    - Ningbo Lishe
  - **Flights covered:** All domestic flights departing from these 4 airports
  - *Source:* CAAC announcement (Sept. 2024 NDRC/CAAC pilot MoU); confirmed by USDA FAS (Aug. 2025), Modern Diplomacy
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**Phased Target (2024 policy framework):**
- 2% by 2025 (policy target; pilot only achieved 1% at 4 airports as of March 19, 2025)
- 15% by 2030
  - *Source:* CAAC; Civil Aviation Administration framework
  - *Confidence:* **PROPOSED** (policy target announced; pilot implementation ongoing; not yet nationwide law)

**14th Five-Year Plan (2021–2025) Non-Mandatory Goal:**
- **20,000 tonnes SAF consumption by 2025** (non-mandatory)
- **50,000 tonnes accumulated consumption** (2021–2025)
  - *Source:* State Council; USDA FAS
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY**

**2026 Outlook:**
- SAF pilot expected to expand; no official nationwide 2% mandate yet published (as of July 2026)
- April 2025: **SAF export pilot programme launched** — China positioning as major SAF exporter, not just domestic user
  - *Rationale:* Biodiesel exports collapsed (EU duties); SAF production seen as alternative export revenue
  - *Source:* USDA FAS; Green Air News
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

## 3. Advanced-Biofuel & Sub-Quotas

**No advanced-biofuel sub-quotas or RFNBO-equivalent targets published.**

**Research & Development Status:**
- Alcohol-to-Jet (ATJ) pathways being explored; no commercialization mandate
- Synthetic/coal-based ethanol production expanding (non-biofuel pathway)
  - *2025 status:* Synthetic ethanol production doubled vs. 2024
  - *Source:* USDA FAS (Aug. 2025)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**Biofuel Feedstock Eligibility:**
- E10 (grain-based): Corn, cassava (limited by food security policy)
- Biodiesel: UCO/used oil; palm stearin (import-based)
- SAF: Feedstock diversification under study (ATJ, hydroprocessing); no standard pathway mandated
  - *Confidence:* **GAP** (no published feedstock criteria)

## 4. Fuel-Tax Treatment of Biofuels

**No dedicated tax incentive or penalty regime for biofuels found.**

**Excise & VAT (Value-Added Tax):**
- China's fuel excise tax applies uniformly to petroleum products (petrol, diesel)
- E10/biodiesel blends: Treated as standard fuels (no reduced rate found)
- **No exemption or incentive for biofuel producers or users** (unlike India or EU)
  - *Source:* USDA FAS; IEA databases
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (negative finding)

**Export Tax Rebate (UCO):**
- **Terminated Dec. 1, 2024** (13% rebate cancelled)
  - *Objective:* Reduce export pressure on domestic biodiesel feedstock
  - *Source:* Ministry of Finance (Nov. 15, 2024 announcement)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

**No specific pricing mechanism for biofuel procurement** (unlike India's OMC-based system)
- *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (negative finding)

## 5. Sustainability & Certification Regime

**No national sustainability criteria or lifecycle-GHG framework.**

**Absence of Key Elements:**
- **No mandatory GHG-saving threshold** for biofuels (unlike EU RED III 65–80% requirement)
- **No feedstock sustainability rules** (no restrictions on palm, soy, or high-ILUC feedstocks at national level)
- **No certification scheme requirement** (ISCC voluntary; no national or mandatory scheme)
- **No registry or monitoring system** for biofuel supply chains
  - *Source:* USDA FAS ("China has not established sustainability criteria or lifecycle emissions standards for biofuels")
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (multiple Tier 2 sources converge on this finding)

**International Certification (Voluntary):**
- **ISCC (International Sustainability and Carbon Certification):** Some Chinese biodiesel exporters use ISCC for EU market access; not mandated domestically
  - *Confidence:* **PRIMARY-ONLY** (inferred from trade practice; no official data)

**14th Five-Year Plan Environmental Framework:**
- Broader ecological and environmental policy adopted (March 12, 2026: Ecological and Environmental Code effective Aug. 15, 2026)
- **No specific biofuel sustainability provisions within this framework**
  - *Source:* Ministry of Ecology and Environment (MEE)
  - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED** (negative finding)

## 8. Five-Line Summary

China has **no national biofuel mandate** for ethanol, biodiesel, or other renewable fuels; a proposed nationwide E10 target (announced 2017 for 2020) was **abandoned in January 2020** due to food security concerns, leaving only 15 regional E10 pilots unchanged since 2019 with variable enforcement. **Sustainable Aviation Fuel mandates announced by CAAC target 2% by 2025 and 15% by 2030**, but actual implementation remains in a **1% pilot phase (started March 19, 2025) at only 4 major airports**, with no nationwide operationalization date. **Shanghai is the only region with mandatory B5 biodiesel**, while biodiesel exports remain elevated (353,826 tonnes, Jan.–May 2026) despite cancellation of the UCO export tax rebate (Dec. 1, 2024) intended to shift the industry toward domestic use. **China has no statutory sustainability criteria, certification mandate, or lifecycle-GHG framework** for biofuels and is the only top-5 SAF-producing nation without dedicated policy incentives or long-term regulatory mandates to support the sector.

### Additional Notes on China's Regulatory Context

**Why No National Biofuel Mandate?**
1. **Food Security Concerns (Primary):** Abandoned 2020 E10 target due to sharp decline in corn stocks and production capacity constraints
   - *Source:* USDA FAS; government sources (as reported)
   - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

2. **EV Adoption Impact:** Electric vehicle growth reducing domestic fuel demand, lowering incentive for ethanol blending expansion
   - *Source:* USDA FAS (Aug. 2025); U.S. EIA
   - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

3. **Export Priorities:** Biodiesel and SAF seen primarily as export revenue streams, not domestic fuel strategy
   - *Source:* USDA FAS; trade press
   - *Confidence:* **CONFIRMED**

4. **Policy Fragmentation:** Energy policy devolved to regional governments; no central mandate authority for biofuels
   - *Source:* USDA FAS analysis
   - *Confidence:* **UNCORROBORATED** (structural observation; limited hard evidence in sources)

**Confidence in "No National Mandate" Finding:**
- **Highly confident (CONFIRMED):** Multiple independent Tier 2 sources converge on this finding
- **No contrary sources found** asserting a national mandate
- **Negative finding is valid** and rigorously supported

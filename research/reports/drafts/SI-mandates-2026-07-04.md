## 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Who is obligated:** fuel suppliers ("dobavitelji goriv") placing petrol/diesel/other transport energy on the Slovenian market. [1]
- **Calculation:** the Uredba sets annual minimum energy shares and the calculation method (multipliers for advanced fuels/renewable electricity), plus fossil-fuel/biofuel category definitions. [1]
- **Banking/transfer:** the 2024 amendment (Ur. l. RS 70/24, 23 Aug 2024) introduced carry-over of energy-share deficits/surpluses across years (deficits/surpluses achieved in 2024 transferable to 2022/2023/2025/2026), and deleted the former Art. 4(2). [7]
- **Registry / certificates:** no tradable-certificate market identified; compliance is a direct energy-share obligation reported to the Ministry of the Environment, Climate and Energy. Reporting mechanics — see sustainability agent.
- **Enforcement:** administrative, via the parent act (FURS/ministry) — see fuel-tax section for any tax interaction.

### 5-line summary — Slovenia
1. **Obligation type:** energy-share RES-in-transport obligation on fuel suppliers (Uredba o OVE v prometu), plus −6% FQD GHG-intensity reduction.
2. **Current level (2026):** minimum **13.8%** RES energy share; advanced-biofuel (IX-A) sub-target ≥1% (2025)→3.5% (2030); IX-B cap 1.7%; crop cap 7%.
3. **Next legislated change:** step-up to 15.8% (2027), 18.3% (2028), 20.8% (2029–2030) per Uredba Art. 4(1).
4. **RED III status:** primary transposition law **ZSROVE-1 adopted (Ur. l. RS 112/2025, 29 Dec 2025)**; late vs deadline but not escalated in June 2026; Commission assessment likely pending.
5. **Flags:** penalty amount = GAP (in ZSROVE-1); 2026+ target rows PRIMARY-ONLY pending independent corroboration.

# ReFuelEU Aviation — Compliance Report (Sections 1–4)

- **Instrument:** ReFuelEU Aviation — Regulation (EU) 2023/2405 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 18 October 2023 on ensuring a level playing field for sustainable air transport
- **Legal form:** directly applicable EU Regulation — **no national transposition**. National roles are limited to designating a competent authority (Art. 11) and imposing penalties (Art. 12). EASA ("the Agency") holds Union-level reporting, data-publication and labelling functions (Arts. 13–14).

- **Report date:** 2026-07-05

- **Corroboration pass:** COMPLETED 2026-07-05 by source-corroborator (trajectory independently corroborated; verification sources appended)

## 1. Scope & who is bound

The Regulation applies to three actor groups in commercial air transport at **Union airports** (Art. 2(1)):

| Actor / term | Definition & threshold | Legal basis | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Union airport** | An airport where **passenger traffic was > 800 000 passengers** OR **freight traffic was > 100 000 tonnes** in the previous reporting period | Art. 3(1) | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, S5 |
| **Aviation fuel supplier** | A fuel supplier supplying aviation fuel or hydrogen for aviation at a Union airport (bound by the SAF share obligation, Art. 4) | Art. 3(19) | CONFIRMED | S1 |
| **Aircraft operator** | A person that operated ≥ **500 commercial passenger** air transport flights, or ≥ **52 commercial all-cargo** air transport flights, departing from Union airports in the reporting period (bound by the uplift/anti-tankering obligation, Art. 5) | Art. 3(3) | CONFIRMED | S1 |
| **Airport managing body** | Also within scope (facilitation/infrastructure duties) | Art. 2(1) | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1 |

**Entry into force & application (Art. 18):**

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry into force | 20th day after publication in the OJ | Art. 18 | (Regulation dated 18 Oct 2023; OJ L series) | CONFIRMED | S1 |
| General application | Applies **from 1 January 2024** | Art. 18 | 2024-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S4 |
| Core obligations deferred | **Articles 4, 5, 6, 8 and 10 apply from 1 January 2025** — i.e. the SAF-supply obligation and the uplift obligation both start 1 Jan 2025 | Art. 18 | 2025-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S4 |

Geographic coverage: obligations attach at each **Union airport** (EU airports above the thresholds above); the supplier obligation is on fuel "made available to aircraft operators at each Union airport."

## 2. Core obligation & trajectory

**Supplier obligation (Art. 4(1)):** aviation fuel suppliers shall ensure that **all aviation fuel made available to aircraft operators at each Union airport contains the minimum shares of SAF** set out in **Annex I**. Annex I also fixes a sub-quota of **synthetic aviation fuel** (e-SAF / RFNBO-derived aviation fuel).

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAF minimum share** | **2 %** | Art. 4(1) + Annex I | 2025-01-01 (2025–2029) | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, S3, S4 |
| SAF minimum share | **6 %** | Annex I | 2030-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, S3, S4 |
| SAF minimum share | **20 %** | Annex I | 2035-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, V1, V2 |
| SAF minimum share | **34 %** | Annex I | 2040-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, V1, V2, V3 |
| SAF minimum share | **42 %** | Annex I | 2045-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, V1, V2, V3 |
| SAF minimum share | **70 %** | Annex I | 2050-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2, S3 |

**Synthetic aviation fuel (e-SAF) sub-quota — Annex I:**

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| e-SAF sub-quota | **1.2 %** as an **average over 2030–2031**, of which a **minimum 0.7 % each year** | Annex I | 2030-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3, V1, V3 |
| e-SAF sub-quota | **2.0 %** as an **average over 2032–2034**; within this: min **1.2 %/yr for 2032–2033**, and min **2.0 % from 1 Jan 2034** | Annex I | 2032-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, V3 |
| e-SAF sub-quota | **5 %** | Annex I | 2035-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, V3 |
| e-SAF sub-quota | **10 %** | Annex I | 2040-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, V3 |
| e-SAF sub-quota | **15 %** | Annex I | 2045-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, V3 |
| e-SAF sub-quota | **35 %** | Annex I | 2050-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, V1, V3 |

Note on the averaging rule: for 2030–2031 and 2032–2034 the synthetic sub-quota is expressed as a **period average** with an annual floor, so a supplier may under-supply e-SAF in the first year of the window provided the two/three-year average and the annual minimum floor are met. This is the "2030–2031 averaging" the task flagged — verified in Annex I and independently corroborated (V3).

**Anti-tankering / uplift obligation (Art. 5) — on aircraft operators, not suppliers:**

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum uplift | The **yearly quantity of aviation fuel uplifted by an aircraft operator at a given Union airport shall be ≥ 90 % of the yearly aviation fuel required** at that airport | Art. 5(1) | 2025-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S2 |

Purpose: prevents "fuel tankering" (carrying excess cheaper fuel loaded elsewhere to avoid uplifting SAF-blended fuel at Union airports). The 90 % floor is measured per operator per Union airport per reporting year.

## 3. Flexibility & mechanics

| Item | Value | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Transitional averaging across airports** | By derogation from Art. 4(1), **from 1 Jan 2025 until 31 Dec 2034** a supplier may meet the Annex I minimum SAF shares as a **weighted average over all aviation fuel it supplied across Union airports** in the reporting period (rather than per-airport) | **Art. 15** | 2025-01-01 → 2034-12-31 | CONFIRMED | S1, S5 |
| Per-airport rule after transition | From **1 Jan 2035** the minimum-share obligation applies **per Union airport** (transitional flexibility expires) | Art. 15 (a contrario) + Art. 4(1) | 2035-01-01 | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1 |
| **Make-up / cumulative shortfall** | A supplier that accumulates a shortfall against the SAF or synthetic sub-obligation in a reporting period **must supply that same fuel quantity equal to the shortfall in the subsequent reporting period, in addition to that period's obligation** | Art. 12(8) | 2025-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1 |
| Book-and-claim | **Not established** in the 2023/2405 text as an operational flexibility for the mandate; a **Union labelling scheme** (Art. 14) and reporting via the EASA digital tool / EU SAF Clearing House exist, and book-and-claim is a **review-clause** topic (Art. 17 reports & review), not a current compliance flexibility | Arts. 14, 17 | n/a | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1, S6 |

Mechanics/administration: suppliers report to the competent authority and the Agency (Art. 10); EASA ("the Agency") publishes an annual technical report with SAF volumes, market conditions and **price information/trends** (Art. 13). Reporting is done via the EASA ReFuelEU Aviation digital reporting tool; EASA also runs the EU SAF Clearing House and publishes a **Reference Price for Aviation Fuels Report** (feeds penalty calculation). Environmental labelling scheme for aircraft operators is Art. 14.

## 4. Penalties & economic instruments

All fines sit in **Article 12 ("Enforcement")** — imposed by Member State competent authorities (Art. 11). Fines must be "proportionate and dissuasive" and meet the statutory floors below. (Note: task referenced "Articles 12–13"; in the consolidated text the **fines are all in Art. 12**, while **Art. 13** is "Data collection and publication." Flagged so the lead does not mis-cite. Corroborator: confirmed — Art. 12 = penalties, Art. 13 = data publication.)

| Item | Value / formula | Legal basis | Effective | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **SAF-shortfall fine (suppliers)** | ≥ **2×** [ (yearly average price of **conventional aviation fuel** − price of **SAF**) **per tonne** ] × (quantity of aviation fuel not complying with the minimum SAF share) | Art. 12(4) | 2025-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| **Synthetic-shortfall fine (suppliers)** | ≥ **2×** [ (yearly average price of **synthetic aviation fuel** − price of **conventional aviation fuel**) **per tonne** ] × (quantity not complying with the synthetic minimum/average share) | Art. 12(5) | 2030-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1, S3 |
| **Anti-tankering fine (aircraft operators)** | ≥ **2×** [ yearly average price of aviation fuel **per tonne** ] × (total yearly **non-tanked** quantity, i.e. the shortfall below the 90 % uplift) | Art. 12(2) | 2025-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1 |
| **Make-up obligation (does not exempt fine)** | Payment of a fine does **not release** the supplier from the Art. 12(8) obligation to supply the shortfall quantity in the next reporting period | Art. 12(8) | 2025-01-01 | CONFIRMED | S1 |
| **Price-methodology transparency** | When imposing a fine, the competent authority must **explain the methodology applied for determining the price**; the Agency publishes price information/trends annually (Art. 13) | Art. 12(7), Art. 13 | 2025-01-01 | PRIMARY-ONLY | S1 |
| Illustrative magnitude (Tier-2, not legal) | Using 2024 EASA reference prices (~€700/t jet fuel, ~€2,000/t biofuel-SAF, ~€7,700/t e-kerosene): SAF-shortfall fine ≈ **€2,700/t**; e-SAF-shortfall fine ≈ **€14,000/t** | (illustration) | — | UNCORROBORATED (single Tier-2) | S3 |

**EU ETS interaction:** ReFuelEU Aviation is a supply/uplift mandate and operates **alongside** the EU Emissions Trading System for aviation and CORSIA (separate instruments); the Regulation itself does not set an ETS price. Detailed ETS/CORSIA interaction is out of this slice's scope — flagged for the section-4 (economic instruments) / another agent to complete. (Note: the aviation/maritime ETS and ReFuelEU are distinct from **ETS2** (road/buildings); do not conflate.)

## 6-line summary

1. **Scope:** directly-applicable EU Regulation (EU) 2023/2405; binds **aviation fuel suppliers** (SAF share, Art. 4) and **aircraft operators** (90% uplift, Art. 5) at **Union airports** (>800,000 pax or >100,000 t freight); applies from 1 Jan 2024, core obligations from 1 Jan 2025.
2. **SAF minimum share (Annex I):** 2% in 2025 → 6% in 2030 → 20% (2035) → 34% (2040) → 42% (2045) → **70% in 2050**. [CONFIRMED]
3. **e-SAF sub-quota (Annex I):** 1.2% averaged over 2030–2031 (≥0.7%/yr floor) → 2.0% averaged over 2032–2034 → 5% (2035) → 10% (2040) → 15% (2045) → 35% (2050). [CONFIRMED]
4. **Anti-tankering (Art. 5):** each aircraft operator must uplift ≥90% of yearly required aviation fuel at each Union airport. [CONFIRMED]
5. **Flexibility (Art. 15):** transitional weighted-average across all Union airports from 1 Jan 2025 to 31 Dec 2034, then per-airport; shortfalls must be made up the following period (Art. 12(8)); no operational book-and-claim in current text.
6. **Penalties (Art. 12):** fines ≥ 2× the per-tonne price difference (conventional vs SAF / vs synthetic) × non-compliant quantity for suppliers, and ≥ 2× fuel price × non-tanked quantity for operators; task's "Arts. 12–13" for penalties is actually **all Art. 12** (Art. 13 = data publication) — confirmed; illustrative €/t magnitudes are UNCORROBORATED single-source.

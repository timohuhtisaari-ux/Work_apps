Federal floor. US: RFS2 (EPA) — 2026 RVO 26.81 bn RINs (advanced 11.10, biomass-based diesel 9.07, cellulosic 1.36); a national volume mandate via RINs, plus the §45Z Clean Fuel Production Credit. Canada: Clean Fuel Regulations (SOR/2022-140) — carbon-intensity cut 3.5 gCO₂e/MJ (2023) → 14 (2030); volumetric floor ≥5% gasoline / ≥3% diesel (Nfld & territories exempt). "Program type" reflects the most stringent in-force state/provincial instrument on top of that floor; a state can run both an LCFS and a blend mandate — the card lists both. Several oft-cited state mandates (NM B5, LA, MT/HI E10, MA advanced) are repealed, suspended or dormant.

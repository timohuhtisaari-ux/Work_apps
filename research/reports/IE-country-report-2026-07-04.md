## Executive summary

Ireland operates the **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)**, a **certificate-based, energy-content** obligation on mineral-oil suppliers administered by the **National Oil Reserves Agency (NORA)**. For **2026 the overall obligation is 32 % by energy content** (up from 25 %), with a **5 % advanced-biofuel + RFNBO sub-target**, a **3 % crop cap**, and a **high-ILUC (palm) cap cut to 60 % of 2019 supply** — all CONFIRMED and effective 1 Jan 2026 via **S.I. No. 664 of 2025**, which transposes the RED III transport elements (Ireland is therefore **not** in the 29 Apr 2026 CJEU referral). Shortfalls are settled by buy-out charges of **€0.05/MJ** (main) and **€0.08/MJ** (advanced). Fuel is taxed via **Mineral Oil Tax** under the Finance Act 1999, split into a non-carbon and a carbon component; **temporary MOT reductions (and a cut NORA levy) are in force but revert to standard rates around 1 August 2026** (petrol €706.14, diesel €615.76 per 1000 L), and biofuels receive partial MOT relief equal to the carbon component. A supplier's core obligations are to open a NORA RTFO account, hold NORA-recognised voluntary-scheme certification with PoS per consignment, claim the correct certificate colour, appoint an independent verifier, and meet the advanced sub-target or pay the €0.08/MJ buy-out.

## 1. Biofuel mandates

Ireland operates the **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)**, a
**certificate-based, energy-content** obligation on suppliers of mineral oil to
ensure a minimum renewable share (by energy content) of the petrol and diesel
they place on the market. It is administered by the **National Oil Reserves
Agency (NORA)**. The operative framework is **S.I. No. 350 of 2022** (European
Union (Renewable Energy) Regulations 2022 — RED II transposition, converting the
former Biofuel Obligation Scheme to the RTFO), amended for 2026 by **S.I. No. 664
of 2025** (European Union (Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation) Regulations 2025),
which implements the **RED III (Directive (EU) 2023/2413) transport elements** and
the updated Annex IX list (Directive (EU) 2024/1405). A parallel 6 % carbon-
intensity reduction obligation sits in **S.I. No. 160 of 2017** (FQD Art. 7a).

### 1.2 Scheme mechanics

- **Certificates (3 colours):** issued by NORA per MJ of qualifying renewable fuel —
  **Green** (Annex IX Part A / advanced feedstocks), **Orange** (other qualifying
  renewable fuels, e.g. UCO/Annex IX-B), **Red** (food/feed crop-based). Advanced
  obligation is met with Green (and RFNBO/renewable-electricity) certificates; the
  crop cap limits Red certificates. (S1)
- **Registry / obligated parties:** NORA maintains electronic accounts and the
  certificate register; obligated parties are suppliers of mineral oil (petrol and
  diesel) placed on the Irish market. (S1)
- **Double counting:** 2 certs/MJ for Annex IX feedstocks. (S1)
- **Buy-out:** an obligated party short of certificates pays the buy-out charge
  (€0.05/MJ; €0.08/MJ advanced) rather than a discretionary penalty — this caps
  compliance cost. (S1, V9)
- **Levy:** a biofuel levy of 0.1 euro-cent per litre applies to renewable
  transport fuel (NORA Act 2007 / 2010 Regs) — a tax-adjacent charge; cross-
  reference Section 2. (S2)
- Banking/borrowing/carry-over of certificates: not confirmed from a primary
  source in this pass — **gap** (note: gov.ie consultation notes up to 15 % of the
  advanced obligation may be met with prior-year advanced certificates).

## 2. Fuel taxation

Ireland levies **Mineral Oil Tax (MOT)** on motor fuels under Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the **Finance Act 1999 (as amended)** — the national instrument implementing the Energy Taxation Directive 2003/96/EC. Each MOT rate is the sum of a **non-carbon component** (Schedule 2 minus Schedule 2A) and a **carbon component / carbon tax** (Schedule 2A), the latter proportionate to CO2 emitted and set by a €-per-tonne-CO2 charge.

**Important timing:** From **25 March 2026** and again from **15 April 2026**, temporary reductions to the *non-carbon* component are in force. These cuts (with the NORA-levy cut) are legislated to be **reversed at the end of July 2026**, restoring the standard rates. The table below gives the **currently-in-force (15 Apr 2026) rates** and, in the notes, the standard rates that resume after reversal.

### Notes / interaction with the mandate

- **Biofuel relief mechanism.** MOT law provides a partial relief for biofuels set equal to the *carbon component* — often called a "carbon tax relief." A biofuel released for consumption therefore pays only the **non-carbon component**; the effective rate tracks the non-carbon rate. This is a fuel-type relief, distinct from the **Renewable Transport Fuel Obligation (RTFO)** certificate scheme (administered via NORA/Dept of Transport) — see Section 1; there is no Swedish/Finnish-style "above-the-obligation only" restriction attached to the MOT relief itself.
- **Substitute fuels.** Fuels used as a propellant "instead of petrol/diesel" are taxed at the corresponding petrol/diesel MOT rate unless a relief (e.g. biofuel relief) is claimed.
- **Carbon-tax trajectory (legislated).** Finance Act 2020 set a 10-year path raising the carbon charge to **€100/t CO2 by 2030**. Propellant fuels (petrol, auto-diesel) step up each October (they moved to **€71/t on 8 Oct 2025**); non-propellant fuels normally step up on 1 May, but the **1 May 2026 increase was postponed to 14 October 2026** (to €71/t). `CONFIRMED` (legislated) — S1.
- **Temporary cuts reverse ~end July 2026.** The 25 Mar / 15 Apr 2026 non-carbon MOT cuts and the NORA-levy cut are set to end 31 Jul 2026, restoring petrol €706.14 / diesel €615.76. `CONFIRMED` — S1, S2.
- **Annual October Budget.** Standard-rate and carbon-component changes are made in the October Budget / Finance Act cycle.
- **ETS2.** EU emissions trading for road-transport fuels (ETS2) — allowance surrender from **2028** (2027 emissions; auctioning from 2027) under **Directive (EU) 2023/959 (ETS2, Chapter IVa of Dir. 2003/87/EC)**. [CORRECTION — the draft's "Regulation (EU) 2026/667" is the 2040 EU climate-target law, NOT ETS2.] `CONFIRMED` (EU-level). (V4, V5)

## 4. Compliance checklist for a fuel supplier

1. **Open an RTFO Account with NORA** as an obligated party (supplier of road-transport mineral oil placed on the Irish market) before placing product on the market. → **NORA**.
2. **Meet the 2026 obligation: 32 % renewable by energy content**, of which a **5 % advanced-biofuel + RFNBO sub-target** must be met with Green/RFNBO/renewable-electricity certificates. → **NORA** online system.
3. **Use voluntary schemes recognised by NORA** (ISCC EU, REDcert-EU, 2BSvs, SURE, UFAS…) whose scope/version is on NORA's list; issue **PoS per consignment**. → certification body / self.
4. **Claim the correct RTFC colour** — Green (Annex IX-A advanced), Orange (other incl. Annex IX-B UCO/animal fat), Red (crop, within the **3 % crop cap** from 2026). → **NORA** online system.
5. **Meet the RED GHG-saving thresholds** (50/60/65 %; 70 % RFNBO) and document per consignment. → self + verifier.
6. **Respect the high-ILUC cap** — high-ILUC-risk fuel limited to **60 % of 2019 disposals** (SI 664/2025), tapering to 0 by 2030. → self + verifier.
7. **Appoint an independent verifier** to assure data submitted to NORA and file the annual assurance report by NORA's determination dates. → verifier / **NORA**.
8. **Onboard to the Union Database (UDB)** and record PoS/transaction data for in-scope consignments (operational since 15 Jan 2024; EU mandatory-use date pending). → **UDB**.
9. **Avoid buy-out exposure** — a shortfall on the main obligation costs **€0.05/MJ** and on the advanced obligation **€0.08/MJ**. → **NORA**.
10. **Account for MOT correctly and watch the ~1 Aug 2026 reversion** — temporary MOT cuts and the reduced NORA levy end 31 Jul 2026, restoring petrol €706.14 / diesel €615.76 per 1000 L; biofuels pay only the non-carbon component. → **Revenue** / **NORA**.

## SAF-from-China — the surveillance question, pinned

**Bottom line:** As of 2026-07-13 the EU applies **no anti-dumping duty, no
countervailing duty, and no formal safeguard/prior-surveillance Regulation** to
sustainable aviation fuel from China. SAF was **deliberately excluded** from the
China biodiesel anti-dumping measure, and is instead **statistically monitored**
through **dedicated TARIC codes** so the Commission can detect an import surge and
act quickly if a complaint is lodged. **This "no duty, monitoring only" conclusion is
`CONFIRMED`** (see per-element labels below).

- **Exclusion:** Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) **2025/261** (definitive
  China biodiesel AD), **recital 22**: "at present SAF has different use and cannot
  be deemed interchangeable or in competition with HVO/FAME" — SAF "may therefore be
  excluded from the scope." SAF meeting ASTM D7566 is carved out. `CONFIRMED`
  (primary 2025/261 recital 22 [1] + independent EBB corroboration of the exclusion [4]).
- **Monitoring mechanism:** the Commission set up a **dedicated, code-based system**.
  The dedicated codes were operationalised in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU)
  **2025/1866** (of **12 September 2025**, OJ **15 September 2025**), whose **Article 1a**
  sets SAF monitoring TARIC codes **2710 19 11 10, 2710 19 15 10, 2710 19 21 10,
  2710 19 25 10, 2710 19 29 10** to track SAF (ASTM D7566-22) separately from dutiable
  biodiesel. `CONFIRMED` that the mechanism exists (primary 2025/1866 [10] + EBB, which
  independently notes the Commission created dedicated tracking codes [4]).
  The **exact 10-digit code list is `PRIMARY-ONLY`** — verified against the authoritative
  OJ text of 2025/1866 (Art 1a), but no independent second enumeration located; a live
  TARIC lookup is still recommended [10].
- **Legal character:** this is **customs monitoring within the AD framework**
  (statistical surveillance to police the SAF/biodiesel scope boundary), **not** a
  stand-alone import prior-surveillance regime under Regulation (EU) 2015/478. **No
  stand-alone Reg (EU) 2015/478 surveillance act on SAF exists** (none located; the codes
  live inside the AD correction Reg 2025/1866). The watchlist label "TARIC
  prior-surveillance" should be amended to **"TARIC monitoring codes (within China
  biodiesel AD framework)."** `PRIMARY-ONLY`.
- **No investigation on SAF:** no AD/CVD investigation on Chinese SAF has been
  initiated. The EBB publicly urged the Commission to act on Chinese SAF; the
  Commission has so far only monitored. `CONFIRMED` (no initiation notice located
  in the OJ C-series or DG TRADE ongoing-investigations list [9], Commission TRON).
  **This is the single most important escalation trigger to watch.**

> Cross-check note: the **US** biodiesel AD Reg 2021/1266 was itself corrected by
> **Reg (EU) 2025/1861** (12 Sep 2025), which — unlike the China measure — *adds*
> SAF/HVO (HEFA-pathway) CN/TARIC references to **clarify that the US measure's scope
> already captures such paraffinic products**. So SAF is treated differently by origin:
> **excluded** for China (2025/261), **clarified as within scope** for the US
> (2021/1266 as corrected). See US rows. `PRIMARY-ONLY` (from 2025/1861 OJ text).

**Gap/next check:** verify each SAF TARIC 10-digit code live in TARIC, and re-scan
the OJ C-series + DG TRADE "ongoing investigations" weekly for any SAF-China
initiation notice.

## Investigations / watch list (ongoing or expected)

1. **SAF — China (PRIORITY):** monitoring only today; watch for an AD/CVD
   **initiation notice** (OJ C-series) — EBB is pressing for one. Any surge in the
   SAF TARIC codes is the leading indicator. `CONFIRMED` no case yet.
2. **Biodiesel — Indonesia CVD (2019/2092):** **expiry review ongoing** (Notice
   C/2024/7405, EBB request 8 Sep 2024) — measures stay in force pending outcome
   (due within ~15 months of initiation). In parallel, **WTO DS618**: panel final
   report to parties 12 Jun 2025, **circulated 22 Aug 2025**, found the EU acted
   inconsistently on the CPO "less-than-adequate-remuneration" subsidy finding and
   parts of the injury/causation analysis; the **EU notified an appeal on 26 Sep 2025**
   (into the non-functioning Appellate Body). Watch: expiry-review result, EU
   appeal/MPIA route, any re-determination. `CONFIRMED`.
3. **Biodiesel — USA AD/CVD (2021/1266 & 2021/1267):** in force; **5-year term
   expires ~Aug 2026** (measures in force since 3 Aug 2021) — watch for an
   **expiry-review initiation** to keep them alive. Separately, the **Braya Renewable
   Fuels (Canada) new-exporter review** (Reg (EU) 2026/1341) is ongoing with the duty
   temporarily repealed and imports registered pending outcome (~9 months).
   `PRIMARY-ONLY` on the 2026 expiry timing.
4. **UCO/UCOME fraud (China & others):** no trade-defence case, but the 18 Jul 2025
   fraud examination found "systemic weaknesses" (not confirmed fraud), and
   Reg (EU) 2025/2181 tightens UCO import chain-of-custody. Watch for a possible
   future circumvention/AD angle or certification-scheme withdrawals. `CONFIRMED`.
5. **Anti-circumvention:** Indonesia→China/UK case **closed** (Decision 2024/1273,
   terminated after the EBB request was withdrawn; no measures extended). No new open
   circumvention case on China biodiesel located. Watch for a fresh circumvention
   initiation if trade shifts to third countries. `CONFIRMED`.

**Cross-references (other agents):** RED III sustainability/UCO certification,
blending mandates, and ReFuelEU SAF mandate levels are out of scope here — see the
sustainability and mandates reports. Reg (EU) 2025/2181 (UCO import conditions) sits
at the customs/sustainability boundary and is flagged for the sustainability agent.

**Non-biofuel measures checked and correctly excluded from this register:**
Reg (EU) **2026/244** (3 Feb 2026) = definitive AD on **high-pressure seamless steel
cylinders** from China (57.7%–90.3%); Reg (EU) **2026/1540** (6 Jul 2026) = definitive
AD on **passenger-car / light-lorry tyres** from China (4.3%–45.3%). Neither concerns
biofuels; both verified as false hits and excluded. `CONFIRMED` (non-biofuel).
